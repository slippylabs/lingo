In this lesson: **shapes**, saying what something **has**, and the **"of"** form.

## Shapes

- **krug** — circle
- **kvadrat** — square
- **trokut** — triangle
- **linija** — line
- **točka** — point

## "Has"

- **Kvadrat ima četiri strane.** — A square has four sides.
- **Trokut ima tri strane.** — A triangle has three sides.

After **tri** and **četiri**, a feminine noun ends in **‑e**: **strana** → **strane**.

## "Of"

A masculine noun adds **‑a** for "of":

- **Vidimo obris kvadrata.** — We see the outline of a square.
- **Stol ima oblik kvadrata.** — The table has the shape of a square.

## Matching describing words

- **Linija je ravna.** — The line is straight. (**linija** is feminine)
- **To je jednostavan oblik.** — This is a simple shape. (**oblik** is masculine)
- **Forma je zanimljiva.** — The shape is interesting. (**forma** is feminine)

> **Watch out:** **oblik** and **forma** both mean "shape", but one is masculine and one is feminine, so their describing words differ.
