In this lesson: **jobs**, and saying what someone **does for a living**.

## Jobs

- **učitelj** — teacher
- **doktor** — doctor
- **inženjer** — engineer
- **radnik** — worker
- **prodavač** — seller
- **vozač** — driver
- **kuhar** — cook

## No "a" before a job

Croatian has no word for "a":

- **Moj brat je učitelj.** — My brother is a teacher.
- **Ona je doktor.** — She is a doctor.
- **Moj otac je kuhar.** — My father is a cook.

## What they do

- **Vozač vozi autobus.** — The driver drives a bus.
- **Prodavač prodaje hranu.** — The seller sells food.
- **Kuhar kuha večeru.** — The cook is preparing dinner.
- **Doktor pomaže ljudima.** — The doctor helps people. (**pomagati** takes the dative: **ljudima**)

> **Watch out:** "people" is **ljudi**, and "to people" is **ljudima**.
