In this lesson: **kitchen things**, and the forms for **with**, **from**, **in** and **full of**.

## In the kitchen

- **nož** — knife
- **vilica** — fork
- **žlica** — spoon
- **tanjur** — plate
- **šalica** — cup
- **lonac** — pot
- **tava** — frying pan

## Little words, new endings

- **On jede vilicom.** — He eats with a fork. (a tool: ending **‑om**, no word for "with")
- **Ona pije iz šalice.** — She drinks from a cup. (**iz** + **‑e** for feminine)
- **Mi kuhamo u loncu.** — We cook in a pot. (**u** + **‑u** for masculine)

## Full of, a lot of

**pun** (full) and **mnogo** (a lot) take the "of" form:

- **Tanjur je pun hrane.** — The plate is full of food.
- **Žlica je puna papra.** — The spoon is full of pepper.
- **Mnogo šećera je u zdjeli.** — There is a lot of sugar in the bowl.

> **Watch out:** **lonac** loses its **a** when it changes: **u loncu**.
