In this lesson: talking about **the weather**.

## No word for "it"

Croatian has no word for the "it" in "it is warm":

- **Danas je toplo.** — It is warm today.
- **U Rusiji je hladno.** — In Russia it is cold.
- **Oblačno je danas.** — It is cloudy today.

## Rain and snow "fall"

- **Pada kiša.** — It is raining. (word for word: "rain falls")
- **Pada snijeg i kiša.** — Snow and rain are falling.

## More weather

- **Sunce svijetli.** — The sun is shining.
- **Vjetar puše jako.** — The wind is blowing strongly.
- **Mraz je jak.** — The frost is strong.
- **Vrijeme se mijenja.** — The weather is changing.

> **Watch out:** **vrijeme** means both "weather" and "time".
