In this lesson: **words for character** and the **conditional** ("would").

## Character

- **dobar** — good
- **hrabar** — brave
- **mudar** — wise
- **iskren** — sincere
- **lijen** — lazy
- **glup** — stupid
- **slab** — weak

A describing word can stand for a person: **Mudar zna mnogo.** — A wise person knows much.

## "Would"

The conditional is a "would" word (**bih**, **bi**, **bismo**, **biste**) plus the same verb form as the past tense:

- **bih** — I would
- **bi** — you / he / she / they would
- **bismo** — we would
- **biste** — you would (several people)

## "If … would"

**kad** + the conditional says "if" about something that is not true:

- **Kad bih bio hrabar, ne bih se bojao.** — If I were brave, I would not be afraid.
- **Kad ne bi bili lijeni, pomogli bismo im.** — If they were not lazy, we would help them.
- **Kad ne biste bili slabi, radili biste.** — If you were not weak, you would work.

> **Watch out:** "I would" is **bih**, with an *h*; **bi** is for you, he, she and they.
