In this lesson: **the seasons** and the **future tense**.

## Seasons

- **proljeće** — spring
- **ljeto** — summer
- **jesen** — autumn
- **zima** — winter

"In summer" and "in winter" are single words; spring and autumn use **u**:

- **ljeti** — in summer
- **zimi** — in winter
- **U proljeće raste cvijeće.** — In spring, flowers grow.
- **U jesen samo pada kiša.** — In autumn, it only rains.

## The future

The future is a short form of "will" (**ću**, **ćeš**, **će**, **ćemo**, **ćete**, **će**) plus the dictionary form of the verb:

- **Zimi će padati snijeg.** — In the winter it will snow.
- **U jesen ćemo vidjeti padajuće lišće.** — In the autumn we will see falling leaves.
- **Zimi ćete se igrati sa snijegom.** — In the winter you will play with snow.
- **Ljeti će biti toplo.** — In the summer it will be warm.

When the verb comes first, it drops its final **‑i** and is written apart: **bit ću** — I will be.

> **Watch out:** say **ljeti** and **zimi** without **u**, but **u proljeće** and **u jesen** with it.
