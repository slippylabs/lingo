In this lesson: **times of day** and the **past tense**.

## Time words

- **jučer** — yesterday
- **danas** — today
- **sutra** — tomorrow
- **ponoć** — midnight
- **Koliko je sati?** — What time is it?

## The past tense

The past tense has two parts: a short form of "to be" (**sam**, **si**, **je**, **smo**, **ste**, **su**) and a verb ending that shows gender and number:

- **bio** — was (man)
- **bila** — was (woman)
- **bilo** — was (neuter)
- **bili** — were (plural)

- **Bio sam kod kuće ujutro.** — I was at home in the morning.
- **Jučer je ona bila kod kuće u osam sati.** — Yesterday she was at home at eight o'clock.
- **On je otišao kući.** — He went home.
- **Jeste li išli u kino?** — Have you gone to the cinema?

## "Did not"

Use **nije**, **nisu** and so on: **Oni ništa nisu radili.** — They did nothing.

Weather and time use the neuter form: **Ujutro nije bilo vrlo toplo.** — In the morning it was not very warm.

> **Watch out:** a man says **Bio sam kod kuće**; a woman says *Bila sam kod kuće*.
