In this lesson: **numbers 11 to 20**.

## The teens

The teens end in **‑naest**:

- **jedanaest** — eleven
- **dvanaest** — twelve
- **trinaest** — thirteen
- **petnaest** — fifteen
- **dvadeset** — twenty

## The noun after them

Like 5 to 10, every number from 11 to 20 takes the plural "of" form of the noun:

- **jedanaest dječaka** — eleven boys
- **dvanaest žena** — twelve women
- **trinaest mačaka** — thirteen cats
- **četrnaest bicikala** — fourteen bicycles

> **Watch out:** some nouns change shape: **pas** (dog) becomes **trinaest pasa**, and **muškarac** (man) becomes **dvanaest muškaraca**.
