In this lesson: **numbers 0 to 10**, and how the noun after a number changes.

## One

"One" matches its noun's gender:

- **jedan dječak** — one boy
- **jedna žena** — one woman
- **jedno polje** — one field

## Two, three, four

"Two" is **dva** for masculine and neuter nouns and **dvije** for feminine ones. After 2, 3 and 4 the noun takes a special form: **‑a** for masculine, **‑e** for feminine.

- **dva dječaka** — two boys
- **dvije žene** — two women
- **tri mačke** — three cats
- **četiri bicikla** — four bicycles

## Five and up

From 5 on, the noun takes the plural "of" form:

- **pet dječaka** — five boys
- **pet mačaka** — five cats
- **šest bicikala** — six bicycles
- **deset satova** — ten clocks

> **Watch out:** **tri mačke** but **pet mačaka**: the noun changes again from five on.
