In this lesson: **nature words**, and the difference between going **into** a place and being **in** it.

## Nature

- **rijeka** — river
- **gora** — mountain
- **šuma** — forest
- **polje** — field
- **dolina** — valley
- **trava** — grass

## Going into vs being in

The ending shows whether you move or stay:

- **Mi idemo u šumu.** — We are going to the forest. (moving: **‑u**)
- **Ruža raste na polju.** — The rose grows in the field. (staying: **‑u** for neuter **polje**)
- **On stoji na kamenu.** — He is standing on the stone.

## More place words

- **Rijeka teče kroz dolinu.** — The river flows through the valley. (**kroz**, through)
- **Dolina leži između gora.** — The valley lies between the mountains. (**između**, between)
- **Polje je iza kuće.** — The field is behind the house. (**iza**, behind)

> **Watch out:** **cvijeće** (flowers) is one collective word, so its verb is singular: **Cvijeće lijepo cvjeta.**
