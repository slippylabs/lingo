In this lesson: **food words** and the **accusative case**, the form of the thing you eat, buy or see.

## Food

- **kruh** — bread
- **meso** — meat
- **mlijeko** — milk
- **jaje** — egg
- **doručak** — breakfast
- **ručak** — lunch
- **večera** — dinner

## The accusative

The accusative is the form of the thing that receives the action.

- Feminine **‑a** becomes **‑u**: **Mi jedemo večeru.** — We are eating dinner.
- Masculine things and neuter words don't change: **Ja jedem kruh.** — I am eating bread. **Ona jede meso.** — She is eating meat.
- Describing words and "my/your" change with the noun: **On jede slatku hranu.** — He eats sweet food. **Mi vidimo tvoju pizzu na stolu.** — We see your pizza on the table.

## "To eat"

- **ja jedem** — I eat (**Ja jedem kruh.**)
- **ti jedeš** — you eat (**Ti jedeš jaje?**)
- **ona jede** — she eats
- **mi jedemo** — we eat

## "Will"

**ću** + the dictionary form makes the future: **Ja ću kupiti maslac.** — I will buy butter.

> **Watch out:** masculine people and animals add **‑a** in the accusative, things do not: **Ja vidim Vlada s hranom** (Vlad → Vlada), but **Ja jedem kruh.**
