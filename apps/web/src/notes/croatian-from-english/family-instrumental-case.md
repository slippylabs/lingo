In this lesson: **family words** and the **instrumental case**, the form a word takes after **s** ("with").

## The family

- **otac** — father
- **majka** — mother
- **brat** — brother
- **djed** — grandfather
- **baka** — grandmother
- **sin** — son
- **kćer** — daughter

## "With" changes the ending

A "case" is a change to the end of a word that shows its job in the sentence. After **s** ("with") the noun takes the instrumental case, usually ending in **‑om** (or **‑em** after soft sounds such as c, č, j):

| | normal | after s |
|---|---|---|
| feminine | **majka** | **s majkom** |
| masculine | **djed** | **s djedom** |

- **Ona ide s majkom.** — She is going with her mother.
- **On govori s djedom.** — He is talking with his grandfather.
- **On živi s tetkom i sa stricem.** — He lives with his aunt and uncle.
- **S kim ti ideš?** — Who are you going with?

"My", "your" and "our" change too:

- **Govorim s tvojom sestrom.** — I am talking with your sister.
- **Mi igramo s našom djecom.** — We are playing with our children.

**s** becomes **sa** before a word starting with s, z, š or ž: **On živi sa sinom.** — He lives with his son.

> **Watch out:** "He is my brother" uses the plain form, not the instrumental: **On je moj brat.**
