In this lesson: **clothes** and the joining words **and**, **but** and **or**.

## Clothes

- **košulja** — shirt
- **hlače** — trousers
- **kaput** — coat
- **šal** — scarf
- **kapa** — hat
- **rukavica** — glove
- **čarapa** — sock

## Joining words

- **i** — and: **Šal je težak i topao.** — The scarf is heavy and warm.
- **a** — and/whereas, for a contrast: **Ona ima crne hlače, a on bijele.** — She has black trousers, and he has white.
- **ili** — or: **Želiš li šal ili košulju?** — Do you want a scarf or a shirt?

**a** sets two things against each other: **Zimi uvijek nosim kapu, a ljeti ne.** — In winter, I always wear a hat, but not in summer.

## "Wear"

**nositi** means both "to wear" and "to carry":

- **On nosi plavu košulju.** — He wears a blue shirt.
- **Nosim rukavice zimi.** — I wear gloves in winter.

> **Watch out:** **hlače** (trousers) and **gaće** (underpants) are always plural: **Nove gaće su udobne.**
