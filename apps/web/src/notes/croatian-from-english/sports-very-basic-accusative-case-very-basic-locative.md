In this lesson: **sports**, the **accusative** (the form of the thing you do something to) and the **locative** (the form after **u** "in" and **na** "at/on").

## The thing you play: accusative

Feminine words ending in **‑a** change to **‑u**. Masculine things stay the same.

- **Ona igra košarku.** — She plays basketball. (**košarka** → **košarku**)
- **On igra tenis.** — He plays tennis.
- **Igram nogomet.** — I play football.

## Where: u / na + locative

After **u** or **na** for a place, masculine words add **‑u**:

- **Stadion je u gradu.** — The stadium is in the city.
- **Ona igra na stadionu.** — She plays at the stadium.

Feminine words change **‑a** to **‑i**:

- **Mi plivamo u rijeci.** — We swim in the river.

## Saying "not"

Put **ne** straight before the verb: **Ja ne igram hokej.** — I do not play hockey.

> **Watch out:** **rijeka** becomes **u rijeci**: a *k* before the new **‑i** turns into *c*.
