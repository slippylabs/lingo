In this lesson: **hobbies** and how a verb's ending changes for **who** is doing it.

## One verb, six endings

Croatian shows the person in the verb's ending, so the word for "I" or "you" is often left out.

- **ja čitam** — I read
- **ti gledaš** — you watch
- **on pjeva** — he sings
- **mi gledamo** — we watch
- **vi učite** — you learn
- **oni slušaju** — they listen

The endings to spot: **‑m** (I), **‑š** (you), **‑mo** (we), **‑te** (you, several).

## In sentences

- **Ja čitam knjigu.** — I am reading a book.
- **Mi gledamo film.** — We are watching a film.
- **Oni slušaju glazbu.** — They are listening to music.
- **Ona sjedi i čita.** — She is sitting and reading.

There is one present tense: **Ja čitam knjigu** means both "I read a book" and "I am reading a book".

Some verbs change their stem: **pisati** (to write) gives **Ona piše pisma.**, and **plesati** (to dance) gives **Ja plešem mnogo**.

> **Watch out:** you play a game with **igrati** (**On igra nogomet.**) but an instrument with *svirati* (**Ona svira gitaru.**).
