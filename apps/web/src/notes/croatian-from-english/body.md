In this lesson: **parts of the body**, and saying what **hurts**.

## The body

- **glava** — head
- **ruka** — hand
- **noga** — leg
- **uho** — ear
- **nos** — nose
- **srce** — heart
- **želudac** — stomach

## It hurts

The part that hurts is the subject of **boli** ("hurts"):

- **Moja ruka boli.** — My hand hurts.
- **Želudac te boli.** — Your stomach hurts. (word for word: "the stomach hurts you")

## Pairs

- **Ja imam dvije ruke i dvije noge i također dva stopala.** — I have two hands and two legs, and also two feet.
- **On ima plave lijepe oči.** — He has beautiful blue eyes.

> **Watch out:** **oko** (eye) has an irregular plural, **oči**, which is feminine: **Moje oči su plave.**
