In this lesson: **school words**, the **locative** (the form after **u** "in" and **na** "on"), and how to say **with a pen**.

## At school

- **škola** — school
- **učenik** — student
- **knjiga** — book
- **zadatak** — assignment
- **bilježnica** — notebook
- **pero** — pen

A teacher is **učitelj** (man) or **učiteljica** (woman): **Ona je učiteljica.** — She is a teacher.

## Where something is

After **u** or **na** for a place, masculine and neuter words end in **‑u**:

- **Knjiga je na stolu.** — The book is on the table. (**stol** → **na stolu**)
- **Škola je u centru.** — The school is in the center.
- **Pišem na papiru perom.** — I am writing on paper with a pen.

Feminine words change **‑a** to **‑i** (*u školi*, "at school").

## With a tool

For the tool you use, Croatian puts the word in the instrumental case (ending **‑om**) and can leave out **s** ("with"): **perom** — with a pen.

> **Watch out:** **zadatak** loses a vowel when it changes: **Ona ima mnogo zadataka.** — She has many assignments.
