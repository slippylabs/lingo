In this lesson: **describing people** and **comparing**: taller, more beautiful, wiser.

## Describing

- **visok** — tall
- **nizak** — short
- **debeo** — fat
- **tanak** — thin
- **mlad** — young
- **star** — old

## Comparing

Most describing words add **‑iji**; some common ones change shape:

- **mudar** → **mudriji** — wiser
- **snažan** → **snažniji** — stronger
- **visok** → **viši** — taller
- **lijep** → **ljepši** — more beautiful
- **velik** → *veći* — bigger

## "Than"

Use **nego**, or **od** with the "of" form:

- **Stari ljudi su mudriji nego mi.** — Old people are wiser than us.
- **On je viši i snažniji od njega.** — He is taller and stronger than him.
- **Tanak nosi veću košulju nego ja.** — The thin one wears a larger shirt than I do.

"As … as" or "like" is **kao**: **Ona je niska i tanka kao on.** — She is short and thin like him.

> **Watch out:** **viši**, **ljepši** and *veći* are irregular, so there is no *visokiji*.
