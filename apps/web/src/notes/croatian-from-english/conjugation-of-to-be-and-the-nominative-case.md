In this lesson: the verb **"to be"**, the words for **"this" and "that"**, and how every noun has a **gender**.

## "To be"

- **ja sam** — I am
- **ti si** — you are (one person you know well)
- **on je** — he is (also **ona je**, she is, and **ono je**, it is)
- **mi smo** — we are
- **vi ste** — you are (several people, or one person you are polite to)
- **oni su** — they are

- **Ja sam mudar.** — I am wise.
- **Vi ste dobri.** — You are good.

## "Not" and questions

Put **nije** for "is not": **To nije moja sestra.** — This is not my sister.

For a yes/no question, start with the verb and add **li**:

- **Je li to dobar auto?** — Is this a good car?
- **Jeste li i vi mudri?** — Are you also wise?
- **Jesu li to dobri prijatelji?** — Are these good friends?

## Every noun has a gender

Croatian nouns are masculine, feminine or neuter (neither), and the ending usually tells you which:

- **grad** — city (ends in a consonant: masculine)
- **žena** — woman (ends in **‑a**: feminine)
- **dijete** — child (ends in **‑e** or **‑o**: neuter)

Describing words change to match: **Ja sam mudar** (a man), **Ona je vrlo mudra** (a woman), **Mi smo vrlo mudri** (a group).

## This and that

- **To je moja sestra.** — This is my sister.
- **To su prijatelji.** — These are friends.
- **Onaj dan je hladan.** — That day is cold.

**To je** ("this is") stays the same whatever comes after it. **Ovaj / ova / ovo** mean "this" and **onaj** means "that over there"; they match the noun's gender.

> **Watch out:** "This is" with more than one thing becomes **To su**, as in **To su prijatelji.**
