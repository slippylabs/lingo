In this lesson: **feelings**, and **adverbs**, the words that say **how** something is done.

## Feelings

- **ljubav** — love
- **strah** — fear
- **mir** — peace
- **sram** — shame
- **zavist** — envy

## Describing word → how

Many adverbs are the describing word with **‑o**:

- **radostan** → **radosno** — joyfully
- **tužan** → **tužno** — sadly
- **ponosan** → **ponosno** — proudly
- **veseo** → **veselo** — cheerfully

- **Tužno je govorio.** — He spoke sadly.
- **Radosno je otišao kući.** — He joyfully went home.
- **On skriva ponosno svoj gnjev.** — He hides his anger proudly.

## How you feel

- **Osjećam se radosno danas.** — I feel joyful today.
- **On osjeća sram.** — He feels shame.

> **Watch out:** "I feel" is **osjećam se**, with **se**; without it, **osjećati** means to feel something, as in **On osjeća sram.**
