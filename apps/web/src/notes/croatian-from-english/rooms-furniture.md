In this lesson: **rooms and furniture**, and small words for **where** things are.

## Furniture

- **soba** — room
- **stol** — table
- **stolica** — chair
- **krevet** — bed
- **prozor** — window
- **ormar** — wardrobe
- **polica** — shelf

## Where things are

Each of these little words puts the next noun in a set form:

- **u sobi** — in the room (**u** + **‑i** for feminine)
- **na stolu** — on the table (**na** + **‑u** for masculine)
- **pod krevetom** — under the bed (**pod** + **‑om**)
- **nad stolom** — above the table (**nad** + **‑om**)
- **blizu stola** — near the table (**blizu** + **‑a**)

## In sentences

- **Stolica je blizu stola.** — The chair is near the table.
- **Komoda je pod krevetom.** — The chest is under the bed.
- **Lampa svijetli nad stolom.** — The lamp shines above the table.
- **U sobi je mirno.** — It is quiet in the room.

> **Watch out:** **vrata** (door) is always plural, even for one door: **Vrata su zatvorena.**
