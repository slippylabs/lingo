In this lesson: **north, south, east and west**, and **left, right and straight on**.

## The compass

- **sjever** — north
- **jug** — south
- **istok** — east
- **zapad** — west

To say "in the north", use **na** and the ending **‑u**:

- **Na sjeveru je snijeg.** — In the north there is snow.
- **Živimo na jugu.** — We live in the south.

To say where you are heading, use **na** without the extra ending: **Idemo na jugozapad.** — We are going to the southwest.

## Describing words

- **sjeverni** — northern
- **južni** — southern
- **zapadni** — western
- **istočni** — eastern

- **Puše zapadni vjetar.** — A western wind is blowing.

## Turning

- **desno** — right
- **lijevo** — left
- **ravno** — straight

> **Watch out:** compass points take **na**, not **u**: **na jugu**, **na sjeveru**.
