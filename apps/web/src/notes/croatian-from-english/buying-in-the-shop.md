In this lesson: **shopping**: prices, pieces and receipts.

## In the shop

- **cijena** — price
- **komad** — piece
- **kupac** — customer
- **račun** — receipt
- **povrat** — return

## Wanting and buying

**želim** ("I want") is followed by the dictionary form of the verb:

- **Želim kupiti kruh.** — I want to buy bread.
- **Ona kupuje knjigu.** — She is buying a book.
- **On prodaje mlijeko.** — He sells milk.

## A piece of, the price of

These take the "of" form:

- **Komad sira je dobar.** — A piece of cheese is good.
- **Komad kruha je malen.** — The piece of bread is small.
- **Cijena maslaca je dobra.** — The price of butter is good.

## Asking and giving

- **Kupac pita za popust.** — The customer asks for a discount.
- **Dat ću račun prodavaču.** — I will give the receipt to the seller. (to someone: dative **‑u**)

> **Watch out:** after **komad** and **cijena** the next noun changes: **komad sira**, not *komad sir*.
