In this lesson: **colours**, and how colour words **match** the noun.

## Colours

- **crn** — black
- **bijel** — white
- **zelen** — green
- **plav** — blue
- **crven** — red
- **žut** — yellow
- **siv** — grey

## Matching the noun

The basic form is masculine; add **‑a** for feminine and **‑o** for neuter:

- **Moj pas je crn.** — My dog is black.
- **Trava je zelena.** — The grass is green.
- **Nebo je plavo.** — The sky is blue.
- **Oblaci su sivi.** — The clouds are grey. (plural: **‑i**)

After a verb like "like" or "wear", a feminine colour ends in **‑u**, like its noun:

- **Ja volim crvenu boju.** — I like the red color.
- **Djevojka nosi bijelu haljinu.** — The girl is wearing a white dress.

> **Watch out:** the ending follows the noun, so it is **Crvena ruža raste ovdje.** but **To je Crveno more.**
