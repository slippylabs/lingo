In this lesson: **pronouns** (words like "me", "you", "us") in their different forms, and the question words **who** and **what**.

## Me and you

- **Mene ne vidiš.** — You don't see me.
- **Ja vidim vas.** — I see you.
- **Razgovaram s tobom.** — I am talking with you.
- **O meni govoriš?** — Are you talking about me?
- **Daj mi vodu.** — Give me water. (**mi** = to me)
- **S nama je uvijek sve veselo.** — With us everything is always cheerful.

## Oneself

**sebi** and **sobom** point back to the subject:

- **On daje sebi mir.** — He gives himself peace.
- **On voli biti sam sa sobom.** — He likes to be alone with himself.

## Who and what

- **Koga ti vidiš?** — Who do you see?
- **Komu ćeš dati dar?** — Who will you give a gift to?
- **O čemu ti govoriš?** — What are you talking about?
- **Čega tu nema u kući?** — What isn't here in the house?

## That one

- **Toga ne znam.** — I don't know that.
- **Tom psu ti daješ kosti.** — You give that dog bones.

> **Watch out:** the short forms **mi**, **te** and *me* can't start a sentence; to put the person first, use the long form, as in **Mene ne vidiš.**
