In this lesson: **countries**, **nationalities** and **languages**.

## People

A nationality has a capital letter and separate words for men and women:

- **Hrvat** — Croat (a man)
- **Hrvatica** — Croat (a woman)
- **Poljak** — Pole
- **Rus** — Russian
- **Nijemac** — German
- **Englez** — Englishman

- **Ja sam Poljak.** — I am a Pole.
- **Ona je Hrvatica.** — She is a Croat.

## Languages

Language words start with a small letter:

- **Hrvat govori hrvatskim jezikom.** — A Croat speaks the Croatian language.
- **Govoriš li njemačkim jezikom?** — Do you speak the German language?
- **Mi učimo ruski jezik.** — We are learning the Russian language.

You will also hear the shorter *govoriš li njemački?*

## Countries

- **Država je Poljska.** — The country is Poland.
- **Nijemac živi u Njemačkoj.** — A German lives in Germany.

Country names ending in **‑ska** or **‑čka** change to **‑oj** after **u** ("in").

> **Watch out:** a nationality takes a capital letter (**Hrvat**) but a language does not (**hrvatskim jezikom**).
