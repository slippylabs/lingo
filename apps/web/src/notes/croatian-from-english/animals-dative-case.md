In this lesson: **animals** and the **dative case**, the form for the one you give something **to** or do something **for**.

## Animals

- **mačka** — cat
- **pas** — dog
- **ptica** — bird
- **konj** — horse
- **miš** — mouse
- **medvjed** — bear
- **riba** — fish

## Giving to someone

The person or animal that receives gets the dative ending:

- feminine **‑a** → **‑i**: **Ja dajem mački ribu.** — I give the cat a fish.
- masculine → **‑u**: **Dajemo konju vodu.** — We give the horse water.
- plural **‑ama** / **‑ima**: **Dajemo žabama vodu.** — We give the frogs water. **Oni nose medvjedima hranu.** — They are carrying food to the bears.

The thing that is given stays in the accusative: **ribu**, **vodu**.

## "Help" takes the dative too

- **On pomaže ptici.** — He helps the bird.
- **Pomažemo vuku.** — We help the wolf.

## "Will give"

- **On će dati mišu sir.** — He will give the mouse cheese.
- **Hoćeš li mi dati ribu?** — Will you give me a fish? (**mi** = to me)

> **Watch out:** **pas** (dog) loses its **a**: **Ona će dati psu dobro ime.**
