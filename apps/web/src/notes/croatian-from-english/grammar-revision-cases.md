In this lesson: a **review of the cases**, the endings that show a word's job in the sentence.

## The cases side by side

- **On vidi svoju ženu.** — He sees his wife. (accusative: the one he sees)
- **Dat ću ženi.** — I will give to the woman. (dative: the one who receives)
- **Ona s mužem govori.** — She talks with her husband. (instrumental: with)
- **To se može rezati nožem.** — This can be cut with a knife. (instrumental: a tool)
- **Ona je u gradu.** — She is in the city. (locative: where)
- **Ona govori o braći.** — She talks about brothers. (locative after **o**, about)
- **Brata tu nema.** — There is no brother here. (genitive: after "there is no")

## "There is no" + genitive

- **Kosti tu nema.** — There is no bone here.
- **Imena on nema.** — He has no name.
- **Više nema banana u kući.** — There are no more bananas in the house.

## The ending carries the meaning

Because the endings show who does what, Croatian can move words around for emphasis: **Muškaraca tu ne vidim.** — I do not see men here.

> **Watch out:** read the **ending**, not the position: in **Brata tu nema.** the word that comes first is still the missing one.
