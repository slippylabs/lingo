In this lesson: **greetings**, **how are you?**, saying your **name**, and short **everyday replies**.

## Greetings through the day

- **Dobro jutro!** — Good morning!
- **Dobar dan!** — Good day!
- **Dobar večer!** — Good evening!
- **Laku noć!** — Good night!
- **Bok!** — Hello! (casual; friends also say it for "bye")

## How are you?

- **Kako si?** — How are you?
- **Dobro sam, hvala!** — I am well, thank you!
- **A ti?** — And you?

## Your name

- **Kako se zoveš?** — What is your name? (word for word: "how do you call yourself?")
- **Zovem se Bartek.** — My name is Bartek.

## Short replies

- **Tako je.** — That is so.
- **Možda.** — Maybe.
- **Ne znam.** — I don't know.
- **I ja.** — Me too.
- **Šteta!** — What a pity!
- **Što to znači?** — What does this mean?

## Friendly and polite "you"

**ti** is for one friend or relative; **vi** is for several people, or for one stranger you want to be polite to. That is why "excuse me" to a stranger is **Oprostite** and "welcome" is **Dobrodošli!**

> **Watch out:** **Bok** is casual. With a stranger, a shop assistant or a teacher, say **Dobar dan!**
