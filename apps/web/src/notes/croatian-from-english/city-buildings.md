In this lesson: **places in town**, and whether a place takes **u** or **na**.

## In town

- **ulica** — street
- **trg** — square
- **most** — bridge
- **trgovina** — shop
- **tržnica** — market
- **crkva** — church
- **stanica** — station

## u or na?

Most buildings take **u** ("in"); open places take **na** ("on/at"). Both are followed by the locative ending:

- **Kupujemo kruh u trgovini.** — We buy bread in the shop.
- **Čitam knjigu u biblioteci.** — I read a book in the library.
- **Na tržnici ima mnogo ljudi.** — There are many people at the market.
- **Na trgu stoji fontana.** — A fountain stands in the square.

## Going to

**do** ("to, as far as") takes the "of" form:

- **Idemo do trga.** — We are going to the square.
- **Ulica vodi do trga.** — The street leads to the square.

> **Watch out:** it is **na trgu** and **na tržnici**, even where English says "in the square" or "at the market".
