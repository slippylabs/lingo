In this lesson: **telling the time**, and **ordinal numbers** (first, second, hundredth).

## Telling the time

Croatian tells the time with ordinary numbers: the hour, **i** ("and"), then the minutes.

- **u pet sati** — at five o'clock
- **u jedan i dvadeset** — at twenty past one
- **u dva i petnaest** — at fifteen past two
- **u osam i pedeset pet** — at eight fifty five

## Ordinals

Ordinals behave like describing words, so they match their noun:

- **stoti dolar** — hundredth dollar
- **trideseti muškarac** — thirtieth man
- **četiristo peta gora** — four hundred fifth mountain (feminine)
- **dvjesto treće računalo** — two hundred third computer (neuter)

In a long number, only the last word becomes an ordinal:

- **dvjesto prvi brat** — two hundred first brother
- **osamsto drugi dolar** — eight hundred second dollar

> **Watch out:** clock times use ordinary numbers, so it is **u dva i petnaest**, not "the second hour".
