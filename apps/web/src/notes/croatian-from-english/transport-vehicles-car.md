In this lesson: **vehicles and the road**.

## Getting around

- **auto** — car
- **autobus** — bus
- **vlak** — train
- **bicikl** — bicycle
- **cesta** — road
- **semafor** — traffic light

## Going somewhere

**ide** means "goes", on foot or by vehicle. Use **na** or **do** for where to:

- **Vlak ide na stanicu.** — The train goes to the station.
- **Autobus ide do centra.** — The bus goes to the center.
- **Vlak ide na sjever.** — The train goes to the north.

## Describing

- **Vozač je oprezan.** — The driver is careful.
- **Cesta je široka.** — The road is wide.
- **Bicikl je pokvaren.** — The bicycle is broken.

> **Watch out:** **auto** ends in **‑o** but is masculine: **Auto je nov i lijep.**
