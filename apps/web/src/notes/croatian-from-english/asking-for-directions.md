In this lesson: **asking the way** and **giving directions**.

## Where is it? Where to?

- **Gdje je stanica?** — Where is the station? (**gdje**: where something is)
- **Kamo idete?** — Which way are you going? (**kamo**: where to)
- **Kamo ide cesta?** — Which way does the road go?

## Directions

- **Idemo desno.** — We are going to the right.
- **Zatim lijevo.** — Then to the left.
- **Stanica je lijevo.** — The station is on the left.
- **Biblioteka je daleko.** — The library is far away.

## Telling someone to go

- **Idi ravno do mosta.** — Go straight to the bridge. (to one friend)
- **Idite ravno i zatim desno.** — Go straight and then to the right. (to several people, or politely)

## Asking politely

- **Možete li pokazati put?** — Can you show me the way?

> **Watch out:** use **gdje** for where something is and **kamo** for where someone is going.
