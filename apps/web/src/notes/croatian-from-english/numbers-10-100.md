In this lesson: **tens up to 100**, and the **last-number rule**.

## The tens

- **dvadeset** — twenty
- **trideset** — thirty
- **četrdeset** — forty
- **pedeset** — fifty
- **sto** — one hundred

## The last number decides

In a number like 51 or 93, the last part decides the noun's form, just as if it stood alone:

- **pedeset jedan sat** — fifty one clocks (ends in 1: singular)
- **dvadeset četiri stvari** — twenty four things (ends in 4)
- **devedeset tri računala** — ninety three computers (ends in 3)
- **sedamdeset osam rubalja** — seventy eight rubles (ends in 8: plural "of" form)
- **trideset pasa** — thirty dogs

> **Watch out:** **sto jedan bicikl** has a singular noun, because the number ends in "one".
