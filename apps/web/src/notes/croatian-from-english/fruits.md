In this lesson: **fruit**, words for **taste and colour**, and how describing words **match** the noun.

## Fruit

- **jabuka** — apple
- **kruška** — pear
- **naranča** — orange
- **jagoda** — strawberry
- **šljiva** — plum
- **limun** — lemon
- **grožđe** — grapes

## Describing words match

A describing word takes the gender and number of its noun: no extra ending for masculine, **‑a** for feminine, **‑e** for feminine plural.

- **Limun je kiseo i žut.** — The lemon is sour and yellow.
- **Višnja je kisela.** — The cherry is sour.
- **Jabuka je crvena.** — The apple is red.
- **Naranče su slatke.** — The oranges are sweet.

Some masculine forms hide a vowel: **kiseo** (masculine) but **kisela** (feminine).

## Made from

**od** + the "of" form says what something is made from:

- **Ona pije sok od naranči.** — She is drinking orange juice.
- **On pravi šljivovicu od šljiva.** — He is preparing plum brandy from plums.

> **Watch out:** **grožđe** (grapes) is singular in Croatian, so it takes a singular verb: **Grožđe raste u vinogradu.**
