In this lesson: **hundreds** and **a thousand**.

## Hundreds

- **sto** — one hundred
- **dvjesto** — two hundred
- **tristo** — three hundred
- **petsto** — five hundred
- **tisuća** — thousand

## Same last-number rule

The last part of the number still decides the noun's form:

- **sedamsto jedan bicikl** — seven hundred one bicycles
- **dvjesto tri računala** — two hundred three computers
- **petsto žena** — five hundred women

## Two thousand

**tisuća** is a feminine noun, so "two thousand" is **dvije tisuće**:

- **dvije tisuće devetsto pasa** — two thousand nine hundred dogs

> **Watch out:** it is **dvije tisuće**, with the feminine **dvije**.
