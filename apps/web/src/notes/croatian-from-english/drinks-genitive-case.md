In this lesson: **drinks** and the **genitive**, the "of" form used for amounts, after **bez** (without), **iz** (from) and after "there is no".

## The "of" form

Feminine **‑a** becomes **‑e**; masculine and neuter words add or end in **‑a**:

- **boca vode** — a bottle of water (**voda** → **vode**)
- **čaša vina** — a glass of wine (**vino** → **vina**)
- **bez mlijeka** — without milk
- **bez šećera** — without sugar
- **iz Poljske** — from Poland

## In sentences

- **Čaša vina za mene, molim.** — A glass of wine for me, please.
- **Boca vode leži na stolu.** — A bottle of water is lying on the table.
- **Ja volim sok bez šećera.** — I like juice without sugar.
- **To vino je iz Poljske.** — This wine is from Poland.

## "Have none"

*nemati* (not to have) takes the genitive:

- **Ja nemam vode.** — I do not have water.
- **On nema kave.** — He does not have coffee.
- **Kod Franka nema votke.** — Frank has no vodka.

> **Watch out:** **nema** means both "he/she doesn't have" and "there is no", as in **Kod Franka nema votke.**
