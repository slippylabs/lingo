In this lesson: **days of the week** and **months**, with **on** and **in**.

## Days

- **ponedjeljak** — Monday
- **utorak** — Tuesday
- **srijeda** — Wednesday
- **četvrtak** — Thursday
- **petak** — Friday
- **subota** — Saturday
- **nedjelja** — Sunday

"On Wednesday" is **u** + the **‑u** form: **U srijedu imam zadatak.** — On Wednesday, I have an assignment.

## Months

Croatian months have their own Slavic names:

- **siječanj** — January
- **veljača** — February
- **srpanj** — July
- **kolovoz** — August
- **listopad** — October
- **prosinac** — December

"In" a month is **u** + the locative:

- **Imam odmor u siječnju.** — I have vacation in January.
- **U kolovozu idem u Honduras.** — In August, I am going to Honduras.
- **U veljači ima mnogo snijega.** — In February there is a lot of snow.

> **Watch out:** days and months take a small letter, and some lose a vowel: **siječanj** → **u siječnju**.
