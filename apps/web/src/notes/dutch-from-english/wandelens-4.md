In this lesson: **hiking** words.

## Outdoors

- **berg** — mountain
- **bank** — bench
- **Het gras is groen** — The grass is green
- **De berg is hoog** — The mountain is high

## Is and are

- **is** — is: **Het bord is vies.** — The sign is dirty.
- **zijn** — are: **Mijn wandelschoenen zijn vies** — My hiking shoes are dirty

## Asking

- **Heb je een kaart?** — Do you have a map?

> **Watch out:** **bank** means "bench" here. It can also mean a bank for money, or a sofa.
