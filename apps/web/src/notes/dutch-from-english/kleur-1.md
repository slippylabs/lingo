In this lesson: **colours**.

## Colours

- **rood** — red
- **blauw** — blue
- **zwart** — black
- **wit** — white

## After "is", no change

- **De wijn is rood.** — The wine is red.
- **De wijn is wit.** — The wine is white.

## Before a noun, add ‑e

- **Ik heb een zwarte zonnebril.** — I have black sunglasses.

> **Watch out:** **zonnebril** (sunglasses) is one thing in Dutch, so it takes **een**.
