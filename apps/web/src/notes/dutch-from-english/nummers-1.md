In this lesson: the numbers **one to five**.

## The numbers

- **een** — one
- **twee** — two
- **drie** — three
- **vier** — four
- **vijf** — five

## Using them

- **Cecille heeft een broer** — Cecille has one brother
- **Cecille is drie jaar oud** — Cecille is three years old

After a number, **jaar** (year) stays singular: **drie jaar**, not "three years".

> **Watch out:** **een** means both "a" and "one". When it clearly means "one", Dutch often writes it with accents: *één*.
