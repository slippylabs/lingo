In this lesson: **water sports** and **at the beach**.

## Questions

- **Kun je zeilen?** — Can you sail?
- **Heb je zonnebrandcrème?** — Do you have sunscreen?

## Compliments

- **Je hebt een prachtige surfplank.** — You've got a nice surfboard.

> **Watch out:** with **wil** or **kun**, the second verb goes to the very end: **Ik wil niet in het water springen** — I don't want to jump in the water.
