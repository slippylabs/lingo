## Greetings and politeness

- **Goedenavond** — Good evening
- **alsjeblieft** — please (to a friend); **alstublieft** — please (polite)

## Two kinds of "you"

Dutch has a casual "you" (**je**, or *jij* for emphasis) and a polite one (**u**):

- **Wat is je naam?** — What's your name? (casual)
- **Uw naam, alstublieft?** — Your name, please? (polite: **uw** = your)
- **Hoe gaat het met jou?** — How are you? (casual)

Use **u** with strangers, in shops and at work.

## Saying who you are

- **Mijn naam is Cecille.** — My name is Cecille.
- **Ik kom uit Den Haag** — I come from The Hague.
- **Goedenavond en bedankt.** — Good evening and thanks.

**Spelling:** the long *-ij-* in *mijn* and *jij* sounds like "ay" in "say".
