In this lesson: **instruments** and **making music**.

## Playing an instrument

No "the" is needed:

- **Ik speel gitaar** — I play guitar
- **Ik speel graag piano** — I like playing the piano

**graag** after a verb means "like to": **Ik speel graag** = I like to play.

## Describing words

Before a noun they usually add **‑e**:

- **Deze elektrische gitaar is prachtig** — This electric guitar is beautiful
- **Ik heb een oude viool** — I have an old violin

> **Watch out:** the plural of words ending in ‑a gets an apostrophe: **Mondharmonica's** — harmonicas.
