In this lesson: the numbers **six to ten**.

## The numbers

- **zes** — six
- **zeven** — seven
- **acht** — eight
- **negen** — nine
- **tien** — ten

## Using them

- **Zes eieren, alstublieft.** — Six eggs, please.
- **Zeven mannen en acht vrouwen.** — Seven men and eight women.

Phone numbers can be read one digit at a time:

- **Mijn telefoonnummer is tien, vijf, acht, drie, nul.** — My phone number is ten, five, eight, three, zero.

> **Watch out:** "eggs" is **eieren**, an unusual plural. One egg is *ei*.
