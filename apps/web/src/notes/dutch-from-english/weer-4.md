In this lesson: **the seasons** and a few **weather words**.

## The seasons

- **Voorjaar** — spring
- **Zomer** — summer
- **Herfst** — autumn

## It's spring

- **Het is voorjaar.** — It's spring.

## More weather

- **droog** — dry
- **mild** — mild
- **bliksem** — lightning

> **Watch out:** **Voorjaar** literally means "fore‑year". You'll also hear *lente*, which means the same.
