In this lesson: **at the cinema**.

## Films

- **film** — film
- **romantische film** — romantic movie
- **Ik hou van horrorfilms.** — I like horror movies.
- **Deze film is melig.** — This movie is cheesy.

## Asking politely

**Kunt u** …? is a polite "Could you …?":

- **Kunt u alstublieft twee bioscoopkaartjes kopen voor zaterdag?** — Could you please buy two movie tickets for Saturday?

> **Watch out:** Dutch writes joined‑up words as one: **bioscoopkaartjes** (cinema tickets), **horrorfilms**.
