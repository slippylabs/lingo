In this lesson: **video games**.

## Talking about a game

- **De graphics in het spel zijn goed** — The graphics in the game are good
- **De spelfiguren overtuigen me niet** — The game characters don't convince me
- **De controllers zijn gebroken** — The game controllers are broken

## Going to …

**Ik ga** + a verb at the end means "I'm going to …":

- **Ik ga je highscore verbreken** — I'm going to beat your highscore

> **Watch out:** for broken things, Dutch speakers usually say *kapot*. **gebroken** is fine, just more formal.
