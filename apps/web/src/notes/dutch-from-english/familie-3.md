In this lesson: **aunts, uncles, cousins** and **grandchildren**.

## I have …

**Ik heb** means "I have":

- **Ik heb een broer en een zus.** — I have a brother and a sister.
- **Ik ben enig kind.** — I am an only child.

## Klein‑ means grand‑

**klein** means "small". Put it in front of son or daughter to make a grandchild:

- **Kleinzoon** — grandson
- **Kleindochter** — granddaughter
- **Hij is mijn kleinzoon.** — He is my grandson.

## Aunts and uncles

- **Tante** — aunt
- **Oom** — uncle

> **Watch out:** **Neef** means both "nephew" and "(male) cousin", and **Nicht** means both "niece" and "(female) cousin".
