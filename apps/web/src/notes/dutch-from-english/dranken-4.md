In this lesson: **drinks**, and **a glass of …**.

## Drinks

- **koffie** — coffee
- **thee** — tea
- **melk** — milk
- **bier** — beer
- **sinaasappelsap** — orange juice

## A glass of …

Dutch leaves out "of":

- **een glas water** — a glass of water
- **ik drink een glas witte wijn** — I drink a glass of white wine

## Colours before a noun get ‑e

- **witte wijn** — white wine
- **rode wijn** — red wine

> **Watch out:** when you raise a glass, say **Proost!** — Cheers!
