In this lesson: **clothes shopping** and **paying**.

## Paying

- **Kan ik contant betalen?** — Can I pay cash?
- **Hier, mijn creditcard.** — Here, my credit card.

## Clothes

- **Ik koop graag schoenen** — I love buying shoes
- **Wat is je kledingmaat?** — What's your clothing size?
- **Ik hou niet van de snit** — I don't like the cut

**te** means "too":

- **Deze broek is te strak.** — These pants are too tight.

> **Watch out:** **broek** (pants) is one thing in Dutch, so it takes **is**, not *zijn*.
