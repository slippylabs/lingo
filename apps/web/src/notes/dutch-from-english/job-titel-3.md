In this lesson: **the office**: colleagues, meetings and trips.

## Colleagues

- **Mijn collega is aardig.** — My colleague is nice.
- **Onze klant is irritant.** — Our client is annoying.

## On the way

- **Ik ben op weg naar kantoor.** — I am on the way to the office.
- **Ik ben op zakenreis.** — I am on a business trip.

## Had

**hadden** means "had" (for we and they):

- **We hadden een lange vergadering.** — We had a long meeting.

> **Watch out:** **Teken je het contract?** means "Are you signing the contract?". **teken** means "sign" here, not "draw".
