In this lesson: **fruit**, and saying what you **like**.

## Fruit

- **Banaan** — banana
- **Sinaasappel** — orange
- **Citroen** — lemon
- **Kers** — cherry
- **Druif** — grape

## Liking

**houdt van** means "likes". **Ik hou niet van** means "I don't like":

- **Cecille houdt van sinaasappels** — Cecille likes oranges
- **Ik hou niet van mango's** — I don't like mangoes
- **Cecille eet een sinaasappel** — Cecille eats an orange

> **Watch out:** words ending in a vowel such as ‑o add *'s* in the plural: **mango's**. That apostrophe is correct in Dutch.
