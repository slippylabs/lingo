In this lesson: **weather words** that describe the day.

## Two endings

Many weather words are made from a noun plus an ending:

- *‑ig*: **zonnig** — sunny, **mistig** — foggy
- *‑achtig*: **regenachtig** — rainy, **stormachtig** — stormy

## As verbs

- **Het sneeuwt.** — It snows.
- **Het stormt** — It's stormy.

> **Watch out:** **bewolkt** (cloudy) and **besneeuwd** (snowy) don't use those endings. Learn them on their own.
