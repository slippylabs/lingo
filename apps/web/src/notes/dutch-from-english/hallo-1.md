In this lesson: **saying hello** and **introducing yourself**.

## Hello

- **Hallo** — Hello
- **Goedemorgen** — Good morning
- **Bedankt** — Thanks

## About you

- **Ik** — I
- **Ik ben** — I am
- **Ik woon in...** — I live in…
- **Wat is je naam?** — What's your name?

> **Watch out:** **Goedemorgen** is written as one word: *goede* (good) + *morgen* (morning).
