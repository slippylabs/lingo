In this lesson: **vegetables** and their **plurals**.

## One and more than one

- **Ik heb een komkommer** — I have a cucumber
- **Komkommers** — cucumbers
- **Ik heb een wortel** — I have a carrot
- **Wortelen** — carrots
- **Uien** — onions

Words ending in **‑er** usually just add **‑s**, like **Komkommers**. Many others add **‑en**.

## I don't like …

- **Ik hou niet van spinazie** — I don't like spinach

> **Watch out:** **Courgette** is the Dutch word for zucchini, the same as in British English.
