In this lesson: **step‑**, *half‑* and **in‑law** family.

## Three handy beginnings

- *stief‑* = step‑: **stiefzoon** — stepson, **stiefdochter** — stepdaughter
- *half‑* = half‑: **halfbroer** — half‑brother, **halfzus** — half‑sister
- *schoon‑* = ‑in‑law: **schoonmoeder** — mother‑in‑law, **schoonzus** — sister‑in‑law

Examples:

- **Ik heb een halfbroer.** — I have a half‑brother.
- **Hij is mijn stiefbroer.** — He is my stepbrother.

> **Watch out:** brother‑in‑law doesn't follow the pattern. It's **zwager**.
