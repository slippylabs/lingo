In this lesson: **culture** and **asking questions**.

## Questions start with the verb

- **Gaan we naar het theater?** — Are we going to the theater?
- **Speel je schaak?** — Do you play chess?

## Answers

- **Nee, ik hou van musicals** — No, I love musicals
- **Ik kijk graag naar dinosaurussen** — I love watching dinosaurs

> **Watch out:** when **je** comes after the verb, the verb loses its ‑t: *je speelt* becomes **Speel je**?
