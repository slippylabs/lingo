In this lesson: **how are you?** and **see you tomorrow**.

## How are you?

- **Hoe gaat het, Jaques?** — How are you, Jaques?

Word for word, this says "How goes it?". It's the everyday way to ask.

## Names and thanks

- **Mijn naam is Cecille** — My name is Cecille
- **Dank je wel, Cecille** — Thanks, Cecille

## Goodbye

- **Tot morgen** — See you tomorrow

> **Watch out:** **Tot morgen** literally means "until tomorrow". Dutch says *tot* + a time to mean "see you then".
