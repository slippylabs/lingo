In this lesson: **more fruit** and **plurals with ‑en**.

## Fruit

- **Aardbei** — strawberry
- **Framboos** — raspberry
- **Meloen** — melon
- **Olijf** — olive

## Plurals with ‑en

Many words add **‑en** for more than one:

- **Hij eet aardbeien en frambozen** — He eats strawberries and raspberries
- **We houden van olijven** — We like olives

> **Watch out:** the last letter can change before **‑en**: *f* becomes *v* (**Olijf** → **olijven**) and *s* becomes *z* (**Framboos** → **frambozen**).
