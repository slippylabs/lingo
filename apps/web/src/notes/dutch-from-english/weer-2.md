In this lesson: **the sky**, and **there are**.

## In the sky

- **de maan** — the moon
- **ik zie de maan** — I see the moon
- **Er zijn wat wolken in de lucht.** — There are a few clouds in the sky.

**Er zijn** means "there are".

## Snow

- **het sneeuwt.** — It's snowing.

> **Watch out:** in **hij is prachtig.** ("It's beautiful"), **hij** ("he") means the moon. Dutch calls many *de*‑words **hij**, even things.
