In this lesson: **more shopping**: sizes and how clothes fit.

## Sizes

- **Welke maat heb je?** — What size do you have?
- **Deze schoenen zijn te klein** — These shoes are too small

## Paying

- **Kan ik contant betalen?** — Can I pay cash?
- **Hier is mijn creditcard.** — Here's my credit card.

> **Watch out:** for how clothes fit, Dutch uses **zit** ("sits"): **Deze broek zit te strak.** — These pants are too tight.
