In this lesson: **grandparents**, **husband and wife**, and **he**.

## The family

- **Oma** — Grandma
- **Opa** — Grandfather
- **Kind** — child
- **Echtgenoot** — husband
- **Vrouw** — wife

## He is …

**Hij** means "he":

- **Hij is een kind** — He is a child
- **Hij is mijn man** — He is my husband

## Married to

- **Mijn oma is getrouwd met mijn opa** — My grandmother is married to my grandfather

> **Watch out:** Dutch is married **met** ("with") someone, not "to". Also, **Vrouw** means both "wife" and "woman".
