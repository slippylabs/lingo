In this lesson: asking **what someone does**.

## Asking about work

- **Wat doe je voor werk?** — What do you do for a living?
- **Wat is je werk?** — What is your profession?

## Right away

- **Ja, ik doe het meteen.** — Yes, I'll do it right away.

> **Watch out:** Dutch often uses the present for the near future: **ik doe het meteen** means "I'll do it right away".
