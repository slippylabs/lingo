In this lesson: talking about **the weather**.

## It is …

**Het is** means "it is":

- **Het is warm** — It's warm
- **Het is zonnig** — It's sunny

## Asking about the weather

- **Wat voor weer is het vandaag?** — How is the weather today?

> **Watch out:** "It's raining" is just **Het regent**, without *is*. Dutch uses the plain verb for things happening now.
