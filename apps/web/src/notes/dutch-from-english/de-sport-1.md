In this lesson: **sports**, and saying what you **play** and **go** do.

## I play …

No "the" is needed before a sport:

- **Ik speel voetbal** — I play soccer
- **Ik speel tennis** — I play tennis

## I go …

**Ik ga** + an activity means "I go …":

- **Ik ga zwemmen** — I go swimming
- **Ik ga klimmen** — I go climbing
- **Ik ga naar de sportschool** — I go to the gym

## I like …

- **Ik hou van joggen** — I like jogging

> **Watch out:** **hard** means "fast": **Ik ren hard** — I run fast.
