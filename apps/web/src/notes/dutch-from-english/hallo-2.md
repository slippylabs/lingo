In this lesson: **names**, **good night**, and the **polite "your"**.

## Asking someone's name

Dutch has two words for "your": a friendly one and a polite one.

- **Wat is uw naam?** — What's your name? (polite: to a stranger or an older person)
- **Hallo, ik ben Cecille** — Hello, I am Cecille
- **Cecille is een mooie naam** — Cecille is a nice name

## Saying good night

- **Goedenacht** — Good night
- **Dank je wel en goedenacht** — Thanks and good night

> **Watch out:** **uw** is the polite "your". Use it with people you don't know well; with friends, say *je*.
