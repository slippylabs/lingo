In this lesson: **greetings**, **thank you**, and short **answers**.

## Greetings

- **Dobro ranjo!** — Good morning!
- **Dobry źeń!** — Good day!
- **Dobry wjacor!** — Good evening!
- **Dobra noc!** — Good night!
- **Witajśo!** — Welcome!

## How are you?

- **Kak sy?** — How are you?
- **Som dobry, źěkuju!** — I am well, thank you!
- **A ty?** — And you?
- **Mójo mě jo Bartek.** — My name is Bartek.

You can leave out "I": **Som dobry** already says who, because **som** only goes with "I".

## Short answers

- **Tak jo.** — That is so.
- **Snano.** — Maybe.
- **Nje wěm.** — I don't know.
- **Škoda!** — What a pity!

> **Watch out:** **jo** means "is", and on its own it also means "yes": **Jo, móžno.** — Yes, it is possible.
