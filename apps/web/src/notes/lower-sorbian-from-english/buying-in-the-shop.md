In this lesson: **shopping**: wanting, buying, selling, and asking for **a piece of** something.

## Want, buy, sell

- **Kśěm kupiś klěb.** — I want to buy bread.
- **Wóna kupujo knigłu.** — She is buying a book.
- **Wón pśedawa měso.** — He sells meat.

After **kśěm** (I want), the next verb stays in its dictionary form: **kupiś**.

## A piece of

**kusk** (piece) takes the "of" ending:

- **Kusk syra jo dobry.** — A piece of cheese is good.
- **Pśedawaŕ dajo kusk kśełbasy.** — The seller gives a piece of sausage.

## Describing

- **Cena jo wusoka.** — The price is high.
- **Kśełbasa jo swěža.** — The sausage is fresh.
- **Tascyna jo śěžka.** — The bag is heavy.

> **Watch out:** after **kusk** the food changes: **kusk syra**, not *kusk syr*.
