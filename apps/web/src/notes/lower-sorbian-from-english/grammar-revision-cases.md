In this lesson: a **review of the cases**, the different endings a word takes depending on its job.

## The cases you know

| Job | Example |
|---|---|
| the thing you see or have | **Wón wiźi swóju žonu.** — He sees his wife. |
| "not", "of" | **Brata how nje ma.** — There is no brother here. |
| giving to | **Dam žonje.** — I give to the woman. |
| "with" | **Powědamy z pśijaśelami.** — We talk with friends. |
| where | **Na pólu wjele źěłam.** — I work a lot in the field. |

## About someone

**wó** (about) takes the "where" ending:

- **Wóna powěda wó bratšach.** — She talks about brothers.

## Many

- **Jo wjele słyńcow we uniwersumje.** — There are many suns in the universe.
- **A teke jo wjele gwězdow.** — And there are also many stars.

> **Watch out:** **swój** means "his own", "her own": **Wón wiźi swóju žonu** is his own wife.
