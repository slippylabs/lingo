In this lesson: **school** words and the **locative case**, the form a place takes after **na** (on) and **we** (in).

## After na and we

- **Knigła jo na blidźe.** — The book is on the table. (**blido** → **blidźe**)
- **Pišom na papjerje z pjerom.** — I am writing on paper with a pen. (**papjer** → **papjerje**)
- **Šula jo we centrje.** — The school is in the center.

## Teachers and students

A woman's job word often ends in **‑ica**: **wucabnik** (male teacher), **wucabnica** (female teacher).

- **Wucabnik powěda z wuknikami.** — The teacher is talking with students.
- **Wuknik ma knigłu.** — The student has a book.

## Have, and "many"

After **wjele** (many) the word takes **‑ow**:

- **Ja mam nadawk.** — I have an assignment.
- **Wóna ma wjele nadawkow.** — She has many assignments.

> **Watch out:** the letter before the ending can soften: **blido** becomes **na blidźe**.
