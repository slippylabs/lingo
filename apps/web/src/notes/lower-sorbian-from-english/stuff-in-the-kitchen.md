In this lesson: **kitchen** things, and **z** for both **with** and **from**.

## In the kitchen

- **Nož ležy na blidźe.** — The knife is lying on the table.
- **Nož jo wóstšy.** — The knife is sharp.
- **Garšk jo gorěcy.** — The pot is hot.
- **Kuchnja jo cyšća.** — The kitchen is clean.
- **Konzerwa jo prozna.** — The can is empty.

## With or from

**z** + the "with" ending means "with"; **z** + the "of" ending means "from":

- **Wón jě z widłami.** — He eats with a fork.
- **Wóna pijo z tasy.** — She drinks from a cup.

**we** + the "where" ending means "in":

- **My warimy we garšku.** — We cook in a pot.

> **Watch out:** **widły** (fork) is a plural word, so it takes **su**: **Widły su slěborne.**
