In this lesson: the **hundreds**, and **a thousand**.

## The hundreds

- **sto** — one hundred
- **dwěsćo** — two hundred
- **tśisćo** — three hundred
- **styrěsćo** — four hundred
- **pěśsćo** — five hundred
- **tysac** — thousand

## With a noun

- **dwěsćo žonow** — two hundred women
- **pěśsćo žonow** — five hundred women
- **wosymsćo gólcow** — eight hundred boys
- **tysac wosym rublow** — one thousand eight rubles

> **Watch out:** after a number, **sto** becomes **‑sćo**: **dwěsćo**, **pěśsćo**.
