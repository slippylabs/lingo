In this lesson: the **hundreds**, and **a thousand**.

## The hundreds

- **sto** — one hundred
- **dwěsćě** — two hundred
- **tśista** — three hundred
- **styrista** — four hundred
- **pěśstow** — five hundred
- **tysac** — thousand

## With a noun

- **dwěsćě žonow** — two hundred women
- **pěśstow žonow** — five hundred women
- **wósymstow gólcow** — eight hundred boys
- **tysac a wósym rublow** — one thousand eight rubles

> **Watch out:** each hundred has its own shape: **dwěsćě** (200), **tśista** (300), **styrista** (400), but **pěśstow** (500) to **źewjeśstow** (900).
