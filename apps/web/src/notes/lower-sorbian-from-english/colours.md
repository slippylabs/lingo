In this lesson: **colours**, which change their ending like every describing word.

## Matching the word

- **Mój pjas jo carny.** — My dog is black.
- **Myš jo běła.** — The mouse is white.
- **Njebjo jo módre.** — The sky is blue.
- **Jej awto jo carne.** — Her car is black.
- **Mrokawy su šere.** — The clouds are grey.

## Before the word

- **Ja lubijom cerwjeny kólor.** — I like the red color.
- **Cerwjena róža rosćo how.** — A red rose grows here.

When a feminine word is the thing you have or carry, both words end in **‑u**:

- **Źowćo njaso běłu sukńu.** — The girl is carrying a white dress.

> **Watch out:** the colour follows the noun's gender: **carny** with **pjas**, but **carne** with **awto**.
