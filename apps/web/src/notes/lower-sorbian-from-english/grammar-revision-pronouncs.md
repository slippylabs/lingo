In this lesson: **me, you, us** and **who**, which change form like nouns.

## Me and you

- **Mje njewiźiš.** — You don't see me.
- **Daj mě wódu.** — Give me water.
- **Ja wiźim was.** — I see you.
- **Powědam z tobu.** — I am talking with you.
- **Wó mě powědaš?** — Are you talking about me?

## Who and what

"Who" is **chto**, but it changes too:

- **Kogo ty wiźiš?** — Who do you see?
- **Komu daš dar?** — Who do you give a gift to?
- **Wó com ty powědaš?** — What are you talking about?

## These

- **Te žony su krasne.** — These women are beautiful.

> **Watch out:** "me" has two forms: **mje** when someone sees you, **mě** when someone gives you something.
