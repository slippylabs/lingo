In this lesson: the verb **to be**, the words for **I, you, he, she**, and **this / that**.

## "To be"

| | |
|---|---|
| I am | **ja som** |
| you are | **ty sy** |
| he / she is | **wón jo** / **wóna jo** |
| we are | **my smy** |
| you (all) are | **wy sćo** |
| they are | **wóni su** |

- **Ja som mudry.** — I am wise.
- **Wóna jo wjelgin mudra.** — She is very wise.
- **My smy wjelgin mudre.** — We are very wise.
- **Chto ty sy?** — Who are you?

## Masculine, feminine, neuter

Every noun is masculine, feminine or neuter. Describing words change to match: **‑y** for masculine, **‑a** for feminine, **‑e** for neuter.

- **To jo dobry cłowjek.** — This is a good person.
- **Ta žona jo dobra.** — This woman is good.
- **Tamto ranjo jo zymne.** — That morning over there is cold.

## This and that

**ten**, **ta**, **to** mean "this"; **tamten**, **tamta** mean "that over there".

- **To jo moja sotša.** — This is my sister.
- **Tamten dom jo krasny.** — That house over there is beautiful.

## Not, and questions

Put **nje** in front of the verb; **jo** becomes **njejo**. For a question, start with the verb.

- **To njejo moja sotša.** — This is not my sister.
- **Jo to dobre awto?** — Is this a good car?

> **Watch out:** the verb changes with the person. "I am wise" is **Ja som mudry**, never *ja jo*.
