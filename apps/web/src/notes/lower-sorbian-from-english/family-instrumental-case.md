In this lesson: **family** words and the **instrumental case**, the form a word takes after **z** ("with").

## Masculine words: ‑om

- **Wón jo z wnukom.** — He is with his grandson.
- **Wón bydli z synom.** — He lives with his son.
- **Wón jo z mójim bratšom.** — He is with my brother.

## Feminine words: ‑u or ‑oju

- **Wóna gra z wnucku.** — She is playing with her granddaughter.
- **Wóni jězu z śěśkoju.** — They are going with their aunt.
- **Źomy do parka z familiju.** — We are going to the park with the family.

## More than one: ‑ami / ‑imi

- **Źomy na spacer z bratšami.** — We are going for a walk with brothers.
- **My gramy z našymi źiśimi.** — We are playing with our children.

"My" and "your" change too: **mójim** with a masculine word, **mójeju** with a feminine one.

- **Wóna powěda z mójeju babku.** — She is talking with my grandmother.
- **Z kim ty źeš?** — Who are you going with?

> **Watch out:** both words change after **z**: **z mójim bratšom**, not *z mój bratš*.
