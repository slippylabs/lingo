In this lesson: **clothes**, and the joining words **a** (and) and **abo** (or).

## And, or

- **Šalik jo śěžki a śopły.** — The scarf is heavy and warm.
- **Wóna ma carne nogawice, a wón běłe.** — She has black trousers, and he has white.
- **Ty kśěš šalik abo košulu.** — You want a scarf or a shirt.
- **Gace su krasne abo grane?** — Is the underwear beautiful or ugly?

## Wearing

- **Wón nosy módru košulu.** — He wears a blue shirt.
- **Nosym rukajce we zymje.** — I wear gloves in winter.
- **Wón woblěkujo nowu košulu.** — He is putting on a new shirt.

**gace** and **nogawice** are always plural, so they take **su**.

> **Watch out:** **nosyś** is "to wear", **njasć** is "to carry": **Wón nosy módru košulu**, but **Źowćo njaso běłu sukńu**.
