In this lesson: describing **how people look**, and comparing with **kaž** (like) and **njež** (than).

## Describing

- **Wón ma dłujke włosy.** — He has long hair.
- **Wóna ma zelene wocy.** — She has green eyes.
- **Jogo włosy su swětłe.** — His hair is blonde.
- **Stara žona nosy capku.** — The old woman wears a hat.
- **Tusty muž źe pó droze.** — The fat man is walking down the street.

**jogo** is "his", **jej** is "her".

## Like and than

- **Wóna jo niska a chuda kaž wón.** — She is short and thin like him.
- **A jogo rědne włosy su śamne kaž noc.** — And his beautiful hair is as dark as the night.
- **Chudy ma mańšu śělesnu masu njež tusty.** — The thin person has less body mass than the fat one.

> **Watch out:** **włosy** (hair) is plural, so it takes **su**: **Jogo włosy su swětłe.**
