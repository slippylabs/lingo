In this lesson: **countries**, **nationalities**, and saying which **language** someone speaks.

## People

- **Wón jo Słowjan.** — He is a Slav.
- **Wón jo Ruso.** — He is a Russian.
- **Wóna jo Chorwatka.** — She is a Croat.

A woman often adds **‑ka**: **Chorwat** → **Chorwatka**.

## Speaking a language

"Speak in the … language" is **w** + a word ending in **‑skej** + **rěcy**:

- **Englez powěda w engelskej rěcy.** — An Englishman speaks the English language.
- **Chorwat powěda w chorwatskej rěcy.** — A Croat speaks the Croatian language.
- **Powědaš lě w nimskej rěcy?** — Do you speak the German language?

## Countries

- **Kraj jo Pólska.** — The country is Poland.
- **Nimc źiwe w Nimskej.** — A German lives in Germany.
- **Wón zna tśi rěcy.** — He knows three languages.

> **Watch out:** people take a capital letter, language words don't: **Nimc**, but **nimskej rěcy**.
