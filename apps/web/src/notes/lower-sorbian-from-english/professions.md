In this lesson: **jobs**, and saying what people do.

## Who does what

- **Mój bratš jo wucabnik.** — My brother is a teacher.
- **Wóna jo lěkaŕ.** — She is a doctor.
- **Mój wótšc jo kuchaŕ.** — My father is a cook.
- **Policaj stoja na droze.** — The policeman is standing on the street.

## At work

- **Lěkaŕ pomaga luźam.** — The doctor helps people.
- **Źěłaśeŕ twari dom.** — The worker is building a house.
- **Inžinjer cyni plan.** — The engineer makes a plan.
- **Šofer wjeźo awtobus.** — The driver drives a bus.

Many job words end in **‑aŕ** or **‑eŕ**: **kuchaŕ**, **pśedawaŕ**, **źěłaśeŕ**.

> **Watch out:** *ŕ* is its own letter, a soft r. Type **lěkaŕ**, not *lěkar*.
