In this lesson: **nature**, and the little words **pśez**, **mjazy**, **do**, **k** and **za**.

## Nature

- **Góra jo wusoka.** — The mountain is high.
- **Tšawa jo zelena.** — The grass is green.
- **Pólo jo šyroke.** — The field is wide.
- **Kwěśeń krasnje kwitnu.** — The flowers bloom beautifully.

## Through, between, into, towards, behind

- **Rěka śěco pśez žłobišćo.** — The river flows through the valley.
- **Žłobišćo ležy mjazy górami.** — The valley lies between the mountains.
- **My źomy do lěsa.** — We are going to the forest.
- **Wóda źe k rěce.** — Water goes to the river.
- **Pólo jo za domom.** — The field is behind the house.

Each one sets the ending of the next word: **lěs** → **do lěsa**, *góry* → **mjazy górami**.

> **Watch out:** **do** changes the place word: **do lěsa**, not *do lěs*.
