In this lesson: **first, second, third**, the ordinal numbers.

## They work like describing words

- **prědny** — first
- **drugi** — second
- **tśeśi** — third
- **styrty** — fourth
- **pěty** — fifth
- **sotny** — hundredth

Like any describing word, the ending matches the noun:

- **sotny dolar** — hundredth dollar
- **šesćsotne kóło** — six hundredth bicycle
- **styrěsćo pěta góra** — four hundred fifth mountain

## Big ordinals

Only the last part becomes an ordinal:

- **dwěsćo prědny bratš** — two hundred first brother
- **wosymsćo drugi dolar** — eight hundred second dollar

> **Watch out:** the ending follows the noun's gender: **pěta góra** (feminine), but **sotny dolar** (masculine).
