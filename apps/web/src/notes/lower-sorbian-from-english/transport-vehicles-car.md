In this lesson: **vehicles and roads**, and two verbs for **going**.

## Vehicles

- **Awto stoja na droze.** — The car is standing on the street.
- **Awtobus jěźo do centra.** — The bus goes to the center.
- **Śug jěźo na dwórnišćo.** — The train goes to the station.
- **Awtobus wjeźo wjele luźi.** — The bus carries many people.

## Roads and bridges

- **Droga jo šyroka.** — The road is wide.
- **Droga źe pśez lěs.** — The road goes through the forest.
- **Most zwězajo dwě měsće.** — The bridge connects two cities.
- **Drogowe swěśełko pokazujo zelene.** — The traffic light shows green.

> **Watch out:** a vehicle "goes" with **jěźo**; a road or a person on foot "goes" with **źe**.
