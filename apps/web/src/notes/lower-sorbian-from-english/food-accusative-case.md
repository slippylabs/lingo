In this lesson: **food**, and the **accusative case**, the form for the thing you eat, buy or see.

## Feminine words change

A feminine word ending in **‑a** switches to **‑u**:

- **Cytaš ty knigłu a jěš zjutšnu?** — Are you reading a book and eating breakfast? (**knigła** → **knigłu**)
- **My wiźimy twóju picu na blidźe.** — We see your pizza on the table.

## Most other words stay the same

- **Ja jěm klěb.** — I am eating bread.
- **Wóna jě měso.** — She is eating meat.
- **Wón lubi mloko.** — He likes milk.
- **Ja kupujom masło.** — I am buying butter.

## "To eat" is irregular

**ja jěm**, **ty jěš**, **wóna jě**:

- **Ty jěš jajco?** — Are you eating an egg?

## "That"

**až** joins two sentences, like English "that":

- **Ja wěm, až ty lubiš jajca.** — I know that you like eggs.

> **Watch out:** a man you see changes too: *Wlad* → **Wlada** in **Ja wiźim Wlada z jědłom** — I see Vlad with food.
