In this lesson: **parts of the body**, saying something **hurts**, and the **pair form** for two.

## Hurting

- **Mója ruka boli.** — My hand hurts.
- **Žołdk śi boli.** — Your stomach hurts.

**śi** means "to you": literally "the stomach hurts to you".

## Describing

- **Wón ma módre krasne wocy.** — He has beautiful blue eyes.
- **Jej wucho njesłyšo derje.** — Her ear does not hear well.
- **Nos jo cerwjeny wót mroza.** — The nose is red from the cold.

**woko** is one eye; **wocy** are eyes.

## Two hands, two legs

After **dwě**, words take their pair ending:

- **Ja mam dwě ruce a dwě noze a teke dwě stopje.** — I have two hands and two legs, and also two feet.

> **Watch out:** the pair form can change a letter: **ruka** → **dwě ruce**, **noga** → **dwě noze**.
