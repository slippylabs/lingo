In this lesson: **hobbies**, and how a verb changes for **I, you, he, we, they**.

## The ending shows who

The dictionary form ends in **‑ś** (**cytaś** — to read). Drop it and add the ending for the person:

| | cytaś / graś / słuchaś |
|---|---|
| I | **cytam** |
| you | **cytaš** |
| he / she | **cyta** |
| we | **gramy** |
| they | **słuchaju** |

- **Ja cytam knigłu.** — I am reading a book.
- **Cytaš ty romany?** — Do you read novels?
- **Wóna sedźi a cyta.** — She is sitting and reading.
- **My gramy w šach.** — We are playing chess.
- **Wóni słuchaju muziku.** — They are listening to music.

## Verbs in ‑owaś

These swap **‑owaś** for **‑ujom**/**‑ojom** with "I":

- **Ja mólojom wobraz.** — I am painting a picture.
- **Ja tancyrujom wjele** — I dance a lot

One form covers both "I read" and "I am reading".

> **Watch out:** don't keep the **‑ś**: "I play" is **gram**, not *graśm*.
