In this lesson: **asking the way**, and saying **near**, **far** and **left**.

## Asking

- **Źo jo dwórnišćo?** — Where is the station?
- **Kuźo źe droga?** — Which way does the road go?
- **Móžeśo lě pokazaś puśik?** — Can you show me the way?
- **Wón pokazujo mě drogu.** — He shows me the way.

## Answers

- **Źi proste do mosta.** — Go straight to the bridge.
- **Pótom na lěwe.** — Then to the left.
- **Dwórnišćo jo na lěwe.** — The station is on the left.

**Źi** tells one person to go; **Źiśo** tells several people, or one person politely.

## Near and far

- **Wobchod jo blisko biblioteki.** — The shop is near the library.
- **Nams jo daloko wót hotela.** — The square is far from the hotel.

> **Watch out:** **blisko** and **wót** take the "of" ending: **biblioteka** → **blisko biblioteki**.
