In this lesson: **sports**, saying **not**, and saying **where** with **we** (in) and **na** (on, at).

## Playing

- **Gram we football.** — I play football.
- **Wóna gra we basketball.** — She plays basketball.
- **Chto gra we tennis?** — Who plays tennis?
- **Ja nje gram we hockey.** — I do not play hockey.

## Where

After **we** and **na** the place word takes a new ending, usually **‑e** or **‑je**:

- **My plowamy we rěce.** — We swim in the river. (**rěka** → **rěce**)
- **Wóna gra na stadionje.** — She plays at the stadium. (**stadion** → **stadionje**)

## How often

- **Wón trenujo kuždy źeń.** — He trains every day.
- **My plowamy cesto.** — We swim often.
- **Wóni běgaju ranim.** — They run in the morning.

> **Watch out:** **nje** ("not") goes straight before the verb: **Ja nje gram we hockey.**
