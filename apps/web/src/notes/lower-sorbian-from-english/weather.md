In this lesson: **weather**, and sentences with **no word for "it"**.

## Weather

- **Słyńco swěśi.** — The sun is shining.
- **Pada dešć.** — It is raining.
- **Sněg ležy na zemji.** — Snow lies on the ground.
- **Mróz jo sylny.** — The frost is strong.
- **Mrokawy su śamne.** — The clouds are dark.

## "It is warm"

There is no "it". The describing word ends in **‑o**:

- **Źinsaj jo śopło.** — It is warm today.
- **Źinsaj jo śopło a sucho.** — It is warm and dry today.

## How the wind blows

**sylny** (strong) becomes **sylnje** (strongly):

- **Wětš dmujo sylnje.** — The wind is blowing strongly.

> **Watch out:** "it is raining" is literally "falls rain": **Pada dešć**.
