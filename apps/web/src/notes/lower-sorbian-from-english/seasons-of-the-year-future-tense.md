In this lesson: the **seasons**, and the **future tense**: what will happen.

## Seasons

- **Zyma ma wjele sněga.** — Winter has a lot of snow.
- **Nazymje ma wjele kólorow.** — Autumn has many colors.
- **We lěśe jo wjele słyńca.** — In the summer, there is a lot of sun.

"In summer" and "in winter" use **we**; "in autumn" uses **na**: **we lěśe**, **we zymje**, **na nazymje**.

## The future

A form of "will be" plus the dictionary form of the verb (ending in **‑ś**):

- **Na nazymje buźomy wiźeś padajuce łopjena.** — In the autumn we will see falling leaves.
- **We zymje buźośo graś ze sněgom.** — In the winter you will play with snow.
- **Na nazymje buźoš wjele śarpiś pśez dešć.** — In the autumn, you will suffer a lot because of the rain.
- **Wóni budu wiźeś zelenu krajinu.** — They will see a green landscape.

> **Watch out:** the second verb doesn't change: **buźomy wiźeś**, not *buźomy wiźimy*.
