In this lesson: **north, south, east, west**, and saying **in the north**.

## Directions

- **Nord jo zymny.** — The north is cold.
- **Sud jo śopły.** — The south is warm.
- **Pódzajtšo jo tam.** — The east is there.
- **Kompas pokazujo nord.** — The compass shows north.

## In the north

**na** + the "where" ending:

- **Na nordźe jo sněg.** — In the north there is snow.
- **Žyjomy na suźe.** — We live in the south.

## Describing words

- **To jo nordowy bok.** — This is the northern side.
- **Zapadny wětš dmujo.** — A western wind is blowing.
- **Pódzajtšna muzika jo zajmna.** — Eastern music is interesting.

> **Watch out:** the last letter softens before the ending: **sud** → **na suźe**, **nord** → **na nordźe**.
