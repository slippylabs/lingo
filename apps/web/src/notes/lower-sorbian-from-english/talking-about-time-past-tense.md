In this lesson: **time words**, and the **past tense**: what someone did or was.

## Time words

- **cera** — yesterday
- **źinsaj** — today
- **zajtša** — tomorrow
- **pśełnoc** — midnight

## "Was"

- **Cera běšo póńźele.** — Yesterday was Monday.
- **Ranim nje běšo wjele śopło.** — In the morning it was not very warm.

## The past tense

Use the helper (**som**, **sy**, **jo**, **smy**, **sćo**, **su**) plus a verb ending in **‑ł** (a man), **‑ła** (a woman) or **‑li** (a group):

- **Ja som był we domje ranim.** — I was at home in the morning.
- **Wy sćo šeźeli do kina?** — Have you gone to the cinema?
- **Źinsaj ty nje sy měł casa za to.** — Today you did not have time for that.

> **Watch out:** the ending shows who did it: a man **šedł**, a woman **šeźeła**, a group **šedli**.
