In this lesson: **rooms and furniture**, and saying where things are with **na**, **pód**, **nad** and **blisko**.

## Where things are

**pód** (under) and **nad** (above) take the "with" ending; **na** (on) takes the "where" ending; **blisko** (near) takes the "of" ending.

- **Skrinja jo pód łóżkom.** — The chest is under the bed.
- **Lampa swěśi nad blidom.** — The lamp shines above the table.
- **Na blidźe ležy knigła.** — A book is lying on the table.
- **Połica wisi na stěnje.** — The shelf hangs on the wall.
- **Stoł jo blisko blida.** — The chair is near the table.

## Describing

- **Łóżko jo měke.** — The bed is soft.
- **Wokno jo wótwórjone.** — The window is open.
- **Rum jo śopły a śichy.** — The room is warm and quiet.
- **Šafa jo pełna.** — The wardrobe is full.

> **Watch out:** **wrota** (door) is always plural, so it takes **su**: **Wrota su zawrěśe.**
