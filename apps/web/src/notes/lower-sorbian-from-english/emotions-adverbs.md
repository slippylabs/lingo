In this lesson: **feelings**, and **how-words** (adverbs) like "proudly".

## Feelings

- **Lubosć jo sylna emocija.** — Love is a strong emotion.
- **Strah jo pśirodna reakcija.** — Fear is a natural reaction.
- **Měr jo ważny za dušu.** — Peace is important for the soul.
- **Ganjba jo wjelgin sylna.** — Shame is very strong.

## Feeling

- **Cujom se wjasołe źinsaj.** — I feel joyful today.
- **My cujomy měr.** — We feel peaceful.

## How-words

Many come from a describing word with a new ending: **górdy** (proud) → **górdo** (proudly).

- **Wón chowa górdo swój gniw.** — He hides his anger proudly.
- **Pśestrašony wón jo ćěkał.** — He was frightened and ran away.

> **Watch out:** the how-word can come first: **Smuśne wón jo powědał** — He spoke sadly.
