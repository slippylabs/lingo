In this lesson: **animals**, and the **dative case**, the form for the one you give something to or help.

## Masculine: ‑owi or ‑oju

- **Dajomy konjowi wódu.** — We give the horse water.
- **Wón pomaga ptaškowi.** — He helps the bird.
- **Pomagamy wjelkoju.** — We help the wolf.

## Feminine: ‑e

- **Ja dam kócce rybu.** — I give the cat a fish. (**kócka** → **kócce**)
- **Ja dam rybje wódu.** — I give the fish water.

## More than one: ‑am

- **My pomagamy ptaškam.** — We help the birds.
- **Dajomy žabam wódu.** — We give the frogs water.

**pomagaś** (to help) always takes this form: you help *to* someone.

> **Watch out:** two words change: the thing you give (**rybu**, **wódu**) and the one who gets it (**kócce**).
