In this lesson: **fruit**, and describing words that **match** the fruit.

## The ending follows the word

- **Banan jo žołty.** — The banana is yellow. (masculine: **‑y**)
- **Gruška jo smatna.** — The pear is tasty. (feminine: **‑a**)
- **Jabłuko jo cerwjene.** — The apple is red. (neuter: **‑e**)
- **Citron jo kisały a žołty.** — The lemon is sour and yellow.
- **Slěwka jo juž zdrała.** — The plum is already ripe.

## More than one

- **Pomarańce su słodke.** — The oranges are sweet.
- **Gruški laže na blidźe.** — The pears are lying on the table.
- **Wišnja rosćo na bomje.** — The cherry grows on a tree.

## "Made from"

**z** with the "of" form means "from": *slěwki* → **z slěwkow**.

- **Wón gotujo slěwowicu z slěwkow.** — He is preparing plum brandy from plums.

> **Watch out:** the ending follows the fruit, not the colour: **Banan jo žołty**, but **Jabłuko jo cerwjene**.
