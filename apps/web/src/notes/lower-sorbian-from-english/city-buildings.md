In this lesson: **places in a city**, and how place words change after **we** and **na**.

## In the city

- **Kupujomy klěb we wobchoźe.** — We buy bread in the shop.
- **Cytam knigłu we bibliotece.** — I read a book in the library.
- **Dom stoja na droze.** — The house stands on the street.
- **Na wikowanišću jo wjele luźi.** — There are many people at the market.

## Going to, near

**do** (to) and **blisko** (near) take the "of" ending:

- **Źomy do nama.** — We are going to the square.
- **Dwórnišćo jo blisko centra.** — The station is near the center.

## Describing

- **Cerkwja jo stara a běła.** — The church is old and white.
- **Most źe pśez rěku.** — The bridge goes over the river.

> **Watch out:** letters change before the "where" ending: **droga** → **na droze**, **wobchod** → **we wobchoźe**.
