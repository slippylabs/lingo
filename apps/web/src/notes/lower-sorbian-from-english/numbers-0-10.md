In this lesson: the numbers **0 to 10**, and how the **noun changes** after each number.

## The numbers

**nul**, **jaden**, **dwa**, **tśi**, **styrjo**, **pěś**, **šesć**, **sedym**, **wosym**, **źewjeś**, **źaset**

## One

"One" matches the noun's gender:

- **jaden gólc** — one boy
- **jadna žona** — one woman
- **jadno pólo** — one field

## Two: a special pair form

After two, the noun takes its own "pair" ending (called the dual):

- **dwaj gólca** — two boys
- **dwě žoni** — two women

## Three and four

- **tśi kócki** — three cats
- **styrjo kóła** — four bicycles

## Five and up: ‑ow

- **pěś gólcow** — five boys
- **šesć kółow** — six bicycles

> **Watch out:** after two it's **dwě žoni**, not the plural *žony*.
