In this lesson: **drinks**, and the **genitive case**, the form after **not**, **without** and amounts like "a bottle of".

## After "not"

Words in **‑a** switch to **‑y**; words in **‑o** switch to **‑a**:

- **Ja nje mam wódy.** — I do not have water. (**wóda** → **wódy**)
- **Wóna nje pijo wina.** — She does not drink wine. (**wino** → **wina**)
- **My nje pijomy piwa.** — We do not drink beer.

"Does not have" is one word: **Wón njama kafi.** — He does not have coffee.

## After bźez (without)

- **Ja lubijom sok bźez cukora.** — I like juice without sugar.
- **Wón nje lubi kafi bźez mloka.** — He does not like coffee without milk.

## Amounts

- **Ty maš flasku soka?** — Do you have a bottle of juice?
- **Cokel wina za mě, prosim.** — A glass of wine for me, please.
- **Butelka wódy ležy na blidźe.** — A bottle of water is lying on the table.

> **Watch out:** after "not" the drink changes: **Ja nje mam wódy**, not *nje mam wóda*.
