In this lesson: **shapes**, the numbers **three** and **four**, and saying **of**.

## Shapes

- **To jo krug.** — This is a circle.
- **Tśirožk ma tśi boki.** — A triangle has three sides.
- **Kwadrat ma styri boki.** — A square has four sides.
- **Ta linija jo dłujka.** — This line is long.
- **Dypk ležy na liniji.** — The point lies on the line.

## "Of a square"

"Of" is an ending, not a word. **kwadrat** becomes **kwadrata**:

- **Wiźimy wobrys kwadrata.** — We see the outline of a square.
- **Blido ma formu kwadrata.** — The table has the shape of a square.

## Matching

- **To jo prosta forma.** — This is a simple shape. (**forma** is feminine)
- **To jo prosty tśirožk.** — This is a simple triangle. (**tśirožk** is masculine)

> **Watch out:** after **ma** (has), **forma** becomes **formu**.
