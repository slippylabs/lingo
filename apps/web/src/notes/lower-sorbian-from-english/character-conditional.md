In this lesson: words for **character**, and **would** (the conditional).

## Character

- **Wón jo dobry cłowjek.** — He is a good person.
- **Wóna jo dobra a šćera.** — She is good and sincere.
- **Wón jo sylny ale wusudny.** — He is strong but brave.
- **Wón jo słaby we sporśe.** — He is weak in sports.

## A describing word as a person

On its own, a describing word can mean "a … person":

- **Mudry wě wjele.** — A smart person knows much.
- **Lěny nje kśě nic cyniś.** — Lazy people do not want to do anything.

## Would

"Would" uses **by** with the past form of the verb: **był by** (would be, one person), **byli by** (would be, several). Learn this lesson's "If…, … would…" sentences as whole phrases.

> **Watch out:** a double "not" is normal: **Lěny nje kśě nic cyniś** is literally "doesn't want nothing to do".
