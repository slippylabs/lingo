In this lesson: **feelings**, and how to say **how** someone does something.

## Feelings

- **miłość** — love
- **strach** — fear
- **wstyd** — shame
- **pokōj** — peace
- **zawiś** — envy

## I feel

- **Czuja sie wesoło dzisiŏj.** — I feel joyful today.
- **My czujymy pokōj.** — We feel peaceful.

## How: words ending in ‑o

A word that says how something is done (an adverb) often ends in **‑o**:

- **wesoły** (joyful) → **Wesoło ôn szedł do dōmu.** — He joyfully went home.
- **smutny** (sad) → **Smutno ôn godoł.** — He spoke sadly.

## "Is" with to

**to** can link two nouns, like "is":

- **Miłość to mocno emocijo.** — Love is a strong emotion.
- **Strach to naturalno reakcyjo.** — Fear is a natural reaction.

> **Watch out:** **barzo** means "very": **Wstyd je barzo mocny.** — Shame is very strong.
