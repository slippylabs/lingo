In this lesson: **describing people**, and how to say **more than**.

## Looks

- **wysoki** — tall
- **niski** — short
- **gruby** — fat
- **chudy** — thin
- **młody** — young
- **stary** — old

## More than

This course compares with **barzij** (more) plus the describing word, then **niż** (than):

- **Ôn je barzij wysoki a mocny niż ôna.** — He is taller and stronger than she is.
- **Stare ludzie sōm barzij mondre niż my.** — Old people are wiser than us.
- **Jej ôczy sōm barzij zielōne niż jego.** — Her eyes are greener than his.

## As... as, like

Use **jak**:

- **Ôna je nisko a chudo jak ôn.** — She is short and thin like him.
- **A jego piykne włosy sōm ćmawe jak noc.** — And his beautiful hair is as dark as the night.

> **Watch out:** **mynij** means "less": **Chudy mo mynij ciała niż gruby.** — The thin person has less body mass than the fat one.
