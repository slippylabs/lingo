In this lesson: **nature words** like river, forest and mountain.

## Landscape

- **rzyka** — river
- **gōra** — mountain
- **dolina** — valley
- **las** — forest
- **pōle** — field
- **kamiyń** — stone

## Plants

- **trowa** — grass
- **blum** — flower
- **strōm** — tree
- **Trowa je zielōno.** — The grass is green.
- **Blumy piyknie kwitnōm.** — The flowers bloom beautifully.

## Going somewhere

- **My idymy do lasa.** — We are going to the forest.
- **Woda idzie do rzyki.** — Water goes to the river.

After **do** (to) the noun changes: **las** becomes **lasa**, **rzyka** becomes **rzyki**.

> **Watch out:** **bez** here means "through": **Ôni idōm bez las, kaj je moc strōmōw.** It also means "without".
