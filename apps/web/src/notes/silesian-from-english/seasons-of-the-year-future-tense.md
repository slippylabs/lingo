In this lesson: **the seasons** and how to talk about the **future**.

## Seasons

- **wiosna** — spring
- **lato** — summer
- **jesiyń** — autumn
- **zima** — winter

"In summer" and "in winter" change the ending:

- **We lecie bydzie ciepło.** — In the summer it will be warm.
- **We zimie bydzie padać śniyg.** — In the winter it will snow.

## "Will"

Use a form of **bydzie** (will be) plus the basic verb:

| | form |
|---|---|
| I will | **byda** |
| you will | **bydziesz** |
| he / she / it will | **bydzie** |
| we will | **bydzymy** |
| they will | **bydōm** |

- **We zimie bydziecie grać ze śniygiem.** — In the winter you will play with snow.
- **Ôni bydōm widzieć zielōny krajôbraz.** — They will see a green landscape.

> **Watch out:** **bydzie** on its own already means "it will be": **Luft we zimie bydzie zimny.** — The air in winter will be cold.
