In this lesson: **clothes**, and the linking words **and**, **or**, **but**.

## Clothes

- **heja** — shirt
- **galoty** — trousers
- **mantyl** — coat
- **czopka** — hat
- **szalik** — scarf
- **ôbuw** — footwear

## Wearing

- **Ôn nosi modro heja.** — He wears a blue shirt.
- **Nosza rynkawiczki we zimie.** — I wear gloves in winter.

## And, or, but

- **a** — and: **Szalik je ciynżki a ciepły.** — The scarf is heavy and warm.
- **abo** — or: **Ty chcesz szalik abo heja.** — You want a scarf or a shirt.
- **a** can also mean "but" when comparing two things: **Ôna mo czŏrne galoty, a ôn biołe.** — She has black trousers, and he has white.

> **Watch out:** **galoty** (trousers) and **gacie** (underwear) are always plural: **Nowe gacie som wygodne.**
