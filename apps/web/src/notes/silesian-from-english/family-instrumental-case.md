In this lesson: **family words** and how to say **"with"** someone.

## Family

- **brat** — brother
- **matka** — mother
- **ôjc** — father
- **cōrka** — daughter
- **starzik** — grandfather
- **babka** — grandmother

## "With" changes the word

After **z** ("with") the noun takes a different ending. Grammar books call this the instrumental case: the form a word takes after "with".

- masculine words add **‑ym**: **Ôn je z moim bratym.** — He is with my brother.
- feminine words in **‑a** switch to **‑ōm**: **Ôna idzie z matkōm.** — She is going with her mother.
- more than one person takes **‑ami**: **Idymy na szpacer z bratami.** — We are going for a walk with brothers.

The word for "my" or "your" changes too:

- **Godōm z twojōm siostrōm.** — I am talking with your sister.
- **Ôna godo z mojōm babkōm.** — She is talking with my grandmother.

## Who with?

- **Z kim ty idziesz?** — Who are you going with?

> **Watch out:** before some words **z** becomes **ze**: **Ôn godo ze starzikym.** — He is talking with his grandfather.
