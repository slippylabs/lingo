In this lesson: **revision**: **me, you, us** and other little words in different sentences.

## Me and you

- **Mnie niy widzisz.** — You don't see me.
- **Jo widza wos.** — I see you.
- **Godōm z tobōm.** — I am talking with you.
- **Ô mnie godosz?** — Are you talking about me?

## Who and what

- **Kogo ty widzisz?** — Who do you see?
- **Komu dōsz darōnek?** — Who do you give a gift to?
- **Ô czym ty godosz?** — What are you talking about?

## This and these

- **Te baby som piykne.** — These women are beautiful.
- **Tego niy wia.** — I don't know that.

## Himself

**sobie** and **sobōm** point back to the person doing the action:

- **Ôn lubi być sam ze sobōm.** — He likes to be alone with himself.

> **Watch out:** "you" changes its form: **ty** (you, doing it), **cie** (you, seen), **tobōm** (with you), **ô tobie** (about you).
