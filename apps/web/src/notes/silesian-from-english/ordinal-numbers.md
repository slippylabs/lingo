In this lesson: **first, second, third**, and how to say **at what time**.

## Order words

These are describing words, so their ending matches the noun:

- **piyrwszy** — first
- **drugi** — second
- **trzeci** — third
- **sztwŏrty** — fourth
- **piōnty** — fifth

- **trzidziesty chop** — thirtieth man
- **ôziymset drugi dolar** — eight hundred second dollar

## Telling the time

Say **ô** (at), the order word with **‑yj**, then **godzinie** (hour):

- **ô piōntyj godzinie** — at the fifth hour
- **ô dziewiōntyj godzinie** — at the ninth hour

Add the minutes at the end:

- **ô drugij godzinie piyntnaście** — at fifteen past the second hour
- **ô piyrwszyj godzinie dwadzieścia** — at twenty past the first hour

> **Watch out:** the hour is an order word ("the second hour"), but the minutes are plain numbers.
