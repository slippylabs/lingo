In this lesson: **places in a town** and how to say where they are.

## In town

- **miasto** — city
- **ulica** — street
- **rynek** — square/market
- **sklep** — shop/store
- **most** — bridge
- **kościōł** — church

## In and at

After **we** (in) and **na** (on, at) the place word changes:

- **sklep** → **Kupujymy chlyb we sklepie.** — We buy bread in the shop.
- **biblijoteka** → **Czytōm ksiōnżka we biblijotece.** — I read a book in the library.
- **ulica** → **Dōm stoji na ulicy.** — The house stands on the street.

## Near and to

- **Banhof je blisko cyntrum.** — The station is near the center.
- **Idymy do rynka.** — We are going to the square.

> **Watch out:** **banhof** is the railway station, from German. Many Silesian town words come from German.
