In this lesson: **drinks**, and how words change after **not**, **without** and **a bottle of**.

## Drinks

- **woda** — water
- **piwo** — beer
- **wino** — wine
- **sok** — juice
- **kafa** — coffee

## After "not have" and "not drink"

After **niy** with verbs like "have" and "drink", the drink takes a new ending. This is the genitive case: the "of" form of a word.

- **woda** → **Jo niy mōm wody.** — I do not have water.
- **wino** → **Ôna niy pije wina.** — She does not drink wine.
- **piwo** → **My niy pijymy piwa.** — We do not drink beer.
- **kafa** → **Ôn niy mo kafy.** — He does not have coffee.

## After "without" and "a bottle of"

The same form follows **bez** (without) and amounts:

- **Pijesz bez mlyka?** — Do you drink without milk?
- **Jo lubia sok bez cukra.** — I like juice without sugar.
- **Ty mosz flaszka soku?** — Do you have a bottle of juice?

> **Watch out:** the change only comes with "not". **Ty pijesz wino?** keeps **wino**; **Ôna niy pije wina.** changes it.
