In this lesson: **kitchen things** and **cooking**.

## Things on the table

- **nōż** — knife
- **widly** — fork
- **łyżka** — spoon
- **talyrz** — plate
- **szolka** — cup

## Cooking

- **garnek** — pot
- **patelnia** — frying pan
- **My warzymy we garnku.** — We cook in a pot.
- **Garnek je na ôgniu.** — The pot is on the flame.

## Full of and from

- **Talyrz je pełny jedzynia.** — The plate is full of food.
- **Ôna pije z szolki.** — She drinks from a cup.

After **pełny** (full of) and **z** (from) the noun changes: **jedzynie** becomes **jedzynia**, **szolka** becomes **szolki**.

> **Watch out:** "with a fork" uses the "with" ending and no little word: **Ôn je widlami.** — He eats with a fork.
