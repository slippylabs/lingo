In this lesson: **sports**, how to say you **play** a game, and **in / at** a place.

## Playing a game

To play a sport, say **gro we** (plays in) before it:

- **Grōm we fusbal.** — I play football.
- **Ôna gro we kôszkôwka.** — She plays basketball.
- **Ôn gro we tenis.** — He plays tennis.
- **Jo niy grōm we hokej.** — I do not play hockey.

## Places: we and na

After **we** (in) and **na** (on, at) a place word changes its ending:

- **rzyka** → **We rzyce**: **My pływōmy we rzyce.** — We swim in the river.
- **miasto** → **We mieście**: **Sztadijōn je we mieście.** — The stadium is in the city.
- **sztadijōn** → **na sztadijōnie**: **Ôna gro na sztadijōnie.** — She plays at the stadium.

## How often, how fast

- **Ôn trenuje kożdy dziyń.** — He trains every day.
- **Ôn biygŏ wartko.** — He runs fast.
- **My pływōmy często.** — We swim often.

> **Watch out:** "not" goes right before the verb: **Jo niy grōm we hokej.**
