In this lesson: **north, south, east, west**, and **left, right, straight**.

## The four directions

- **pōłnoc** — north
- **połednie** — south
- **weschōd** — east
- **zachōd** — west

## In the north

After **na** (in, on) the direction changes its ending:

- **Na pōłnocy je śniyg.** — In the north there is snow.
- **Miyszkomy na połedniu.** — We live in the south.
- **Słōńce wstōwŏ na weschodzie.** — The sun rises in the east.

## Describing words

- **To je pōłnocno strōna.** — This is the northern side.
- **Zachodni wiater dmie.** — A western wind is blowing.
- **Połedniowe kraje som ciepłe.** — The southern regions are warm.

## Left, right, straight

- **prowo** — right
- **lewo** — left
- **prosto** — straight

> **Watch out:** **pōłnoc** also means "midnight". The sentence tells you which one is meant.
