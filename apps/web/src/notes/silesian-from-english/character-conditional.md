In this lesson: **character words** like brave and lazy, and how to say **would**.

## Character

- **dobry** — good
- **mondry** — wise
- **głupi** — stupid
- **lyńiwy** — lazy
- **szczery** — sincere
- **ôdwŏżny** — brave

- **Mondry wia moc.** — A smart person knows much.
- **Szczery kamrat je drogi.** — A sincere friend is valuable.

## "Would"

To say "would", use a past form with **by** plus a personal ending:

| | form |
|---|---|
| I would be | **bōł bych** |
| you would be | **bōł byś** |
| we would be | **byli byśmy** |
| you (all) would be | **byli byście** |

"If" is **jak**, and "then" is **to**:

- **Jak byś ty niy bōł głupi, to byś niy robiōł głupot.** — If you were not stupid, you would not do foolish things.

> **Watch out:** a describing word can stand for a person: **Głupi sie niy uczy.** — Stupid people do not learn.
