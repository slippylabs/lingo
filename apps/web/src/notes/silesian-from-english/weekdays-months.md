In this lesson: **days of the week** and **months**.

## Days

- **pōniydziałek** — Monday
- **wtorek** — Tuesday
- **środa** — Wednesday
- **czwartek** — Thursday
- **piyńtek** — Friday
- **sobota** — Saturday
- **niedziela** — Sunday

## In a month

After **we** (in) the month changes its ending:

- **styczeń** → **Mōm we styczniu wakyjōm.** — I have vacation in January.
- **siyrpień** → **We siyrpniu ida do Honduras.** — In August, I am going to Honduras.
- **luty** → **We lutym je moc śniyga.** — In February there is a lot of snow.

## On a day

- **We piyńtek wieczorym idymy na film.** — On Friday evening we are going to the movies.
- **Pōniydziałek je piyrwszy dziyń we tydniu.** — Monday is the first day of the week.

> **Watch out:** days and months are written with a small letter: **pōniydziałek**, **styczeń**.
