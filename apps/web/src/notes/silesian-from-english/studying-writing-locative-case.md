In this lesson: **school words**, and where things are: **on** and **in**.

## School

- **szkōła** — school
- **ksiōnżka** — book
- **zeszyt** — notebook
- **pjyro** — pen
- **papjōr** — paper

## On and in

After **na** (on) and **we** (in), a noun takes the locative ending: the form for "where something is".

- **stōł** → **Ksiōnżka je na stole.** — The book is on the table.
- **papjōr** → **Pisza na papjōrze z pjyrym.** — I am writing on paper with a pen.

## Jobs after "is"

To say what someone's job is, the job word takes **‑ym** (men) or **‑ōm** (women):

- **Ôna je rechtōrkōm.** — She is a teacher.
- **Ôn je uczńym.** — He is a student.
- **Ôn je moim rechtōrym.** — He is my teacher.

> **Watch out:** a teacher is **rechtōr** for a man and **rechtōrka** for a woman.
