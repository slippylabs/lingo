In this lesson: **shopping**: prices, buying and selling.

## In the shop

- **cena** — price
- **tasza** — bag
- **kōnsek** — piece
- **parōgon** — receipt
- **sprzedŏwca** — seller

## Buying and selling

- **Chca kupić chlyb.** — I want to buy bread.
- **Ôna kupuje ksiōnżka.** — She is buying a book.
- **Ôn sprzedŏwŏ miyso.** — He sells meat.

## A piece of

After **kōnsek** (a piece of) and **cena** (the price of), the next noun changes:

- **Kōnsek syra je dobry.** — A piece of cheese is good.
- **Kōnsek chlyba je mały.** — The piece of bread is small.
- **Cena masła je dobro.** — The price of butter is good.

> **Watch out:** "I want" is **chca**, and the next verb stays in its basic form: **Chca kupić chlyb.**
