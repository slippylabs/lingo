In this lesson: **hobbies** and how verbs change for **I, you, he, we, they**.

## Verb endings

The end of a verb shows who is doing it. Here is **czytać** (to read):

| | form |
|---|---|
| I | **czytōm** |
| you | **czytosz** |
| he / she | **czyto** |

- **Jo czytōm ksiōnżka.** — I am reading a book.
- **Czytosz ty romany?** — Do you read novels?
- **Ôna siedzi a czyto.** — She is sitting and reading.

For "we" and "they" you'll see **‑my** and **‑ōm**:

- **My ôglōndōmy film.** — We are watching a film.
- **Ôni suchajōm muzyka.** — They are listening to music.

## Some verbs use ‑a for "I"

- **Jo maluja ôbraz.** — I am painting a picture.
- **Jo szyja ôblyczynia.** — I am sewing clothes.

## One form, two meanings

Silesian has no separate "-ing" form. **Ôn pisze dziynnik.** can mean "He is writing a diary" or "He writes a diary".

> **Watch out:** "to learn" needs **sie**: **Uczymy sie sam.** — We are learning here.
