In this lesson: **revision**: how nouns change their endings in different sentences.

## The basic form

The dictionary form is used for the one doing the action:

- **Ôna je we mieście.** — She is in the city.

## "There is no..."

After **niy mo** (there is no), the noun changes:

- **Brata sam niy mo.** — There is no brother here.
- **Kości sam niy mo.** — There is no bone here.
- **Już niy mo bananōw we dōmu.** — There are no more bananas in the house.

## "To" and "with"

- "to someone": **Dōm babie.** — I give to the woman.
- "with": **Godōmy ze kamratami.** — We talk with friends.
- "with a tool": **To idzie rzōńzać nōżym.** — This can be cut with a knife.

## "About"

After **ô** (about) the noun takes the "where" ending:

- **Ôna godo ô bratach.** — She talks about brothers.

> **Watch out:** the word order is free. **Ôna z chopym godo.** and "Ôna godo z chopym" say the same thing.
