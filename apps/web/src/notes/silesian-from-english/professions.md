In this lesson: **jobs**, and how to say what someone **is**.

## Jobs

- **rechtōr** — teacher
- **dŏchtōr** — doctor
- **inżyniyr** — engineer
- **kucharz** — cook
- **szofer** — driver
- **sprzedŏwca** — seller

## Saying someone's job

After **je** (is), the job takes the ending **‑ym**:

- **Mój brat je rechtōrym.** — My brother is a teacher.
- **Ôna je dŏchtōrym.** — She is a doctor.
- **Mój ôjc je kucharzym.** — My father is a cook.

## What they do

- **Kucharz warzi wieczerza.** — The cook is preparing dinner.
- **Dŏchtōr pōmogo ludziōm.** — The doctor helps people.
- **Sprzedŏwca roboto we sklepie.** — The seller works in the shop.

> **Watch out:** **robić** and **robotować** both mean "to work". **robić** can also mean "to do / make": **Inżyniyr robi plan.** — The engineer makes a plan.
