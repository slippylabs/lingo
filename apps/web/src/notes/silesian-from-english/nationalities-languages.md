In this lesson: **nationalities** and how to say someone **speaks a language**.

## Nationalities

- **Polŏk** — Pole
- **Anglik** — Englishman
- **Niymiec** — German
- **Kroatka** — Croat (a woman)

After **je** (is) the word takes the ending **‑ym** for a man and **‑ōm** for a woman:

- **Jo żech je Polŏkym.** — I am a Pole.
- **Ôn je Rusym.** — He is a Russian.
- **Ôna je Kroatkōm.** — She is a Croat.

## Speaking a language

Use **godo po** with a word ending in **‑u**:

- **Anglik godo po angielsku.** — An Englishman speaks the English language.
- **Kroat godo po chorwacku.** — A Croat speaks the Croatian language.
- **Godosz po niymiecku?** — Do you speak the German language?

## Country and language

- **gŏdka** — language
- **kraj** — country
- **Kraj je Polska.** — The country is Poland.

> **Watch out:** nationalities start with a capital letter (**Anglik**), but "in English" does not (**po angielsku**).
