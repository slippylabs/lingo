In this lesson: **"to be"**, the words for **I, you, he, she**, and **this / that**.

## Silesian letters

Silesian has a few letters English doesn't. Two you'll see all the time:

- **ô** sounds like "wo": **ôn** — he
- *ō* sounds between "o" and "u": **dōm** — house

## "To be"

The verb changes with the person. In this course "I am" and "you are" carry a small extra word, **żech** or **żeś**:

| | form |
|---|---|
| I am | **jo żech je** |
| you are | **ty żeś je** |
| he / she / it is | **ôn je** / **ôna je** / ôno je |
| we are | **my żechmy sōm** |
| you (all) are | **wy żeście sōm** |
| they are | **ôni sōm** |

- **Jo żech je mondry.** — I am wise.
- **Ôn je dobry kamrat** — He is a good friend
- **To sōm kamraty.** — These are friends.

## This and that

The word for "this" changes with the gender of the word after it. Every Silesian noun is masculine, feminine or neuter (a grammar label, not a meaning):

- **tyn** (masculine): **Tyn chop je miły.** — This man is kind.
- **ta** (feminine): **Ta baba je dobro.** — This woman is good.
- **to** (neuter): **To je przijymne miasto.** — This is a pleasant city.

Add *tam‑* for "over there": **Tamtyn dōm je piykny.** — That house over there is beautiful.

## Describing words match too

A describing word (adjective) changes its ending to match the noun: **‑y** for masculine, **‑o** for feminine, **‑e** for neuter.

- **Tyn dziyń je zimny.** — This day is cold.
- **Ta baba je wysoko.** — This woman is tall.
- **Tamto rano je zimne.** — That morning over there is cold.

> **Watch out:** **To** also means "this is" on its own: **To je moja siostra.** — This is my sister. Put **niy** after it to say "not": **To niy moja siostra.**
