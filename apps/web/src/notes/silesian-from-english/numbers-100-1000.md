In this lesson: **hundreds and thousands**.

## The hundreds

- **sto** — one hundred
- **dwieście** — two hundred
- **trzysta** — three hundred
- **czterysta** — four hundred
- **piyńćset** — five hundred
- **tysiōnc** — thousand

From 500, the hundreds end in **‑set**: **sześćset**, **siedymset**, **ôziymset**, **dziewiyńćset**.

## Examples

- **dwieście bab** — two hundred women
- **trzysta pōl** — three hundred fields
- **czterysta siedym dolarōw** — four hundred seven dollars
- **dwa tysiōnce dziewiyńćset psōw** — two thousand nine hundred dogs

## The last number decides

- **dwieście trzi kōmputry** — two hundred three computers (ends in 3)
- **sześćset kōtōw** — six hundred cats

> **Watch out:** two thousand is **dwa tysiōnce**, not "dwa tysiōnc".
