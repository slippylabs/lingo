In this lesson: **greetings**, **thank you**, and short **everyday replies**.

## Hello and goodbye

- **Dobre rano!** — Good morning!
- **Dobry dziyń!** — Good day!
- **Dobry wieczōr!** — Good evening!
- **Witejcie!** — Welcome!
- **Z Bogiem!** — Go with God!

## How are you?

- **Jak żeś je?** — How are you?
- **Żech je dobrze, dziynkuja!** — I am well, thank you!
- **A ty?** — And you?
- **Dziynkuja ci barzo.** — Thank you very much.

## Names

- **Jak sie mianujesz?** — What is your name?
- **Jo sie mianuja Bartek.** — My name is Bartek.

## Short replies

- **Tak je.** — That is so.
- **Ja, idzie.** — Yes, it is possible.
- **Niy, niy idzie.** — No, it is not possible.
- **Niy wia.** — I don't know.
- **Szkoda!** — What a pity!

> **Watch out:** **ja** means "yes" here, not "I". "I" is **jo**: **Jo sie mianuja Bartek.**
