In this lesson: **fruit** and **colours and tastes** to describe it.

## Fruit

- **jabłko** — apple
- **gruszka** — pear
- **banan** — banana
- **wiśnia** — cherry
- **śliwka** — plum
- **citrōna** — lemon

## Describing fruit

The describing word matches the fruit: **‑y** for masculine words, **‑o** or **‑a** for feminine, **‑e** for neuter.

- **Banan je żōłty.** — The banana is yellow.
- **Gruszka je smakowito.** — The pear is tasty.
- **Jabłko je czyrwone.** — The apple is red.

## More than one

- **Apelsiny som słodke.** — The oranges are sweet.
- **Gruszki leżōm na stole.** — The pears are lying on the table.

Use **som** (are) for more than one, **je** (is) for one.

> **Watch out:** **a** means "and": **Melōn je wielgi a słodki.** — The melon is big and sweet.
