In this lesson: **food words** and the **thing you eat, buy or see**.

## Food

- **chlyb** — bread
- **miyso** — meat
- **mlyko** — milk
- **masło** — butter
- **jajco** — egg

## Meals

- **śniodanie** — breakfast
- **ôbiad** — lunch
- **wieczerza** — dinner

## To eat

- **Jo jym chlyb.** — I am eating bread.
- **Ty jes jajco?** — Are you eating an egg?
- **Ôna je miyso.** — She is eating meat.
- **My jymy wieczerza.** — We are eating dinner.

## The thing after the verb

The thing that receives the action is called the object. In this lesson the object nouns keep their dictionary form:

- **Ôn lubi mlyko.** — He likes milk.
- **Ôni kupujōm masło i chlyb.** — They are buying butter and bread.
- **Widza ôbiad na stole.** — I see lunch on the table.

> **Watch out:** **je** means both "is" and "eats". **Ôna je miyso.** is "She is eating meat", not "She is meat"!
