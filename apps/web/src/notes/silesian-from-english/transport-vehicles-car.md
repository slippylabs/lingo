In this lesson: **cars, buses and trains**, and how to say they **go** somewhere.

## Getting around

- **auto** — car
- **autobus** — bus
- **cug** — train
- **droga** — road
- **szofer** — driver

## Going by vehicle

Vehicles use **jedzie** (goes, rides), not **idzie** (goes on foot):

- **Autobus jedzie do cyntrum.** — The bus goes to the center.
- **Cug jedzie na banhof.** — The train goes to the station.
- **Szofer jedzie wartko.** — The driver drives fast.

## Describing

- **Auto je nowe a piykne.** — The car is new and beautiful.
- **Szofer je ôstrożny.** — The driver is careful.
- **Droga je szyroko.** — The road is wide.

> **Watch out:** a road that "goes" somewhere uses **idzie**, not **jedzie**: **Droga idzie bez las.** — The road goes through the forest.
