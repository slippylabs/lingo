In this lesson: **shapes** and **lines**.

## Shapes

- **koło** — circle
- **kwadrat** — square
- **trziôkąt** — triangle
- **linijo** — line
- **pōnkt** — point

## Has and is

- **Kwadrat mo sztyry strōny.** — A square has four sides.
- **Trziôkąt mo trzi strōny.** — A triangle has three sides.
- **To je prosty trziôkąt.** — This is a simple triangle.

## Describing a shape

The describing word matches the noun:

- **To je prosty kształt.** — This is a simple shape.
- **Ta linijo je dugo.** — This line is long.
- **Forma je ciekawo.** — The shape is interesting.

> **Watch out:** **mo** means "has": **Stōł mo kształt kwadrata.** — The table has the shape of a square.
