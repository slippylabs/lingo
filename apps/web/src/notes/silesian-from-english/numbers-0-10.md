In this lesson: **numbers 0 to 10**, and how the noun changes after a number.

## One

"One" matches the noun's gender:

- **jedyn synek** — one boy
- **jedna baba** — one woman
- **jedno pōle** — one field

## Two to four

After 2, 3 and 4 the noun is in the ordinary plural:

- **dwa synki** — two boys
- **dwie baby** — two women
- **trzi kōty** — three cats
- **sztyry dolary** — four dollars

"Two" is **dwa** for masculine words and **dwie** for feminine ones.

## Five and up

From 5, the noun takes the "of" form, often ending in **‑ōw**:

- **piyńć synkōw** — five boys
- **piyńć kōtōw** — five cats
- **dziesiyńć zegarkōw** — ten clocks

> **Watch out:** compare **trzi kōty** (three cats) with **piyńć kōtōw** (five cats): the ending changes at 5.
