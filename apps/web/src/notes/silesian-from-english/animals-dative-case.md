In this lesson: **animals**, and how to say you **give** something **to** someone.

## Animals

- **kōt** — cat
- **pies** — dog
- **kōń** — horse
- **ptōk** — bird
- **mysz** — mouse
- **żaba** — frog

## "To" the animal

The one who receives something takes a new ending. This is the dative case: the "to" or "for" form of a word.

- masculine words add **‑u** or **‑owi**: **Jo dōm kotu ryba.** — I give the cat a fish.
- **Dowōmy kōniowi woda.** — We give the horse water.
- feminine words in **‑a** switch to **‑ie**: **My dowōmy żabie chlyb.** — We give bread to the frog.
- more than one takes **‑ōm**: **My pōmogōmy ptōkōm.** — We help the birds.

## Helping

"Help" takes the same "to" form:

- **Ôn pōmogo ptōkowi.** — He helps the bird.
- **Pōmogōmy wilkowi.** — We help the wolf.

> **Watch out:** **pies** (dog) changes a lot: **Ôna dŏ psu dobre miano.** — She gives the dog a good name.
