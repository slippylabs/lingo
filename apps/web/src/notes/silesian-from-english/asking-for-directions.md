In this lesson: **asking the way**, and **left, right, straight**.

## Questions

- **Kaj je banhof?** — Where is the station?
- **Kandy idzie droga?** — Which way does the road go?
- **Możecie mi pokozać droga?** — Can you show me the way?
- **Kaj je bliski hotel?** — Where is the nearest hotel?

## Directions

- **Idymy na prowo.** — We are going to the right.
- **Potym na lewo.** — Then to the left.
- **Idź prosto do mosta.** — Go straight to the bridge.

## Near and far

- **Biblijoteka je daleko.** — The library is far away.
- **Sklep je blisko biblijoteki.** — The shop is near the library.
- **Rynek je daleko ôd hotela.** — The square is far from the hotel.

> **Watch out:** **kaj** asks "where?", **kandy** asks "which way?"
