In this lesson: **time words** and how to talk about the **past**.

## Time

- **godzina** — hour
- **minuta** — minute
- **sekunda** — second
- **wcora** — yesterday
- **jutro** — tomorrow
- **Wiela je czasu?** — What time is it?

## "Was"

The past of "to be" changes with the person's gender:

- **bōł** (he): **Wcora bōł pōniydziałek.** — Yesterday was Monday.
- **bōła** (she): **Wcora ôna bōła we dōmu ô ôsmyj godzinie.** — Yesterday she was at home at the eighth hour.
- **bōło** (it): **Ô trzecij godzinie dwadzieścia bōło zimno.** — At twenty past the third hour it was cold.

## Other verbs in the past

Most verbs work the same way, ending in **‑ł** (he) or **‑li** (they):

- **Ôn szedł do dōmu.** — He went home.
- **Ôni nic niy robili.** — They did nothing.

For "I" and "you" the course adds **żech** and **żeś**:

- **Jo żech bōł we dōmu rano.** — I was at home in the morning.

> **Watch out:** **pōłnoc** here means "midnight", not "north": **Ôn śpi do pōłnocy.** — He sleeps until midnight.
