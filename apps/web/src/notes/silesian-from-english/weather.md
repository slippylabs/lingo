In this lesson: **weather**: rain, snow, sun and wind.

## Weather words

- **wjedro** — weather
- **dyszcz** — rain
- **śniyg** — snow
- **słōńce** — sun
- **wiater** — wind
- **chmura** — cloud

## It is raining

Silesian says "falls rain":

- **Pado dyszcz.** — It is raining.
- **Pado śniyg a dyszcz.** — Snow and rain are falling.

## Warm and cold

For "it is warm / cold" there is no "it":

- **Dzisiŏj je ciepło.** — It is warm today.
- **We Rusyji je zimno.** — In Russia it is cold.
- **Ôbłōczno je dzisiŏj.** — It is cloudy today.

## The sun and wind

- **Słōńce świyci.** — The sun is shining.
- **Wiater dmie mocno.** — The wind is blowing strongly.

> **Watch out:** **luft** means "air", not "loft": **Luft je czysty.** — The air is clean.
