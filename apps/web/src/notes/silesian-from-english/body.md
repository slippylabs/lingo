In this lesson: **parts of the body**, and how to say something **hurts**.

## The body

- **gowa** — head
- **rōńczka** — hand
- **noga** — leg
- **ucho** — ear
- **nos** — nose
- **siyrce** — heart

## It hurts

Use **boli** (hurts) with the body part:

- **Moja rōńczka boli.** — My hand hurts.
- **Żōłōndek mie boli.** — My stomach hurts.

## Two of them

- **Jo mōm dwie rōńczki a dwie nogi i tyż dwie stopy.** — I have two hands and two legs, and also two feet.

## Describing

- **Ôn mo modre piykne ôczy.** — He has beautiful blue eyes.
- **Ôna mo dugo noga.** — She has a long leg.

> **Watch out:** "eye" is **ôko**, but "eyes" is **ôczy**.
