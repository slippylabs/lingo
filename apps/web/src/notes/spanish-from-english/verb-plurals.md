## We, you all, they

| | comer | beber | ser |
|---|---|---|---|
| we — **nosotros / nosotras** | **comemos** | **bebemos** | **somos** |
| you all — **vosotros / vosotras** | **coméis** | **bebéis** | **sois** |
| they — **ellos / ellas** | **comen** | **beben** | **son** |

- **Nosotras comemos plátanos** — We eat bananas
- **Vosotras bebéis agua** — You (all) drink water
- **Ellas beben cerveza** — They drink beer
- **Ellos son perros** — They are dogs

## The -as / -os forms

**Nosotras, vosotras, ellas** are for groups of women only. **Nosotros, vosotros, ellos** are for men or mixed groups.

**vosotros** ("you all") is used in Spain. In Latin America people say *ustedes* instead.

**Common mistake:** forgetting the accent: **coméis**, **bebéis**.
