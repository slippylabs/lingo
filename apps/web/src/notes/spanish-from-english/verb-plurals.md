In this lesson: **we, you all, they**.

## The words for "we", "you all" and "they"

- **nosotros** — we
- **vosotros** — you all
- **ellos** — they

If the group is **only women**, the words end in **‑as** instead: **nosotras, vosotras, ellas**.

## The verb endings

| | eat | drink | be |
|---|---|---|---|
| we | **comemos** | **bebemos** | **somos** |
| you all | **coméis** | **bebéis** | **sois** |
| they | **comen** | **beben** | **son** |

- **Nosotras comemos plátanos** — We eat bananas
- **Vosotras bebéis agua** — You (all) drink water
- **Ellas beben cerveza** — They drink beer
- **Ellos son perros** — They are dogs

> **Watch out:** **coméis** and **bebéis** need the accent on the *é*. (Also, *vosotros* is only used in Spain. In Latin America people say *ustedes* instead.)
