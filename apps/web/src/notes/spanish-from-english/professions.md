In this lesson: **jobs**, and more **verbs**.

## Jobs for a man or a woman

Most job words end in **‑o** for a man and **‑a** for a woman:

- **médico** / **médica** — doctor
- **bombero** / **bombera** — firefighter
- **granjero** / **granjera** — farmer

Examples:

- **La médica bebe agua** — The doctor (a woman) drinks water
- **El granjero ve los animales** — The farmer sees the animals

## More verbs

- **El camarero trabaja** — The waiter works
- **El cantante canta bien** — The singer sings well
- **Las bomberas llegan** — The firefighters arrive

For one person, these verbs end in **‑a**. For more than one, they end in **‑an**.

> **Watch out:** you don't say "a" before a job: **Ella es enfermera** means "She is a nurse", with no *una*.
