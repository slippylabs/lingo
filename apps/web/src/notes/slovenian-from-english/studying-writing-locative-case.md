In this lesson: **school** words and the *locative case*, the form for **where** something is.

## School

- **šola** — school
- **učitelj** — teacher (a man)
- **učiteljica** — teacher (a woman)
- **učenec** — student
- **knjiga** — book
- **naloga** — assignment
- **zvezek** — notebook
- **pero** — pen

## Where: the locative case

After **v** (in) and **na** (on), when you say where something is, the word takes the *locative case*. Feminine words ending in **‑a** change it to **‑i**; masculine and neuter words add **‑u**:

- **miza** — table → **Knjiga je na mizi.** — The book is on the table.
- **Šola je v centru.** — The school is in the center.

## "With a pen"

"With" (**s** / **z**) uses another form, the instrumental case. **pero** becomes **peresom**:

- **Jaz hitro pišem s tem peresom.** — I am writing quickly with this pen.

## Writing

- **Mi pišemo nalogo.** — We are writing the assignment.
- **Ona ima veliko nalog.** — She has many assignments.

> **Watch out:** jobs have a separate word for a woman: **On je moj učitelj.** but **Ona je učiteljica.**
