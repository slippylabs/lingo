In this lesson: the **weather**.

## Weather words

- **vreme** — weather
- **dež** — rain
- **sneg** — snow
- **sonce** — sun
- **veter** — wind
- **oblak** — cloud
- **mraz** — frost
- **zrak** — air

## "It is warm"

For "it is …" Slovenian uses **je** with a word ending in **‑o**, and no word for "it":

- **Danes je toplo.** — It is warm today.
- **V Rusiji je hladno.** — In Russia it is cold.
- **Danes je oblačno.** — It is cloudy today.

## Rain and snow "fall"

- **Pada dež.** — It is raining.
- **Pada sneg in dež.** — Snow and rain are falling.
- **Sneg leži na zemlji.** — Snow lies on the ground.

## The sun and wind

- **Sonce sveti.** — The sun is shining.
- **Veter močno piha.** — The wind is blowing strongly.
- **Hladen veter piha s severa.** — A cold wind is blowing from the north.

> **Watch out:** there is no "it" in **Pada dež.** The verb comes first and the rain is the subject: literally "falls rain".
