In this lesson: the **points of the compass**, **left, right** and **straight on**.

## North, south, east, west

- **sever** — north
- **jug** — south
- **vzhod** — east
- **zahod** — west

Combined directions are one word:

- **Gremo proti jugozahodu.** — We are going to the southwest.
- **Jugovzhod je tam, kjer greje sonce.** — The southeast is where the sun warms.

## "In the north"

Use **na** and add **‑u**:

- **Na severu je sneg.** — In the north there is snow.
- **Živimo na jugu.** — We live in the south.
- **Sonce vzhaja na vzhodu.** — The sun rises in the east.
- **Ono zahaja na zahodu.** — It sets in the west.

## Describing words

- **To je severna stran.** — This is the northern side.
- **Južni kraji so topli.** — The southern regions are warm.
- **Zahodni veter piha.** — A western wind is blowing.

## Left, right, straight on

- **Gremo zdaj desno in potem naravnost.** — We are going right now and then straight.
- **Zahod je levo na zemljevidu.** — West is left on the map.

> **Watch out:** the compass words take **na**, not **v**: **na severu** — in the north.
