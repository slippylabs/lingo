In this lesson: **drinks** and the *genitive case*, the form after "not", "without" and amounts.

## The genitive case

Words take the *genitive case* in three common places: after "not have" or "not drink", after **brez** (without), and after an amount like "a bottle of".

Feminine words ending in **‑a** change it to **‑e**:

- **Jaz nimam vode.** — I do not have water.
- **On nima kave.** — He does not have coffee.

Masculine and neuter words end in **‑a**:

- **Ona ne pije vina.** — She does not drink wine.
- **Ona ne pije čaja.** — She does not drink tea.
- **Mi ne pijemo piva.** — We do not drink beer.

## After "without" and amounts

- **Piješ brez mleka?** — Do you drink without milk?
- **Jaz ljubim sok brez sladkorja.** — I like juice without sugar.
- **Ti imaš steklenico soka?** — Do you have a bottle of juice?
- **Kozarec vina zame, prosim.** — A glass of wine for me, please.

## "Not have"

"Not have" is one word: **nimam** (I don't have), **nima** (he / she doesn't have).

- **Pri Franku ni vodke.** — Frank has no vodka.

> **Watch out:** with "yes" the drink keeps its basic form, with "no" it changes: **Ti piješ vino?** but **Ona ne pije vina.**
