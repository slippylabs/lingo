In this lesson: **animals** and the *dative case*, the form for **to whom** you give or help.

## Animals

- **mačka** — cat
- **pes** — dog
- **konj** — horse
- **ptica** — bird
- **riba** — fish
- **miš** — mouse
- **medved** — bear
- **volk** — wolf

## The dative case

The one who receives something takes the *dative case*. Masculine words add **‑u**:

- **pes** → **Ona da psu dobro ime.** — She gives the dog a good name.
- **Dajemo konju vodo.** — We give the horse water.
- **On pomaga medvedu.** — He helps the bear.

Feminine words end in **‑i**:

- **Jaz dam mački ribo.** — I give the cat a fish.
- **Mi dajemo žabi kruh.** — We give bread to the frog.
- **On pomaga ptici.** — He helps the bird.

For more than one, add **‑am** to feminine words: **Mi pomagamo pticam.** — We help the birds.

## Giving

**dati** (to give): **dam** — I give, **da** — he / she gives, **dajemo** — we give.

> **Watch out:** **pomagati** (to help) takes the dative too: you help **to** someone, so it is **On pomaga medvedu.**, not *medveda*.
