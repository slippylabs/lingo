In this lesson: the **body** and saying something **hurts**.

## Parts of the body

- **glava** — head
- **roka** — hand
- **noga** — leg
- **stopalo** — foot
- **oko** — eye
- **uho** — ear
- **nos** — nose
- **srce** — heart
- **želodec** — stomach

## "It hurts"

**boli** — hurts. The part that hurts is the subject:

- **Moja roka boli.** — My hand hurts.
- **Želodec me boli.** — My stomach hurts. (literally "the stomach hurts me")

## Pairs: the dual

For exactly two things Slovenian uses a special ending, the *dual*:

- **Jaz imam dve roki in dve nogi ter tudi dve stopali.** — I have two hands and two legs, and also two feet.

## Irregular words

Two words change a lot: **oko** (eye) becomes **oči** (eyes), and **uho** (ear) becomes **ušesom** after "with":

- **On ima modre lepe oči.** — He has beautiful blue eyes.
- **Ona te sliši samo z enim ušesom.** — She hears you only with one ear.

> **Watch out:** **možgani** (brain) is plural in Slovenian: **Njegovi možgani so hitri.** — His brain is quick.
