In this lesson: **places in the city** and the difference between **going to** and **being at** a place.

## In the city

- **mesto** — city
- **ulica** — street
- **trg** — square
- **most** — bridge
- **postaja** — station
- **trgovina** — shop
- **tržnica** — market
- **knjižnica** — library
- **cerkev** — church

## Going to vs. being at

With **na** and **v**, the ending tells you whether you are going there or already there:

- **Gremo na trg.** — We are going to the square.
- **Na trgu stoji fontana.** — A fountain stands in the square.

Being in a shop or library (feminine words) ends in **‑i**:

- **Kupujemo kruh v trgovini.** — We buy bread in the shop.
- **Berem knjigo v knjižnici.** — I read a book in the library.
- **Na tržnici je veliko ljudi.** — There are many people at the market.

## "Near"

After **blizu** (near) the place takes the genitive case:

- **Postaja je blizu centra.** — The station is near the center.
- **Cerkev stoji blizu knjižnice.** — The church stands near the library.

> **Watch out:** **trg** (square) and **tržnica** (market) are different places, and so are **trgovina** (shop) and **tržnica**.
