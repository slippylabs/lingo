In this lesson: the numbers **11 to 20**.

## The numbers

- **enajst** — 11
- **dvanajst** — 12
- **trinajst** — 13
- **štirinajst** — 14
- **petnajst** — 15
- **šestnajst** — 16
- **sedemnajst** — 17
- **osemnajst** — 18
- **devetnajst** — 19
- **dvajset** — 20

The ending **‑najst** works like English "‑teen".

## The noun after the number

All of these numbers take the noun's "of" form (the genitive plural), just like five to ten:

- **enajst fantov** — eleven boys
- **dvanajst žensk** — twelve women
- **trinajst mačk** — thirteen cats
- **štirinajst koles** — fourteen bicycles
- **devetnajst računalnikov** — nineteen computers

> **Watch out:** feminine nouns often lose their ending in this form: **ženska** becomes **dvanajst žensk**, **mačka** becomes **trinajst mačk**.
