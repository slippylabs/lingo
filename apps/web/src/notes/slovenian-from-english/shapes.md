In this lesson: **shapes** and saying **two** of something.

## Shapes

- **krog** — circle
- **kvadrat** — square
- **trikotnik** — triangle
- **linija** — line
- **točka** — point
- **oblika** — shape
- **obris** — outline

## Simple sentences

- **To je krog.** — This is a circle.
- **Ta linija je dolga.** — This line is long.
- **Točka leži na liniji.** — The point lies on the line.
- **Kvadrat ima štiri strani.** — A square has four sides.
- **Trikotnik ima tri strani.** — A triangle has three sides.

## Exactly two: the dual

Slovenian has a special form for exactly two things, called the *dual*. With two, "are" is **sta**, not **so**:

- **Kvadrat in krog sta osnovni obliki.** — Square and circle are basic shapes.

## "Of a square"

"Of" is shown by changing the ending (the genitive case). Masculine words add **‑a**:

- **Vidimo obris kvadrata.** — We see the outline of a square.
- **Miza ima obliko kvadrata.** — The table has the shape of a square.

> **Watch out:** "two" things are not "more than one" in Slovenian grammar: two shapes are **osnovni obliki**, with the dual ending, not the plural.
