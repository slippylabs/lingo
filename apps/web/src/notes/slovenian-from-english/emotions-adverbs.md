In this lesson: **feelings**, and turning a describing word into a word for **how** something is done.

## Feelings

- **ljubezen** — love
- **strah** — fear
- **sram** — shame
- **mir** — peace
- **zavist** — envy
- **radost** — joy

- **Ljubezen je močno čustvo.** — Love is a strong emotion.
- **Strah je naravna reakcija.** — Fear is a natural reaction.

## How: ‑o

To say **how** someone does something, use the describing word with the ending **‑o** (like English "‑ly"):

- **Danes se počutim radostno.** — I feel joyful today.
- **Žalostno je govoril.** — He spoke sadly.
- **On mu jezno naroči, naj dela.** — He angrily tells him to work.
- **On ponosno skriva svojo jezo.** — He hides his anger proudly.
- **Prestrašeno je zbežal.** — He was frightened and ran away.

## Feeling

**čutiti** — to feel:

- **On čuti sram.** — He feels shame.
- **Mi čutimo mir.** — We feel peaceful.

> **Watch out:** "I feel happy" uses the **‑o** form, not the describing word: **Danes se počutim radostno.**
