In this lesson: **shopping** and saying **a piece of** something.

## In the shop

- **cena** — price
- **torba** — bag
- **kos** — piece
- **račun** — receipt
- **kupec** — customer
- **prodajalec** — seller

## Buying and selling

- **Hočem kupiti kruh.** — I want to buy bread.
- **Ona kupuje knjigo.** — She is buying a book.
- **On prodaja meso.** — He sells meat.

## "A piece of", "the price of"

The thing after **kos** or **cena** takes the genitive case ("of"):

- **Kos sira je dober.** — A piece of cheese is good.
- **Kos kruha je majhen.** — The piece of bread is small.
- **Cena masla je dobra.** — The price of butter is good.
- **Prodajalec da kos klobase.** — The seller gives a piece of sausage.

## Asking

- **Kupec sprašuje za popust.** — The customer asks for a discount.
- **Kupec sprašuje o ceni.** — The customer asks about the price.

> **Watch out:** "There are no returns" uses the genitive: **Vračila ni.**, not *vračilo ni*.
