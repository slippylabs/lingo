In this lesson: **rooms and furniture**, and words for **where** things are.

## Furniture

- **soba** — room
- **miza** — table
- **stol** — chair
- **postelja** — bed
- **okno** — window
- **vrata** — door
- **omara** — wardrobe
- **polica** — shelf
- **svetilka** — lamp

## Where things are

Each small word for a place asks for its own ending on the noun:

- **Na mizi leži knjiga.** — A book is lying on the table.
- **Polica visi na steni.** — The shelf hangs on the wall.
- **Skrinja je pod posteljo.** — The chest is under the bed.
- **Svetilka sveti nad mizo.** — The lamp shines above the table.
- **Stol je blizu mize.** — The chair is near the table.
- **Miza stoji v sredini sobe.** — The table stands in the middle of the room.

## Describing the room

- **Soba je topla in tiha.** — The room is warm and quiet.
- **Okno je odprto.** — The window is open.
- **Stol je star, a udoben.** — The chair is old, but comfortable.

> **Watch out:** **vrata** (door) is always plural, even for one door, so it takes **so**: **Vrata so zaprta.**
