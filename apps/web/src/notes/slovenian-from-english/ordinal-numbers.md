In this lesson: **first, second, third** and telling **the time**.

## First to tenth

**prvi** first · **drugi** second · **tretji** third · **četrti** fourth · **peti** fifth · **šesti** sixth · **sedmi** seventh · **osmi** eighth · **deveti** ninth

These are describing words, so they match the noun:

- **trideseti moški** — thirtieth man
- **štiristo peta gora** — four hundred fifth mountain
- **šeststoto kolo** — six hundredth bicycle

## Big ordinals

Only the last part becomes "‑th":

- **dvesto prvi brat** — two hundred first brother
- **dvesto tretji računalnik** — two hundred third computer
- **osemsto drugi dolar** — eight hundred second dollar

## Telling the time

"At … o'clock" is **ob**, the ordinal, and **uri** (hour). The minutes come after:

- **ob peti uri** — at the fifth hour
- **ob deseti uri** — at the tenth hour
- **ob drugi uri petnajst** — at fifteen past the second hour

> **Watch out:** **ura** (hour) is feminine, so after **ob** the ordinal ends in **‑i**: **ob peti uri**.
