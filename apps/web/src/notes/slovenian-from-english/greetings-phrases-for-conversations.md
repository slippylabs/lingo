In this lesson: **greetings** and short **phrases for a conversation**.

## Greetings

- **Dobro jutro!** — Good morning!
- **Dober dan!** — Good day!
- **Dober večer!** — Good evening!
- **Lahko noč!** — Good night!
- **Zdravo!** — Hello!
- **Z Bogom!** — Goodbye!

## How are you?

- **Kako si?** — How are you?
- **Dobro sem, hvala!** — I am well, thank you!
- **In ti?** — And you?
- **Kako ti je ime?** — What is your name?
- **Moje ime je Bartek.** — My name is Bartek.

## Short answers

- **Tako je.** — That is so.
- **Mogoče.** — Maybe.
- **Ne vem.** — I don't know.
- **Tudi jaz.** — Me too.
- **Pardon, ne razumem.** — Excuse me, I don't understand.

"Not" is **ne** before a verb. With "is" it joins into one word, **ni**: **Ni možno.** — Not possible.

> **Watch out:** **Lahko noč!** is what you say when someone goes to bed. **Dober večer!** is a hello in the evening, not a goodbye.
