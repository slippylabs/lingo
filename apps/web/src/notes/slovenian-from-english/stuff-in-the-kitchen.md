In this lesson: **things in the kitchen** and saying **full of** something.

## In the kitchen

- **nož** — knife
- **vilica** — fork
- **žlica** — spoon
- **krožnik** — plate
- **skodelica** — cup
- **lonec** — pot
- **ponev** — frying pan
- **skleda** — bowl

## Where things are

- **Nož leži na mizi.** — The knife is lying on the table.
- **Mi kuhamo v loncu.** — We cook in a pot.
- **Na ponvi je meso.** — There is meat in the frying pan.
- **Krožnik leži na tleh.** — The plate is lying on the floor.

## "Full of"

After **poln** (full) the thing inside takes the genitive case:

- **Krožnik je poln jedi.** — The plate is full of food.
- **Žlica je polna popra.** — The spoon is full of pepper.

"Filled with" uses **z** and the instrumental case: **Skodelica je napolnjena z vodo.** — The cup is filled with water.

## Describing

- **Nož je oster.** — The knife is sharp.
- **Lonec je vroč.** — The pot is hot
- **Kuhinja je čista.** — The kitchen is clean.

> **Watch out:** "full" matches the noun: **Krožnik je poln** (masculine) but **Žlica je polna** (feminine).
