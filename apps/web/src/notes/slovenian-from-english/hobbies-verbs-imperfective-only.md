In this lesson: **hobbies** and the **present tense** of verbs.

## Verb endings show the person

Most verbs follow this pattern. Here is **brati** (to read):

| | |
|---|---|
| jaz **berem** — I read | mi *beremo* — we read |
| ti **bereš** — you read | vi *berete* — you (all) read |
| on / ona **bere** — he / she reads | oni *berejo* — they read |

The endings are **‑m, ‑š**, nothing, **‑mo, ‑te, ‑jo**. You will see them on every verb:

- **Jaz slikam sliko.** — I am painting a picture.
- **Ti gledaš serijo?** — Are you watching a series?
- **On igra nogomet.** — He is playing football.
- **Mi igramo šah.** — We are playing chess.
- **Oni poslušajo glasbo.** — They are listening to music.

## One form for "I read" and "I am reading"

Slovenian has no separate "‑ing" form. **Jaz berem knjigo.** means both "I read a book" and "I am reading a book".

## Verbs with "se"

Some verbs always come with **se**, like **učiti se** (to learn):

- **Vi se učite jezika.** — You are learning a language.
- **Učimo se tukaj.** — We are learning here.

> **Watch out:** the "they" ending is **‑jo**, not **‑mo**: **mi gledamo** (we watch) but **oni poslušajo** (they listen).
