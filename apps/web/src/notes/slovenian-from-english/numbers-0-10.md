In this lesson: the numbers **0 to 10**, and how the **noun changes** after a number.

## The numbers

**nič** 0 · **en** 1 · **dva** 2 · **tri** 3 · **štiri** 4 · **pet** 5 · **šest** 6 · **sedem** 7 · **osem** 8 · **devet** 9 · **deset** 10

## One and two match the noun

"One" has three forms, for masculine, feminine and neuter nouns:

- **en fant** — one boy
- **ena ženska** — one woman
- **eno polje** — one field

"Two" is **dva** for masculine nouns and **dve** for the others. The noun takes a special ending for exactly two, called the *dual*:

- **dva fanta** — two boys
- **dve ženski** — two women
- **dve stvari** — two things

## Three and four

After **tri** and **štiri** the noun is in its normal plural:

- **tri mačke** — three cats
- **štiri kolesa** — four bicycles

With masculine nouns, three and four are **trije** and **štirje**:

- **štirje dolarji** — four dollars

## Five and up

From five on, the noun takes its "of" form (the genitive plural), as if you said "five of boys":

- **pet fantov** — five boys
- **pet mačk** — five cats
- **šest koles** — six bicycles
- **osem dolarjev** — eight dollars

> **Watch out:** the noun changes after five: **tri mačke** but **pet mačk**.
