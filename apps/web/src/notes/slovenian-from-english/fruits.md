In this lesson: **fruit** and describing words that **match** their noun.

## Fruit

- **jabolko** — apple
- **hruška** — pear
- **banana** — banana
- **pomaranča** — orange
- **jagoda** — strawberry
- **sliva** — plum
- **limona** — lemon
- **grozdje** — grapes

## Describing words match

Every noun is masculine, feminine or neuter. A describing word after **je** ("is") takes a matching ending:

- **Hruška je okusna.** — The pear is tasty. (feminine: **‑a**)
- **Banana je rumena.** — The banana is yellow.
- **Jabolko je rdeče.** — The apple is red. (neuter: **‑o** or **‑e**)

With more than one, the ending changes again:

- **Pomaranče so sladke.** — The oranges are sweet.
- **Jagode so v skledi.** — The strawberries are in the bowl.

## Two describing words

Join them with **in** (and):

- **Limona je kisla in rumena.** — The lemon is sour and yellow.
- **Melona je velika in sladka.** — The melon is big and sweet.

> **Watch out:** **grozdje** (grapes) is one singular word for the whole bunch: **Grozdje raste v vinogradu.** — Grapes grow in a vineyard.
