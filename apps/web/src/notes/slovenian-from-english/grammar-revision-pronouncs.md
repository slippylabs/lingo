In this lesson: **pronouns** — words like **me, you, him, this** — in their different forms.

## Long and short forms

Personal pronouns have a long form and a short form. The long form is used for stress and after small words like **s** (with) or **o** (about):

- **Mene ne vidiš.** — You don't see me. (stress on "me")
- **O meni govoriš?** — Are you talking about me?
- **Govorim s teboj.** — I am talking with you.
- **Ne, jaz ne govorim o tebi.** — No, I don't talk about you.
- **Z nami je vedno vse veselo.** — With us everything is always cheerful.

The short form is the everyday one, placed early in the sentence:

- **Daj mi vodo.** — Give me water.

## Question words change too

- **Koga ti vidiš?** — Who do you see?
- **Komu boš dal darilo?** — Who will you give a gift to?
- **O čem ti govoriš?** — What are you talking about?

## "This" and "that"

- **Tega ne vem.** — I don't know that.
- **O tem že dolgo vem.** — I have known about that for a long time.
- **Te ženske so lepe.** — These women are beautiful.

## Oneself

**seboj** / **sebi** mean "oneself": **On je rad sam s seboj.** — He likes to be alone with himself.

> **Watch out:** **te** can be "these" (**Te ženske so lepe.**) or the short form of "you". The rest of the sentence tells you which.
