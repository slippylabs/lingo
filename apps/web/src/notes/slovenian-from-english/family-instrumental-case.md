In this lesson: **family** words and how to say **"with" someone**.

## The family

- **mati** — mother
- **oče** — father
- **brat** — brother
- **sestra** — sister
- **sin** — son
- **hči** — daughter
- **dedek** — grandfather
- **babica** — grandmother

## "With" changes the word

Slovenian words change their ending depending on their job in the sentence. These forms are called *cases*. After **z** or **s** ("with") a word takes the *instrumental case*.

Masculine words add **‑om** (or **‑em** after a soft sound like c, j):

- **On govori z dedkom.** — He is talking with his grandfather.
- **On živi s sinom.** — He lives with his son.
- **On živi s teto in s stricem.** — He lives with his aunt and uncle.

Feminine words ending in **‑a** change it to **‑o**:

- **Govorim s tvojo sestro.** — I am talking with your sister.
- **Grem v park z družino.** — I am going to the park with the family.

Two words are irregular: **mati** becomes **z materjo** and **hči** becomes **s hčerjo**.

- **Ona gre z materjo.** — She is going with her mother.
- **Gremo s hčerjo.** — We are going with our daughter.

For more than one person, add **‑i**: **Gremo na pohod z brati.** — We are going for a walk with brothers.

## Z or s?

It is the same word. Use **s** before the sounds c, č, f, h, k, p, s, š, t, and **z** before everything else: **s teto**, **s sinom** but **z dedkom**, **z materjo**.

> **Watch out:** "my brother" after **je** ("is") stays in its basic form: **On je moj brat.** The "with" form is only for "with": **On je z vnukom.** — He is with his grandson.
