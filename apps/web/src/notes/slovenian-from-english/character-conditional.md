In this lesson: words for **character** and the **conditional**, for saying what **would** happen.

## Character

- **dober** — good
- **slab** — bad
- **močan** — strong
- **šibak** — weak
- **moder** — wise
- **neumen** — stupid
- **iskren** — sincere
- **len** — lazy

- **Iskren prijatelj je dragocen.** — A sincere friend is valuable.
- **On je šibak v športu.** — He is weak in sports.

## "Would": bi

The conditional is **bi** plus the verb's past form (**‑l, ‑la, ‑li**). **bi** is the same for every person:

- **Če vi ne bi bili šibki, potem bi delali.** — If you were not weak, you would work.
- **Če oni ne bi bili leni, potem bi jim pomagali.** — If they were not lazy, we would help them.
- **Če ti ne bi bil neumen, potem ne bi delal neumnosti.** — If you were not stupid, you would not do foolish things.

**če** means "if", and **potem** ("then") starts the result. "Not" goes before **bi**: **ne bi**.

> **Watch out:** **bi** never changes, but the verb after it does: **bi bil** for one man, **bi bili** for a group.
