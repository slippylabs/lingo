In this lesson: the **seasons** and the **future tense**.

## The seasons

| season | in the … |
|---|---|
| **pomlad** — spring | **spomladi** — in spring |
| **poletje** — summer | **poleti** — in summer |
| **jesen** — autumn | **jeseni** — in autumn |
| **zima** — winter | **pozimi** — in winter |

- **Jeseni samo dežuje.** — In autumn it only rains.

## The future tense

The future is **bom, boš, bo, bomo, boste, bodo** ("will") plus the same verb form as the past (ending **‑l, ‑la, ‑li**):

- **Poleti bo toplo.** — In the summer it will be warm.
- **Pozimi bo padal sneg.** — In the winter it will snow.
- **Pomlad bo prinesla cvetje.** — Spring will bring flowers.
- **Jeseni bomo videli padajoče listje.** — In the autumn we will see falling leaves.
- **Pozimi se boste igrali s snegom.** — In the winter you will play with snow.
- **Oni bodo videli zeleno pokrajino.** — They will see a green landscape.

> **Watch out:** **bo** alone already means "will be": **Poleti bo toplo.** needs no other verb.
