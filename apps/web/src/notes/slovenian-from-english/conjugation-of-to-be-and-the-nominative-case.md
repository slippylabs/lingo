In this lesson: the verb **"to be"**, the words for **I, you, he, she**, and **this / that**.

## "To be"

Slovenian changes the verb for every person:

| | |
|---|---|
| **jaz sem** — I am | **mi smo** — we are |
| **ti** **si** — you are | **vi ste** — you (all) are |
| **on** / **ona** / **ono** **je** — he / she / it is | **oni / one so** — they are |

- **Jaz sem moder.** — I am wise.
- **Kdo si ti?** — Who are you?
- **Mi smo zelo modri** — We are very wise
- **To so prijatelji.** — These are friends.

Because the verb already shows the person, the word for "I" or "you" is often left out: **Ste tudi modri?** — Are you also wise?

## "Is not" and questions

"Is not" is one word, **ni**:

- **To ni moja sestra.** — This is not my sister.

To ask a question, put the verb first:

- **Je to dober avto?** — Is this a good car?
- **So to dobri prijatelji?** — Are these good friends?

## This and that

- **To je moj otrok.** — This is my child.
- **Ta ženska je visoka.** — This woman is tall.
- **Tisti moški je prijazen.** — That man is kind.
- **Tisti dom tam je lep.** — That house over there is beautiful.

## Describing words match the noun

Every noun is masculine, feminine or neuter (a grammar group, not about meaning). The describing word changes its ending to match, and again for more than one:

- **Jaz sem moder.** — I am wise. (a man)
- **Ona je zelo modra** — She is very wise
- **Mi smo zelo modri** — We are very wise

> **Watch out:** **oni** and **one** both mean "they". Use **one** for a group of only women, **oni** for any other group: **One so zelo prijazne.** but **Oni so zelo prijazni.**
