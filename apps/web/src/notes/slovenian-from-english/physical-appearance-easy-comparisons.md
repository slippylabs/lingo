In this lesson: describing **how people look** and comparing with **more ... than**.

## Describing people

- **visok** — tall
- **nizek** — short
- **debel** — fat
- **suh** — thin
- **mlad** — young
- **star** — old
- **lep** — beautiful
- **grd** — ugly

- **On ima dolge lase.** — He has long hair.
- **Ona ima zelene oči.** — She has green eyes.
- **Njegovi lasje so svetli.** — His hair is blonde.

## More ... than

The easy way to compare is **bolj** ("more") before the describing word and **kot** ("than") before the other person:

- **Stari ljudje so bolj modri kot mi.** — Old people are wiser than us.
- **Oči so bolj zelene kot pri njem.** — Her eyes are greener than his.

For "less", use **manj**: **Suh ima manj telesne mase kot debel.** — The thin person has less body mass than the fat one.

Many common words also have their own one-word form, such as *višji* (taller) and *lepši* (more beautiful).

## Like

**kot** also means "like" or "as":

- **Lepe oči se bleščijo kot zvezde na nebu.** — Beautiful eyes shine like stars in the sky.
- **In njegovi lepi lasje so temni kot noč.** — And his beautiful hair is as dark as the night.

> **Watch out:** **lasje** (hair) is plural, so it takes **so**: **Njegovi lasje so svetli.**
