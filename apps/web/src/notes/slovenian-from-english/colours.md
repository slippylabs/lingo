In this lesson: **colours** and how they **match** their noun.

## Colours

- **črn** — black
- **bel** — white
- **rdeč** — red
- **zelen** — green
- **moder** — blue
- **rumen** — yellow
- **siv** — grey
- **oranžen** — orange

## A colour matches its noun

The form above is masculine. Feminine nouns take **‑a**, neuter nouns **‑o** or **‑e**:

- **Moj pes je črn.** — My dog is black.
- **Miš je bela.** — The mouse is white.
- **Nebo je modro.** — The sky is blue.
- **Sonce je rumeno.** — The sun is yellow.

More than one: **Cvetovi so rdeči.** — The flowers are red. **Oblaki so sivi.** — The clouds are grey.

## As an object

When the noun is the object, a feminine colour ends in **‑o**, like its noun:

- **Jaz ljubim rdečo barvo.** — I like the red color.
- **Dekle nese belo obleko.** — The girl is carrying a white dress.
- **On ima oranžno torbo.** — He has an orange bag.

> **Watch out:** **moder** means "blue" here, but it is also the word for "wise" from the first lesson. The sentence tells you which.
