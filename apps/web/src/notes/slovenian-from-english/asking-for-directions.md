In this lesson: **asking the way** and **giving directions**.

## Asking

- **Kje je postaja?** — Where is the station?
- **Kje je najbližji hotel?** — Where is the nearest hotel?
- **Kod gre cesta?** — Which way does the road go?
- **Mi lahko pokažete pot?** — Can you show me the way?

**Kje** asks where something is; **kod** asks which way something goes.

## Giving directions

- **Pojdi naravnost do mostu.** — Go straight to the bridge.
- **Pojdite naravnost in potem desno.** — Go straight and then to the right.
- **Potem levo.** — Then to the left.
- **Postaja je levo.** — The station is on the left.

**Pojdi** is "go!" to one person you know well; **pojdite** is polite, or to several people.

## Near and far

- **Trgovina je blizu knjižnice.** — The shop is near the library.
- **Knjižnica je daleč.** — The library is far away.
- **Trg je daleč od hotela.** — The square is far from the hotel.

> **Watch out:** to a stranger use the polite form: **Pojdite**, and **pokažete**, not **pojdi**.
