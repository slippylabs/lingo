In this lesson: **jobs**, with separate words for **women** and **men**.

## Jobs

- **učitelj** — teacher
- **zdravnik** — doctor
- **inženir** — engineer
- **delavec** — worker
- **prodajalec** — seller
- **šofer** — driver
- **kuhar** — cook
- **policist** — policeman
- **umetnik** — artist

## A woman's job title

Many jobs add an ending for a woman:

- **Zdravnik pomaga ljudem.** — The doctor helps people.
- **Ona je zdravnica.** — She is a doctor.

## Saying what someone does

No word for "a" is needed:

- **Moj brat je učitelj.** — My brother is a teacher.
- **Moj oče je kuhar.** — My father is a cook.
- **On je inženir v mestu.** — He is an engineer in the city.

## At work

- **Prodajalec dela v trgovini.** — The seller works in the shop.
- **Delavec dela na mostu.** — The worker is working on the bridge.
- **Šofer vozi avtobus.** — The driver drives a bus.

> **Watch out:** for a woman doctor say **zdravnica**, not **zdravnik**: **Ona je zdravnica.**
