In this lesson: **nationalities**, **countries** and **languages**.

## People

A word for a person from a country starts with a capital letter. There is a separate word for a woman:

- **Jaz sem Poljak.** — I am a Pole.
- **Ona je Hrvatica.** — She is a Croat.
- **On je Rus.** — He is a Russian.
- **Si ti Anglež?** — Are you an Englishman?
- **Nemec živi v Nemčiji.** — A German lives in Germany.

## Languages

The word for a language is a describing word, written with a small letter:

- **Govoriš nemški jezik?** — Do you speak the German language?
- **Anglež govori angleški jezik.** — An Englishman speaks the English language.
- **Hrvat govori hrvaški jezik.** — A Croat speaks the Croatian language.

## Knowing languages

- **On zna tri jezike.** — He knows three languages.
- **On govori vsak jezik dobro.** — He speaks every language well.
- **Mi se učimo ruskega jezika.** — We are learning the Russian language.

> **Watch out:** capital for the person, small letter for the language: **Anglež** but **angleški jezik**.
