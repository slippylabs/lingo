In this lesson: the **tens**, and numbers like **24** or **78**.

## The tens

**deset** 10 · **dvajset** 20 · **trideset** 30 · **štirideset** 40 · **petdeset** 50 · **šestdeset** 60 · **sedemdeset** 70 · **osemdeset** 80 · **devetdeset** 90 · **sto** 100

## Units come first

Slovenian says the units before the tens, joined by **in** ("and"), all as one word — "four-and-twenty":

- **štiriindvajset stvari** — twenty four things
- **enainpetdeset ur** — fifty one clocks
- **oseminsedemdeset rubljev** — seventy eight rubles
- **triindevetdeset računalnikov** — ninety three computers

## The noun after the number

After round tens the noun takes its "of" form, like after five:

- **dvajset žensk** — twenty women
- **trideset psov** — thirty dogs

When a number ends in one or two, the noun follows that last word:

- **sto eno kolo** — one hundred one bicycles
- **sto dve sonci** — one hundred two suns

> **Watch out:** say the units first: 24 is **štiriindvajset**, never "dvajset štiri".
