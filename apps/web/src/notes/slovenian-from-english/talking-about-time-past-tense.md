In this lesson: **time words** and the **past tense**.

## Time words

- **danes** — today
- **včeraj** — yesterday
- **jutri** — tomorrow
- **minuta** — minute
- **sekunda** — second
- **polnoč** — midnight
- **Koliko je ura?** — What time is it?

## The past tense

The past is made of two parts: the "to be" word (**sem, si, je, smo, ste, so**) and a past form of the verb that matches whether the person is male or female:

- **Jaz sem bil doma zjutraj.** — I was at home in the morning. (a man)
- **Včeraj je bila doma ob osmi uri.** — Yesterday she was at home at the eighth hour.
- **On je šel domov.** — He went home.
- **Ste šli v kino?** — Did you go to the cinema?
- **Zvečer smo šli v restavracijo.** — In the evening we went to the restaurant.

The endings: **‑l** for a man, **‑la** for a woman, **‑lo** for "it", **‑li** for a group.

## "It was"

For weather and general situations, use **je bilo**:

- **Ob tretji uri dvajset je bilo hladno.** — At twenty past the third hour it was cold.
- **Zjutraj ni bilo zelo toplo.** — In the morning it was not very warm.

## Did not

The "to be" word starts with **ni**: **nisem, nisi, ni, niso**:

- **Oni niso nič delali.** — They did nothing.
- **Danes ti nisi imel časa za to.** — Today you did not have time for that.

> **Watch out:** a woman says **bila**, a man **bil**: **Včeraj je bila doma.** — Yesterday she was at home.
