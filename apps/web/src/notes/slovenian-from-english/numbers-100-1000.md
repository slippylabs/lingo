In this lesson: the **hundreds** and **a thousand**.

## The hundreds

**sto** 100 · **dvesto** 200 · **tristo** 300 · **štiristo** 400 · **petsto** 500 · **šeststo** 600 · **sedemsto** 700 · **osemsto** 800 · **devetsto** 900 · **tisoč** 1000

They are one word: "two" plus **sto**.

## The noun after the number

After a round hundred the noun takes its "of" form:

- **sto fantov** — one hundred boys
- **dvesto žensk** — two hundred women
- **petsto žensk** — five hundred women
- **osemsto dolarjev** — eight hundred dollars

When the number ends in a smaller number, the noun follows that last word:

- **sedemsto eno kolo** — seven hundred one bicycles
- **sedemsto ena ura** — seven hundred one hours
- **tristo štiri stvari** — three hundred four things
- **štiristo sedem dolarjev** — four hundred seven dollars

## Thousands

- **dva tisoč devetsto psov** — two thousand nine hundred dogs

> **Watch out:** in **sedemsto eno kolo** the noun is singular, because the number ends in "one", even though there are 701 bicycles.
