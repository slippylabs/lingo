In this lesson: **clothes** and the joining words **in** (and), **a** (but) and **ali** (or).

## Clothes

- **srajca** — shirt
- **majica** — t-shirt
- **hlače** — trousers
- **plašč** — coat
- **šal** — scarf
- **kapa** — hat
- **rokavice** — gloves
- **nogavice** — socks

## Wearing

**nositi** — to wear: **nosim** (I wear), **nosi** (he / she wears).

- **On nosi modro srajco.** — He wears a blue shirt.
- **Nosim rokavice pozimi.** — I wear gloves in winter.

## Joining words

**in** — and, simply adding:

- **Šal je težek in topel.** — The scarf is heavy and warm.

**a** — but, or "and" for a contrast:

- **Ona ima črne hlače, a on bele.** — She has black trousers, and he has white.
- **Pozimi nosim vedno kapo, a poleti ne.** — In winter, I always wear a hat, but not in summer.

**ali** — or:

- **Ti hočeš šal ali srajco.** — You want a scarf or a shirt.

> **Watch out:** **hlače** (trousers) and **spodnjice** (underwear) are always plural: **Nove spodnjice so udobne.** — New underwear is comfortable.
