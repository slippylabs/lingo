In this lesson: the **days of the week** and the **months**.

## Days

- **ponedeljek** — Monday
- **torek** — Tuesday
- **sreda** — Wednesday
- **četrtek** — Thursday
- **petek** — Friday
- **sobota** — Saturday
- **nedelja** — Sunday

- **Ponedeljek je prvi dan v tednu.** — Monday is the first day of the week.

## "On Wednesday"

"On" a day is **v** plus the day; a feminine day changes **‑a** to **‑o**:

- **V sredo imam nalogo.** — On Wednesday, I have an assignment.
- **V petek zvečer gremo na film.** — On Friday evening we are going to the movies.

## Months

**januar, februar, marec, april, maj, junij, julij, avgust, september, oktober, november, december**

"In" a month is **v** and the ending **‑u**:

- **Imam v januarju počitnice.** — I have vacation in January.
- **V avgustu grem v Honduras.** — In August, I am going to Honduras.
- **V februarju je veliko snega.** — In February there is a lot of snow.

> **Watch out:** days and months are written with a small letter: **ponedeljek**, **januar**.
