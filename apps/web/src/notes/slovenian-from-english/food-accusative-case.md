In this lesson: **food** and the *accusative case*, the form for the thing you eat, buy or see.

## Eating

**jesti** (to eat) is irregular:

- **Jaz jem kruh.** — I am eating bread.
- **Ti ješ jajce?** — Are you eating an egg?
- **Ona je meso.** — She is eating meat.
- **Mi jemo večerjo.** — We are eating dinner.

## The accusative case

The thing that receives the action (the object) takes the *accusative case*. Feminine words ending in **‑a** change it to **‑o**:

- **večerja** — dinner → **Mi kuhamo večerjo vsak dan.** — We prepare dinner every day.
- **knjiga** — book → **Bereš ti knjigo in ješ zajtrk?** — Are you reading a book and eating breakfast?
- **Mi vidimo tvojo pico na mizi.** — We see your pizza on the table.

Masculine words for things and neuter words stay the same:

- **On kuha kosilo.** — He is preparing lunch.
- **On ljubi mleko.** — He likes milk.
- **Oni kupujejo maslo in kruh.** — They are buying butter and bread.

## "That"

To join two sentences with "that", use **da** after a comma:

- **Jaz vem, da ti ljubiš jajca.** — I know that you like eggs.

> **Watch out:** **je** means "is", but it is also "he / she eats". **Ona je meso.** means "She is eating meat", not "She is meat".
