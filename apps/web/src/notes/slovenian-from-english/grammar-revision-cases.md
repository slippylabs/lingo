In this lesson: a **review of the cases**, the endings a word takes for its job in the sentence.

## The six cases at a glance

- Nominative (the basic form, the subject): **Oči so ogledalo duše.** — Eyes are the mirror of the soul.
- Genitive ("of", and after "not"): **Brata tu ni.** — There is no brother here.
- Dative (to whom): **Dam ženski.** — I give to the woman.
- Accusative (the object): **On vidi svojo ženo.** — He sees his wife.
- Locative (where, after **v**, **na**, **o**): **Ona je v mestu.** — She is in the city.
- Instrumental (with): **Govorim s prijatelji.** — I talk with friends.

## "There is no …"

"There isn't" is **ni**, and the missing thing takes the genitive:

- **Kosti tu ni.** — There is no bone here.
- **Ni več banan v hiši.** — There are no more bananas in the house.

## "With" a tool

The instrumental also means "by means of":

- **To se da rezati z nožem.** — This can be cut with a knife.

## "About"

**o** (about) takes the locative: **Ona govori o bratih.** — She talks about brothers.

> **Watch out:** after "not have" the thing changes form: **Imena on nima.** — He has no name. (**ime** becomes **imena**)
