In this lesson: **nature** and small words for **through, between, on and into**.

## Nature

- **reka** — river
- **gora** — mountain
- **dolina** — valley
- **gozd** — forest
- **polje** — field
- **trava** — grass
- **cvet** — flower
- **kamen** — stone
- **drevo** — tree

## Going somewhere vs. being somewhere

When you go or look *into* a place, the noun takes the accusative case (feminine **‑a** becomes **‑o**):

- **Mi gremo v gozd.** — We are going to the forest.
- **Oni gledajo v dolino.** — They are looking into the valley.

When you are **on** a place, the noun takes the locative case:

- **On stoji na kamnu.** — He is standing on the stone.
- **Mi stojimo na zemlji.** — We are standing on the earth.

## Through and between

- **Reka teče čez dolino.** — The river flows through the valley.
- **Dolina leži med gorami.** — The valley lies between the mountains.
- **Reka gre med polji.** — The river goes between the fields.

## Growing

- **Trava raste hitro.** — The grass grows fast.
- **Vrtnica raste na polju.** — The rose grows in the field.

> **Watch out:** a field takes **na**, not **v**: **na polju** — in the field.
