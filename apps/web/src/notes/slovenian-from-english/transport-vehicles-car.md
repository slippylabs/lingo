In this lesson: **transport** and the **road**.

## Getting around

- **avto** — car
- **avtobus** — bus
- **vlak** — train
- **kolo** — bicycle
- **cesta** — road
- **most** — bridge
- **semafor** — traffic light

## "Goes" for vehicles

For vehicles, "goes" is **vozi**, from **voziti** (to drive):

- **Avtobus vozi do centra.** — The bus goes to the center.
- **Vlak vozi na postajo.** — The train goes to the station.
- **Šofer vozi hitro.** — The driver drives fast.

## Describing

- **Avto je nov in lep.** — The car is new and beautiful.
- **Cesta je široka.** — The road is wide.
- **Kolo je razbito.** — The bicycle is broken.
- **Šofer je previden.** — The driver is careful.

## Two of something

For exactly two things Slovenian uses a special ending, the dual: **Most povezuje dve mesti.** — The bridge connects two cities.

> **Watch out:** **kolo** is neuter, so its describing word ends in **‑o**: **Kolo je razbito.**
