In this lesson: **shapes**, and **"has"** with the numbers three and four.

## Shapes

- **To je kruh.** — This is a circle.
- **To je jednoduchý trojúhelník.** — This is a simple triangle.
- **Čtverec a kruh jsou základní formy.** — Square and circle are basic shapes.

## "Has"

- **Čtverec má čtyři strany.** — A square has four sides.
- **Trojúhelník má tři strany.** — A triangle has three sides.

After **tři** and **čtyři**, the noun is in the plural: **strana** → **strany**.

## Describing words match

- **Ta linie je dlouhá.** — This line is long. (feminine)
- **To je jednoduchý tvar.** — This is a simple shape. (masculine)
- **Forma je zajímavá.** — The shape is interesting. (feminine)

## "Of a square"

- **Stůl má tvar čtverce.** — The table has the shape of a square.
- **Vidíme obrys čtverce.** — We see the outline of a square.

Here **čtverec** takes the genitive, the "of" form: **čtverce**.

> **Watch out:** **tvar** and **forma** both mean "shape". **tvar** is the everyday word, and it is masculine (**jednoduchý tvar**), while **forma** is feminine (**Forma je zajímavá**).
