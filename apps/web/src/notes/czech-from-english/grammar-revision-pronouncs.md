In this lesson: **pronouns** like me, you and us in their different forms, and the two verbs for **"to know"**.

## Me, you, us

Pronouns change form just like nouns:

- **Dej mi vodu.** — Give me water.
- **O mně mluvíš?** — Are you talking about me?
- **Mluvím s tebou.** — I am talking with you.
- **Ne, já nemluvím o tobě.** — No, I don't talk about you.
- **S námi je vždycky všechno veselé.** — With us everything is always cheerful.
- **On dává sobě dar.** — He gives himself a gift.

## Who?

- **Koho vidíš?** — Who do you see?
- **Komu dáš dar?** — Who do you give a gift to?

## Two verbs for "to know"

- **znát** — to know a person or a thing: **To neznám.** — I don't know that.
- **vědět** — to know a fact: **O tom už dlouho vím.** — I have known about that thing for a long time.

> **Watch out:** Czech uses a double negative: **O té věci já nic nevím** — I know nothing about that thing (word for word, "I don't know nothing").
