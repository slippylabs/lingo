In this lesson: **fruit**, and how describing words **match** the noun.

## Describing words match

A describing word (adjective) changes its ending to match the noun's gender:

- **Banán je žlutý.** — The banana is yellow. (masculine ‑ý)
- **Hruška je chutná.** — The pear is tasty. (feminine ‑á)
- **Jablko je červené.** — The apple is red. (neuter ‑é)
- **Pomeranče jsou sladké.** — The oranges are sweet. (plural)

## More than one

- **Jahody jsou v misce.** — The strawberries are in the bowl.
- **Hrušky leží na stole.** — The pears are lying on the table.

Feminine nouns in ‑a usually make their plural with ‑y: **jahoda** → **jahody**.

## Grapes

- **My kupujeme hroznové víno.** — We are buying grapes.
- **Hroznové víno roste na vinici.** — Grapes grow in a vineyard.

> **Watch out:** **hroznové víno** is the fruit, grapes. Plain **víno** is wine.
