In this lesson: **jobs**, and saying **what someone does**.

## Saying someone's job

- **Můj bratr je učitel.** — My brother is a teacher.
- **Můj otec je kuchař.** — My father is a cook.
- **On je inženýr ve městě.** — He is an engineer in the city.
- **Ona je doktorka.** — She is a doctor.

Most jobs have a woman's form ending in ‑ka: **doktor** → **doktorka**, **učitel** → **učitelka**.

## What they do

- **Doktor pomáhá lidem.** — The doctor helps people.
- **Prodavač prodává jídlo.** — The seller sells food.
- **Řidič řídí autobus.** — The driver drives a bus.
- **Kuchař vaří večeři.** — The cook is preparing dinner.
- **Dělník staví dům.** — The worker is building a house.

> **Watch out:** after **je** you can use the job's basic form (**Můj bratr je učitel**) or its instrumental form (**Ona je učitelkou**). Both are correct.
