In this lesson: **nature words**, and small words for **through**, **between**, **into** and **towards**.

## Movement and place words

Each little word needs a particular case, so learn the whole phrase:

- **Řeka teče přes údolí.** — The river flows through the valley.
- **Údolí leží mezi horami.** — The valley lies between the mountains.
- **My jdeme do lesa.** — We are going to the forest.
- **Jdeme k dubu.** — We are going towards the oak.
- **On stojí na kameni.** — He is standing on the stone.
- **Pole je za domem.** — The field is behind the house.

## Growing and blooming

- **Tráva roste rychle.** — The grass grows fast.
- **Květy krásně kvetou.** — The flowers bloom beautifully.
- **Květ roste ze země.** — A flower grows from the earth.

> **Watch out:** **do** means into a place (**do lesa**), while **k** means up to or towards it (**k dubu**).
