In this lesson: **tens up to 100**, and how to build numbers like 78.

## The tens

- 20 to 40 end in ‑cet: **dvacet**, **třicet**, **čtyřicet**
- 50 to 90 end in ‑desát: **padesát**, **šedesát**, **sedmdesát**, **osmdesát**, **devadesát**
- **sto** — one hundred

## Numbers in between

Say the tens, then the units, as in English:

- **sedmdesát osm rublů** — seventy eight rubles
- **osmdesát sedm dolarů** — eighty seven dollars

There is also an older way, units first and joined with ‑a‑: **jednapadesát hodinek** — fifty one clocks.

## The noun follows the last number

- **devadesát tři počítače** — ninety three computers (ends in 3: normal plural)
- **dvacet čtyři věci** — twenty four things
- **třicet psů** — thirty dogs (a round ten: "of" form)

> **Watch out:** in a two-part number, the last part decides the noun: **devadesát tři počítače** but **devět počítačů**.
