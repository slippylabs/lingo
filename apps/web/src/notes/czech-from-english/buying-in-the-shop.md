In this lesson: **shopping**: buying, selling, prices and pieces of things.

## Buying and selling

- **Chci koupit chléb.** — I want to buy bread.
- **Ona kupuje knihu.** — She is buying a book.
- **On prodává maso.** — He sells meat.
- **Cena je vysoká.** — The price is high.

## A piece of

**kus** (a piece) is followed by the genitive, the "of" form:

- **Kus sýra je dobrý.** — A piece of cheese is good.
- **Prodavač dá kus klobásy.** — The seller gives a piece of sausage.
- **Kus chleba je malý.** — The piece of bread is small.

## Asking

**ptát se** with *na* means "to ask about":

- **Zákazník se ptá na cenu.** — The customer asks about the price.
- **Zákazník se ptá na slevu.** — The customer asks for a discount.

> **Watch out:** **chléb** (bread) changes its vowel in the "of" form: **kus chleba**, not *kus chléba*.
