In this lesson: **vehicles**, and the verb **jet**, "to go" by vehicle.

## Going by vehicle

Czech has two verbs for "go": *jít* on foot and **jet** by vehicle. Vehicles always use **jet**:

- **Autobus jede do centra.** — The bus goes to the center.
- **Vlak jede na stanici.** — The train goes to the station.
- **Řidič jede rychle.** — The driver drives fast.

Roads and paths "walk", so they take **jde**:

- **Cesta jde přes les.** — The road goes through the forest.

## On the street

- **Auto stojí na ulici.** — The car is standing on the street.
- **Semafor ukazuje červenou.** — The traffic light shows red.
- **Most spojuje dvě města.** — The bridge connects two cities.
- **Kolo je rozbité.** — The bicycle is broken.

> **Watch out:** a vehicle **jede**, it never *jde*: **Vlak jede na sever**.
