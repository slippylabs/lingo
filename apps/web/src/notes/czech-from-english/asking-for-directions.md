In this lesson: **asking the way**: where, where to, left, right, near and far.

## Where and where to

- **Kde je stanice?** — Where is the station?
- **Kde je nejbližší hotel?** — Where is the nearest hotel?
- **Kam jdete?** — Which way are you going?
- **Kam jede ten vlak?** — Where is that train going?

## Left, right, straight on

- **Jděte přímo a potom vpravo.** — Go straight and then to the right.
- **Stanice je vlevo.** — The station is on the left.
- **Jdi přímo k mostu.** — Go straight to the bridge.

**Jdi** tells one friend where to go; **Jděte** is for several people or anyone you speak to politely.

## Near and far

- **Obchod je blízko knihovny.** — The shop is near the library.
- **Knihovna je daleko.** — The library is far away.
- **Můžete mi ukázat cestu?** — Can you show me the way?

> **Watch out:** **kde** asks where something is, but **kam** asks where it is going: **Kde je stanice?** but **Kam jede ten vlak?**
