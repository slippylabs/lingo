In this lesson: **weather**, and sentences like "It is warm" that have **no word for "it"**.

## "It is warm"

Czech says these with **je** and a word ending in ‑o, with no "it":

- **Dnes je teplo.** — It is warm today.
- **V Rusku je chladno.** — In Russia it is cold.
- **Dnes je oblačno.** — It is cloudy today.

## Rain and snow "fall"

- **Padá déšť.** — It is raining.
- **Padá sníh a déšť.** — Snow and rain are falling.
- **Sníh leží na zemi.** — Snow lies on the ground.

## The sun and the wind

- **Slunce svítí.** — The sun is shining.
- **Vítr fouká silně.** — The wind is blowing strongly.
- **Počasí se mění.** — The weather is changing.

> **Watch out:** **teplo** says "it is warm" about the weather, but a warm thing takes the describing word **teplý**: **Dnes je teplo** but **Pokoj je teplý**.
