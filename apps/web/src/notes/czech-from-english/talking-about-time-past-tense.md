In this lesson: the **past tense**, and **time words** like hour, minute, yesterday and tomorrow.

## The past tense

The past form of a verb ends in ‑l, and its ending shows who did it:

- **byl** (a man), **byla** (a woman), **bylo** (a thing), **byli** (a group)
- **Včera bylo pondělí.** — Yesterday was Monday.
- **Včera ona byla doma v osm hodin.** — Yesterday she was at home at eight o'clock.

For I, you and we, add the helper words **jsem**, **jsi**, **jsme**, **jste**:

- **Já jsem byl doma ráno.** — I was at home in the morning.
- **Večer jsme šli do restaurace.** — In the evening we went to the restaurant.
- **Vy jste šli do kina?** — Did you go to the cinema?

For he, she, it and they there is no helper:

- **On šel domů.** — He went home.
- **Oni nic nedělali.** — They did nothing.

## Time words

- **Kolik je hodin?** — What time is it?
- **Zítra je příliš pozdě.** — Tomorrow is too late.
- **Sekunda je krátká.** — A second is short.

> **Watch out:** never add a helper for he, she, it or they: say **On šel domů**, not *On je šel domů*.
