In this lesson: **ordinal numbers** (first, second, third…) and **telling the time**.

## First, second, third

Ordinal numbers work like describing words and match the noun:

- **první** — first, **druhý** — second, **třetí** — third, **čtvrtý** — fourth, **pátý** — fifth
- **třicátý muž** — thirtieth man
- **stý dolar** — hundredth dollar
- **čtyřstá pátá hora** — four hundred fifth mountain (feminine, so both parts end in ‑á)

## Telling the time

Czech gives the time with ordinary numbers and **hodina** (hour):

- **v jednu hodinu dvacet** — at one twenty
- **ve dvě hodiny patnáct** — at two fifteen
- **ve čtyři hodiny sedm minut** — at seven minutes past four
- **v osm hodin padesát pět minut** — at eight fifty five

The word for hour follows the number rule: one **hodinu**, two to four **hodiny**, five and up **hodin**.

> **Watch out:** "at" a time is **v**, which becomes **ve** before **dvě**, **tři** and **čtyři**: **ve dvě hodiny**.
