In this lesson: **colours**, which change their ending like any describing word.

## Colours match the noun

- **Můj pes je černý.** — My dog is black. (masculine)
- **Myš je bílá.** — The mouse is white. (feminine)
- **Nebe je modré.** — The sky is blue. (neuter)
- **Květy jsou červené.** — The flowers are red. (plural)

## After a verb

When a feminine noun is the thing being worn, loved or had, its colour changes from ‑á to ‑ou (the accusative case):

- **Dívka nosí bílou sukni.** — The girl wears a white skirt.
- **Já miluji červenou barvu.** — I love the color red.
- **On má oranžovou tašku.** — He has an orange bag.

A masculine thing doesn't change:

- **On nosí hnědý kabát.** — He wears a brown coat.

> **Watch out:** **Rudé moře** is the Red Sea. *rudý* is an old word for red used in names; everyday red is **červený**.
