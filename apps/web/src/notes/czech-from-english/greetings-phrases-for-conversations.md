In this lesson: **greetings**, **thank you**, and short **everyday replies**.

## Hello and goodbye

- **Dobré ráno!** — Good morning!
- **Dobrý den!** — Good day! (the polite hello all day long)
- **Dobrý večer!** — Good evening!
- **Dobrou noc!** — Good night! (when someone goes to bed)
- **Ahoj!** — Hello! (to friends; it also means bye)

## How are you?

- **Jak se máš?** — How are you?
- **Mám se dobře, děkuji!** — I am well, thank you!
- **A ty?** — And you?
- **Jak se jmenuješ?** — What is your name?
- **Jmenuji se Bartek.** — My name is Bartek.

## Yes, no, maybe

- **Ano, je to možné.** — Yes, it is possible.
- **Ne, není to možné.** — No, it is not possible.
- **Možná.** — Perhaps.
- **Nevím.** — I don't know.
- **Pardon, nerozumím.** — Excuse me, I don't understand.

> **Watch out:** "yes" is **ano** and "no" is **ne**. You will also hear Czechs say *no* in conversation: it means "yeah", not "no".
