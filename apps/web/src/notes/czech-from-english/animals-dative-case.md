In this lesson: **animals** and the **dative case**, the form for the one who **receives** something ("to the cat").

## The receiver

The dative case marks who gets something or who is helped:

- **Já dávám kočce rybu.** — I give the cat a fish. (**kočka** → **kočce**)
- **My dáváme žábě chléb.** — We give bread to the frog. (**žába** → **žábě**)
- **Dáváme koni vodu.** — We give the horse water.
- **Ona to ukazuje psovi.** — She shows it to the dog.
- **On pomáhá medvědovi.** — He helps the bear.

Masculine animals usually add ‑ovi; feminine ‑a becomes ‑e or ‑ě.

## More than one

- **My pomáháme ptákům.** — We help the birds.
- **Dáváme žábám vodu.** — We give the frogs water.

## "Help" takes the dative

In Czech you help *to* someone:

- **Pomáháme vlkovi.** — We help the wolf.

## Giving now or later

- **dávám** — I give (now, or regularly)
- **Ty dáš mně rybu?** — Will you give me a fish?
- **Vy dáte koni vodu.** — You will give the horse water.

> **Watch out:** a k before the ending turns into c: **kočka** becomes **kočce**, just like **řeka** becomes **řece**.
