In this lesson: **days of the week**, **months**, and how to say **on Wednesday** and **in January**.

## Days

**pondělí, úterý, středa, čtvrtek, pátek, sobota, neděle**

To say "on" a day, use **v** (or **ve**) and the accusative case:

- **Ve středu mám úkol.** — On Wednesday, I have an assignment.
- **V pátek večer jdeme na film.** — On Friday evening we are going to the movies.

## Months

The Czech months don't look like the English ones: **leden** (January), **únor** (February), **srpen** (August), **říjen** (October).

To say "in" a month, use **v** and the locative case:

- **Mám v lednu prázdniny.** — I have vacation in January.
- **V únoru je mnoho sněhu.** — In February there is a lot of snow.
- **V srpnu jedu do Hondurasu.** — In August, I am going to Honduras.

> **Watch out:** days and months start with a small letter in Czech: **pondělí**, **leden**. They only get a capital at the start of a sentence.
