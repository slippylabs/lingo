In this lesson: a **review of the cases**, the different forms a noun takes for its job.

## The cases in one place

- **On vidí svou ženu.** — He sees his wife. (accusative: the thing seen)
- **Dám ženě.** — I give to the woman. (dative: the receiver)
- **Ona dává dar bratrům.** — She gives a gift to the brothers. (dative plural)
- **Ona je ve městě.** — She is in the city. (locative: where)
- **Ona mluví o bratrech.** — She talks about brothers. (locative after **o**, "about")
- **Mluvíme s přáteli.** — We talk with friends. (instrumental: with)
- **To se může řezat nožem.** — This can be cut with a knife. (instrumental: the tool)

## Many of something

**mnoho** (many, a lot) is followed by the genitive plural:

- **A také je mnoho hvězd.** — And there are also many stars.
- **Ve městech uvidíš mnoho lidí.** — In the cities you will see many people.

## None here

- **Bratr tu není.** — There is no brother here.
- **Nevidím tu žádné muže.** — I do not see any men here.

> **Watch out:** after **mnoho**, the noun must be in the genitive plural: **mnoho hvězd**, **mnoho lidí**.
