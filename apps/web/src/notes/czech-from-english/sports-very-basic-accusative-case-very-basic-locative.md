In this lesson: **sports**, the **accusative case** (the thing you play), and **v / na** with the **locative case** (where).

## The thing you play

The accusative case is the form of the thing that receives the action. For sports, which are masculine, it looks the same as the basic form:

- **Hraji fotbal.** — I play football.
- **Ona hraje basketbal.** — She plays basketball.
- **Já nehraji hokej.** — I do not play hockey.

## Where: v and na

After **v** (in) and **na** (on, at), a noun saying where takes the locative case. It often ends in ‑e, ‑ě or ‑u:

- **My plaveme v řece.** — We swim in the river. (**řeka** → **řece**)
- **Stadion je ve městě.** — The stadium is in the city. (**město** → **městě**)
- **Ona hraje na stadionu.** — She plays at the stadium.

**v** becomes **ve** when the next word is hard to say after it, as in **ve městě**.

## How often and when

- **On trénuje každý den.** — He trains every day.
- **Oni běhají ráno.** — They run in the morning.
- **My plaveme často.** — We swim often.

> **Watch out:** the locative can change a consonant, not just the ending: **řeka** becomes **v řece** (k turns into c).
