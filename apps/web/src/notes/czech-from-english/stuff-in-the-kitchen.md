In this lesson: **kitchen things**, and saying **"with a fork"**, **"from a cup"** and **"full of"**.

## With, from, in

- **On jí vidličkou.** — He eats with a fork. (a tool: no word for "with")
- **Ona pije z hrnku.** — She drinks from a cup.
- **My vaříme v hrnci.** — We cook in a pot.
- **Na pánvi je maso.** — There is meat in the frying pan.

## Full of

**plný** (full) is followed by the genitive, the "of" form:

- **Talíř je plný jídla.** — The plate is full of food.
- **Lžíce je plná pepře.** — The spoon is full of pepper.

## More than one

- **Vidličky a nože jsou na stole.** — Forks and knives are on the table.
- **Nůž je ostrý.** — The knife is sharp.

> **Watch out:** **hrnek** is a cup or mug, but **hrnec** is a cooking pot: **Ona pije z hrnku** but **My vaříme v hrnci**.
