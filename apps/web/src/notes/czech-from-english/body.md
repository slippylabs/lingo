In this lesson: **body parts**, and saying that **something hurts**.

## It hurts

- **Má ruka bolí.** — My hand hurts.
- **Bolí mě žaludek.** — My stomach hurts. (word for word, "the stomach hurts me")

## Irregular plurals

A few body words have special plurals:

- **oko** → **oči**: **On má krásné modré oči.** — He has beautiful blue eyes.
- **ruka** → **ruce**: **Já mám dvě ruce a dvě nohy a také dvě chodidla.** — I have two hands and two legs, and also two feet.

## Describing the body

- **Její srdce bije rychle.** — Her heart beats fast.
- **Moje tělo je zdravé.** — My body is healthy.
- **Ucho slyší hudbu.** — The ear hears music.
- **Ona tě slyší pouze jedním uchem.** — She hears you only with one ear.

> **Watch out:** **oči** (eyes) is feminine plural, so its describing word ends in ‑é: **Moje oči jsou modré.**
