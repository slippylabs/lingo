In this lesson: **school words**, the **locative case** after **na** and **v**, and saying **"with a pen"**.

## Where something is

After **na** (on) and **v** (in), the noun takes the locative case:

- **Kniha je na stole.** — The book is on the table. (**stůl** → **stole**)
- **Škola je v centru.** — The school is in the center.
- **Píšu na papíře perem.** — I am writing on paper with a pen.

## Jobs after "is"

Czech often puts a job into the instrumental case (‑ou for women, ‑em for men) after **je**:

- **Ona je učitelkou.** — She is a teacher.
- **On je žákem.** — He is a student.
- **On je mým učitelem.** — He is my teacher.

**učitel** is a male teacher and **učitelka** a female one.

## The tool you use

For the tool you do something with, Czech uses the instrumental with no word for "with":

- **Píšu na papíře perem.** — I am writing on paper with a pen. (**pero** → **perem**)
- **Já rychle píšu tímto perem.** — I am writing quickly with this pen.

> **Watch out:** for a tool, don't add **s**: write **perem**. *S perem* would mean "together with a pen", as if it were a friend.
