In this lesson: **places in town**, and **v** and **na** for "in" and "at".

## In or at

Buildings usually take **v** (in); open places take **na** (on, at):

- **Kupujeme chléb v obchodě.** — We buy bread in the shop.
- **Čtu knihu v knihovně.** — I read a book in the library.
- **Na tržišti je mnoho lidí.** — There are many people at the market.
- **Dům stojí na ulici.** — The house stands on the street.
- **Jdeme na náměstí.** — We are going to the square.

## Near and over

- **Stanice je blízko centra.** — The station is near the center.
- **Kostel stojí blízko knihovny.** — The church stands near the library.
- **Most jde přes řeku.** — The bridge goes over the river.

## Full of people

- **Náměstí je plné lidí.** — The square is full of people.

> **Watch out:** squares, streets and markets take **na**, not **v**: **na náměstí**, **na ulici**, **na tržišti**.
