In this lesson: **rooms and furniture**, and words for **where things are**: on, under, above, near.

## Where things are

Each of these little words needs its own case, so learn them as whole phrases:

- **Na stole leží kniha.** — A book is lying on the table.
- **Truhla je pod postelí.** — The chest is under the bed.
- **Lampa svítí nad stolem.** — The lamp shines above the table.
- **Židle je blízko stolu.** — The chair is near the table.
- **Stůl stojí uprostřed pokoje.** — The table stands in the middle of the room.
- **Ve skříni je mnoho oblečení.** — There are many clothes in the wardrobe.

So "table" appears as **stole**, **stolem** and **stolu**, depending on the word before it.

## Describing the room

- **Pokoj je teplý a tichý.** — The room is warm and quiet.
- **Postel je měkká.** — The bed is soft.
- **Okno je otevřené.** — The window is open.

> **Watch out:** **dveře** (door) is always plural, even for one door: **Dveře jsou zavřené.**
