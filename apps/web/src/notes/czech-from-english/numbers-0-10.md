In this lesson: **numbers 0 to 10**, and the form of the noun that follows them.

## The numbers

**nula, jeden, dva, tři, čtyři, pět, šest, sedm, osm, devět, deset**

## One and two have genders

- **jeden chlapec** — one boy (masculine)
- **jedna žena** — one woman (feminine)
- **jedno pole** — one field (neuter)
- **dva chlapci** — two boys
- **dvě ženy** — two women

## The noun after the number

- 2, 3 and 4 take the normal plural: **tři kočky** — three cats
- 5 and up take the genitive plural, the "of" form: **pět koček** — five cats
- **čtyři dolary** — four dollars
- **osm dolarů** — eight dollars

> **Watch out:** from **pět** upwards the noun changes again: **čtyři dolary** but **osm dolarů**.
