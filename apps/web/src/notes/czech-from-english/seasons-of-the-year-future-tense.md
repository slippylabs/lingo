In this lesson: **the seasons** and the **future tense** with **budu**.

## The seasons

**jaro, léto, podzim, zima** — spring, summer, autumn, winter.

"In" a season takes **v** or **na**, and you just have to learn which:

- **V létě bude teplo.** — In the summer it will be warm.
- **V zimě bude padat sníh.** — In the winter it will snow.
- **Na jaře rostou květy.** — In spring, flowers grow.
- **Na podzim hodně prší.** — In autumn, it rains a lot.

## The future: budu + verb

Put a form of **budu** before the basic form of the verb (its "to" form):

| who | will |
|---|---|
| I | **budu** |
| you | **budeš** |
| he / she / it | **bude** |
| we | **budeme** |
| you (several) | **budete** |
| they | **budou** |

- **Na podzim budeme vidět padající listí.** — In the autumn we will see falling leaves.
- **V zimě budete hrát se sněhem.** — In the winter you will play with snow.
- **Oni budou vidět zelenou krajinu.** — They will see a green landscape.

> **Watch out:** verbs for a single, finished action already talk about the future in their present form: **Jaro přinese květy** — Spring will bring flowers. Never add **bude** to them.
