In this lesson: **north, south, east, west**, and **left, right, straight on**.

## In the north

Use **na** for "in" with the compass points:

- **Na severu je sníh.** — In the north there is snow.
- **Žijeme na jihu.** — We live in the south.
- **Slunce vychází na východě.** — The sun rises in the east.
- **Ono zapadá na západě.** — It sets in the west.

## Northern, southern…

Add ‑ní to make a describing word:

- **To je severní strana.** — This is the northern side.
- **Jižní kraje jsou teplé.** — The southern regions are warm.
- **Východní hudba je zajímavá.** — Eastern music is interesting.

## Going somewhere

- **On cestuje na východ.** — He travels eastward.
- **Jdeme k jihozápadu.** — We are going to the southwest.
- **Jdeme teď vpravo a potom přímo.** — Now we are going right and then straight.

> **Watch out:** **na severu** is "in the north" (where you are), but **na sever** is "to the north" (where you go): **Vlak jede na sever.**
