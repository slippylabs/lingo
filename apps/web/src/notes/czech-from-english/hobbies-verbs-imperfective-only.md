In this lesson: **hobbies** and how Czech **verbs change for who does them**.

## Verb endings

A Czech verb changes its ending for I, you, he and so on. Here are forms from this lesson:

- **Já čtu knihu.** — I am reading a book.
- **Čteš ty romány?** — Do you read novels?
- **Ona sedí a čte.** — She is sitting and reading.
- **Já zpívám se skupinou.** — I am singing with a group.
- **On zpívá písně.** — He is singing songs.
- **Já hodně tančím.** — I dance a lot.

Czech has one present tense, so **čtu** means both "I read" and "I am reading".

## Verbs with "se"

Some verbs always come with the little word **se**:

- **My se díváme na film.** — We are watching a film.
- **Díváš se na seriál?** — Are you watching a series?
- **Vy se učíte jazyk.** — You are learning a language.

## Playing

- **On hraje fotbal.** — He is playing football.
- **My hrajeme šachy.** — We are playing chess.
- **Ona hraje na kytaru.** — She is playing the guitar.

> **Watch out:** for a musical instrument add **na**: **hraje na kytaru**. For a sport or game there is no **na**: **hrajeme šachy**.
