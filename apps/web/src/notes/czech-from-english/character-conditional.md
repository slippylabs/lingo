In this lesson: **character words** (brave, lazy, wise) and the **conditional**: "would" and "if".

## "Would"

Use the past form of the verb (ending in ‑l) with a small word that shows the person:

| who | would |
|---|---|
| I | **bych** |
| you | **bys** |
| he / she / they | **by** |
| we | **bychom** |
| you (several) | **byste** |

## "If"

"If" is **kdyby**, and it takes the same person endings: **kdybych** (if I), **kdybys** (if you):

- **Kdybych byl smělý, tak bych se nebál.** — If I were brave, I would not be afraid.
- **Kdybys nebyl hloupý, tak bys nedělal hlouposti.** — If you were not stupid, you would not do foolish things.
- **Kdyby oni nebyli líní, tak bychom jim pomáhali.** — If they were not lazy, we would help them.
- **Kdybyste nebyli slabí, tak byste pracovali.** — If you were not weak, you would work.

## Describing people

A describing word can stand for a person on its own:

- **Moudrý ví mnoho.** — A smart person knows much.
- **Líný nechce nic dělat.** — Lazy people do not want to do anything.

> **Watch out:** **kdyby** already carries the person, so say **kdybych** for "if I", never *kdyby já*.
