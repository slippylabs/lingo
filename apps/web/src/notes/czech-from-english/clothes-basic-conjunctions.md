In this lesson: **clothes**, and the joining words **a** (and), **ale** (but) and **nebo** (or).

## Joining words

- **Šála je těžká a teplá.** — The scarf is heavy and warm.
- **Ona má černé kalhoty a on bílé.** — She has black trousers, and he has white.
- **Chceš šálu, nebo košili?** — Do you want a scarf or a shirt?
- **V zimě nosím vždy čepici, a v létě ne.** — In winter, I always wear a hat, but not in summer.

A comma goes before **nebo** in a question like this one.

## Wearing

- **On nosí modrou košili.** — He wears a blue shirt.
- **Nosím rukavice v zimě.** — I wear gloves in winter.
- **On si obléká novou košili.** — He is putting on a new shirt.

## Always plural

- **Nové kalhoty jsou pohodlné.** — The new trousers are comfortable.

> **Watch out:** **kalhoty** (trousers) is always plural, so it takes **jsou**: **Kalhoty jsou krásné nebo ošklivé?**
