In this lesson: **north, south, east, west**, and **where** vs **where to**.

## The four directions

- **sever** — north
- **juh** — south
- **východ** — east
- **západ** — west

## Where something is: na + ‑e / ‑u

- **Na severe je sneh.** — In the north there is snow.
- **Žijeme na juhu.** — We live in the south.
- **Slnko vychádza na východe.** — The sun rises in the east.

## Where to: na + the plain word

- **Ideme na juhozápad.** — We are going to the southwest.
- **On cestuje na východ.** — He travels eastward.
- **Pozeráme na sever.** — We are looking to the north.

## Describing words

- **To je severná strana.** — This is the northern side.
- **Západný vietor duje.** — A western wind is blowing.
- **Južné kraje sú teplé.** — The southern regions are warm.

> **Watch out:** the ending tells "where" from "where to": **na severe** (in the north) but **na sever** (to the north).
