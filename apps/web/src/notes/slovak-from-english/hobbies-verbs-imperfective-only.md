In this lesson: **hobbies** and the **present tense** of verbs.

## Verb endings show who

Slovak verbs change their ending for each person. Here is **čítať** (to read):

| | ending | example |
|---|---|---|
| I | **‑m** | **Ja čítam knihu.** |
| you | **‑š** | **Čítaš ty romány?** |
| he / she | — | **Ona sedí a číta.** |

More verbs from this lesson:

- **On hrá futbal.** — He is playing football.
- **My pozeráme film.** — We are watching a film.
- **Oni počúvajú hudbu.** — They are listening to music.
- **On spieva piesne.** — He is singing songs.
- **Ona píše listy.** — She is writing letters.

## Verbs in ‑ovať

Verbs ending in **‑ovať** change to *‑uj‑* in the present:

- **maľovať** — to paint: **Ja maľujem obraz.** — I am painting a picture.
- **tancovať** — to dance: **Ja tancujem veľa** — I dance a lot

## Verbs with sa

Some verbs come with the little word **sa** ("oneself"):

- **Vy sa učíte jazyk.** — You are learning a language.
- **Učíme sa tu.** — We are learning here.

> **Watch out:** Slovak has one present tense for both "I read" and "I am reading": **Ja čítam knihu** means either.
