In this lesson: **pronouns** (I, you, me, us…) and how they **change**.

## Me, you, us

| | as subject | after a little word |
|---|---|---|
| I / me | **ja** | **o mne** |
| you | **ty** | **s tebou**, **o tebe** |
| we / us | **my** | **s nami** |

- **Mňa nevidíš.** — You don't see me.
- **Daj mi vodu.** — Give me water.
- **O mne hovoríš?** — Are you talking about me?
- **Hovorím s tebou.** — I am talking with you.
- **Ja vidím vás.** — I see you.
- **S nami je vždy všetko veselé.** — With us everything is always cheerful.

## Who, whom, what

- **Koho ty vidíš?** — Who do you see?
- **Komu dáš dar?** — Who do you give a gift to?
- **O čom ty hovoríš?** — What are you talking about?

> **Watch out:** "me" has a short form **mi** for "to me" (**Daj mi vodu**) and a stressed form **mňa** for "me" at the front (**Mňa nevidíš**).
