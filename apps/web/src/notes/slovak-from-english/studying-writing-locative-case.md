In this lesson: **school words**, saying **where** something is, and **writing with** something.

## Where: na and v

After **na** ("on") and **v** ("in") the word changes, usually to **‑e** or **‑i**:

- **Kniha je na stole.** — The book is on the table.
- **Píšem na papieri perom.** — I am writing on paper with a pen.
- **Škola je v centre.** — The school is in the center.

## Writing "with" a pen

The tool you use takes the ending **‑om** (masculine and neuter), without any word for "with":

- **Ja rýchlo píšem týmto perom.** — I am writing quickly with this pen.

## School words

- **To je moja škola.** — This is my school.
- **Žiak má knihu.** — The student has a book.
- **My píšeme úlohu.** — We are writing the assignment.
- **Ten predmet je matematika.** — This subject is mathematics.

> **Watch out:** **stôl** loses its *ô* when the ending changes: **na stole**, not *na stôle*.
