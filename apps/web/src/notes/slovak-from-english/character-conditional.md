In this lesson: **character words** and **"would"** (the conditional).

## Character words

- **Silný duch je dôležitý.** — A strong spirit is important.
- **Ona je dobrá a úprimná.** — She is good and sincere.
- **On je slabý v športe.** — He is weak in sports.

A describing word can stand alone for a person:

- **Múdry vie veľa.** — A smart person knows much.
- **Lenivý nechce nič robiť.** — Lazy people do not want to do anything.

## "Would": by + the past form

Put **by** with the past form (**bol**, **boli**…). "I", "you", "we" and "you all" add **som**, **si**, **sme**, **ste**:

- **Ak by som ja bol smelý, tak by som sa nebál.** — If I were brave, I would not be afraid.
- **Ak by si ty nebol hlúpy, tak by si nerobil hlúposti.** — If you were not stupid, you would not do foolish things.
- **Ak by ste vy neboli slabí, tak by ste pracovali.** — If you were not weak, you would work.

**ak by** … **tak** … is "if …, then …".

> **Watch out:** **by** appears in both halves: **Ak by som ja bol smelý, tak by som sa nebál.**
