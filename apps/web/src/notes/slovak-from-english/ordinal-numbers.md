In this lesson: **first, second, third…** and **telling the time**.

## Ordinals are describing words

They take the noun's ending like any adjective:

- **prvý** — first
- **druhý** — second
- **tretí** — third
- **štvrtý** — fourth
- **piaty** — fifth

- **stý dolár** — hundredth dollar
- **štyristo piata hora** — four hundred fifth mountain (**hora** is feminine)

In a long number only the last word is an ordinal:

- **dvesto prvý brat** — two hundred first brother

## Telling the time

Slovak says the time with **o** and the feminine ordinal: "at the fifth (hour)".

- **o piatej hodine** — at the fifth hour (at five)
- **o druhej hodine pätnásť** — at fifteen past the second hour
- **o deviatej hodine** — at the ninth hour

> **Watch out:** **tretí** (third) ends in **‑í**, not **‑ý**.
