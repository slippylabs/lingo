In this lesson: **food**, the verb **"to eat"**, and the form a word takes when it is **the thing you eat or see**.

## "To eat"

- **Ja jem chlieb.** — I am eating bread.
- **Ty ješ vajce?** — Are you eating an egg?
- **Ona je mäso.** — She is eating meat.
- **My jeme večeru.** — We are eating dinner.

## The thing you eat, buy or see

The word for the thing an action is done to takes the accusative case:

- feminine **‑a** becomes **‑u**: **večera** → **My jeme večeru.**
- masculine things and neuter words don't change: **Ja kúpim maslo.**, **On varí obed.**
- a masculine person or animal adds **‑a**: **Ja vidím Vlada s jedlom** — I see Vlad with food

"Your" and "my" follow: **My vidíme tvoju pizzu na stole.** — We see your pizza on the table.

## "That …"

- **Ja viem, že ty ľúbiš vajcia.** — I know that you like eggs.

> **Watch out:** **je** means both "is" and "eats". **Ona je mäso** is "she is eating meat", not "she is meat". **On je sladké jedlo** means "he eats sweet food".
