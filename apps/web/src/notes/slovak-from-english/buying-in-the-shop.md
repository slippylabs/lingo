In this lesson: **shopping**, **prices**, and **"a piece of"**.

## Buying and selling

- **Chcem kúpiť chlieb.** — I want to buy bread.
- **On predáva mäso.** — He sells meat.
- **Ona kupuje knihu.** — She is buying a book.

## "A piece of", "the price of"

The second word takes the "of" form (the genitive):

- **Kus syra je dobrý.** — A piece of cheese is good.
- **Kus chleba je malý.** — The piece of bread is small.
- **Predavač dá kus klobásy.** — The seller gives a piece of sausage.
- **Cena masla je dobrá.** — The price of butter is good.

## Asking

- **Zákazník sa pýta na cenu.** — The customer asks about the price.
- **Zákazník sa pýta na zľavu.** — The customer asks for a discount.

> **Watch out:** "to ask" is **pýtať sa**, with **sa**, and "about" is **na**: **sa pýta na cenu**.
