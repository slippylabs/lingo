In this lesson: **kitchen things**, **"full of"**, and saying **where** in the kitchen.

## Kitchen things

- **Nôž je ostrý.** — The knife is sharp.
- **Vidlička je strieborná.** — The fork is silver.
- **Hrniec je horúci.** — The pot is hot
- **Kuchyňa je čistá.** — The kitchen is clean.

## "Full of" and "a lot of"

After **plný** ("full") and **veľa** ("a lot"), the word takes the "of" form (the genitive):

- **Tanier je plný jedla.** — The plate is full of food.
- **Lyžica je plná korenia.** — The spoon is full of pepper.
- **Veľa cukru je v miske** — There is a lot of sugar in the bowl.

"Filled with" uses the **‑ou** ending instead:

- **Pohár je naplnený vodou.** — The glass is filled with water.

## Where

- **My varíme v hrnci.** — We cook in a pot.
- **Na panvici je mäso.** — There is meat in the frying pan.
- **Ona pije z pohára.** — She drinks from a glass.

> **Watch out:** **plný** changes with the noun like any describing word: **Tanier je plný**, but **Lyžica je plná**.
