In this lesson: a **review of the cases**, the endings a noun takes for different jobs.

## The cases you have met

- "of", "no …": **Brata tu niet.** — There is no brother here.
- "to" someone: **Dám žene.** — I give to the woman.
- the thing you see: **On vidí svoju ženu.** — He sees his wife.
- "with", "by means of": **Hovorím s priateľmi.** — I talk with friends.
- "about", "in": **Ona hovorí o bratoch.** — She talks about brothers.

## "There is no …"

**niet** ("there is no") and **nemá** ("doesn't have") take the "of" form:

- **Kostí tu niet.** — There are no bones here.
- **Mena on nemá.** — He has no name.
- **Už niet banánov v dome.** — There are no more bananas in the house.

## Tools

The tool takes the **‑om** ending:

- **To sa dá rezať nožom.** — This can be cut with a knife.

> **Watch out:** in Slovak the ending, not the word order, shows who does what, so **Ona s mužom hovorí** and *Ona hovorí s mužom* both mean "She talks with her husband".
