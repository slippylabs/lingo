In this lesson: **places in town**, and when to say **v** or **na**.

## Buildings: v

- **Kupujeme chlieb v obchode.** — We buy bread in the shop.
- **Čítam knihu v knižnici.** — I read a book in the library.
- **To je môj dom v meste.** — This is my house in the city.

## Open places: na

- **Dom stojí na ulici.** — The house stands on the street.
- **Na námestí stojí fontána.** — A fountain stands in the square.
- **Na trhovisku je veľa ľudí.** — There are many people at the market.

## Going and near

- **Ideme na námestie.** — We are going to the square.
- **Stanica je blízko centra.** — The station is near the center.
- **Most ide cez rieku.** — The bridge goes over the river.

> **Watch out:** a square, street or market takes **na**, even where English says "in": **na námestí** — in the square.
