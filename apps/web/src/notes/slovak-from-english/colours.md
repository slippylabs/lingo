In this lesson: **colours**, and how a colour word **agrees** with the noun.

## Three endings

| | masculine | feminine | neuter / plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| ending | **‑y / ‑ý** | **‑a / ‑á** | **‑e / ‑é** |
| example | **čierny** | **biela** | **modré** |

- **Môj pes je čierny.** — My dog is black.
- **Myš je biela.** — The mouse is white.
- **Nebo je modré.** — The sky is blue.
- **Kvety sú červené.** — The flowers are red.
- **Oblaky sú sivé.** — The clouds are grey.

## Before a feminine thing you have or wear

When a feminine noun is the thing someone wears, has or likes, the colour ends in **‑ú**:

- **Dievča nosí bielu sukňu.** — The girl is wearing a white skirt.
- **On má oranžovú tašku.** — He has an orange bag.
- **Ja ľúbim červenú farbu.** — I like the red color.

> **Watch out:** the colour copies the noun's ending, so the same colour looks different: **Jeho auto je čierne**, **Môj pes je čierny**.
