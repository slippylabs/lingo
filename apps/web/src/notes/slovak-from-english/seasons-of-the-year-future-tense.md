In this lesson: **the seasons** and the **future tense**.

## The future with bude

Use **budem**, **budeš**, **bude**, **budeme**, **budete**, **budú** plus a verb's dictionary form:

- **V zime bude padať sneh.** — In the winter it will snow.
- **Na jeseň budeme vidieť padať lístie.** — In the autumn we will see falling leaves.
- **V zime sa budete hrať so snehom.** — In the winter you will play with snow.
- **Oni budú vidieť zelenú krajinu.** — They will see a green landscape.

On its own, **bude** means "will be":

- **V lete bude teplo.** — In the summer it will be warm.

## Some verbs are already future

A few verbs mean a single, finished action, so their present form already means the future:

- **Jar prinesie kvety.** — Spring will bring flowers.
- **Leto sa čoskoro skončí.** — Summer will soon end.

> **Watch out:** it's **v lete** and **v zime**, but **na jar** and **na jeseň**.
