In this lesson: the verb **"to be"**, **masculine, feminine and neuter** words, and **this / that**.

## "To be"

- **Ja som múdry.** — I am wise.
- **Kto si ty?** — Who are you?
- **To je moja sestra.** — This is my sister.
- **My sme veľmi múdri** — We are very wise
- **Vy ste dobrí.** — You are good.
- **To sú priatelia.** — These are friends.

## Every noun has a gender

Slovak sorts nouns into three groups, called genders. You can usually tell from the ending:

| gender | usual ending | example |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | a consonant | **dom**, **muž** |
| feminine | **‑a** | **žena**, **sestra** |
| neuter | **‑o** or **‑e** | **mesto**, **auto** |

A describing word (an adjective) takes a matching ending:

- **Ten muž je milý.** — That man is kind.
- **Tá žena je dobrá.** — This woman is good.
- **To je dobré auto?** — Is this a good car?

## This and that

**ten** (masculine), **tá** (feminine) and **to** (neuter) can mean "this" or "that". For something further away, add *tam‑* at the front:

- **Tamten dom je krásny.** — That house over there is beautiful.
- **Tamto ráno je chladné.** — That morning over there is cold.

## "Not"

Put **nie** before **je**:

- **To nie je moja sestra.** — This is not my sister.

> **Watch out:** "they" has two words. **oni** is for a group with at least one man (**Oni sú veľmi milí**); **one** is for a group of only women or things (**One sú veľmi milé**).
