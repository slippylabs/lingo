In this lesson: **asking the way** and giving **directions**.

## Where? Which way?

- **Kde je stanica?** — Where is the station?
- **Kde je najbližší hotel?** — Where is the nearest hotel?
- **Kade ide cesta?** — Which way does the road go?

## Directions

- **Ideme vpravo.** — We are going to the right.
- **Potom vľavo.** — Then to the left.
- **Choď rovno k mostu.** — Go straight to the bridge.
- **Stanica je vľavo.** — The station is on the left.

## Near and far

**blízko** (near) and **ďaleko od** (far from) take the "of" form:

- **Obchod je blízko knižnice.** — The shop is near the library.
- **Námestie je ďaleko od hotela.** — The square is far from the hotel.

## Asking politely

- **Môžete ukázať cestu?** — Can you show me the way?

> **Watch out:** **kde** asks where something is; **kade** asks which way to go.
