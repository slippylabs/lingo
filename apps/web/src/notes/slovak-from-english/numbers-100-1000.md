In this lesson: **hundreds** and **a thousand**.

## The hundreds

Each is one word: the number plus **‑sto**:

- **sto** — one hundred
- **dvesto** — two hundred
- **tristo** — three hundred
- **päťsto** — five hundred
- **deväťsto** — nine hundred
- **tisíc** — thousand

## The noun after them

- **dvesto žien** — two hundred women
- **tristo polí** — three hundred fields
- **šesťsto mačiek** — six hundred cats
- **osemsto chlapcov** — eight hundred boys

## Thousands

- **dvetisíc deväťsto psov** — two thousand nine hundred dogs

> **Watch out:** two hundred is **dvesto**, with **dve**, and two thousand is **dvetisíc**.
