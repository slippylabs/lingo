In this lesson: **shapes**, **"of"**, and the numbers **three** and **four**.

## Shapes

- **To je kruh.** — This is a circle.
- **Štvorec má štyri strany.** — A square has four sides.
- **Trojuholník má tri strany.** — A triangle has three sides.
- **Línia je rovná.** — The line is straight.

## "Of": the outline of a square

"Of" is shown by the ending. Masculine words add **‑a**:

- **Vidíme obrys štvorca.** — We see the outline of a square.
- **Stôl má tvar štvorca.** — The table has the shape of a square.

## Two words for "shape"

- **To je jednoduchý tvar.** — This is a simple shape. (**tvar** is masculine)
- **Forma je zaujímavá.** — The shape is interesting. (**forma** is feminine)

> **Watch out:** after **tri** and **štyri**, use the normal plural: **štyri strany**, not the "of" form.
