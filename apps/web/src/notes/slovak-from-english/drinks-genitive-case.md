In this lesson: **drinks**, **"without"**, and **"a bottle of / a glass of"**.

## "Without" and "of"

After **bez** ("without"), and after a container like a bottle or a glass, the word takes the genitive case. Feminine **‑a** becomes **‑y**, neuter **‑o** becomes **‑a**:

- **Piješ bez mlieka?** — Do you drink without milk?
- **Ja ľúbim džús bez cukru.** — I like juice without sugar.
- **Fľaša vody leží na stole.** — A bottle of water is lying on the table.
- **Pohár vína pre mňa, prosím.** — A glass of wine for me, please.
- **Ty máš fľašku džúsu?** — Do you have a bottle of juice?

**z** ("from") takes the same form:

- **To víno je z Poľska.** — This wine is from Poland.

## "To drink"

- **Ty piješ víno?** — Do you drink wine?
- **Ona nepije čaj.** — She does not drink tea.
- **My nepijeme pivo.** — We do not drink beer.

## Polite requests

- **Dajte mi pohár vína, prosím.** — Give me a glass of wine, please.

> **Watch out:** **piť** (to drink) adds a *j*: **piješ**, **pije**, **pijeme**.
