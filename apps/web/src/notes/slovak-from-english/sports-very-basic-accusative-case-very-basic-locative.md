In this lesson: **sports**, saying **what** you play, and saying **where**.

## What you play

The sport just follows the verb, in its dictionary form:

- **Hrám futbal.** — I play football.
- **Ona hrá basketbal.** — She plays basketball.
- **On hrá tenis.** — He plays tennis.

## Saying "not"

*ne‑* is written together with the verb:

- **Ja nehrám hokej.** — I do not play hockey.

## Where: v and na

After **v** ("in") and **na** ("on, at") a word takes a new ending, usually **‑e** or **‑i**. This is the locative case.

- **My plávame v rieke.** — We swim in the river.
- **Štadión je v meste.** — The stadium is in the city.
- **Ona hrá na štadióne.** — She plays at the stadium.

## More sport phrases

- **On trénuje každý deň.** — He trains every day.
- **Oni behajú ráno.** — They run in the morning.
- **Treba trénovať.** — It is necessary to train.

> **Watch out:** *ne‑* is never a separate word in front of a verb: write **nehrám**, not *ne hrám*.
