In this lesson: **parts of the body** and saying **something hurts**.

## The body

- **Hlava je na tele.** — The head is on the body.
- **Jej srdce bije rýchlo.** — Her heart beats fast.
- **Jeho mozog je rýchly.** — His brain is quick.
- **Ona má dlhú nohu.** — She has a long leg.

## It hurts

The body part is the subject, and "me" is **ma**:

- **Moja ruka bolí.** — My hand hurts.
- **Žalúdok ma bolí.** — My stomach hurts.

## Two of them

- **Ja mám dve ruky a dve nohy a tiež dve chodidlá.** — I have two hands and two legs, and also two feet.

> **Watch out:** **oko** (eye) has an irregular plural, **oči**: **On má modré krásne oči.**
