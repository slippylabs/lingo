In this lesson: **animals**, and the form for **giving to** or **helping** someone.

## "To" someone

The one who receives something takes the dative case:

| word type | ending | example |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | **‑ovi** | **psovi**, **koňovi** |
| feminine | **‑e** | **mačke**, **žabe** |
| plural | **‑om** | **vtákom** |

- **Ja dám mačke rybu.** — I give the cat a fish.
- **Dávame koňovi vodu.** — We give the horse water.
- **My dávame žabe chlieb.** — We give bread to the frog.
- **Ona dá psovi dobré meno.** — She gives the dog a good name.

## Helping

**pomáhať** (to help) also takes this form:

- **On pomáha medveďovi.** — He helps the bear.
- **Pomáhame vlkovi.** — We help the wolf.
- **My pomáhame vtákom.** — We help the birds.

## "To me"

- **Ty dáš mne rybu?** — Will you give me a fish?

> **Watch out:** a few words have their own form: **myš** → **myši** (**On dá myši syr.**) and **zviera** → **zvieraťu**.
