In this lesson: **rooms and furniture**, and words for **where things are**.

## Furniture

- **Posteľ je mäkká.** — The bed is soft.
- **Okno je otvorené.** — The window is open.
- **Skriňa je plná.** — The wardrobe is full.
- **Polica drží knihy.** — The shelf holds books.

## Where things are

Each little position word changes the ending of the word after it:

- **Na stole leží kniha.** — A book is lying on the table.
- **Polica visí na stene.** — The shelf hangs on the wall.
- **V izbe je pokojne.** — It is quiet in the room.
- **Lampa svieti nad stolom.** — The lamp shines above the table.
- **Truhlica je pod posteľou.** — The chest is under the bed.
- **Stolička je blízko stola.** — The chair is near the table.

## In the middle

- **Stôl stojí v strede izby.** — The table stands in the middle of the room.

> **Watch out:** **dvere** (door) is always plural, even for one door: **Dvere sú zatvorené.**, **Dvere vedú do kuchyne.**
