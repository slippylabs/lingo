In this lesson: **greetings**, **thank you** and short **everyday replies**.

## Greetings by time of day

- **Dobré ráno!** — Good morning!
- **Dobrý deň!** — Good day!
- **Dobrý večer!** — Good evening!
- **Dobrú noc!** — Good night!
- **Zdravím!** — Hello!

## Getting to know someone

- **Ako sa voláš?** — What is your name?
- **Volám sa Bartek.** — My name is Bartek.
- **Ako si?** — How are you?
- **A ty?** — And you?

## Thanks and sorry

- **Ďakujem ti veľmi pekne.** — Thank you very much.
- **Pardon, nerozumiem.** — Excuse me, I don't understand.
- **prepáč** — excuse me

## Short replies

- **Áno, je to možné.** — Yes, it is possible.
- **Neviem.** — I don't know.
- **Aj ja.** — Me too.
- **Škoda!** — What a pity!
- **Poďme!** — Let's go!

> **Watch out:** it's **Dobrú noc**, not *Dobrá noc*: you are wishing someone a good night, and that changes the ending to **‑ú**.
