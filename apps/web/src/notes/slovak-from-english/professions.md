In this lesson: **jobs**, and saying what someone **does for a living**.

## Jobs

- **Robotník pracuje na moste.** — The worker is working on the bridge.
- **Predavač pracuje v obchode.** — The seller works in the shop.
- **Doktor pomáha ľuďom.** — The doctor helps people.
- **Kuchár varí večeru.** — The cook is preparing dinner.
- **Umelec kreslí portrét.** — The artist is drawing a portrait.

## "He is a teacher"

With a job after **je**, this course uses the **‑om** ending for men and **‑ou** for women:

- **Môj brat je učiteľom.** — My brother is a teacher.
- **Môj otec je kuchárom.** — My father is a cook.
- **Ona je doktorkou.** — She is a doctor.

## Women's job names

Many end in **‑ka**:

- **doktor** → **doktorkou** — (she is) a doctor
- **učiteľ** → **učiteľka** — teacher

> **Watch out:** Slovak has no word for "a" or "the": **Šofér vedie autobus** is "The driver drives a bus".
