In this lesson: **vehicles**, **the road**, and the verb for **going by vehicle**.

## Going by vehicle

**jazdiť** is used for vehicles that go somewhere regularly:

- **Autobus jazdí do centra.** — The bus goes to the center.
- **Vlak jazdí na stanicu.** — The train goes to the station.
- **Šofér jazdí rýchlo.** — The driver drives fast.

## Describing vehicles and roads

- **Auto je nové a krásne.** — The car is new and beautiful.
- **Bicykel je rozbitý.** — The bicycle is broken.
- **Cesta je široká.** — The road is wide.
- **Šofér je opatrný.** — The driver is careful.

## Traffic

- **Semafor ukazuje červenú.** — The traffic light shows red.
- **Rýchlosť je obmedzená.** — The speed is limited.

> **Watch out:** "two" is **dve** for feminine and neuter words: **Most spája dve mestá.** (**mesto** is neuter.)
