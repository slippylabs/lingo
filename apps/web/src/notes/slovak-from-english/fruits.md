In this lesson: **fruit**, **colours and tastes**, and making describing words **agree**.

## The describing word matches the fruit

An adjective takes the ending of the noun's gender:

- **Banán je žltý.** — The banana is yellow. (masculine, **‑ý**)
- **Hruška je chutná.** — The pear is tasty. (feminine, **‑á**)
- **Jablko je červené.** — The apple is red. (neuter, **‑é**)
- **Pomaranče sú sladké.** — The oranges are sweet. (plural, **‑é**)

## More than one

- **Jahody sú v miske.** — The strawberries are in the bowl.
- **Hrušky ležia na stole.** — The pears are lying on the table.

## Growing and buying

- **Višňa rastie na strome.** — The cherry grows on a tree.
- **My kupujeme hrozno.** — We are buying grapes.
- **Slivka je už zrelá.** — The plum is already ripe.

> **Watch out:** the ending follows the fruit, not the colour: **Citrón je kyslý** (masculine) but **Višňa je kyslá** (feminine).
