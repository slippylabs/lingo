In this lesson: **feelings**, and **adverbs**, the words that say **how** something is done.

## Feelings

- **On cíti hanbu.** — He feels shame.
- **My cítime pokoj.** — We feel peaceful.
- **Ja mám žiaľ v srdci.** — I have sadness in my heart.
- **Hanba je veľmi silná.** — Shame is very strong.

## How: ‑e or ‑o

An adverb is made from a describing word by changing the ending to **‑e** or **‑o**:

- **radostný** → **Cítim sa radostne dnes.** — I feel joyful today.
- **veselý** → **Ona im ukazuje svoju prácu veselo.** — She shows them her work joyfully.
- **hrdý** → **On skrýva hrdo svoj hnev.** — He hides his anger proudly.
- **prestrašený** → **Prestrašene on utekal.** — He was frightened and ran away.

## Feeling with cítiť sa

- **Cítim sa radostne dnes.** — I feel joyful today.

> **Watch out:** an adverb never changes its ending for the person: **Radostne šiel domov**, whoever is going.
