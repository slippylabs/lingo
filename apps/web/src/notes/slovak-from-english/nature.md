In this lesson: **nature words** and the little words for **into, through, between**.

## Nature

- **Hora je vysoká.** — The mountain is high.
- **Tráva je zelená.** — The grass is green.
- **Pole je široké.** — The field is wide.
- **Kvety krásne kvitnú.** — The flowers bloom beautifully.

## Going somewhere

- **My ideme do lesa.** — We are going to the forest.
- **Rieka tečie cez dolinu.** — The river flows through the valley.
- **Ideme k dubu.** — We are going towards the oak.

## Where something is

- **On stojí na kameni.** — He is standing on the stone.
- **Ruža rastie na poli.** — The rose grows in the field.
- **Dolina leží medzi horami.** — The valley lies between the mountains.
- **Pole je za domom.** — The field is behind the house.

> **Watch out:** each little word fixes the ending after it: **do lesa** (into), **cez les** (through), **medzi horami** (between). Learn them as pairs.
