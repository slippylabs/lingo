In this lesson: **nationalities**, **countries** and **languages**.

## People

- **Ja som Poliak.** — I am a Pole.
- **On je Rus.** — He is a Russian.
- **Ona je Chorvátka.** — She is a Croat.
- **Si ty Angličan?** — Are you an Englishman?

A woman's nationality usually ends in **‑ka**: **Chorvát** → **Chorvátka**.

## Countries

- **Štát je Poľsko.** — The country is Poland.
- **Nemec žije v Nemecku.** — A German lives in Germany.

## Languages

- **Angličan hovorí anglickým jazykom.** — An Englishman speaks the English language.
- **Hovoríš nemeckým jazykom?** — Do you speak the German language?
- **My sa učíme ruský jazyk.** — We are learning the Russian language.
- **On vie tri jazyky.** — He knows three languages.

> **Watch out:** people take a capital letter (**Nemec**, **Angličan**), but the describing word for a language does not (**nemeckým jazykom**, **ruský jazyk**).
