In this lesson: **tens up to 100**.

## Two endings for the tens

20, 30 and 40 end in *‑dsať*; from 50 they end in *‑desiat*:

- **dvadsať** — twenty
- **tridsať** — thirty
- **štyridsať** — forty
- **päťdesiat** — fifty
- **šesťdesiat** — sixty
- **deväťdesiat** — ninety
- **sto** — one hundred

## The noun after them

Tens take the "of" form:

- **dvadsať žien** — twenty women
- **tridsať psov** — thirty dogs
- **štyridsať mužov** — forty men
- **osemdesiat chlapcov** — eighty boys

## Tens and units

- **sedemdesiat osem rubľov** — seventy eight rubles
- **osemdesiat sedem dolárov** — eighty seven dollars

> **Watch out:** it's **štyridsať** (*‑dsať*) but **päťdesiat** (*‑desiat*). The switch happens at fifty.
