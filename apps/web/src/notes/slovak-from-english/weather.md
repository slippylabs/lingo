In this lesson: **weather** and sentences with **no "it"**.

## No word for "it"

Slovak weather sentences have no subject at all:

- **Dnes je teplo.** — It is warm today.
- **V Rusku je chladno.** — In Russia it is cold.
- **Dnes je oblačno.** — It is cloudy today.

## Rain falls, the sun shines

- **Padá dážď.** — It is raining.
- **Padá sneh a dážď.** — Snow and rain are falling.
- **Slnko svieti.** — The sun is shining.
- **Vietor duje silno.** — The wind is blowing strongly.

## Questions

- **Je vietor silný?** — Is the wind strong?

## Changing

- **Počasie sa mení.** — The weather is changing.

> **Watch out:** don't add **to** for "it": say **Dnes je teplo**, not *To je teplo dnes*.
