In this lesson: **time words** and the **past tense**.

## The past ends in ‑l

The past form ends in **‑l** and changes with the person's gender:

- **Včera bol pondelok.** — Yesterday was Monday.
- **Včera ona bola doma o ôsmej hodine.** — Yesterday she was at home at the eighth hour.
- **Ráno nebolo veľmi teplo.** — In the morning it was not very warm.
- **Oni nič nerobili.** — They did nothing.

## I and you add som, si

For "I", "you", "we" and "you all", add **som**, **si**, **sme**, **ste**:

- **Ja som bol doma ráno.** — I was at home in the morning.
- **Dnes si na to nemal čas.** — Today you did not have time for that.

For he, she and they, nothing is added: **On išiel domov.** — He went home.

## Time questions

- **Koľko je hodín?** — What time is it?
- **Koľko minút trvá film?** — How many minutes does the film last?

> **Watch out:** never add **je** in the past: **On išiel domov**, not *On je išiel*.
