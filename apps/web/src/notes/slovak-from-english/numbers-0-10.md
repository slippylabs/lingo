In this lesson: **numbers 0 to 10**, and how the noun **changes after a number**.

## One and two have genders

- **jeden chlapec** — one boy
- **jedna žena** — one woman
- **jedno pole** — one field
- **dva chlapci** — two boys
- **dve ženy** — two women

**dva** is for men; **dve** is for women and things: **dve veci** — two things.

## 2, 3 and 4: the plural

- **tri mačky** — three cats
- **štyri bicykle** — four bicycles
- **štyri doláre** — four dollars

## 5 and up: the "of" form

From **päť** the noun takes the "of" form (genitive plural):

- **päť chlapcov** — five boys
- **päť mačiek** — five cats
- **šesť bicyklov** — six bicycles
- **desať hodiniek** — ten clocks

> **Watch out:** the noun changes at five: **štyri bicykle** but **šesť bicyklov**.
