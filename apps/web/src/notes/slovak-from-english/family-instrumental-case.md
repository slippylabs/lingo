In this lesson: **family words** and how to say **"with"** someone.

## The family

- **matka** — mother
- **otec** — father
- **brat** — brother
- **sestra** — sister
- **syn** — son
- **dcéra** — daughter

## "With" changes the ending

After **s** ("with"), a word takes a new ending. This form is called the instrumental case.

| word type | ending | example |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | **‑om** | **s vnukom** |
| feminine | **‑ou** | **s matkou** |
| plural | **‑mi** | **s bratmi** |

- **Ona ide s matkou.** — She is going with her mother.
- **On hovorí s dedom.** — He is talking with his grandfather.
- **Idem do parku s rodinou.** — I am going to the park with the family.
- **Ideme na výlet s bratmi.** — We are going on a trip with our brothers.

"My" and "your" change too:

- **Hovorím s tvojou sestrou.** — I am talking with your sister.
- **Ona hovorí s mojou babkou.** — She is talking with my grandmother.

## Asking "with whom?"

- **S kým ty ideš?** — Who are you going with?

> **Watch out:** before a word starting with **s** or a cluster of consonants, **s** becomes **so**: **On žije so synom**, **so strýcom**.
