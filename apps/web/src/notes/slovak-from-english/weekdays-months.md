In this lesson: **days of the week**, **months**, and **"on Monday" / "in May"**.

## Days

- **pondelok** — Monday
- **utorok** — Tuesday
- **streda** — Wednesday
- **štvrtok** — Thursday
- **piatok** — Friday
- **sobota** — Saturday
- **nedeľa** — Sunday

## "On" a day: v + the thing form

- **V stredu mám úlohu.** — On Wednesday, I have an assignment.
- **V piatok večer ideme na film.** — On Friday evening we are going to the movies.

## "In" a month: v + ‑i

- **Mám v januári prázdniny.** — I have vacation in January.
- **V auguste idem do Hondurasu.** — In August, I am going to Honduras.
- **Vo februári je veľa snehu.** — In February there is a lot of snow.

> **Watch out:** days and months start with a small letter in Slovak: **pondelok**, **január**.
