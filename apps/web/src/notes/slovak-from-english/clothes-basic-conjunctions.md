In this lesson: **clothes**, and the joining words **a** (and) and **alebo** (or).

## Wearing clothes

- **On nosí modrú košeľu.** — He wears a blue shirt.
- **Nosím rukavice v zime.** — I wear gloves in winter.
- **On si oblieka novú košeľu.** — He is putting on a new shirt.

## And, or

- **Šál je ťažký a teplý.** — The scarf is heavy and warm.
- **Ty chceš šál alebo košeľu.** — You want a scarf or a shirt.

**a** can also contrast two things, where English would say "but":

- **V zime nosím vždy čiapku, a v lete nie.** — In winter, I always wear a hat, but not in summer.

## Plural-only clothes

- **Moje ponožky sú krátke.** — My socks are short.
- **Nové oblečenie je drahé.** — New clothes are expensive.

> **Watch out:** **nohavice** (trousers) and **nohavičky** (underwear) are always plural: **Nové nohavičky sú pohodlné.**
