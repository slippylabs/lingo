In this lesson: **rooms and furniture**, and words for **where** things are.

## Furniture

- **stol** — table
- **stul** — chair
- **postelj** — bed
- **šafa** — wardrobe
- **polica** — shelf
- **lampa** — lamp
- **okno** — window
- **dveri** — doors

## Where things are

These little words show position. Each one changes the noun after it:

- **Stul jest blizko stola.** — The chair is near the table.
- **Skrinja jest pod postelju.** — The chest is under the bed.
- **Lampa světi nad stolom.** — The lamp shines above the table.
- **Polica visi na stěně.** — The shelf hangs on the wall.
- **Stol stoji v sredině pokoja.** — The table stands in the middle of the room.

## Describing a room

- **Pokoj jest teply i tihy.** — The room is warm and quiet.
- **Okno jest otkryto.** — The window is open.
- **Dveri sut zakryte.** — The doors are closed.

> **Watch out:** **dveri** — doors is always plural, so it takes **sut**: **Dveri sut zakryte.**
