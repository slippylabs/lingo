In this lesson: **fruit**, and describing words that **match** the noun.

## Fruit

- **jabloko** — apple
- **gruška** — pear
- **banan** — banana
- **pomaranča** — orange
- **višnja** — cherry
- **sliva** — plum
- **limon** — lemon

## Describing words match

A describing word (an adjective) changes its ending to match the noun's gender:

- **‑y** for masculine: **Banan jest žolty.** — The banana is yellow.
- **‑a** for feminine: **Gruška jest vkusna.** — The pear is tasty.
- **‑o** for neuter: **Jabloko jest črveno.** — The apple is red.

More than one thing usually takes **‑e**:

- **Pomaranče sut sladke.** — The oranges are sweet.

## More sentences

- **Melon jest veliky i sladky.** — The melon is big and sweet.
- **Sliva jest uže dozrěla.** — The plum is already ripe.
- **Ona pije sok iz pomaranča.** — She is drinking orange juice.

> **Watch out:** **jest** is "is" and **sut** is "are": **Višnja jest kysla** but **Jagody sut v miskě**.
