In this lesson: **feelings**, and turning a describing word into **how** something is done.

## Feelings

- **radost** — joy
- **žalost** — sadness
- **strah** — fear
- **spokoj** — peace
- **zavist** — envy
- **styd** — shame
- **ljubov** — love

- **Ja imam žalost v srci.** — I have sadness in my heart.
- **My čujemo spokoj.** — We feel peaceful.
- **Strah jest v nas.** — Fear is inside us.

## How: ‑y becomes ‑o

To say **how** someone does something, change the **‑y** ending of a describing word to **‑o**. This makes an adverb:

- **vesely** → **veselo**: **Ona pokazuje im svoju rabotu veselo.** — She shows them her work joyfully.
- **gordy** → **gordo**: **On skryva gordo svoj gněv.** — He hides his anger proudly.
- **radostny** → **radostno**: **Radostno šel do doma.** — He joyfully went home.
- **žalostny** → **žalostno**: **Žalostno on govoril.** — He spoke sadly.

## "Is" with to

**to** can stand in for "is" when you say what something is:

- **Ljubov to silna emocija.** — Love is a strong emotion.
- **Strah to naturalna reakcija.** — Fear is a natural reaction.

> **Watch out:** **čuti** — to feel uses **se** for how you feel: **Čujem se radostno tutdenj.**
