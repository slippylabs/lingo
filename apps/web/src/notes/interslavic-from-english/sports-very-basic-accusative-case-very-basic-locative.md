In this lesson: **sports**, **playing a game**, and saying **where** something happens.

## Playing a sport

To say you play a game, use **igrati**, then **v** and the name of the game:

- **Igram v futbol.** — I play football.
- **Ona igra v basketbol.** — She plays basketball.
- **Ja ne igram v hokej.** — I do not play hockey.
- **Kto igra v tenis?** — Who plays tennis?

## Where: the place form

After **v** ("in") and **na** ("on, at") meaning a place, a noun takes a different ending. This is called the locative case. Many nouns end in **‑u** or **‑ě**:

- **Stadion jest v městu.** — The stadium is in the city.
- **Ona igra na stadionu.** — She plays at the stadium.
- **My plavamo v rěkě.** — We swim in the river.

## Other sport words

- **On trenuje každy denj.** — He trains every day.
- **Oni běgajut utrom.** — They run in the morning.
- **Plavanje jest dobro za zdravje.** — Swimming is good for health.

> **Watch out:** **v** + the name of a game means "play it", but **v** + a place means "in it": **v tenis** but **v městu**.
