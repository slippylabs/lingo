In this lesson: **hundreds** and **thousand**.

## The hundreds

Most hundreds are a number plus **‑sto**:

- **sto** — one hundred
- **dvěsto** — two hundred
- **tristo** — three hundred
- **četyristo** — four hundred
- **petsto** — five hundred
- **šeststo** — six hundred
- **sedmsto** — seven hundred
- **osmsto** — eight hundred
- **devetsto** — nine hundred
- **tyseč** — thousand

## The noun after them

Like the tens, the hundreds take the "of many" form:

- **sto hlapcev** — one hundred boys
- **dvěsto žen** — two hundred women
- **šeststo kotov** — six hundred cats
- **osmsto dolarov** — eight hundred dollars

## Bigger numbers

Put the parts in order, biggest first:

- **tyseč osm rubljev** — one thousand eight rubles
- **četyristo sedm dolarov** — four hundred seven dollars

> **Watch out:** two hundred is **dvěsto**, with the feminine-looking **dvě**, not *dvasto*.
