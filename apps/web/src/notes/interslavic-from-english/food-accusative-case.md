In this lesson: **food words** and the form a noun takes when it is **the thing you eat, buy or see**.

## The object form

The thing a verb acts on is called the object. In this form (the accusative case), feminine words in **‑a** change to **‑u**:

- **večerja** → **My jemo večerju.** — We are eating dinner.
- **kniga** → **Čitaš ty knigu i ješ zautraku?** — Are you reading a book and eating breakfast?
- **bljudo** stays: **Ona nese bljudo.** — She is carrying a dish.

Masculine words for things don't change:

- **Ja jem hlěb.** — I am eating bread.
- **On gotovi oběd.** — He is preparing lunch.

Masculine words for people and animals take **‑a**:

- **Ja vidim Vlada s jedoju** — I see Vlad with food

## To eat

**jesti** — to eat is irregular:

- **Ja jem hlěb.** — I am eating bread.
- **Ty ješ jajce?** — Are you eating an egg?
- **Ona je meso.** — She is eating meat.
- **My jemo večerju.** — We are eating dinner.

> **Watch out:** **Ona je meso** means "She is eating meat". Here **je** is the verb "eats", not "is".
