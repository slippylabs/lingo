In this lesson: **school and writing**, and saying **where** things are.

## School words

- **škola** — school
- **učitelj** — male teacher
- **učiteljka** — female teacher
- **učenik** — student
- **kniga** — book
- **pero** — pen
- **zadanje** — assignment

## Where: the place form

After **v** — in and **na** — on, nouns take the place form (the locative case). Masculine nouns often end in **‑u**, and others in **‑ě**:

- **Kniga jest na stolu.** — The book is on the table.
- **Škola jest v centru.** — The school is in the center.
- **Pišu na papirě perom.** — I am writing on paper with a pen.

## Writing "with" something

To say what you write with, use the "with" form on its own:

- **Pišu na papirě perom.** — I am writing on paper with a pen.

Or with **s**:

- **Ja brzo pišem s tym perom.** — I am writing quickly with this pen.

## Saying someone's job

- **On jest učenikom.** — He is a student.
- **Ona jest učiteljkoju.** — She is a teacher.

> **Watch out:** a female teacher is **učiteljka**, not **učitelj**: **Ona jest učiteljkoju.**
