In this lesson: a **review of the noun forms** (the cases) you have met so far.

## The forms side by side

Each form answers a different question. Here they are with **žena** — woman and **brat** — brother:

| form | used for | example |
|---|---|---|
| object | the thing you see or have | **On vidi svoju ženu.** |
| "of" | not having, "no ..." | **Brata tu nema.** |
| "to" | giving to someone | **Dam ženě.** |
| "with" | with someone, using something | **Ona s mužem govori.** |
| place | in, on, about | **Ona govori o bratah.** |

## Examples

- **On vidi svoju ženu.** — He sees his wife.
- **Brata tu nema.** — There is no brother here.
- **Dam ženě.** — I give to the woman.
- **To možno rězati nožem.** — This can be cut with a knife.
- **Ona jest v gradu.** — She is in the city.

## More than one

- **Govorim s prijateljami.** — I talk with friends.
- **Ona daje dar bratam.** — She gives a gift to the brothers.
- **V gradah uvidiš mnogo ljudij.** — In the cities you will see many people.

> **Watch out:** **nema** — there is no takes the "of" form: **Kosti tu nema**, **Imene on nema**.
