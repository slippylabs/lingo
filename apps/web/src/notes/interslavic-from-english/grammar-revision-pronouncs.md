In this lesson: **I, you, we** and the other pronouns in their **different forms**.

## Pronouns change too

Like nouns, pronouns change with their job in the sentence:

| | object | "to" | "with" | place |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I / me | **mene** | **mně** | | **o mně** |
| you | **tebe** | **tobě** | **toboju** | **o tobě** |
| we / us | **nas** | | **nami** | |

- **Mene ne vidiš.** — You don't see me.
- **Daj mně vodu.** — Give me water.
- **Govorim s toboju.** — I am talking with you.
- **S nami vsegda vse veselo.** — With us everything is always cheerful.
- **O mně govoriš?** — Are you talking about me?

## Question words

- **Kogo ty vidiš?** — Who do you see?
- **Komu daš dar?** — Who do you give a gift to?
- **O čem ty govoriš?** — What are you talking about?

## Oneself

*sebe*, **sobě**, **soboju** point back to the subject:

- **On ljubi byti sam s soboju.** — He likes to be alone with himself.

> **Watch out:** **mene** is "me" as the object, but **mně** is "to me": **Mene ne vidiš** but **Daj mně vodu**.
