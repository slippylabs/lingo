In this lesson: **animals** and the **"to / for" form** of a noun, used for the one who **receives** something.

## Animals

- **kot** — cat
- **pes** — dog
- **konj** — horse
- **ptica** — bird
- **ryba** — fish
- **medvěd** — bear
- **myš** — mouse

## The "to" form

When you give something **to** someone, or help someone, that person or animal takes the dative case:

| | ending | example |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | **‑u** | **kotu**, **konju**, **psu** |
| feminine | **‑ě** | **žabě**, **rybě** |

- **Ja dam kotu rybu.** — I give the cat a fish.
- **Dajemo konju vodu.** — We give the horse water.
- **My dajemo žabě hlěb.** — We give bread to the frog.
- **On pomaga medvědu.** — He helps the bear.

The thing you give stays in the object form: **rybu**, **vodu**.

## To me, to him

- **Ty daš mně rybu?** — Will you give me a fish?
- **Ona pomogla jemu s zvěrom.** — She helped him with the animal.

> **Watch out:** **pes** loses its *e* before an ending: **psu**, not *pesu*.
