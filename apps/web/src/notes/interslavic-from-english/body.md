In this lesson: **parts of the body**, and saying **something hurts**.

## The body

- **glava** — head
- **ruka** — hand
- **noga** — leg
- **stopa** — foot
- **oko** — eye
- **uho** — ear
- **nos** — nose
- **lice** — face
- **srdce** — heart

## Two of them

"Eyes" has its own form: **oko** → **oči**.

- **Ja imam dvě ruki i dvě nogi i tož dvě stopy.** — I have two hands and two legs, and also two feet.
- **On ima modre krasne oči.** — He has beautiful blue eyes.

## Something hurts

The body part is the subject, and the verb is **boli**:

- **Moja ruka boli.** — My hand hurts.
- **Želudok tobě boli.** — Your stomach hurts.

## Seeing and hearing

- **Uho slyši muzyku.** — The ear hears music.
- **Oči gledajut v knigu.** — The eyes are looking at the book.

> **Watch out:** "eye" is **oko** but "eyes" is **oči**, a completely different form.
