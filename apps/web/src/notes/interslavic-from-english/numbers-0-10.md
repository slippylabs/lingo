In this lesson: **numbers 0 to 10**, and how the noun **changes after a number**.

## Numbers

- **nula** — zero
- **jedin** — one
- **dva** — two
- **tri** — three
- **četyri** — four
- **pet** — five
- **šest** — six
- **sedm** — seven
- **osm** — eight
- **devet** — nine
- **deset** — ten

## One and two match the noun

"One" has three forms, one per gender:

- **jedin hlapec** — one boy
- **jedna žena** — one woman
- **jedno polje** — one field

"Two" is **dva** for masculine nouns and **dvě** for feminine ones:

- **dva hlapci** — two boys
- **dvě ženy** — two women

## Two to four, then five and up

After 2, 3 and 4, the noun takes its normal plural:

- **četyri velosipedy** — four bicycles
- **četyri dolary** — four dollars

From 5 up, the noun takes the "of many" form, often ending in **‑ov** or **‑ev**:

- **pet hlapcev** — five boys
- **šest velosipedov** — six bicycles
- **osm dolarov** — eight dollars

> **Watch out:** compare **četyri dolary** with **osm dolarov**: the ending changes after 4.
