In this lesson: **first, second, third**, and telling **the time** with them.

## First, second, third

Ordinal numbers say which one in a row. They are describing words, ending in **‑y** like **dobry**:

- **prvy** — first
- **vtory** — second
- **tretij** — third
- **četvrty** — fourth
- **pety** — fifth
- **sotny** — hundredth

They match the noun's gender:

- **tridesety muž** — thirtieth man
- **četyristo peta gora** — four hundred fifth mountain

## Big ordinals

Only the last part becomes an ordinal:

- **dvěsto prvy brat** — two hundred first brother
- **dvěsto tretij kompjuter** — two hundred third computer
- **osmsto vtory dolar** — eight hundred second dollar

## Telling the time

The course names the hour with an ordinal: "at the fifth hour". Use **o** with the place form, ending in **‑oj**:

- **o petoj časině** — at the fifth hour
- **o desetoj časině** — at the tenth hour

Minutes come after the hour:

- **o prvoj časině dvadeset** — at twenty past the first hour
- **o vtoroj časině petnadset** — at fifteen past the second hour

> **Watch out:** "third" is **tretij**, which has its own pattern: **o tretej časině**, not *o tretoj*.
