In this lesson: **tens from 10 to 100**.

## The tens

Most tens are a number plus **‑deset**:

- **dvadeset** — twenty
- **trideset** — thirty
- **četyrideset** — forty
- **petdeset** — fifty
- **šestdeset** — sixty
- **sedmdeset** — seventy
- **osmdeset** — eighty
- **devetdeset** — ninety
- **sto** — one hundred

## The noun after them

The tens take the "of many" form of the noun:

- **dvadeset žen** — twenty women
- **trideset psov** — thirty dogs
- **četyrideset mužev** — forty men
- **sto časovnikov** — one hundred clocks

## Tens and ones

The ones come after the tens, like in English:

- **sedmdeset osm rubljev** — seventy eight rubles
- **osmdeset sedm dolarov** — eighty seven dollars

> **Watch out:** **pes** — dog loses its *e* in other forms: **trideset psov**.
