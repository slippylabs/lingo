In this lesson: **nature words**, and **through**, **between** and **towards**.

## Nature

- **rěka** — river
- **gora** — mountain
- **dolina** — valley
- **lěs** — forest
- **polje** — field
- **trava** — grass
- **cvět** — flower
- **drěvo** — tree

## Little words that change the noun

- **črěz** — through: **Rěka teče črěz dolinu.** — The river flows through the valley.
- **medžu** — between: **Dolina leži medžu gorami.** — The valley lies between the mountains.
- **k** — towards: **Idemo k dubu.** — We are going towards the oak.
- **iz** — from: **Cvět raste iz zemje.** — A flower grows from the earth.
- **za** — behind: **Polje jest za domom.** — The field is behind the house.

## Describing nature

- **Gora jest vysoka.** — The mountain is high.
- **Trava jest zelena.** — The grass is green.
- **Zemja jest mokra.** — The earth is wet.

> **Watch out:** **v** + the object form means going into: **My idemo v lěs.** — We are going to the forest. **na** + the place form means being on: **On stoji na kamenju.**
