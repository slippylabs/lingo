In this lesson: **the weather**, and sentences with **no "it"**.

## Weather words

- **pogoda** — weather
- **dožd** — rain
- **sněg** — snow
- **solnce** — sun
- **větr** — wind
- **mraz** — frost
- **oblak** — cloud

## No "it" needed

English says "it is warm" or "it is raining". Here there is no word for "it":

- **Tutdenj jest teplo.** — It is warm today.
- **Pada dožd.** — It is raining.
- **V Rosiji jest hladno.** — In Russia it is cold.
- **Oblačno jest tutdenj.** — It is cloudy today.

## Weather doing things

- **Solnce světi.** — The sun is shining.
- **Větr duje silno.** — The wind is blowing strongly.
- **Pogoda se izměnja.** — The weather is changing.
- **Solnce grěje nas.** — The sun heats us.

> **Watch out:** "it is raining" is literally "falls rain": **Pada dožd**. The same verb works for snow: **Pada sněg i dožd.**
