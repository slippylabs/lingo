In this lesson: **hobbies**, and how verbs change for **I, you, he, we, they**.

## Verb endings

Verbs change their ending to show who is doing something. Here is **čitati** — to read:

| | |
|---|---|
| **ja čitam** — I read | *my čitamo* — we read |
| *ty čitaš* — you read | *vy čitate* — you read |
| *ona čita* — she reads | *oni čitajut* — they read |

Most verbs ending in **‑ati** follow this pattern:

- **Ja čitam knigu.** — I am reading a book.
- **Ona sědi i čita.** — She is sitting and reading.
- **My igramo v šah.** — We are playing chess.
- **Oni slušajut muzyku.** — They are listening to music.

## Other verbs

Some verbs have *‑e‑* in their endings instead:

- **Ona piše pisma.** — She is writing letters.
- **Ja šijem oděždu.** — I am sewing clothes.
- **Ja malujem obraz.** — I am painting a picture.

## One form, two meanings

**Ja čitam** means both "I read" and "I am reading". There is no separate "‑ing" form.

> **Watch out:** **učiti se** — to learn needs **se**: **Učimo se tu.** — We are learning here.
