In this lesson: **minutes, hours, yesterday and tomorrow**, and talking about **the past**.

## Time words

- **minuta** — minute
- **sekunda** — second
- **časina** — hour
- **včera** — yesterday
- **zautra** — tomorrow
- **polnoč** — midnight

- **Koliko jest časa?** — What time is it?
- **Koliko minut trva film?** — How many minutes does the film last?

## The past

The past tense ends in **‑l**, plus an ending that matches who did it:

| | |
|---|---|
| **byl** — he was | **byla** — she was |
| **bylo** — it was | **byli** — they were |

- **Včera byl ponedělok.** — Yesterday was Monday.
- **Včera ona byla v domu o osmoj časině.** — Yesterday she was at home at the eighth hour.
- **Utrom ne bylo mnogo teplo.** — In the morning it was not very warm.
- **Oni ničto ne dělali.** — They did nothing.

## I was, you were

For "I" and "you", you can add **jesm** or **jesi**:

- **Ja jesm byl v domu utrom.** — I was at home in the morning.

## Went

"Went" is irregular: **šel**, **šla**, **šli**:

- **On šel do doma.** — He went home.
- **Večerom my šli v restoran.** — In the evening we went to the restaurant.

> **Watch out:** the ending follows the person, not the time: a woman says *ja byla*, a man says **Ja jesm byl**.
