In this lesson: **asking the way**, and **left, right, straight, near, far**.

## Asking

- **Kde jest stancija?** — Where is the station?
- **Kde jest blizky hotel?** — Where is the nearest hotel?
- **Kudy ide doroga?** — Which way does the road go?
- **Možete li pokazati put?** — Can you show me the way?

**kde** asks "where", and **kudy** asks "which way".

## Directions

- **Idemo napravo.** — We are going to the right.
- **Potom nalěvo.** — Then to the left.
- **Idi rovno do mosta.** — Go straight to the bridge.
- **Idete premo i potom napravo.** — Go straight and then to the right.

## Near and far

**blizko** — near and **daleko** — far are followed by the "of" form:

- **Magazin jest blizko biblioteky.** — The shop is near the library.
- **Trg jest daleko od hotela.** — The square is far from the hotel.

> **Watch out:** "far from" needs **od**: **daleko od hotela**.
