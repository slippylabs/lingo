In this lesson: **the seasons** and talking about **the future**.

## Seasons

- **vesna** — spring
- **lěto** — summer
- **jesenj** — autumn
- **zima** — winter

- **V lětu bude teplo.** — In the summer it will be warm.
- **V zimě bude padati sněg.** — In the winter it will snow.
- **V jesenji toliko pada dožd.** — In autumn, it rains so much.

## The future

Use a form of **byti** — to be, plus the basic form of the verb:

| | |
|---|---|
| **budu** — I will | **budemo** — we will |
| **buděš** — you will | **budete** — you will |
| **bude** — he/she/it will | **budut** — they will |

- **V jesenji budemo viděti padajuči listy.** — In the autumn we will see falling leaves.
- **V zimě budete igrati s sněgom.** — In the winter you will play with snow.
- **Oni budut viděti zeleny krajobraz.** — They will see a green landscape.

On its own, **bude** means "will be":

- **Vozduh v zimě bude hladny.** — The air in winter will be cold.

> **Watch out:** after **budu**, **bude** and the rest, the verb stays in its dictionary form, ending in **‑ti**: **bude padati**, not *bude pada*.
