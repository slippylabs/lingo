In this lesson: the verb **"to be"**, **he, she, it**, and **this / that**.

## "To be"

| | |
|---|---|
| **ja jesm** — I am | **my** **jesmo** — we are |
| **ty jesi** — you are | **vy jeste** — you are (more than one) |
| **on** / **ona** / **ono** **jest** — he / she / it is | **oni / one sut** — they are |

- **Ja jesm mudry.** — I am wise.
- **Vy jeste dobri.** — You are good.
- **To sut prijatelje.** — These are friends.
- **Kto ty jesi?** — Who are you?

The verb is often left out when the meaning is clear:

- **To moja sestra.** — This is my sister.
- **Ona mnogo mudra** — She is very wise

## Every noun has a gender

Gender is a grammar group, and you can usually tell it from the last letter:

- ends in a consonant → masculine: **dom** — house, **muž** — man
- ends in **‑a** → feminine: **žena** — woman, **sestra** — sister
- ends in **‑o** or **‑e** → neuter: **město** — city, **děte** — child

## This and that

- **Ta žena jest dobra.** — This woman is good.
- **Toj muž mily.** — That man is kind.
- **Tamtoj dom jest krasny.** — That house over there is beautiful.

## Saying "not"

Put **ne** in front:

- **To ne moja sestra.** — This is not my sister.

> **Watch out:** **oni** is "they" for a group with at least one man; **one** is "they" for a group of women: **Oni sut mnogo mili** but **One sut mnogo mile**.
