In this lesson: **kitchen things**, and saying what you **eat or cook with**.

## In the kitchen

- **nož** — knife
- **vilica** — fork
- **ložka** — spoon
- **talirka** — plate
- **čaša** — cup
- **grnec** — pot
- **skovorodka** — frying pan
- **miska** — bowl

## Full of, a lot of

"Full of" and "a lot of" use the "of" form (the genitive):

- **Talirka jest polna jedy.** — The plate is full of food.
- **Ložka jest polna peprca.** — The spoon is full of pepper.
- **Mnogo cukra jest v miskě** — There is a lot of sugar in the bowl.

## Where things are

- **Nož leži na stolu.** — The knife is lying on the table.
- **Grnec jest na plameni.** — The pot is on the flame.
- **My gotovimo v grncu.** — We cook in a pot.

## More than one

- **Vilice i nože sut na stolu.** — Forks and knives are on the table.

> **Watch out:** **grnec** loses its *e* when it takes an ending: **v grncu**, not *v grnecu*.
