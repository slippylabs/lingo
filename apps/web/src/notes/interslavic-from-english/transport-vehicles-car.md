In this lesson: **vehicles**, **roads**, and the verb **to go (by vehicle)**.

## Vehicles

- **avto** — car
- **avtobus** — bus
- **pojezd** — train
- **kolo** — bicycle
- **svetofor** — traffic light
- **doroga** — road

## Going by vehicle

**jehati** means to go in or on something that moves. Its forms use *jed‑*:

- **Avtobus jede do centra.** — The bus goes to the center.
- **Pojezd jede na stanciju.** — The train goes to the station.
- **Šofer jede brzo.** — The driver drives fast.

On foot or for roads, use **ide**:

- **Doroga ide črěz lěs.** — The road goes through the forest.

## Describing

- **Avto jest novo i krasno.** — The car is new and beautiful.
- **Doroga jest široka.** — The road is wide.
- **Šofer jest ostražny.** — The driver is careful.
- **Kolo jest razbito.** — The bicycle is broken.

## Traffic lights

- **Svetofor pokazuje črveno.** — The traffic light shows red.
- **Svetofor pokazuje zeleno.** — The traffic light shows green.

> **Watch out:** **avto** and **kolo** are neuter, so their describing words end in **‑o**: **Avto jest novo**, **Kolo jest razbito**.
