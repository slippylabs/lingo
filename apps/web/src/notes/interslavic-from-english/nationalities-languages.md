In this lesson: **countries, nations and languages**.

## People

- **Poljak** — Pole
- **Rus** — Russian
- **Němec** — German
- **Angličanin** — Englishman
- **Hrvatka** — Croat (a woman)

To say what nationality someone is, this course uses the "with" form (**‑om** for men, **‑oju** for women) after **jest**:

- **Ja jesm Poljakom.** — I am a Pole.
- **On jest Rusom.** — He is a Russian.
- **Ona jest Hrvatkoju.** — She is a Croat.

## Speaking a language

"To speak in a language" is **na** … **jezyku**:

- **Ona govori na slovjanskom jezyku.** — She speaks the Slavic language.
- **Angličanin govori na anglijskom jezyku.** — An Englishman speaks the English language.
- **Govoriš li na němečskom jezyku?** — Do you speak the German language?

You can also just name the language:

- **Ona zna rusky i slovjansky.** — She knows Russian and Slavic.

## Country and nation

- **To jest ime mojej državy.** — This is the name of my country.
- **My ljubimo svoj narod.** — We love our nation.

> **Watch out:** names of nationalities start with a capital letter (**Poljak**, **Rus**), but language words don't (**rusky**, **anglijskom**).
