In this lesson: **places in a city**, and **going to** versus **being in**.

## Places

- **grad** — city
- **ulica** — street
- **trg** — square/market
- **most** — bridge
- **stancija** — station
- **magazin** — shop/store
- **biblioteka** — library
- **crkva** — church

## Going to a place

**do** — to takes the "of" form (the genitive):

- **Idemo do trga.** — We are going to the square.
- **Ulica ide do magazina.** — The street goes to the shop.

## Being in a place

**v** — in and **na** — on, at take the place form (the locative):

- **Kupujemo hlěb v magazinu.** — We buy bread in the shop.
- **Čitam knigu v bibliotekě.** — I read a book in the library.
- **Na trgu stoji fontana.** — A fountain stands in the square.
- **To jest moj dom v gradu.** — This is my house in the city.

## Near

**blizko** — near also takes the "of" form:

- **Stancija jest blizko centra.** — The station is near the center.
- **Crkva stoji blizko biblioteky.** — The church stands near the library.

> **Watch out:** a square is **na trgu** — on the square, but a shop is **v magazinu** — in the shop.
