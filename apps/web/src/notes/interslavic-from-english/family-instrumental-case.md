In this lesson: **family words** and the **"with" form** of a noun.

## Family

- **brat** — brother
- **sestra** — sister
- **matka** — mother
- **otec** — father
- **děd** — grandfather
- **babka** — grandmother
- **syn** — son
- **dočerka** — daughter

## The "with" form

After **s** ("with"), a noun changes its ending. This is called the instrumental case:

| | ending | example |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | **‑om** | **s dědom** |
| feminine | **‑oju** | **s matkoju** |
| more than one | **‑ami** | **s bratami** |

- **On govori s dědom.** — He is talking with his grandfather.
- **Ona ide s matkoju.** — She is going with her mother.
- **Idemo na pohod s bratami.** — We are going for a walk with brothers.
- **My igramo s našimi dětmi.** — We are playing with our children.

"My" and "your" change too: **Govorim s tvojeju sestroju.** — I am talking with your sister.

## Saying who someone is

This course often uses the same form after **jest**:

- **On jest moim bratom.** — He is my brother.
- **On jest tvojim otcom** — He is your father

> **Watch out:** **s** alone means "with". **S kim ty ideš?** — Who are you going with?
