In this lesson: **clothes**, and joining ideas with **and**, **but** and **or**.

## Clothes

- **košulja** — shirt
- **teniska** — t-shirt
- **nogavice** — trousers
- **paljto** — coat
- **šapka** — hat
- **šalik** — scarf
- **rukavica** — glove
- **obuvka** — footwear

## Wearing

- **On nosi modru košulju.** — He wears a blue shirt.
- **Nosim rukavice v zimě.** — I wear gloves in winter.
- **On oblěka novu košulju.** — He is putting on a new shirt.

## And, but, or

- **i** — and, joining two similar things: **Šalik jest težky i teply.** — The scarf is heavy and warm.
- **a** — and / but, when there is a contrast: **Ona ima črne nogavice, a on běle.** — She has black trousers, and he has white.
- **ili** — or: **Ty hčeš šalik ili košulju.** — You want a scarf or a shirt.

## A contrast with "but not"

- **Zimoju nosim vsegda šapku, a v lětu ne.** — In winter, I always wear a hat, but not in summer.

> **Watch out:** use **a**, not **i**, when the two parts contrast: **Bridke rukavice ležet na stolu, a krasno paljto nosim.**
