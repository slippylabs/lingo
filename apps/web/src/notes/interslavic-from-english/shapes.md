In this lesson: **shapes** and **lines**, and **counting sides**.

## Shapes

- **krug** — circle
- **kvadrat** — square
- **trikutnik** — triangle
- **linija** — line
- **točka** — point
- **forma** — shape

## Describing shapes

- **To jest krug.** — This is a circle.
- **Ta linija jest dolga.** — This line is long.
- **Linija jest ravna.** — The line is straight.
- **Figura jest kompleksna.** — The figure is complex.

## "Has" and numbers

**ima** means "has":

- **Kvadrat ima četyri strany.** — A square has four sides.
- **Trikutnik ima tri strany.** — A triangle has three sides.

## "Of a square"

"Of" uses the "of" form (the genitive), with **‑a** on masculine words:

- **Vidimo obrys kvadrata.** — We see the outline of a square.
- **Stol ima oblik kvadrata.** — The table has the shape of a square.

> **Watch out:** **forma** and **oblik** both mean "shape". **forma** is feminine (**Forma jest interesna**) and **oblik** is masculine (**To jest prosty oblik**).
