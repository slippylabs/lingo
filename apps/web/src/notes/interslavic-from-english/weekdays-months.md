In this lesson: **the days of the week**, **the months**, and saying **on Monday, in May**.

## Days of the week

- **ponedělok** — Monday
- **vtorok** — Tuesday
- **srěda** — Wednesday
- **četvrtok** — Thursday
- **petok** — Friday
- **subota** — Saturday
- **nedělja** — Sunday

## On a day

Use **v** with the object form:

- **V srědu imam zadanje.** — On Wednesday, I have an assignment.
- **V petok večerom idemo na film.** — On Friday evening we are going to the movies.

## In a month

Use **v** with the place form, ending in **‑u**:

- **Imam v januaru vakacije.** — I have vacation in January.
- **V fevruaru jest mnogo sněga.** — In February there is a lot of snow.
- **V avgustu idem do Honduras.** — In August, I am going to Honduras.

## Before and after

- **Vtorok jest po ponedělku.** — Tuesday is after Monday.
- **Četvrtok jest prěd petkom.** — Thursday is before Friday.

> **Watch out:** days and months are written with a small letter: **ponedělok**, **januar**. Only the first word of a sentence gets a capital.
