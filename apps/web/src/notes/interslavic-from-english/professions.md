In this lesson: **jobs**, and how to say **what someone does**.

## Jobs

- **učitelj** — teacher
- **doktor** — doctor
- **inženjer** — engineer
- **robotnik** — worker
- **prodavec** — seller
- **šofer** — driver
- **kuhar** — cook
- **artist** — artist

## Saying someone's job

This course puts the job in the "with" form (**‑om**) after **jest**:

- **Moj brat jest učiteljem.** — My brother is a teacher.
- **Ona jest doktorom.** — She is a doctor.
- **Moj otec jest kuharom.** — My father is a cook.

## What they do

- **Doktor pomaga ljudam.** — The doctor helps people.
- **Prodavec prodaje jedu.** — The seller sells food.
- **Robotnik stroi dom.** — The worker is building a house.
- **Kuhar gotovi večerju.** — The cook is preparing dinner.
- **Šofer vede avtobus.** — The driver drives a bus.

## Two verbs for "work"

**rabotati** and **pracovati** both mean "to work":

- **Prodavec rabota v magazinu.** — The seller works in the shop.
- **Robotnik pracuje na mostu.** — The worker is working on the bridge.

> **Watch out:** a driver **jede** (goes) somewhere, but **vede** (leads, drives) a vehicle or people: **Šofer jede brzo**, **Šofer vede avtobus**.
