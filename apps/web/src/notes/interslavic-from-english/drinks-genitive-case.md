In this lesson: **drinks**, and the **"of" form** of a noun used after **without**, **from** and **not having**.

## The "of" form

This form is called the genitive case. Feminine words in **‑a** change to **‑y**; masculine and neuter words take **‑a**:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **voda** — water | → | **vody** |
| **kava** — coffee | → | **kavy** |
| **sok** — juice | → | **soka** |
| **vino** — wine | → | **vina** |

## When to use it

After **bez** — without:

- **Piješ li bez mlěka?** — Do you drink without milk?
- **Ja ljubu sok bez cukra.** — I like juice without sugar.

After "a bottle of", "a glass of":

- **Ty imaš fljašku soka?** — Do you have a bottle of juice?
- **Čaša vina za mene, prosim.** — A glass of wine for me, please.

When you don't have or don't drink something:

- **Ja ne imam vody.** — I do not have water.
- **Ona ne pije čaja.** — She does not drink tea.
- **On nema kavy.** — He does not have coffee.

> **Watch out:** "I have water" uses the plain form, but "I don't have water" uses the "of" form: **Ja ne imam vody.**
