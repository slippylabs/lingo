In this lesson: **shopping**: **prices**, **buying and selling**, and **a piece of** something.

## Buying and selling

- **Hčem kupiti hlěb.** — I want to buy bread.
- **Ona kupuje knigu.** — She is buying a book.
- **On prodaje meso.** — He sells meat.
- **Dam ček prodavcu.** — I give the receipt to the seller.

## The price

- **Cena jest vysoka.** — The price is high.
- **Cena masla jest dobra.** — The price of butter is good.
- **Kupec pyta o cenu.** — The customer asks about the price.
- **Kupec pyta za znižku.** — The customer asks for a discount.

## A piece of

**kus** — piece is followed by the "of" form:

- **Kus syra jest dobry.** — A piece of cheese is good.
- **Kus hlěba jest maly.** — The piece of bread is small.
- **Prodavec da kus kolbasy.** — The seller gives a piece of sausage.

## "I want"

**hčem** — I want is followed by the basic verb form:

- **Hčem kupiti hlěb.** — I want to buy bread.

> **Watch out:** don't mix up the two people in a shop: the **kupec** buys, the **prodavec** sells.
