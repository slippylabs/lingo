In this lesson: **north, south, east, west**, and **left, right, straight**.

## The four directions

- **sěver** — north
- **jug** — south
- **vozhod** — east
- **zapad** — west

## In the north, to the north

- **na** + the place form = in: **Živemo na jugu.** — We live in the south.
- **Na sěveru jest sněg.** — In the north there is snow.
- **k** = towards: **Gledamo k sěveru.** — We are looking to the north.

## Describing words

Add **‑ny** to make a describing word:

- **sěverny** — northern: **To jest sěverna strana.** — This is the northern side.
- **zapadny** — western: **Zapadny větr duje.** — A western wind is blowing.
- **vozhodny** — eastern: **Vozhodna muzika jest interesna.** — Eastern music is interesting.

## Left, right, straight

- **Zapad jest lěvo na mapě.** — West is left on the map.
- **Idemo tutčas vpravo i potom premo.** — We are going right now and then straight.

> **Watch out:** the south is **jug**, but "southern" is **južny**: the *g* becomes *ž*.
