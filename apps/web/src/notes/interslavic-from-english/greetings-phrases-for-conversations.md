In this lesson: **greetings**, **thank you**, and short **everyday replies**.

## Saying hello and goodbye

- **Dobro utro!** — Good morning!
- **Dobry denj!** — Good day!
- **Dobry večer!** — Good evening!
- **Dobra noč!** — Good night!
- **Zdrav!** — Hello!
- **Dobrodošli!** — Welcome!
- **S Bogom!** — Go with God!

## How are you?

- **Kako jesi?** — How are you?
- **Jesm dobro, hvala!** — I am well, thank you!
- **I ty?** — And you?
- **Kako se zoveš?** — What is your name?
- **Ja se zovem Bartek.** — My name is Bartek.

## Short replies

- **Tako jest.** — That is so.
- **Može byti.** — Maybe.
- **Ne znam.** — I don't know.
- **Škoda!** — What a pity!
- **Tož ja.** — Me too.
- **Pardon, ne razuměm.** — Excuse me, I don't understand.

## Asking yes-or-no questions

Add **li** after the first word:

- **Jesi li gotovy?** — Are you ready?
- **Jesi li ty zdrav?** — Are you healthy?

> **Watch out:** **Dobry denj** and **Dobry večer** use **dobry**, but **Dobro utro** uses **dobro** and **Dobra noč** uses **dobra**, because the describing word matches the gender of the noun.
