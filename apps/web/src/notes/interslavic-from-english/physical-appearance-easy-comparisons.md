In this lesson: **describing how people look**, and **comparing** with **than** and **like**.

## Describing people

- **vysoky** — tall
- **nizky** — short
- **debely** — fat
- **chudy** — thin
- **stary** — old
- **mlady** — young
- **krasivy** — beautiful
- **bridky** — ugly

- **Debely muž ide po ulici.** — The fat man is walking down the street.
- **Stara žena nosi šapku.** — The old woman wears a hat.
- **Ona ima zelene oči.** — She has green eyes.
- **Jej vlasy sut světle i dolge.** — Her hair is light and long.

## Than and like

- **než** — than: **Chudy ima menje tělesnoj masy než debely.** — The thin person has less body mass than the fat one.
- **kak** — like, as: **A jego krasne vlasy sut temne kak noč.** — And his beautiful hair is as dark as the night.

## More than

In this lesson's sentences, "more" is shown with **vyše** before the describing word:

- **Stari ljudi sut vyše mudri než my.** — Old people are wiser than us.
- **Oči sut vyše zelene než u njego.** — Her eyes are greener than his.

*Standard Interslavic more often uses* *bolje* *or a one-word form such as* *vyšši* *(taller).*

> **Watch out:** **vlasy** — hair is plural, so it takes **sut**: **Jego vlasy sut plave.**
