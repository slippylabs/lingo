In this lesson: **numbers 11 to 20**.

## Building 11 to 19

Take the number and add *‑nadset*, meaning "on ten":

- **jedinnadset** — eleven
- **dvanadset** — twelve
- **trinadset** — thirteen
- **četyrinadset** — fourteen
- **petnadset** — fifteen
- **šestnadset** — sixteen
- **sedmnadset** — seventeen
- **osmnadset** — eighteen
- **devetnadset** — nineteen

Twenty is **dvadeset**: "two tens".

## The noun after them

All of these take the "of many" form of the noun:

- **jedinnadset hlapcev** — eleven boys
- **dvanadset žen** — twelve women
- **trinadset kotov** — thirteen cats
- **devetnadset kompjuterov** — nineteen computers
- **dvadeset kotov** — twenty cats

> **Watch out:** feminine nouns in **‑a** lose the ending completely in this form: **žena** → **dvanadset žen**.
