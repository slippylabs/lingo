In this lesson: **colours**, which change their ending to **match the noun**.

## Colours

- **črny** — black
- **běly** — white
- **črveny** — red
- **zeleny** — green
- **modry** — blue
- **žolty** — yellow
- **sivy** — grey
- **oranževy** — orange
- **kaštanovy** — brown

## Matching the noun

Colour words are describing words, so their ending follows the noun's gender:

- **Moj pes jest črny.** — My dog is black. (masculine **‑y**)
- **Myš jest běla.** — The mouse is white. (feminine **‑a**)
- **Nebo jest modro.** — The sky is blue. (neuter **‑o**)
- **Cvěty sut črvene.** — The flowers are red. (more than one)

## Before the noun

- **Ja ljubim črveny kolor.** — I like the red color.
- **Črvena roza raste tu.** — A red rose grows here.

As the object of a verb, a feminine colour word ends in **‑u**, like its noun:

- **Děva nese bělu suknju.** — The girl is carrying a white skirt.
- **On ima oranževu torbu.** — He has an orange bag.

> **Watch out:** **avto** is neuter, so it is **Jego avto jest črno**, not *črny*.
