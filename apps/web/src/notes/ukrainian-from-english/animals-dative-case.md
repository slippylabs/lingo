In this lesson: **animals** and the dative case for **giving to** and **helping**.

## Giving to someone

The dative case is the form for the person or animal who receives something ("to the cat", "for the dog"). The thing given stays in the accusative.

| | ending | example |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | ‑ові / ‑еві / ‑у | **Я дам котові рибу.** — I will give the cat a fish. |
| feminine | ‑і | **Ми даємо жабі хліб.** — We give bread to the frog. |
| plural | ‑ам / ‑ям | **Даємо жабам воду.** — We give the frogs water. |

- **Даємо коню воду.** — We give the horse water.
- **Вона дасть псу добре ім'я.** — She will give the dog a good name.
- **Вони несуть ведмедям їжу.** — They are carrying food to the bears.

## Helping someone

**помагати** (to help) also takes the dative:

- **Він помагає птахові.** — He helps the bird.
- **Помагаємо вовкові.** — We help the wolf.
- **Ми помагаємо птахам.** — We help the birds.

## "Will give"

**дати** is the "do it once" verb, so its present-looking forms mean the future:

- **Я дам рибі воду.** — I will give the fish water.
- **Ти даси мені рибу?** — Will you give me a fish?
- **Він дасть миші сир.** — He will give the mouse cheese.

> **Watch out:** **Я дам** is "I will give", but **Даємо** (from **давати**) is "we give" right now.
