In this lesson: **food**, the verb **to eat**, and the accusative case.

## The thing you eat, buy or carry

The accusative case is the form for the thing an action is done to. Masculine and neuter things don't change:

- **Я їм хліб.** — I am eating bread.
- **Вона їсть м'ясо.** — She is eating meat.
- **Він готує обід.** — He is preparing lunch.

Feminine words ending in ‑а change it to **‑у**, and describing words change too:

- **Вона несе страву.** — She is carrying a dish. (страва)
- **Він їсть солодку їжу.** — He eats sweet food. (солодка їжа)
- **Ми бачимо твою піцу на столі.** — We see your pizza on the table.

## "To eat" is irregular

| | |
|---|---|
| I eat | **Я їм хліб.** |
| you eat | **Ти їси яйце?** |
| he / she eats | **Вона їсть м'ясо.** |
| we eat | **Ми їмо вечерю.** |

## "That" after "I know"

- **Я знаю, що ти любиш яйця.** — I know that you like eggs.

> **Watch out:** **їм** is "I eat", not "they eat". **Я їм хліб.** — I am eating bread.
