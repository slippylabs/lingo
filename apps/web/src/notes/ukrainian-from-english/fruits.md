In this lesson: **fruit** and making **describing words** match.

## Describing words match the noun

A describing word (adjective) takes an ending that matches the noun's gender:

| | ending | example |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | ‑ий | **Лимон є кислий і жовтий.** — The lemon is sour and yellow. |
| feminine | ‑а | **Вишня кисла.** — The cherry is sour. |
| neuter | ‑е | **Яблуко червоне.** — The apple is red. |
| plural | ‑і | **Помаранчі солодкі.** — The oranges are sweet. |

- **Диня велика і солодка.** — The melon is big and sweet.
- **Грушка смачна.** — The pear is tasty.

## More than one

Feminine nouns in ‑а usually make the plural with **‑и**:

- **Грушки лежать на столі.** — The pears are lying on the table.
- **Ягоди є в мисці.** — The berries are in the bowl.

## Where fruit grows

- **Вишня росте на дереві.** — The cherry grows on a tree.
- **Виноград росте на винограднику.** — Grapes grow on a vineyard.

> **Watch out:** **виноград** is one word for all the grapes together. **Ми купуємо виноград.** — We are buying grapes.
