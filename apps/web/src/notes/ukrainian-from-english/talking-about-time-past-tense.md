In this lesson: **time words** and the **past tense**.

## The past tense follows gender, not person

The past tense doesn't change for I, you or we. It changes for masculine, feminine, neuter and plural:

| | "was" |
|---|---|
| masculine | **був** |
| feminine | **була** |
| neuter | **було** |
| plural | **були** |

- **Я був удома вранці.** — I was at home in the morning. (said by a man)
- **Вчора вона була вдома о восьмій годині.** — Yesterday she was at home at the eighth hour.
- **Вчора був понеділок.** — Yesterday was Monday.

## "It was" with no "it"

The neuter **було** is used for weather and general states:

- **Вранці не було дуже тепло.** — In the morning it was not very warm.
- **О третій годині двадцять було холодно.** — At twenty past the third hour it was cold.

## Went

- **Він пішов додому.** — He went home.
- **Ввечері ми пішли в ресторан.** — In the evening we went to the restaurant.
- **Ви йшли до кінотеатру?** — Were you going to the cinema?

## Asking the time

- **Котра година?** — What time is it?
- **Скільки хвилин триває фільм?** — How many minutes does the film last?

> **Watch out:** a woman says *я була*, a man says **Я був**. The verb follows the speaker's gender.
