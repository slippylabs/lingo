In this lesson: **nature words**, and going **into** a place versus being **in** it.

## Going somewhere vs being somewhere

With a verb of movement, **в** and **на** take the accusative case (the "where to" form). For a place where something simply is, they take the locative (the "where" form, often ‑і):

- **Ми йдемо в ліс.** — We are going to the forest. (where to)
- **Вони дивляться в долину.** — They are looking into the valley. (where to)
- **Він стоїть на камені.** — He is standing on the stone. (where)
- **Роза росте на полі.** — The rose grows in the field. (where)

## Through, between, behind

- **Ріка тече через долину.** — The river flows through the valley.
- **Долина лежить між горами.** — The valley lies between the mountains.
- **Ріка йде між полями.** — The river goes between the fields.
- **Поле за домом.** — The field is behind the house.

## Growing and blooming

- **Трава росте швидко.** — The grass grows fast.
- **Квіти красиво цвітуть.** — The flowers bloom beautifully.

> **Watch out:** rivers and water "go" in Ukrainian: **Вода йде до ріки.** — Water goes to the river.
