In this lesson: **"to be"**, the words for **this and that**, and the three **genders**.

## "Am", "is" and "are" are usually left out

In the present tense Ukrainian normally drops the verb "to be":

- **Я мудрий.** — I am wise.
- **Ми дуже мудрі** — We are very wise
- **Хто ти?** — Who are you?

You will also see **є** ("is/are") in some sentences. It is correct, but more formal:

- **Та жінка є добра.** — That woman is good.

## "This is …"

**Це** at the start of a sentence means "this is" or "these are", and it never changes:

- **Це моя сестра.** — This is my sister.
- **Це друзі.** — These are friends.
- **Це не твій дім.** — This is not your house.

## Three genders

Every noun is masculine, feminine or neuter. This is its gender: a grammar group, not about meaning. Words that describe a noun change their ending to match it:

| | this | that |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | **цей** | **той** |
| feminine | **ця** | **та** |

- **Той чоловік милий.** — That man is kind.
- **Та жінка є висока.** — That woman is tall.
- **Це приємне місто.** — This is a pleasant city. (neuter, ending ‑е)
- **Отой дім є красивий.** — That house over there is beautiful.

## I, you, he, she …

**я** (I), **ти** (you, one friend), **він** (he), **вона** (she), **воно** (it), **ми** (we), **ви** (you, several people or polite), **вони** (they).

> **Watch out:** don't look for a word for "is" in **Це мій дім** — This is my house. Ukrainian simply leaves it out.
