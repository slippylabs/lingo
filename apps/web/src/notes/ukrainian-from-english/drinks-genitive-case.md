In this lesson: **drinks**, **"not any"**, **"without"** and **"a bottle of"**.

## The genitive case

The genitive case is the form for "of" and "none of". You'll see it in three places in this lesson.

### After "not" with "have" or "drink"

- **Я не маю води.** — I do not have water. (вода)
- **Вона не п'є вина.** — She does not drink wine. (вино)
- **Він не має кави.** — He does not have coffee. (кава)
- **Вона не п'є чаю.** — She does not drink tea. (чай)

### After "without" (без)

- **П'єш без молока?** — Do you drink without milk?
- **Я люблю сік без цукру.** — I like juice without sugar.

### After "a bottle of" or "a glass of"

- **Ти маєш пляшку соку?** — Do you have a bottle of juice?
- **Келих вина для мене, прошу.** — A glass of wine for me, please.

The endings: ‑а becomes **‑и**, ‑о becomes **‑а**, and short masculine words often take **‑у**.

## Without "not"

In a plain sentence the drink keeps its normal form:

- **Ти п'єш вино?** — Do you drink wine?

> **Watch out:** compare **Ти п'єш вино?** with **Вона не п'є вина.** The word **не** changes вино to вина.
