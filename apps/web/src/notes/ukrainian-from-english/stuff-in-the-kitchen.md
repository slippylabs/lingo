In this lesson: **kitchen things**, eating **with** something, and drinking **from** something.

## Eating with a fork

For a tool, Ukrainian changes the ending (‑ою, ‑ом) and uses no word for "with":

- **Він їсть виделкою.** — He eats with a fork. (виделка)
- **Чашка наповнена водою.** — The cup is filled with water.

## From and in

- **Вона п'є з чашки.** — She drinks from a cup. (з + ‑и)
- **Ми готуємо в горщику.** — We cook in a pot.
- **На сковорідці є м'ясо.** — There is meat in the frying pan.

## Full of, a lot of

"Full of" and "a lot of" take the genitive case (the "of" form):

- **Тарілка повна їжі.** — The plate is full of food.
- **Багато цукру є в мисці.** — There is a lot of sugar in the bowl.
- **Ложка повна перцю.** — The spoon is full of pepper.

## More than one

- **Виделки і ножі на столі.** — Forks and knives are on the table. (ніж → ножі)

> **Watch out:** **ніж** (knife) loses its і in the plural: **ножі**.
