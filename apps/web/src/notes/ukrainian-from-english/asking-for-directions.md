In this lesson: **asking the way** and giving **directions**.

## Where and where to

Ukrainian has two words for "where":

- **Де станція?** — Where is the station? (**де**: where something is)
- **Куди йде дорога?** — Which way does the road go? (**куди**: where to)
- **Куди їде той поїзд?** — Where is that train going?

## Left, right, straight

- **Йдемо направо.** — We are going to the right.
- **Потім наліво.** — Then to the left.
- **Йди прямо до моста.** — Go straight to the bridge.

## Telling someone to go

**Йди** is said to one friend; **Йдіть** is for several people or for someone you are polite to:

- **Йди прямо до моста.** — Go straight to the bridge.
- **Йдіть прямо і потім направо.** — Go straight and then to the right.

## Near and far

- **Магазин близько бібліотеки.** — The shop is near the library.
- **Майдан далеко від готелю.** — The square is far from the hotel.
- **Де найближчий готель?** — Where is the nearest hotel?

> **Watch out:** with a verb of going, ask **куди**, not **де**: **Куди йдете?** — Which way are you going?
