In this lesson: **hobbies** and how verbs change for **I, you, he, we and they**.

## One form for "I read" and "I am reading"

Ukrainian has no separate ‑ing form. The same verb covers both:

- **Я читаю книжку.** — I am reading a book.
- **Чи читаєш романи?** — Do you read novels?

## Verb endings

The dictionary form of a verb ends in **‑ти** (**читати** — to read). In the present tense the ending changes with the person. For **грати** (to play):

| | ending | example |
|---|---|---|
| I | ‑ю | **Я танцюю багато** — I dance a lot |
| he / she | ‑є | **Він грає в футбол.** — He is playing football. |
| we | ‑ємо | **Ми граємо в шахи.** — We are playing chess. |
| they | ‑ють | **Вони слухають музику.** — They are listening to music. |

Some verbs have **‑ить** for he/she instead:

- **Вона сидить і читає.** — She is sitting and reading.

## Verbs with ‑ся

A few verbs carry **‑ся** (or the short **‑сь**) on the end. It stays on every form:

- **Вчимося тут.** — We are learning here.
- **Ви вчитесь мови.** — You are learning a language.

> **Watch out:** the verb ending already tells you who acts, so the pronoun is often dropped: **Малюємо з друзями картини.** — We are painting pictures with friends.
