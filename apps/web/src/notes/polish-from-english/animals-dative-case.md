In this lesson: **animals** and the **dative case**, the form for the one you give something to or help.

## Giving to someone

- **Ja dam kotu rybę.** — I will give the cat a fish. (kot → kotu)
- **Ona pokazuje psu piłkę.** — She shows the dog a ball. (pies → psu)
- **Dajemy koniowi wodę.** — We give the horse water. (koń → koniowi)
- **My dajemy żabie chleb.** — We give bread to the frog. (żaba → żabie)

Masculine animals usually take **‑owi** (a few short ones take **‑u**). Feminine ones take **‑e** or **‑y**:

- **Ona niesie myszy ser.** — She carries cheese to the mouse.

In the plural, the ending is **‑om**:

- **My pomagamy ptakom.** — We help the birds.

## Helping

**Pomagać** (to help) also takes the dative:

- **On pomaga ptakowi.** — He helps the bird.
- **Pomagamy wilkowi.** — We help the wolf.

## "I will give"

**Dam**, **dasz**, **da** are the future of "give":

- **Dasz mi rybę?** — Will you give me a fish?

> **Watch out:** the one you help takes the dative, not the accusative: **Pomagamy wilkowi.**
