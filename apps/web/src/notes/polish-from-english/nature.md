In this lesson: **nature words**, and little words for **through, between, to** and **behind**.

## Each little word picks an ending

- **Rzeka płynie przez dolinę.** — The river flows through the valley. (**przez** + accusative)
- **Dolina leży między górami.** — The valley lies between the mountains. (**między** + instrumental)
- **Pole jest za domem.** — The field is behind the house. (**za** + instrumental)
- **My idziemy do lasu.** — We are going to the forest. (**do** + genitive)
- **On stoi na kamieniu.** — He is standing on the stone. (**na** + locative)

## Growing

- **Róża rośnie na polu.** — The rose grows in the field.
- **Trawa rośnie szybko.** — The grass grows fast.
- **Kwiaty pięknie kwitną.** — The flowers bloom beautifully.

> **Watch out:** **do** means "to a place" and always takes the genitive: **do lasu**, **do rzeki**.
