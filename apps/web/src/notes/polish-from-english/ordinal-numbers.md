In this lesson: **first, second, third…** and **telling the time**.

## Ordinals are describing words

Ordinals end like describing words and match the noun:

- **setny dolar** — hundredth dollar
- **dwusetny zegarek** — two hundredth clock
- **czterysta piąta góra** — four hundred fifth mountain (feminine)
- **trzydziesty mąż** — thirtieth husband

In a long number, only the last word is an ordinal:

- **dwieście pierwszy brat** — two hundred first brother

## Telling the time

"At" a time is **o** + the hour as an ordinal (feminine, because *godzina* "hour" is feminine). The minutes follow as ordinary numbers:

- **o pierwszej dwadzieścia** — at twenty past one
- **o drugiej piętnaście** — at fifteen past two
- **o ósmej pięćdziesiąt pięć** — at eight fifty five
- **o piątej godzinie** — at the fifth hour

> **Watch out:** the hour is an ordinal (**o drugiej**), but the minutes are not (**piętnaście**).
