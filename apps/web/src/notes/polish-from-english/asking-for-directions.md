In this lesson: **asking the way** and **giving directions**.

## Asking

- **Gdzie jest stacja?** — Where is the station?
- **Gdzie jest najbliższy hotel?** — Where is the nearest hotel?
- **Dokąd idziecie?** — Which way are you going?
- **Możecie pokazać mi drogę?** — Can you show me the way?

## Giving directions

- **Idź prosto do mostu.** — Go straight to the bridge.
- **Idźcie prosto i potem w prawo.** — Go straight and then to the right.
- **Potem w lewo.** — Then to the left.
- **Stacja jest po lewej.** — The station is on the left.

**Idź** is said to one person, **idźcie** to several.

## Near and far

- **Biblioteka jest daleko.** — The library is far away.
- **Sklep jest blisko biblioteki.** — The shop is near the library.

> **Watch out:** **gdzie** asks where something is; **dokąd** asks where someone is going.
