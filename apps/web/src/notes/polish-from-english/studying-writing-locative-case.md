In this lesson: **school words** and the **locative case**, the form after **na** (on) and **w** (in) when you say where something is.

## Where: na / w + locative

- **Książka jest na stole.** — The book is on the table. (stół → stole)
- **Piszę na papierze piórem.** — I am writing on paper with a pen. (papier → papierze)
- **Szkoła jest w centrum.** — The school is in the center.

Many masculine and neuter words take **‑e**, and the last consonant often softens: *ł* becomes *l*, *r* becomes *rz*.

## Saying someone's role

As in the family lesson, **jest** + a person uses the instrumental:

- **Ona jest nauczycielką.** — She is a teacher.
- **On jest uczniem.** — He is a student.
- **On jest moim nauczycielem.** — He is my teacher.

## With a tool: no "z"

For the tool you use, put it in the instrumental with no **z**:

- **Ja szybko piszę tym piórem.** — I am writing quickly with this pen.

> **Watch out:** "with a pen" is just **piórem**. Adding **z** would mean "together with", as with people.
