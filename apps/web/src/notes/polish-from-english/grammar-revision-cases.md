In this lesson: a **review of the cases**, the endings that show what job a word does.

## The cases you have met

- **On widzi swoją żonę.** — He sees his wife. (accusative: the thing you see)
- **Ona daje prezent braciom.** — She gives a gift to the brothers. (dative: who you give to)
- **Rozmawiamy z przyjaciółmi.** — We talk with friends. (instrumental: with)
- **To można ciąć nożem.** — This can be cut with a knife. (instrumental: the tool)
- **Ona jest w mieście.** — She is in the city. (locative: where)
- **Ona mówi o braciach.** — She talks about brothers. (locative: about)

## Genitive: "no", "many", "of"

- **Brata tu nie ma.** — There is no brother here.
- **Już nie ma bananów w domu.** — There are no more bananas in the house.
- **Jest dużo słońc we wszechświecie.** — There are many suns in the universe.
- **Oczy są zwierciadłem duszy.** — Eyes are the mirror of the soul.

> **Watch out:** "there is no…" is **nie ma** + the genitive: **Nie ma tu kości.** — There is no bone here.
