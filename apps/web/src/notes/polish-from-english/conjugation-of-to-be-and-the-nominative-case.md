In this lesson: **"to be"**, the words for **I, you, he, she**, and **this / that**.

## "To be": być

| who | form |
|---|---|
| ja (I) | **jestem** |
| ty (you) | **jesteś** |
| on, ona, ono (he, she, it) | **jest** |
| my (we) | **jesteśmy** |
| wy (you, more than one) | **jesteście** |
| oni, one (they) | **są** |

- **Ja jestem mądry.** — I am wise.
- **Kto ty jesteś?** — Who are you?
- **Ta kobieta jest dobra.** — This woman is good.
- **Wy jesteście dobrzy.** — You are good.

## "This is…"

**To** at the start of a sentence means "this is". You can leave out **jest**:

- **To moja siostra.** — This is my sister.
- **To jest mój dom.** — This is my house.
- **To nie twój dom.** — This is not your house.

## Describing words match the noun

Every Polish noun is masculine, feminine or neuter (a grammar group, not real gender). Describing words change their ending to match:

- **To dobry człowiek.** — This is a good person. (masculine: ‑y)
- **Ta kobieta jest wysoka.** — This woman is tall. (feminine: ‑a)
- **To dobre auto?** — Is this a good car? (neuter: ‑e)

"This" works the same way: **ten**, **ta**, **to**. "That over there" is **tamten**, **tamta**:

- **Tamten dom jest piękny.** — That house over there is beautiful.

## Two words for "they"

**Oni** is for a group with at least one man; **one** is for every other group.

- **Oni są bardzo mili.** — They are very kind.
- **One są bardzo miłe.** — They are very kind.

> **Watch out:** the describing word changes with **oni** too: **Oni są bardzo mili**, but **One są bardzo miłe**.
