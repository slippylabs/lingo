In this lesson: **colours**, which change their ending to **match** the noun.

## Colours match

- **Mój pies jest czarny.** — My dog is black. (masculine: ‑y)
- **Mysz jest biała.** — The mouse is white. (feminine: ‑a)
- **Niebo jest niebieskie.** — The sky is blue. (neuter: ‑e)
- **Kwiaty są czerwone.** — The flowers are red. (plural: ‑e)

## After a verb

After a verb like **nosi** (wears) or **ma** (has), a feminine colour and noun change to **‑ą** and **‑ę**:

- **Dziewczyna nosi białą sukienkę.** — The girl is wearing a white dress.
- **On ma pomarańczową torbę.** — He has an orange bag.

A masculine thing doesn't change:

- **On nosi brązowy płaszcz.** — He wears a brown coat.

> **Watch out:** in names, the colour can come after the noun: **To jest Morze Czerwone.** — This is the Red Sea.
