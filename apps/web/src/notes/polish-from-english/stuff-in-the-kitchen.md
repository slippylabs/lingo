In this lesson: **kitchen things**, and **in**, **from** and **with** a tool.

## In the kitchen

- **Nóż leży na stole.** — The knife is lying on the table.
- **Garnek jest gorący.** — The pot is hot
- **Kuchnia jest czysta.** — The kitchen is clean.
- **Talerz jest pełny jedzenia.** — The plate is full of food.

## With a tool

The tool goes in the instrumental, with no **z**:

- **On je widelcem.** — He eats with a fork. (widelec → widelcem)

## In and from

- **My gotujemy w garnku.** — We cook in a pot. (**w** + locative)
- **Na patelni jest mięso.** — There is meat in the frying pan.
- **Ona pije z filiżanki.** — She drinks from a cup. (**z** + genitive)

> **Watch out:** **z** has two meanings. With the instrumental it means "with" (**Ona idzie z matką**); with the genitive it means "out of" (**Ona pije z filiżanki**).
