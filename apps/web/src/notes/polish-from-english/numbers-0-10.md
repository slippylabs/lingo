In this lesson: **numbers 0 to 10**, and how the noun changes after a number.

## "One" matches the noun

- **jeden rower** — one bicycle
- **jedna kobieta** — one woman
- **jedno pole** — one field

## Two, three, four

"Two" is **dwa** for masculine and neuter things and **dwie** for feminine ones. After 2, 3 and 4 the noun is in its ordinary plural:

- **dwie kobiety** — two women
- **trzy koty** — three cats
- **cztery rowery** — four bicycles

## Five and up

From 5 on, the noun takes the genitive plural, often ending in **‑ów**:

- **pięć kotów** — five cats
- **sześć rowerów** — six bicycles
- **dziesięć zegarków** — ten clocks

## Groups of men

Groups of male people have their own number forms:

- **dwóch chłopców** — two boys
- **pięciu chłopców** — five boys

> **Watch out:** say **pięć kotów**, not *pięć koty*. From 5 up, the noun changes.
