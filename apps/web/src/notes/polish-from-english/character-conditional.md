In this lesson: **character words** and the **conditional**, for "would" and "if".

## "Would"

The conditional is the past form plus *by*, with a personal ending:

| who | "would be" |
|---|---|
| I | **byłbym** |
| you | **byłbyś** |
| he, she, it | **byłby** |
| we | **bylibyśmy** |
| you (more than one) | **bylibyście** |
| they | **byliby** |

## "If… then…"

- **Jakby oni nie byli leniwi, to byśmy im pomagali.** — If they were not lazy, we would help them.
- **Jakbyście nie byli słabi, to byście pracowali.** — If you were not weak, you would work.

*By* can move to the front of the clause and carry the ending: **byśmy** (we), **byście** (you all).

## Character

- **Ona jest dobra i szczera.** — She is good and sincere.
- **On jest słaby w sporcie.** — He is weak in sports.

A describing word can stand alone for a kind of person:

- **Mądry wie dużo.** — A smart person knows much.
- **Leniwy nie chce nic robić.** — Lazy people do not want to do anything.

> **Watch out:** the ending goes on *by*, not on the verb: **byśmy im pomagali** — we would help them.
