In this lesson: **weather**, and sentences like "it is warm" that have **no word for "it"**.

## "It is…" without "it"

- **Dzisiaj jest ciepło.** — It is warm today.
- **W Rosji jest zimno.** — In Russia it is cold.
- **Pochmurno jest dzisiaj.** — It is cloudy today.

Rain and snow "fall":

- **Pada deszcz.** — It is raining.
- **Pada śnieg i deszcz.** — Snow and rain are falling.

## Weather words

- **Słońce świeci.** — The sun is shining.
- **Wiatr wieje mocno.** — The wind is blowing strongly.
- **Chmury są ciemne.** — The clouds are dark.
- **Pogoda się zmienia.** — The weather is changing.

> **Watch out:** "it is warm" is **ciepło**, but a warm thing is **ciepły**: **Dzisiaj jest ciepło**, **Pokój jest ciepły i cichy.**
