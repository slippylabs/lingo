In this lesson: **places in a town**, and saying **in** and **to** a place.

## In a place

- **Kupujemy chleb w sklepie.** — We buy bread in the shop.
- **Czytam książkę w bibliotece.** — I read a book in the library.
- **Na targowisku jest dużo ludzi.** — There are many people at the market.

## To a place

- **Ulica idzie do sklepu.** — The street goes to the shop.
- **Ulica prowadzi do placu/rynku.** — The street leads to the square.

## Near

**Blisko** takes the genitive:

- **Kościół stoi blisko biblioteki.** — The church stands near the library.
- **Stacja jest blisko centrum.** — The station is near the center.

## Many

**Dużo** (many) takes the genitive plural:

- **Miasto ma dużo domów.** — The city has many houses.
- **Stacja ma dużo pociągów.** — The station has many trains.

> **Watch out:** squares and markets take **na**, buildings take **w**: **na targowisku**, but **w sklepie**.
