In this lesson: **clothes** and the joining words **i**, **a**, **ale** and **czy**.

## Joining words

- **i** (and) adds: **Szalik jest ciężki i ciepły.** — The scarf is heavy and warm.
- **a** (and, whereas) contrasts two things: **Ona ma czarne spodnie, a on białe.** — She has black trousers, and he has white.
- **ale** (but): **Krzesło jest stare, ale wygodne.** — The chair is old, but comfortable.
- **czy** (or) in questions: **Chcesz szalik czy koszulę?** — Do you want a scarf or a shirt?

## Wearing

- **On nosi niebieską koszulę.** — He wears a blue shirt.
- **Noszę rękawiczki w zimie.** — I wear gloves in winter.
- **Zimą noszę zawsze czapkę, a w lecie nie.** — In winter, I always wear a hat, but not in summer.

## Plural-only words

Some clothes are always plural:

- **Nowe majtki są wygodne.** — New underwear is comfortable.

> **Watch out:** **a** is not plain "and". Use it to set two things against each other (**a on białe**); to simply add, use **i**.
