In this lesson: **food** and the **accusative case**, the form for the thing you eat, buy or see.

## Feminine words change

Feminine words ending in **‑a** take **‑ę** when they are the thing you eat or see:

- **My jemy kolację.** — We are eating dinner. (kolacja → kolację)
- **Widzimy twoją pizzę na stole.** — We see your pizza on the table.
- **Czy czytasz książkę i jesz śniadanie?** — Are you reading a book and eating breakfast?

## Most other words stay the same

Neuter words and masculine things don't change:

- **Ja jem chleb.** — I am eating bread.
- **Ona je mięso.** — She is eating meat.
- **Oni kupują masło i chleb.** — They are buying butter and bread.

Masculine people and animals add **‑a**:

- **Widzę Vlada z jedzeniem** — I see Vlad with food.

## "To eat" is irregular

- **Ty jesz jajko?** — Are you eating an egg?
- **On pije mleko i je mięso.** — He is drinking milk and eating meat.
- **Oni jedzą śniadanie.** — They are having breakfast.

> **Watch out:** **je** here means "eats", not "is": **Ona je mięso.** — She is eating meat.
