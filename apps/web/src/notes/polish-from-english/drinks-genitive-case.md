In this lesson: **drinks** and the **genitive case**, the form used after **nie** ("not"), after **bez** ("without") and for "a bottle of".

## After "not"

In a negative sentence, the thing you have or drink takes the genitive:

- **Ja nie mam wody.** — I do not have water. (woda → wody)
- **On nie ma kawy.** — He does not have coffee. (kawa → kawy)
- **Ona nie pije wina.** — She does not drink wine. (wino → wina)
- **My nie pijemy piwa.** — We do not drink beer.

Without **nie**, the word doesn't change:

- **Ty pijesz wino?** — Do you drink wine?

## After "without"

- **Pijesz bez mleka?** — Do you drink without milk?
- **Ja lubię sok bez cukru.** — I like juice without sugar.

## "A bottle of", "a glass of"

- **Kieliszek wina dla mnie, proszę.** — A glass of wine for me, please.
- **Butelka wody leży na stole.** — A bottle of water is lying on the table.

Feminine **‑a** becomes **‑y**, neuter **‑o** becomes **‑a**, and many masculine drinks and foods take **‑u**: **soku**, **cukru**.

> **Watch out:** adding **nie** changes the ending: **Ty pijesz wino?** but **Ona nie pije wina.**
