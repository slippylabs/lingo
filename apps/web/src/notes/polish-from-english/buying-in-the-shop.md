In this lesson: **shopping** words and asking about **price**.

## Buying and selling

- **Chcę kupić chleb.** — I want to buy bread.
- **Ona kupuje książkę.** — She is buying a book.
- **On sprzedaje mleko.** — He sells milk.
- **Cena jest wysoka.** — The price is high.

## "A piece of", "the price of"

These take the genitive:

- **Kawałek sera jest dobry.** — A piece of cheese is good. (ser → sera)
- **Kawałek chleba jest mały.** — The piece of bread is small.
- **Cena masła jest dobra.** — The price of butter is good.

## Asking about something

*pytać o* + the accusative is "to ask about":

- **Klient pyta o cenę.** — The customer asks about the price.
- **Klient się pyta o zniżkę.** — The customer asks for a discount.

> **Watch out:** **kupić** is "to buy" once, all the way through; **kupuje** is "is buying": **Chcę kupić chleb**, **Ona kupuje książkę.**
