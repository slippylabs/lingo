In this lesson: **food**, the irregular verb **"to eat"**, and the **accusative case**, the form of the thing you eat, buy or see.

## What changes in the accusative

- feminine ‑а becomes ‑у: **Мы ямо вячэру.** — We are eating dinner.
- feminine describing words end in ‑ую: **Ён есць салодкую ежу.** — He eats sweet food.
- masculine things and neuter words stay the same: **Я ем хлеб.** — I am eating bread.
- **Яна есць мяса.** — She is eating meat.
- masculine people and animals add ‑а: **Я бачу Улада з ежай** — I see Vlad with food

## "To eat" is irregular

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| я | **ем** | мы | **ямо** |
| ты | **ясі** | вы | *ясце* |
| ён, яна | **есць** | яны | *ядуць* |

## Meals

- **сняданак** — breakfast
- **абед** — lunch
- **вячэра** — dinner
- **Яны снедаюць.** — They are having breakfast.

## Will buy

**Я куплю масла.** — I will buy butter. **Куплю** looks like the present, but it comes from **купіць**, a verb for one finished action, so it means "I will buy".

> **Watch out:** "she eats" is **есць**, which is not the same word as **ёсць** ("there is").
