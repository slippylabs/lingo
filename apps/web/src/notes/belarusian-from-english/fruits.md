In this lesson: **fruit**, **plurals**, and describing words that **match** their noun.

## Describing words match

- **Яблык чырвоны.** — The apple is red. (masculine)
- **Груша смачная.** — The pear is tasty. (feminine)
- **Лімон кіслы і жоўты.** — The lemon is sour and yellow.
- **Дыня вялікая і салодкая.** — The melon is big and sweet.

There's no word for "is": you just put the noun and the describing word together.

## Plurals

Most nouns make the plural with ‑ы, or ‑і after soft consonants. Plural describing words end in ‑ыя or ‑ія:

- **Апельсіны салодкія.** — The oranges are sweet.
- **Грушы ляжаць на стале.** — The pears are lying on the table.
- **Ягады ў місцы.** — The berries are in the bowl.

## Grapes

**вінаград** is one word for a whole bunch, so it is singular:

- **Мы купляем вінаград.** — We are buying grapes.
- **Вінаград расце на вінаградніку.** — Grapes grow on a vineyard.

> **Watch out:** ш is always hard in Belarusian, so the plural of **груша** is **грушы**, with ‑ы.
