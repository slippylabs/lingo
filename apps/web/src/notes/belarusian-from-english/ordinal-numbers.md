In this lesson: **ordinal numbers**, words like **first**, **second** and **hundredth**.

## Ordinals are describing words

They change their ending to match the noun, just like describing words do:

- **трыццаты мужчына** — thirtieth man
- **соты долар** — hundredth dollar
- **чатырыста пятая гара** — four hundred fifth mountain (feminine)

## In a long number, only the last word changes

- **дзвесце першы брат** — two hundred first brother
- **дзвесце трэці камп'ютар** — two hundred third computer
- **восемсот другі долар** — eight hundred second dollar

## Round hundreds are one word

- **двухсоты гадзіннік** — two hundredth clock
- **шасцісоты ровар** — six hundredth bicycle

## With "hour"

**гадзіна** (hour) is feminine, so ordinals used with it take feminine endings: **першай**, **другой**, **трэцяй**, **пятай**.

> **Watch out:** **трэці** (third) has soft endings: **трэцяй**, not *трэцай*.
