In this lesson: **hundreds and thousands**.

## The hundreds

- **дзвесце** — two hundred
- **трыста** — three hundred
- **чатырыста** — four hundred
- **пяцьсот** — five hundred
- **дзевяцьсот** — nine hundred
- **тысяча** — thousand

Three and four hundred end in ‑ста; five to nine hundred end in ‑сот.

## The last word still decides

- **семсот адзін ровар** — seven hundred one bicycles
- **дзвесце тры камп'ютары** — two hundred three computers
- **дзевяцьсот ровараў** — nine hundred bicycles

## Thousands

**тысяча** is feminine, so "two thousand" uses **дзве**:

- **дзве тысячы дзевяцьсот сабак** — two thousand nine hundred dogs

> **Watch out:** Belarusian uses an apostrophe to keep sounds apart: **камп'ютары**.
