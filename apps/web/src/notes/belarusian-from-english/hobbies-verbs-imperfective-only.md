In this lesson: **hobbies** and the **present tense**: how a verb's ending shows who is doing the action.

## The ending shows the person

Most verbs here follow one of two patterns. The first is like **чытаць** (to read):

| | ending | example |
|---|---|---|
| я | ‑ю | **чытаю** |
| ты | ‑еш | **чытаеш** |
| ён, яна | ‑е | **чытае** |
| мы | ‑ем | **гуляем** |
| яны | ‑юць | **слухаюць** |

- **Я чытаю кнігу.** — I am reading a book.
- **Яны слухаюць музыку.** — They are listening to music.

The second pattern has ‑і- in its endings, like **глядзець** (to watch):

- **Ты глядзіш серыял?** — Are you watching a series?
- **Мы глядзім фільм.** — We are watching a film.
- **Вы вучыце мову.** — You are learning a language.

## One form, two meanings

All the verbs in this lesson are *imperfective*: the kind used for ongoing or repeated actions. So **Я малюю карціну** means both "I paint a picture" and "I am painting a picture".

You can drop the pronoun, because the ending already says who: **Вучымся тут.** — We are learning here.

> **Watch out:** you play a game with **гуляе ў** but an instrument with **грае на**: **Ён гуляе ў футбол** but **Яна грае на гітары**.
