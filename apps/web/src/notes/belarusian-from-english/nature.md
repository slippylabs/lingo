In this lesson: **nature words** and small words for **direction and position**.

## Through, between, towards, behind

- **Рака цячэ праз даліну.** — The river flows through the valley. (**праз** + accusative)
- **Даліна ляжыць між гарамі.** — The valley lies between the mountains. (**між** + instrumental)
- **Вада ідзе да ракі.** — Water goes to the river. (**да** + genitive)
- **Ідзём да дуба.** — We are going towards the oak.
- **Поле за домам.** — The field is behind the house. (**за** + instrumental)

## Into a place, or in it

Going into a place uses the accusative; being somewhere uses the locative:

- **Мы ідзём у лес.** — We are going to the forest.
- **Ружа расце на полі.** — The rose grows in the field.
- **Ён стаіць на камені.** — He is standing on the stone.

## Plurals

- **Камяні цяжкія.** — The stones are heavy.
- **Кветкі прыгожа квітнеюць.** — The flowers bloom beautifully.

> **Watch out:** with movement, keep the accusative: **у лес** means "into the forest".
