In this lesson: **tens up to a hundred**, and how to build numbers like **seventy-eight**.

## The tens

- **дваццаць** — twenty
- **трыццаць** — thirty
- **сорак** — forty
- **пяцьдзясят** — fifty
- **дзевяноста** — ninety
- **сто** — one hundred

**сорак** and **дзевяноста** don't follow the pattern, so learn them separately.

## Building numbers

Say the tens, then the units, as separate words:

- **семдзесят восем рублёў** — seventy eight rubles
- **восемдзесят сем долараў** — eighty seven dollars

## The last word decides

The noun follows the last number word:

- **пяцьдзясят адзін гадзіннік** — fifty one clocks (after "one": singular)
- **дваццаць чатыры рэчы** — twenty four things (after 2–4: plural)
- **сорак долараў** — forty dollars (otherwise: the "of" form)

> **Watch out:** **сто адзін ровар** has a singular noun, because the last word is "one".
