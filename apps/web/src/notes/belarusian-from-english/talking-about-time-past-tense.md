In this lesson: **telling the time** and the **past tense**.

## The past matches gender, not person

The past tense ending depends on whether the subject is masculine, feminine, neuter or plural:

| | ending | "was" |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | ‑ў | **быў** |
| feminine | ‑ла | **была** |
| neuter | ‑ла | **было** |
| plural | ‑лі | **былі** |

- **Учора быў панядзелак.** — Yesterday was Monday.
- **Учора яна была дома ў восем гадзін.** — Yesterday she was at home at eight o'clock.
- **Раніцай не было вельмі цёпла.** — In the morning it was not very warm.
- **Вы ішлі ў кіно?** — Were you going to the cinema?

"I" and "you" use the same forms, so a man says **Я быў дома раніцай** and a woman says *я была*.

## Nothing

- **Яны нічога не рабілі.** — They did nothing.

## The time

- **Колькі зараз часу?** — What time is it?
- **Бачымся ўвечары ў дзевяць гадзін.** — We will see each other in the evening at nine o'clock.

> **Watch out:** the masculine past ends in **‑ў**, never ‑л: **быў**, **меў**.
