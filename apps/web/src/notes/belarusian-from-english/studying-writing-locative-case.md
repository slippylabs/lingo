In this lesson: **school words**, the **locative case** (the form for where something is, after **на** and **у**), and writing **with** a pen.

## Where: на and у

- **Кніга на стале.** — The book is on the table. (стол → на стале)
- **Школа ў цэнтры.** — The school is in the center.
- **Пішу на паперы пяром.** — I am writing on paper with a pen.

The ending is usually ‑е; after р and some other hard sounds it is ‑ы (**ў цэнтры**, **на паперы**).

## Writing with something

The tool you use takes the instrumental ending, with no word for "with":

- **Я хутка пішу гэтым пяром.** — I am writing quickly with this pen.

## Teachers and students

- **настаўнік** — male teacher
- **Яна настаўніца.** — She is a teacher.
- **Ён вучань.** — He is a student.

## Many

**шмат** (many, a lot of) is followed by the "of" form: **Яна мае шмат заданняў.** — She has many assignments.

> **Watch out:** a place changes its ending after **на** and **у**: it's **на стале**, not *на стол*.
