In this lesson: **shopping**: **buying, selling, prices** and **pieces of** things.

## Buy and sell

- **Хачу купіць хлеб.** — I want to buy bread.
- **Яна купляе кнігу.** — She is buying a book.
- **Ён прадае мяса.** — He sells meat.

**купіць** is for one finished purchase; *купляць* is for buying in general or right now.

## A piece of, the price of

The "of" form (genitive) follows:

- **Кавалак сыру добры.** — A piece of cheese is good.
- **Кавалак хлеба малы.** — The piece of bread is small.
- **Цана масла добрая.** — The price of butter is good.

## Asking about

- **Пакупнік пытае пра цану.** — The customer asks about the price.
- **Пакупнік пытае пра зніжку.** — The customer asks for a discount.

## To the seller

- **Дам чэк прадаўцу.** — I will give the receipt to the seller.

> **Watch out:** **прадавец** loses its е when it changes: **прадаўцу**.
