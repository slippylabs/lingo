In this lesson: **rooms and furniture**, and small words for **where things are**.

## Where things are

Each of these words needs its own case:

- **Крэсла каля стала.** — The chair is near the table. (**каля** + genitive)
- **Скрыня пад ложкам.** — The chest is under the bed. (**пад** + instrumental)
- **Лампа свеціць над сталом.** — The lamp shines above the table. (**над** + instrumental)
- **На стале ляжыць кніга.** — A book is lying on the table. (**на** + locative)
- **У пакоі спакойна.** — It is quiet in the room. (**у** + locative)

## Made of

- **Скрыня з дрэва.** — The chest is made of wood.

## Always plural

**дзверы** (door) only exists in the plural, so its describing word is plural too:

- **Дзверы закрытыя.** — The doors are closed.
- **Дзверы вядуць у кухню.** — The doors lead to the kitchen.

> **Watch out:** the "o" in **стол** is only written while it is stressed. When the ending takes the stress it becomes **а**: **каля стала**.
