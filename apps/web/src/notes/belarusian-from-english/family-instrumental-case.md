In this lesson: **family words** and the **instrumental case**, the form a noun takes after **з** ("with").

## "With" changes the ending

After **з** meaning "with", the noun changes its ending:

| | ending | example |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | ‑ам | **з дзедам** |
| feminine | ‑ай / ‑ой | **з цёткай**, **з дачкой** |
| plural | ‑амі | **з братамі** |

- **Ён гаворыць з дзедам.** — He is talking with his grandfather.
- **Ён жыве з сынам.** — He lives with his son.
- **Яны едуць з цёткай.** — They are going with their aunt.
- **Ідзём з дачкой.** — We are going with our daughter.
- **Ідзём на прагулку з братамі.** — We are going for a walk with brothers.

## "My", "your" and "our" change too

- **з маёй бабуляй** — with my grandmother
- **з тваёй сястрой** — with your sister
- **з нашымі дзецьмі** — with our children

## Two to remember

- **маці** (mother) never changes: **Яна ідзе з маці.** — She is going with her mother.
- **дзеці** (children) becomes **дзецьмі**.

To ask "with whom?", say **з кім**: **З кім ты ідзеш?** — Who are you going with?

> **Watch out:** don't leave the noun as it is after **з**: it's **з дзедам**, never *з дзед*.
