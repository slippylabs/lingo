In this lesson: **asking the way** and **giving directions**.

## Where is it, where is it going

- **Дзе вакзал?** — Where is the station?
- **Дзе бліжэйшы гатэль?** — Where is the nearest hotel?
- **Куды ідзяце?** — Which way are you going?
- **Куды едзе той цягнік?** — Where is that train going?

## Giving directions

Tell one friend **ідзі**; tell several people, or one person politely, **ідзіце**:

- **Ідзі проста да моста.** — Go straight to the bridge.
- **Ідзіце проста і потым направа.** — Go straight and then to the right.
- **Вакзал налева.** — The station is on the left.

## Near and far

- **Бібліятэка далёка.** — The library is far away.
- **Плошча далёка ад гатэля.** — The square is far from the hotel.

## Asking politely

- **Можаце паказаць шлях?** — Can you show me the way?

> **Watch out:** **дзе** asks where something is; **куды** asks where someone or something is going.
