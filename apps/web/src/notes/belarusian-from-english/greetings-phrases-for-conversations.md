In this lesson: **greetings**, **thank you** and **sorry**, and short **everyday replies**.

## Greetings

- **Добрай раніцы!** — Good morning!
- **Добры дзень!** — Good day!
- **Добры вечар!** — Good evening!
- **Дабранач!** — Good night!
- **Прывітанне!** — Hello!

## A short conversation

- **Як справы?** — How are you?
- **Я добра, дзякуй!** — I am well, thank you!
- **А ты?** — And you?
- **Як цябе завуць?** — What is your name?
- **Мяне завуць Бартэк.** — My name is Bartek.

## Thanks and sorry

- **Вялікі дзякуй.** — Thank you very much.
- **Прабачце, не разумею.** — Excuse me, I don't understand.

**Прабачце** is polite, or for several people; **прабач** is for one friend.

## Yes, no, maybe

- **Так, можа быць.** — Yes, it is possible.
- **Не, нельга.** — No, it is not possible.
- **Не ведаю.** — I don't know.

A yes/no question can start with **ці**: **Ці гэта магчыма?** — Is this possible?

> **Watch out:** Belarusian is spelled the way it sounds, so an unstressed "o" is written **а**. That is why it's **Дабранач** (good night), even though "good" is **добры**.
