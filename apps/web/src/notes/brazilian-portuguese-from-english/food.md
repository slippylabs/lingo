In this lesson: **food and drink**, **eating and drinking**, and **please**.

## "The" is o

All the food words in this lesson use **o** for "the":

- **o pão** — the bread
- **o leite** — the milk
- **o macarrão** — the pasta
- **o casal** — the couple

## Eating and drinking

The verb's ending changes depending on who is doing it:

- **como** — I eat
- **come** — he / she eats
- **bebe** — he / she drinks

Examples:

- **Eu como macarrão** — I eat pasta
- **José come pão** — José eats bread
- **Cecília bebe água** — Cecília drinks water

## Please

- **por favor** — please
- **Água, por favor** — Water, please
- **Bom apetite** — Enjoy your meal (said before eating)

> **Watch out:** **‑ão** (as in **pão** and **macarrão**) is a nasal sound. Say "ow" through your nose.
