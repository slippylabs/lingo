## Eating and drinking

The verb ending changes with the person:

| | comer (eat) | beber (drink) |
|---|---|---|
| I — **eu** | **como** | — |
| he / she / José | **come** | **bebe** |

- **Eu como macarrão** — I eat pasta
- **José come pão** — José eats bread
- **Cecília bebe água** — Cecília drinks water

## Masculine nouns again

**o pão** (the bread), **o leite** (the milk), **o macarrão** (the pasta) and **o casal** (the couple) are all masculine, so they take **o**.

## Polite phrases

- **Por favor** — Please: **Água, por favor** — Water, please
- **Bom apetite** — Enjoy your meal

**Watch out:** the ending *-ão* (as in **pão**, **macarrão**) is a nasal sound — say "ow" through your nose.
