In this lesson: **jobs**, and more **verbs**.

## Jobs for a man or a woman

Most job words end in **‑o** for a man and **‑a** for a woman:

- **médico** / **médica** — doctor
- **bombeiro** / **bombeira** — firefighter
- **fazendeiro** / **fazendeira** — farmer

Examples:

- **A médica bebe água** — The doctor (a woman) drinks water
- **O fazendeiro vê os animais** — The farmer sees the animals

## More verbs

- **O garçom trabalha** — The waiter works
- **O cantor canta muito bem** — The singer sings very well
- **As bombeiras chegam** — The firefighters arrive

For one person, these verbs end in **‑a**. For more than one, they end in **‑am**.

> **Watch out:** you don't say "a" before a job: **Ela é enfermeira** means "She is a nurse", with no *uma*.
