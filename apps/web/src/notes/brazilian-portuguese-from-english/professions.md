## Jobs have a masculine and a feminine form

| man | woman |
|---|---|
| **médico** | **médica** |
| **bombeiro** | **bombeira** |
| **fazendeiro** | **fazendeira** |

- **A médica bebe água** — The (female) doctor drinks water
- **O fazendeiro vê os animais** — The farmer sees the animals
- **As bombeiras chegam** — The firefighters arrive

## -ar verbs

Verbs ending in **-ar** take **-a** for he/she and **-am** for they:

- **O garçom trabalha** — The waiter works
- **O cantor canta muito bem** — The singer sings very well
- **As bombeiras chegam** — The firefighters arrive

**Common mistake:** after **ser** you don't say "a" for a job: **Ela é enfermeira** — She is a nurse.
