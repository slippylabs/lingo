In this lesson: Portuguese has **two verbs for "to be"**.

## ser: what someone or something **is**

Use **ser** for who someone is, their job, or what they're like:

- **Eu sou um estudante** — I am a student
- **Ela é enfermeira** — She is a nurse
- **Eles são inteligentes** — They are smart

## estar: **where** it is, or **how** it is right now

- **Eu estou na escola** — I am at school
- **Ela está doente** — She is ill
- **Eles estão prontos** — They are ready

## The forms

| | ser | estar |
|---|---|---|
| I | **sou** | **estou** |
| you, he, she | **é** | **está** |
| we | **somos** | **estamos** |
| you all, they | **são** | **estão** |

**na** means "in the" or "at the": **na escola** — at school, **na cidade** — in the city.

> **Quick test:** is it about **where** something is, or how it is **right now**? Use **estar**. Otherwise use **ser**.
