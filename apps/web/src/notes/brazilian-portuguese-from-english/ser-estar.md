## Two verbs for "to be"

**ser** — what something *is*: identity, job, character

- **Eu sou um estudante** — I am a student
- **Ela é enfermeira** — She is a nurse
- **Eles são inteligentes** — They are smart

**estar** — where something is, or how it is *right now*

- **Eu estou na escola** — I am at school
- **Ela está doente** — She's ill
- **O gato está limpo** — The cat is clean
- **Eles estão prontos** — They are ready

| | ser | estar |
|---|---|---|
| I | **sou** | **estou** |
| you / he / she | **é** | **está** |
| we | **somos** | **estamos** |
| you all / they | **são** | **estão** |

## in + the

**em** ("in, at") merges with the article: *em + a =* **na**, *em + o = no*: **na escola** — at school, **na cidade** — in the city.

A quick test: if it's *where* or *how someone is right now*, use **estar**.
