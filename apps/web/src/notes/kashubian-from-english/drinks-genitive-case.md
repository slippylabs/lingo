In this lesson: **drinks** and the **genitive case**, the form a word takes after **not** and **without**.

## Drinks

- **wòda** — water, and **winò** — wine
- **piwò** — beer, and **kawa** — coffee
- **czaj** — tea, and **sok** — juice

## After "not have", "not drink"

When you say you don't have or don't drink something, the drink changes its ending:

- **Jô nie móm wòdë.** — I do not have water. (**wòda**)
- **Òna nie pije wina.** — She does not drink wine. (**winò**)
- **Më nie pijemë piwa.** — We do not drink beer. (**piwò**)
- **Òn nie mô kawë.** — He does not have coffee. (**kawa**)

## After "without" and "of"

**bez** ("without") takes the same form, and so does the drink in "a bottle of …":

- **Pijesz bez mléka?** — Do you drink without milk?
- **Të môsz flaszkã sokù?** — Do you have a bottle of juice?

> **Watch out:** compare **Të pijesz winò?** (do you drink wine) with **Òna nie pije wina.** (she does not drink wine). The **nie** changes the ending.
