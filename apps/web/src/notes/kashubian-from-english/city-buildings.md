In this lesson: **places in the city**.

## Buildings

- **To je wiôldżi gard.** — This is a big city.
- **Stacëjô je bliskò centra.** — The station is near the center.
- **Môst jidze przez rzékã.** — The bridge goes over the river.
- **Kòscoł je stôri i biôłi.** — The church is old and white.
- **Skłôd je òdemkłi dzysô.** — The shop is open today.

## In and at

- **Kùpùjemë chléb w skłôdze.** — We buy bread in the shop. (**skłôd**)
- **Czëtóm knégã w knéżnicë.** — I read a book in the library. (**knéżnica**)
- **Na tôrgu stoji fontana.** — A fountain stands in the square. (**tôrg**)

## Going to

- **Jidzemë do tôrgu.** — We are going to the square.

> **Watch out:** **bliskò** ("near") changes the next word: **bliskò centra**, **bliskò knéżnicë**.
