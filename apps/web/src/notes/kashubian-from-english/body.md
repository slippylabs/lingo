In this lesson: **parts of the body** and saying **it hurts**.

## The body

- **głowa** — head, and **lëco** — face
- **rãka** — hand, and **noga** — leg
- **ùchò** — ear, and **nôs** — nose
- **serce** — heart

## It hurts

**bòli** means "hurts":

- **Mòja rãka bòli.** — My hand hurts.
- **Żołądk cébie bòli.** — Your stomach hurts.

## Describing

- **Òna mô długą nogã.** — She has a long leg.
- **Òn mô mòdré snôżé òczë.** — He has beautiful blue eyes.
- **Jej serce biéje chùtczi.** — Her heart beats fast.

> **Watch out:** **òkò** ("eye") has an irregular plural: **òczë** ("eyes").
