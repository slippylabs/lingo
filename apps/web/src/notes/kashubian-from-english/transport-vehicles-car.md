In this lesson: **cars, buses, trains** and **roads**.

## Getting around

- **Aùto stoji na ùlicë.** — The car is standing on the street.
- **Aùtobus jidze do centra.** — The bus goes to the center.
- **Ban jidze na stacëjã.** — The train goes to the station.
- **Droga jidze przez las.** — The road goes through the forest.
- **Szôfera je òstrożni.** — The driver is careful.

## "Goes" for vehicles

Kashubian uses **jidze** ("goes") for cars and trains too:

- **Szôfera jidze chùtczi.** — The driver drives fast.

## Traffic

- **Semafor pòkôzëje czerwòné.** — The traffic light shows red.
- **Chùtosc je ògraniczonô.** — The speed is limited.

> **Watch out:** **ban** is the train and **aùtobus** is the bus: **Ban jidze na nordã.** — The train goes to the north.
