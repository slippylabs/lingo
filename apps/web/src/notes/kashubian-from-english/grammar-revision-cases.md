In this lesson: a **review of the cases**, the different endings a word takes depending on its job in the sentence.

## "There is no …"

After **nie mô** and **nie je** ("there is no"), the word takes the "of" form (the genitive case):

- **Brata tu nie je.** — There is no brother here. (**brat**)
- **Miona on nie mô.** — He has no name. (**miono**)
- **Chłopów tu nie widzymë.** — We do not see men here.

## Giving to someone

- **Dóm białce.** — I give to the woman. (**białka**)

## Where

- **Òna je w gardze.** — She is in the city. (**gard**)
- **Òna gôdô ò bratach.** — She talks about brothers.

## With

- **Òna z chłopã gôdô.** — She talks with her husband.
- **To mòżna rézac nożã.** — This can be cut with a knife.

> **Watch out:** one word can show up in many forms: **brat**, **Brata tu nie je**, **ò bratach**. Learn the small word in front with it.
