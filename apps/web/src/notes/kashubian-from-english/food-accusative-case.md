In this lesson: **food words** and the **accusative case**, the form of the thing you eat, buy or see.

## Food

- **chléb** — bread, and **masło** — butter
- **mlékò** — milk, and **miãso** — meat
- **jajkò** — egg
- **sniôdanié** — breakfast, **òbiôd** — lunch, **wieczérzô** — dinner

## The thing you act on

Many words stay the same:

- **Jô jem chléb.** — I am eating bread.
- **Òna je miãso.** — She is eating meat.
- **Òn miłuje mlékò.** — He likes milk.

Women's words ending in **‑a** or **‑ô** change to **‑ã**:

- **Më jemë wieczérzã.** — We are eating dinner. (**wieczérzô**)
- **Czëtôsz të knégã i jesz sniôdanié?** — Are you reading a book and eating breakfast? (**knéga**)

> **Watch out:** **je** can mean "is" or "eats". **Òna je miãso** means "She is eating meat"; the rest of the sentence tells you which.
