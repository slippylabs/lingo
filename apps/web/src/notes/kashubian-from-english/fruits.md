In this lesson: **fruit** and how describing words match the fruit's gender.

## Fruit

- **Jôbłkò je czerwòné.** — The apple is red.
- **Grëszka je smaczna.** — The pear is tasty.
- **Banan je żôłti.** — The banana is yellow.
- **Limòna je kwasnô i żôłtô.** — The lemon is sour and yellow.
- **Melón je wiôldżi i słodczi.** — The melon is big and sweet.

## One fruit, many fruits

- **Pòmarańczë są słodcé.** — The oranges are sweet.
- **Grëszczi leżą na stole.** — The pears are lying on the table.

## Juice "from" fruit

**z** plus the fruit says what something is made from:

- **Òna pije sok z pòmarańczów.** — She is drinking orange juice.

> **Watch out:** the describing word changes with the fruit: **Banan je żôłti**, but **Limòna je kwasnô i żôłtô**.
