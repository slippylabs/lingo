In this lesson: **words for character**: good, brave, wise, lazy, and how to say **would**.

## Character

- **Òn to je dobri człowiek.** — He is a good person.
- **Òn je słabi w sporce.** — He is weak in sports.
- **Òna je dobrô i sczérô.** — She is good and sincere.
- **Złé słowa bólą.** — Bad words hurt.

## A describing word as a person

A describing word on its own can mean "a … person":

- **Mądri wie wiele.** — A smart person knows much.
- **Òdwôżni gôdô prôwdã.** — Brave people speak the truth.
- **Głëpi sã nie ùczi.** — Stupid people do not learn.
- **Leniwi nie chce nic robic.** — Lazy people do not want to do anything.

## "Would" and "if"

**bë** means "would". Put it with the past form of the verb:

- **jô bë béł** — I would be
- **òni bë bëlë** — they would be

"If" is **czej bë**, and the second half starts with **to** ("then"):

- **Czej bë jô béł òdwôżni, to jô bë sã nie bòjôł.** — If I were brave, I would not be afraid.
- **Czej bë wë nie bëlë słabi, to wë bë robòtalë.** — If you were not weak, you would work.

> **Watch out:** the describing word changes for a woman: **dobri człowiek**, but **Òna je dobrô**.
