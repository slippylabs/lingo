In this lesson: **words for character**: good, brave, wise, lazy.

## Character

- **On to je dobri człowiek.** — He is a good person.
- **On je słabi w sporce.** — He is weak in sports.
- **Òna je dobrô i sczérô.** — She is good and sincere.
- **Złé słowa bólą.** — Bad words hurt.

## A describing word as a person

A describing word on its own can mean "a … person":

- **Mądri wie wiele.** — A smart person knows much.
- **Òdwôżni gôdô prôwdã.** — Brave people speak the truth.
- **Głëpi sã nie ùczi.** — Stupid people do not learn.
- **Leniwi nie chce nic robic.** — Lazy people do not want to do anything.

## "If …"

Sentences with **Jak** … **to** mean "if …, then …". This lesson's "if" sentences are long; read them as whole phrases.

> **Watch out:** the describing word changes for a woman: **dobri człowiek**, but **Òna je dobrô**.
