In this lesson: **rooms**, **furniture**, and verbs for where things are.

## Furniture

- **Stół stoji w pòstrzódku pòkoju.** — The table stands in the middle of the room.
- **Stólc je bliskò stola.** — The chair is near the table.
- **Łóżko je miãtczi.** — The bed is soft.
- **Òkno je òdemkłé.** — The window is open.
- **Dzwierzé są zamkłé.** — The doors are closed.
- **Pòłca wisy na scanie.** — The shelf hangs on the wall.

## Stands, lies, hangs

Kashubian says how something is placed:

- **stoji** — stands: **Stół stoji w pòstrzódku pòkoju.**
- **leżi** — lies: **Na stole leżi knéga.** — A book is lying on the table.
- **wisy** — hangs

## One and many

More than one thing takes **są** ("are") instead of **je** ("is"):

- **Łóżko je nowé.** — The bed is new.
- **Dzwierzé są zamkłé.** — The doors are closed.

> **Watch out:** **stół** is "table" and **stólc** is "chair". They look alike.
