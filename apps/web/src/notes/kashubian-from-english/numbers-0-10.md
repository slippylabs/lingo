In this lesson: **numbers 0 to 10** and the form of the noun after a number.

## One

"One" follows the noun's gender:

- **jeden** — one, for masculine words
- **jednô białka** — one woman
- **jedné pòle** — one field

## Two to four

The noun is plural. "Two" is **dwa** for most words and **dwie** for feminine ones:

- **dwa chłôpce** — two boys
- **dwie białczi** — two women
- **trzë kòtë** — three cats
- **sztërë dolary** — four dollars

## Five and up

From five on, the noun takes the "of many" form, often ending in **‑ów**:

- **piãc kòtów** — five cats
- **òsmë dolarów** — eight dollars
- **dzesãc gòdzënków** — ten clocks

> **Watch out:** the noun changes after the number: **trzë kòtë**, but **piãc kòtów**.
