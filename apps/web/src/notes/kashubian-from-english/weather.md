In this lesson: **the weather** and short sentences with **it is**.

## Weather words

- **Pògòda je dobrô.** — The weather is good.
- **Pôdô deszcz.** — It is raining.
- **Sniég leżi na zemie.** — Snow lies on the ground.
- **Słuncé swiecy.** — The sun is shining.
- **Wiatrzënk dmie sëlno.** — The wind is blowing strongly.

## "It is warm"

Kashubian needs no word for "it":

- **Dzysô je ciepło.** — It is warm today.
- **Dzysô je chmùrno.** — It is cloudy today.
- **W Rusczi je zëmno.** — In Russia it is cold.

> **Watch out:** rain and snow "fall": **Pôdô deszcz** literally says "rain falls".
