In this lesson: **hobbies** and how verbs change for **I, you, he, we, they**.

## Hobbies

- **Ja czëtóm knégã.** — I am reading a book.
- **Òna pisze lëstë.** — She is writing letters.
- **On grô w balë.** — He is playing football.
- **Më òbzérómë film.** — We are watching a film.
- **Òni słëchają mùzëkã.** — They are listening to music.
- **On spiéwô spiéwkã.** — He is singing songs.

## One form for "I read" and "I am reading"

Kashubian has no separate "‑ing" form. **Ja czëtóm** means both "I read" and "I am reading".

## The verb changes for each person

Dictionary forms end in **‑c**: **czëtac** — to read, **grac** — to play. Then the ending changes:

- **Ja czëtóm** — I read
- **Czëtôsz të romani?** — Do you read novels?
- **Òna sedzy i czëtô.** — She is sitting and reading.

> **Watch out:** "to learn" comes with **sã**: **Wë ùczëce sã jãzëka.** — You are learning a language.
