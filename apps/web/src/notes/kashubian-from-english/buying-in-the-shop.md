In this lesson: **shopping**: prices, buying and selling.

## In the shop

- **Chce kùpic chléb.** — He wants to buy bread.
- **On przedôwô miãso.** — He sells meat.
- **Òna kùpùje knégã.** — She is buying a book.
- **Cena je wësokô.** — The price is high.
- **Tôrba je cãżkô.** — The bag is heavy.
- **Kiełbasa je swiéżô.** — The sausage is fresh.

## "A piece of …"

**kùs** ("piece") takes the "of" form after it:

- **Kùs sera je dobri.** — A piece of cheese is good.
- **Kùs chleba je môłi.** — The piece of bread is small.

> **Watch out:** **kùpic** is "to buy" and **przedôwac** is "to sell". The seller is the **przedôwca**.
