In this lesson: **days of the week** and **months**.

## Days

- **pòlëdzôłk** — Monday, and **wtórk** — Tuesday
- **strzódka** — Wednesday, and **czwiôrtk** — Thursday
- **piątk** — Friday, **sobòta** — Saturday, **niedzela** — Sunday
- **Pòlëdzôłk je pierszi dzéń w tidzéniu.** — Monday is the first day of the week.
- **Niedzela to je dzéń òdpòczënkù.** — Sunday is the day of rest.

## On a day

Use **w**:

- **W piątk wieczór jidzemë na film.** — On Friday evening we are going to the movies.

## Months

Most Kashubian months have their own names, not the Latin ones:

- **stëcznik** — January, and **gromicznik** — February
- **lëpińc** — July, and **zélnik** — August
- **rujan** — October, and **gòdnik** — December
- **W gromicznikù je wiele sniegù.** — In February there is a lot of snow.

> **Watch out:** "in a month" changes the ending: **gromicznik** becomes **W gromicznikù**.
