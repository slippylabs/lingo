In this lesson: **north, south, east, west** and **left, right, straight**.

## The four directions

- **norda** — north, and **pòłnié** — south
- **pòrénk** — east, and **zôpôd** — west
- **Norda je zëmnô.** — The north is cold.
- **Pòłnié je ciepłé.** — The south is warm.
- **Na nordze je sniég.** — In the north there is snow.
- **Żëjemë na pòłniu.** — We live in the south.

## Directions as describing words

- **Zôpôdni wiatrzënk dmie.** — A western wind is blowing.
- **Pòrénkòwô mùzëka je jinteresantnô.** — Eastern music is interesting.

## Left, right, straight

- **wprôwò** — right, **wlewò** — left, **prosto** — straight
- **Jidzemë dzysô wprôwò i pòtemù prosto.** — Today we are going right and then straight.

> **Watch out:** "in the north" uses **na**: **Na nordze**, **na pòłniu**.
