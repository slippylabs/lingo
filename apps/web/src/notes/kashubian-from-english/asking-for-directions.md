In this lesson: **asking the way** and **giving directions**.

## Asking

- **Dze je stacëjô?** — Where is the station?
- **Kùdë jidze droga?** — Which way does the road go?
- **Dze je bliski hotel?** — Where is the nearest hotel?
- **Mòżece pòkôzac drogã?** — Can you show me the way?

## Answering

- **Jidzë prosto do môsta.** — Go straight to the bridge.
- **Jidzece prosto i pòtemù wprôwò.** — Go straight and then to the right.
- **Stacëjô je wlewò.** — The station is on the left.
- **Knéżnica je dalekò.** — The library is far away.

> **Watch out:** **dze** asks "where (is it)?", **kùdë** asks "which way (does it go)?".
