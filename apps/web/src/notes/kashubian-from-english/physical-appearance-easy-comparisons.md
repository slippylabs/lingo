In this lesson: **describing people's looks** and making **simple comparisons**.

## Looks

- **On mô długé włosë.** — He has long hair.
- **Òna mô zielené òczë.** — She has green eyes.
- **Jegò włoscë są plëwé.** — His hair is blonde.
- **Stôrô białka nosy czapkã.** — The old woman wears a hat.
- **Grëbi chłop jidze pò ùlicë.** — The fat man is walking down the street.

## More … than

This lesson compares with **wicy** ("more") and **jakno** ("than"):

- **Stôri lëdze są wicy mądri jakno më.** — Old people are wiser than us.
- **Grëbi chłop smieje sã wicy jakno ten brzëtczi chłop.** — The fat man laughs more than that ugly man.

**mnié** means "less":

- **Chùdi mô mnié całówi masë jakno grëbi.** — The thin person has less body mass than the fat one.

## As … as

**jakno** also means "like":

- **Snôżé òczë lësną jakno gwiôzdë na niebie.** — Beautiful eyes shine like stars in the sky.

> **Watch out:** "hair" is plural in Kashubian, so it takes **są**: **Jegò włoscë są plëwé.**
