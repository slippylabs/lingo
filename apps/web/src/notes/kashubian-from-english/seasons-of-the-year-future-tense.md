In this lesson: **the seasons** and the **future tense**.

## Seasons

- **lato** — summer, and **zëma** — winter
- **jeséń** — autumn, and **zymk** — spring
- **W lece je wiele słunka.** — In the summer, there is a lot of sun.
- **Zëma mô wiele sniegù.** — Winter has a lot of snow.

## "Will be"

The future uses **bëc** ("to be") in a future form:

| | singular | plural |
|---|---|---|
| I / we | **bãdã** | **bãdzemë** |
| you / you all | **bãdzësz** | **bãdzëce** |
| he, she, it / they | **bãdze** | **bãdą** |

- **W lece bãdze ciepło.** — In the summer it will be warm.

## "Will do"

Put the future form before a verb's dictionary form:

- **W zëmie bãdze padac sniég.** — In the winter it will snow.
- **Òni bãdą widzec zieleną krajãòbraz.** — They will see a green landscape.

> **Watch out:** "in" a season changes the word: **lato** becomes **W lece**, **zëma** becomes **W zëmie**.
