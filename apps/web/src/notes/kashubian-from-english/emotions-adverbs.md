In this lesson: **feelings**, and **adverbs**, words that say **how** something is done.

## Feelings

- **miłota** — love, and **strach** — fear
- **rôdosc** — joy, and **żôłosc** — sadness
- **Ja mëm żôłosc w sercu.** — I have sadness in my heart.
- **Më czëjemë spòkój.** — We feel peaceful.

## How: adverbs end in ‑o

Many "how" words end in **‑o**:

- **Czëjã sã rôdostno dzysô.** — I feel joyful today.
- **Żôłostno on gôdôł.** — He spoke sadly.
- **Rôdostno szedł do dodomù.** — He joyfully went home.
- **Òna pòkôzëje jim swòją robòtã wiesôło.** — She shows them her work joyfully.

> **Watch out:** the describing word **rôdostni** ("joyful") describes a person; the adverb **rôdostno** describes how something is done.
