In this lesson: **tens up to 100**.

## The tens

Most tens end in *‑dzescãt*:

- **trzidzescãt psów** — thirty dogs
- **sztërdzescãt dolarów** — forty dollars
- **piãcdzescãt białków** — fifty women
- **szescdzescãt kòtów** — sixty cats
- **sto gòdzënków** — one hundred clocks

## Tens and ones

Say the ten first, then the one:

- **sedemdzescãt òsem rublów** — seventy eight rubles
- **dzewiãcdzescãt trzë kòmpùterów** — ninety three computers

> **Watch out:** there is no word for "and" between the ten and the one: **òsemdzescãt sedem dolarów** — eighty seven dollars.
