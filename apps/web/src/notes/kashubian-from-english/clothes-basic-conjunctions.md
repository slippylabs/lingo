In this lesson: **clothes**, and the linking words **and**, **but** and **or**.

## Clothes

- **On nosy mòdrą kòszëlã.** — He wears a blue shirt.
- **Szal je cãżczi i ciepłi.** — The scarf is heavy and warm.
- **Kòszëla je w szôfie.** — The shirt is in the wardrobe.
- **Czapka wisy na scanie.** — The hat hangs on the wall.
- **Mòje skarpétczi są krótké.** — My socks are short.

## Linking words

- **i** — and: **Szal je cãżczi i ciepłi.**
- **a** — and/but, for a contrast: **Òna mô czôrné bùksë, a on biôłé.** — She has black trousers, and he has white.
- **czë** — or: **Të chòcesz szal czë kòszëlã.** — You want a scarf or a shirt.

> **Watch out:** **i** simply adds; **a** sets two things against each other. Put a comma before **a**.
