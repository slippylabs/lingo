In this lesson: **jobs** and how to say what someone **is**.

## Jobs

- **ùczëcel** — teacher, and **doktór** — doctor
- **jinżenier** — engineer, and **kùcharz** — cook
- **przedôwca** — seller, and **policjant** — policeman

## "He is a …"

After **je** ("is"), the job word takes the "with" form (the instrumental case):

- **Mój brat je ùczëcelã.** — My brother is a teacher.
- **Mój òjciec je kùcharzã.** — My father is a cook.
- **Òn je jinżenierã w gardze.** — He is an engineer in the city.
- **Òna je doktórkã.** — She is a doctor.

## What they do

- **Doktór pòmôgô lëdzóm.** — The doctor helps people.
- **Kùcharz gotuje wieczérzã.** — The cook is preparing dinner.
- **Przedôwca przedôwô jiedzã.** — The seller sells food.

> **Watch out:** as the subject, the job keeps its dictionary form: **Doktór pòmôgô lëdzóm**, but **Òna je doktórkã**.
