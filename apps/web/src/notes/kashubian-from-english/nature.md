In this lesson: **nature words**: rivers, mountains, forests and fields.

## Nature

- **Rzéka płënie przez dolëznã.** — The river flows through the valley.
- **Góra je wësokô.** — The mountain is high.
- **Trawa je zelonô.** — The grass is green.
- **Pòle je szëroké.** — The field is wide.
- **Zemia je mòkrô.** — The earth is wet.

## Going somewhere

**do** means "to" and changes the next word:

- **Më jidzemë do lasu.** — We are going to the forest.
- **Wòda jidze do rzéki.** — Water goes to the river.

## Where things are

- **przez** — through, and **midzë** — between
- **Dolëzna leżi midzë górama.** — The valley lies between the mountains.
- **Pòle je za domã.** — The field is behind the house.

> **Watch out:** **rzéka** becomes **do rzéki**, and **las** becomes **do lasu**: the ending after **do** depends on the word.
