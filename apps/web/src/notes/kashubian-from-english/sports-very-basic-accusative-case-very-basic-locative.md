In this lesson: **sports**, how to say **play a game**, and **where** something happens.

## Playing a sport

Use **grac** ("to play") with **w** plus the sport:

- **Gróm w bal.** — I play football.
- **Òna grô w kòszôrtã.** — She plays basketball.
- **On grô w tenis.** — He plays tennis.
- **Ja nie gróm w hokej.** — I do not play hockey.

## Where: "na" and "w"

**na** means "on/at" and **w** means "in". The place word changes its ending after them:

- **Òna grô na stadiónie.** — She plays at the stadium. (**stadion**)
- **Stadion je w gardze.** — The stadium is in the city. (**gard**)

## Other sports words

- **Òni biégają zôranô.** — They run in the morning.
- **On trenuje czéżdë dzéń.** — He trains every day.
- **Pławanié je dobré dlô zdrowiô.** — Swimming is good for health.

> **Watch out:** the place word does not stay in its dictionary form: **gard** becomes **w gardze**.
