In this lesson: **time words** and the **past tense** of "to be" and "to go".

## Time words

- **wczora** — yesterday, **dzysô** — today, **jùtrô** — tomorrow
- **minuta** — minute, and **sekùnda** — second
- **Kòle je gòdzënów?** — What time is it?

## "Was" changes with the person

The past tense ends differently for a man, a woman, a thing, or a group:

- **béł** — was (masculine): **Wczora béł pòlëdzôłk.** — Yesterday was Monday.
- **bëła** — was (feminine): **Wczora òna bëła w dodomù ò òsmi gòdzënie.** — Yesterday she was at home at the eighth hour.
- **bëło** — was (neuter): **Ò trzeci gòdzënie dwadzescãt bëło zëmno.** — At twenty past the third hour it was cold.

## Went

- **On szedł do dodomù.** — He went home.
- **Wieczór më szlë do restaurantu.** — In the evening we went to the restaurant.

> **Watch out:** a woman says "went" with **szła**, a man with **szedł**.
