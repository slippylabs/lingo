In this lesson: **shapes**, **lines** and **sides**.

## Shapes

- **To je krąg.** — This is a circle.
- **To je prosti trójkąt.** — This is a simple triangle.
- **Ta lënia je długa.** — This line is long.
- **Lënia je rôwnô.** — The line is straight.
- **Tôczka leżi na lënie.** — The point lies on the line.

## "Has"

**mô** means "has":

- **Stół mô òblëk kòwadrota.** — The table has the shape of a square.

## Drawing

- **Ja malëjã wiôldżi krąg.** — I am drawing a big circle.
- **On maluje figùrë na papiórze.** — He is drawing figures on paper.

> **Watch out:** **lënia** becomes **na lënie** after "on", the same way places change after **na**.
