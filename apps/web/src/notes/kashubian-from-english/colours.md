In this lesson: **colours**, and how they match the thing they describe.

## Colours

- **Mój pies je czôrny.** — My dog is black.
- **Mësz je biôłô.** — The mouse is white.
- **Trawa je zelenô.** — The grass is green.
- **Niebo je mòdré.** — The sky is blue.
- **Słuncé je żôłté.** — The sun is yellow.
- **Kwiatë są czerwòné.** — The flowers are red.

## Endings follow gender

A colour changes its ending to match its noun:

- masculine: **Mój pies je czôrny.**
- feminine: **Czerwònô róża ròsce tu.** — A red rose grows here.
- neuter: **Jegò aùto je czôrné.** — His car is black.

> **Watch out:** with more than one thing, the colour changes too: **Chmùrë są szarë.** — The clouds are grey.
