In this lesson: **hundreds** and **a thousand**.

## The hundreds

- **sto chłôpczów** — one hundred boys
- **dwasta białków** — two hundred women
- **sztëresta kòtów** — four hundred cats
- **piãcset białków** — five hundred women
- **szescset kòtów** — six hundred cats
- **dzewiãcset kòłów** — nine hundred bicycles

From five hundred on, the hundreds end in **‑set**.

## A thousand

- **tësąc òsmë rublów** — one thousand eight rubles
- **dwa tësące dzewiãcset psów** — two thousand nine hundred dogs

> **Watch out:** two, three and four hundred are irregular: **dwasta**, **trzësta**, **sztëresta**.
