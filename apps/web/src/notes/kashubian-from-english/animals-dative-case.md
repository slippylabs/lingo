In this lesson: **animals** and the **dative case**, the form for the one who receives something ("to the cat").

## Animals

- **kòt** — cat, and **pies** — dog
- **kòń** — horse, and **ptôch** — bird
- **mësz** — mouse, and **żaba** — frog
- **niedwiedź** — bear, and **wëlk** — wolf

## Giving to someone

The one who receives takes a new ending. Many words for males end in **‑owi**; feminine words often end in **‑ie**:

- **Ja dóm kòtowi rëbã.** — I give the cat a fish.
- **Dajemë kòniowi wòdã.** — We give the horse water.
- **On pòmôgô ptôchòwi.** — He helps the bird.
- **Më dajemë żabie chléb.** — We give bread to the frog.
- **Ja dóm rëbie wòdã.** — I give the fish water.

## Helping

**pòmògac** ("to help") also takes this form: **Pòmôgómë wëlkòwi.** — We help the wolf.

> **Watch out:** the thing you give and the one who gets it both change: in **Ja dóm kòtowi rëbã**, **rëba** becomes **rëbã** and **kòt** becomes **kòtowi**.
