In this lesson: **kitchen things** and **cooking**.

## In the kitchen

- **Nôż leżi na stole.** — The knife is lying on the table.
- **Widelc je srebrni.** — The fork is silver.
- **Talérz je peļni jiedzë.** — The plate is full of food.
- **Czôszka je napełnionô wòdą.** — The cup is filled with water.
- **Rondel je gòrący.** — The pot is hot
- **Kùchnia je czëstô.** — The kitchen is clean.

## In and from

- **Më gotujemë w rondlu.** — We cook in a pot.
- **Òna pije z czôszki.** — She drinks from a cup.

## "Full of"

**peļni** ("full") is followed by the "of" form of the word:

- **Lëżka je peļnô pieprzu.** — The spoon is full of pepper.

> **Watch out:** **peļni** changes with the noun: **Talérz je peļni** but **Lëżka je peļnô**.
