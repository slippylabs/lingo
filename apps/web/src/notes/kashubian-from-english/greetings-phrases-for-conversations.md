In this lesson: **greetings**, **thank you**, and short **everyday replies**.

## Greetings

- **Dobri dzéń!** — Good day!
- **Dobré zôranô!** — Good morning!
- **Dobri wieczór!** — Good evening!
- **Dobru noc!** — Good night!
- **Jak sã môsz?** — How are you?
- **Jem dobri, dzãka!** — I am well, thank you!

## Names

- **Jak sã nazëwôsz?** — What is your name?
- **Ja sã nazëwôm Bartek.** — My name is Bartek.

## Short replies

- **Jo** — yes, and **nie** — no
- **Mòże bëc.** — Maybe.
- **Nie wiém.** — I don't know.
- **Dzãka wam wiele.** — Thank you very much.
- **Przeproszenié, nie rozmiem.** — Excuse me, I don't understand.

> **Watch out:** **sã** is a small word that belongs to the verb, as in **Jak sã môsz?** and **Ja sã nazëwôm Bartek.** Don't leave it out.
