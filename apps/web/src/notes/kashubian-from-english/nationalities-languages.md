In this lesson: **countries**, **nationalities** and how to say **speak a language**.

## Who you are

- **Jô jem Pòlôchã.** — I am a Pole.
- **Òn je Ruskã.** — He is a Russian.
- **Òna je Charwatkòj.** — She is a Croat.

After **je** ("is") the nationality takes the "with" form (the instrumental case), the same as job words.

## Speaking a language

Use **gôdac** ("speak") with **pò** and a word ending in **‑ù**:

- **Gôdôsz pò miemieckù?** — Do you speak the German language?
- **Anielczón gôdô pò anielskù.** — An Englishman speaks the English language.
- **Charwat gôdô pò charwackù.** — A Croat speaks the Croatian language.

## Country and nation

- **Państwò je Pòlskô.** — The country is Poland.
- **To je mój jãzëk.** — This is my language.

> **Watch out:** the dictionary form is **Pòlôch**, but after **je** it becomes **Pòlôchã**: **Jô jem Pòlôchã.**
