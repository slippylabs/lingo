In this lesson: **ordinal numbers** (first, second, third …) and **telling the hour**.

## First, second, third

- **пръвъ** — first
- **въторъ** — second
- **трєтии** — third
- **чєтврьтъ** — fourth
- **пѧтъ** — fifth

They match their noun like describing words:

- **тридесѧтъи мѫжь** — thirtieth man
- **чєтꙑристє пѧта гора** — four hundred fifth mountain
- **сътънъ доларъ** — hundredth dollar

## Telling the hour

This course says "at the fifth hour" with *о* and a feminine ordinal:

- **о пѧтои части** — at the fifth hour
- **о дєвѧтои части** — at the ninth hour
- **о дъванадесѧтои части** — at the twelfth hour

> **Watch out:** "fifth" is **пѧтъ**, but "five" is **пѧть**; only the last letter differs.
