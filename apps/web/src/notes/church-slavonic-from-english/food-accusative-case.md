In this lesson: **food**, and the **accusative case**: the form of the thing you eat, buy or see.

## The thing you act on

Feminine words ending in **‑а** change it to **‑ѫ** (or **‑ѭ** after a soft sound):

- **ѥда** → **Онъ ꙗстъ сладъкѫ ѥдѫ.** — He eats sweet food.
- **вєчєрꙗ** → **Она любитъ готовити вєчєрѭ.** — She likes to prepare dinner.

Masculine things and neuter words stay as they are:

- **Азъ ꙗмь хлѣбъ.** — I am eating bread.
- **Она ꙗстъ мѧсо.** — She is eating meat.
- **Онъ любитъ млѣко.** — He likes milk.
- **Они кꙋпꙋѭтъ масло и хлѣбъ.** — They are buying butter and bread.

## "That"

- **Азъ знаѭ, чьто тꙑ любиши ꙗица.** — I know that you like eggs.

> **Watch out:** "eats" is **ꙗстъ** and "is" is **ѥстъ**; they differ by one letter. **Она ꙗстъ мѧсо.** — She is eating meat.
