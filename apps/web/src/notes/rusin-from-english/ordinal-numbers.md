In this lesson: **ordinal numbers** (first, second, third…) and **telling the time**.

## Ordinals are describing words

They change their ending like any describing word:

- **сотый долар** — hundredth dollar
- **тридцятый муж** — thirtieth man
- **штириста пятая гора** — four hundred fifth mountain (feminine: **‑ая**)

In a long number, only the last part becomes an ordinal:

- **двісти третий компьютер** — two hundred third computer
- **осемсот другый долар** — eight hundred second dollar

## Telling the time

Use **о**, the ordinal, and **годинї** (hour):

- **о пятой годинї** — at the fifth hour
- **о девятой годинї** — at the ninth hour
- **о первой годинї двадцять** — at twenty past the first hour

The minutes come after the hour. The answers in this lesson say it word for word: "at twenty past the first hour".

> **Watch out:** **година** (hour) is feminine, so the ordinal takes the feminine form: **о пятой годинї**.
