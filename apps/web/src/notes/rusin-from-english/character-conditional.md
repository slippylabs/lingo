In this lesson: **character words** like brave, lazy and wise, and sentences with **"would"**.

## Describing character

- **Она є добра і щира.** — She is good and sincere.
- **Он є слабый в спортї.** — He is weak in sports.
- **Сильный дух є важный.** — A strong spirit is important.
- **Щирый приятель є дорогый.** — A sincere friend is valuable.

## A describing word as a person

A describing word on its own can mean "a person who is…":

- **Смїлый говорить правду.** — Brave people speak the truth.
- **Мудрый знає много.** — A smart person knows much.
- **Глупый ся не учить.** — Stupid people do not learn.
- **Лїнивый не хоче нич дїлати.** — Lazy people do not want to do anything.

## If and would

- **кед** — if, when: **Мудрый мовчить кед треба.** — A wise person stays silent when needed.
- **бы** — would. You'll find it in the long "if … would" sentences of this lesson, such as **они были бы тихі** — they would be silent.

Learn those long sentences as whole phrases.

> **Watch out:** **Мудрый** on its own means "a wise person", not just "wise".
