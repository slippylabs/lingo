In this lesson: **the weather**, and how to say **"it is warm"** and **"it is raining"**.

## It is warm, it is cold

Rusin has no word for "it" here, just **є** and a word ending in **‑о**:

- **Сьогодні є тепло.** — It is warm today.
- **В Росії є хладно.** — In Russia it is cold.
- **Сьогодні є хмарно.** — It is cloudy today.

## Rain, snow, sun

- **Падає дождь.** — It is raining. (word for word, "rain falls")
- **Падає снїг і дождь.** — Snow and rain are falling.
- **Сонце світить.** — The sun is shining.
- **Вїтер дує сильно.** — The wind is blowing strongly.

## Changing

- **Погода ся змінює.** — The weather is changing.

> **Watch out:** **хладно** means "it is cold"; to describe a thing, use **хладный**: **Хладный вїтер дує из сївера** — a cold wind is blowing from the north.
