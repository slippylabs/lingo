In this lesson: **hobbies**, and how a verb changes for **I, you, he, we and they**.

## Verb endings

A verb (an action word) changes its ending to show who does the action:

- **Я чітаю кнїгу.** — I am reading a book. (I: **‑ю**)
- **Ты позераєш серію?** — Are you watching a series? (you: **‑ш**)
- **Он грає в футбол.** — He is playing football. (he, she: **‑є**)
- **Мы граєме в шахы.** — We are playing chess. (we: **‑ме**)
- **Вы учите ся языка.** — You are learning a language. (you, several: **‑те**)
- **Они слухають музику.** — They are listening to music. (they: **‑ють**)

## Learning: учити ся

**учити** means "to teach". Add **ся** ("yourself") and it means "to learn":

- **Учиме ся ту.** — We are learning here.

## Playing

Use **в** for games and **на** for instruments:

- **Он грає в футбол.** — He is playing football.
- **Она грає на гитарі.** — She is playing the guitar.

> **Watch out:** one present form covers both English forms: **Я чітаю кнїгу** can mean "I read a book" or "I am reading a book".
