In this lesson: a **review of pronouns** (words like I, me, you, us) in their different forms.

## Me and you

- **Мене не видиш.** — You don't see me.
- **Дай мі воду.** — Give me water.
- **Я вижу вас.** — I see you.
- **Говориме з тобов.** — We are talking with you.
- **Не, я не говорю о тобі.** — No, I don't talk about you.
- **З нами все весело.** — With us everything is always cheerful.

## Who and what change too

- **Кого ты видиш?** — Who do you see?
- **Кому даси дар?** — Who do you give a gift to?
- **О чім ты говориш?** — What are you talking about?

## Oneself

- **Он любить быти сам з собов.** — He likes to be alone with himself.

> **Watch out:** the form depends on the little word in front: **з тобов** (with you), but **о тобі** (about you).
