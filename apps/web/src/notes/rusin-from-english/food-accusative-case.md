In this lesson: **food**, and the **accusative case**: the form of the thing you eat, buy or carry.

## Feminine words change ‑а to ‑у

- **Он їсть солодку їду.** — He eats sweet food. (*їда* → **їду**)
- **Мы видиме твою піцу на столї.** — We see your pizza on the table.
- **Мы їме вечерю.** — We are eating dinner. (*вечеря* → **вечерю**)

The describing word changes with it: *солодка* becomes **солодку**.

## Most other things stay the same

- **Я їм хлїб.** — I am eating bread.
- **Она їсть мясо.** — She is eating meat.
- **Он любить молоко.** — He likes milk.

## People and animals change

A man's name or a male animal takes **‑а**:

- **Я вижу Влада з їдов** — I see Vlad with food

## "To eat" is irregular

- **Я їм хлїб.** — I am eating bread.
- **Ты їси яйце?** — Are you eating an egg?
- **Она їсть мясо.** — She is eating meat.
- **Мы їме вечерю.** — We are eating dinner.

> **Watch out:** learn the forms of "to eat" as they are: **їм**, **їси**, **їсть**, **їме**. They don't follow the usual endings.
