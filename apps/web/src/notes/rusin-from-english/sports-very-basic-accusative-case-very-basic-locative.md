In this lesson: **sports**, and the ending a place word takes after **в** ("in") and **на** ("at, on").

## Sport names stay the same

The name of a sport doesn't change when you play or love it:

- **Она грає в баскетбол.** — She plays basketball.
- **Он грає в теніс.** — He plays tennis.
- **Они люблять спорт.** — They love sport.

## Where: the locative ending

After **в** and **на**, a place word changes its ending. This form is called the locative case, the "place" form.

- **Стадіон є в містї.** — The stadium is in the city. (*місто* → **містї**)
- **Она грає на стадіонї.** — She plays at the stadium.
- **Мы плаваєме в рїцї.** — We swim in the river. (*рїка* → **рїцї**)

## When and how often

- **Он тренує кажный дїнь.** — He trains every day.
- **Они бігають утром.** — They run in the morning.
- **Мы плаваєме часто.** — We swim often.
- **Треба тренировати.** — It is necessary to train.

> **Watch out:** *к* turns into *ц* before this ending: *рїка* (river) becomes **в рїцї** (in the river).
