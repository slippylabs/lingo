In this lesson: **family words** and the **instrumental case**, the form a word takes after **з** ("with").

## "With" changes the ending

After **з** (with), the noun takes a new ending:

| | ending | example |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | **‑ом** | **з дїдом** — with grandfather |
| feminine | **‑ов** | **з тетков** — with aunt |
| plural | **‑ами** | **з братами** — with brothers |

- **Он говорить з дїдом.** — He is talking with his grandfather.
- **Они ідут з тетков.** — They are going with their aunt.
- **Она іде з мамов.** — She is going with her mother.
- **Ідеме на поход з братами.** — We are going for a walk with brothers.
- **Он жиє з сыном.** — He lives with his son.

Words like "my" and "your" change too:

- **Говорю з твоєв сестров.** — I am talking with your sister.
- **Она говорить з моєв бабков.** — She is talking with my grandmother.

## Family words

- **мама** — mother
- **отець** — father
- **брат** — brother
- **сестра** — sister
- **сын** — son
- **дщерка** — daughter
- **дїдо** — grandfather
- **бабка** — grandmother
- **тетка** — aunt
- **стрый** — uncle

## Who with?

- **З кім ты ідеш?** — Who are you going with?

> **Watch out:** **з** becomes **зо** before some words: **Он жиє з тетков і зо стрыєм.** — He lives with his aunt and uncle.
