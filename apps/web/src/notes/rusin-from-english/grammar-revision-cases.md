In this lesson: a **review of the cases**, the different endings that show a word's job in the sentence.

## Each ending has a job

- **Он видить свою жену.** — He sees his wife. (accusative: the one you see)
- **Брата ту не є.** — There is no brother here. (genitive: after "no, not")
- **Дам женї.** — I give to the woman. (dative: the one you give to)
- **Она дає дар братам.** — She gives a gift to the brothers. (dative plural)
- **Она є в містї.** — She is in the city. (locative: after "in")
- **Она говорить о братах.** — She talks about brothers. (locative: after "about")
- **Говориме з приятелями.** — We talk with friends. (instrumental: after "with")
- **Тото можна рїзати ножом.** — This can be cut with a knife. (instrumental: the tool)

## Many and none

- **Уж не є бананов в домї.** — There are no more bananas in the house.
- **В містах увидиш много людей.** — In the cities you will see many people.

> **Watch out:** word order is free in Rusin. In **Імня он не має** ("He has no name") the ending, not the position, tells you who does what.
