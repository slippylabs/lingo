In this lesson: the verb **"to be"**, the words for **I, you, he, she**, and **this** and **that**.

## "To be"

Rusin changes the verb "to be" for each person:

| | Rusin |
|---|---|
| I am | **я єм** |
| you are (one person) | **ты єси** |
| he, she, it is | **є** |
| we are | **сме** |
| you are (several people, or polite) | **вы єсьте** |
| they are | **суть** |

- **Я єм мудрый.** — I am wise.
- **Хто ты єси?** — Who are you?
- **Тото є мой дом.** — This is my house.
- **Вы єсьте добры.** — You are good.
- **Тото суть приятелї.** — These are friends.

## Every noun has a gender

A noun (a word for a person or thing) is masculine, feminine or neuter. You can usually tell from the ending:

- masculine words often end in a consonant: **дом** — house
- feminine words often end in **‑а**: **жена** — woman
- neuter words often end in **‑о**, **‑е** or **‑я**: **місто** — city

A describing word changes its ending to match:

- **Тото добрый чоловік.** — This is a good person.
- **Тота жена є добра.** — This woman is good.
- **Тото є приємне місто.** — This is a pleasant city.

## This and that

- **тото** — this (also "this is…"): **Тото моя сестра.** — This is my sister.
- **тотот** — that, with masculine words: **Тотот дїнь є хладный.** — That day is cold.
- **тамтото** — that over there: **Тамтото утро є хладноє.** — That morning over there is cold.

## Saying "not"

Put **не** in front: **Тото не моя сестра.** — This is not my sister.

> **Watch out:** **є** is only for he, she, it and things. "I am" is **єм** and "they are" is **суть**.
