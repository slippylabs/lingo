In this lesson: **hundreds and thousands**.

## The hundreds

- **сто** — one hundred
- **двеста** — two hundred
- **триста** — three hundred
- **четиристо** — four hundred
- **петсто** — five hundred
- **шестсто** — six hundred

## The noun after them

A round hundred takes the genitive plural:

- **сто дечака** — one hundred boys
- **двеста жена** — two hundred women
- **петсто жена** — five hundred women

As with smaller numbers, the *last* number decides the noun:

- **седамсто један бицикл** — seven hundred one bicycles
- **двеста три рачунара** — two hundred three computers

## Thousands

- **хиљаду осам рубаља** — one thousand eight rubles
- **две хиљаде деветсто паса** — two thousand nine hundred dogs

> **Watch out:** "one thousand" is just **хиљаду**, with no word for "one".
