In this lesson: **shopping**, prices and **a piece of** something.

## "I want to buy"

"Want to do" is **хоћу да** plus the verb:

- **Хоћу да купим хлеб.** — I want to buy bread.

## A piece of, the price of

"Of" is shown by the genitive ending:

- **Комад сира је добар.** — A piece of cheese is good.
- **Комад хлеба је мали.** — The piece of bread is small.
- **Цена путера је добра.** — The price of butter is good.

## Buying and selling

- **Она купује књигу.** — She is buying a book.
- **Он продаје месо.** — He sells meat.
- **Купац тражи попуст.** — The customer asks for a discount.

## The receipt

The person you give something to takes the dative:

- **Дајем рачун продавцу.** — I give the receipt to the seller.

> **Watch out:** **продавац** (seller) drops its second **а** in other forms: **продавцу**.
