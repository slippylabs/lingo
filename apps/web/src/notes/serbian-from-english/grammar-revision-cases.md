In this lesson: a **review of the cases**, the endings that show a word's job.

## The cases you have met

- after **нема** ("there is no"), the genitive: **Овде нема брата.** — There is no brother here.
- "to someone", the dative: **Дајем жени.** — I give to the woman.
- the thing you see, the accusative: **Он види своју жену.** — He sees his wife.
- "in", the locative: **Она је у граду.** — She is in the city.
- "about", the locative too: **Она говори о браћи.** — She talks about brothers.
- "with", the instrumental: **Говорим с пријатељима.** — I talk with friends.
- a tool, the instrumental with no "with": **Ово се може сећи ножем.** — This can be cut with a knife.

## Plural and "many"

**много** ("many") takes the genitive plural:

- **У свемиру има много сунаца.** — There are many suns in the universe.
- **У градовима ћеш видети много људи.** — In the cities you will see many people.

## Eyes and soul

- **Очи су огледало душе.** — Eyes are the mirror of the soul.
- **У очима видим твоју душу.** — In the eyes I see your soul.

> **Watch out:** **брат** has a special plural, *браћа*, so "about brothers" is **о браћи**.
