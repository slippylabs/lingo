In this lesson: **greetings**, **"how are you"**, and short **replies** for a conversation.

## Greetings by time of day

- **Добро јутро!** — Good morning!
- **Добар дан!** — Good day!
- **Добро вече!** — Good evening!
- **Лаку ноћ!** — Good night!
- **Здраво!** — Hello!

## "You": ти or ви

Use **ти** with one friend or family member. Use **ви** with several people, or to be polite with a stranger. The verb changes with it:

- **Како си?** — How are you? (to a friend)
- **Добар дан, како сте?** — Good day, how are you? (polite)
- **Добро сам, хвала!** — I am well, thank you!
- **А ти?** — And you?

## Useful replies

- **Тако је.** — That is so.
- **Не знам.** — I don't know.
- **Можда.** — Perhaps.
- **Пардон, не разумем.** — Excuse me, I don't understand.
- **Хвала ти много.** — Thank you very much.

## Your name

- **Како се зовеш?** — What is your name?
- **Ја се зовем Бартек.** — My name is Bartek.

> **Watch out:** **Добро вече** and **Лаку ноћ** are not the same: **Лаку ноћ** is only for saying goodbye at night or before sleep.
