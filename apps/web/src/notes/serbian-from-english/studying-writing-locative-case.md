In this lesson: **school words**, **where** things are, and what you **write with**.

## Where: на and у

After **на** ("on") and **у** ("in"), a noun takes the locative case. Masculine and neuter nouns usually end in **‑у**:

- **Књига је на столу.** — The book is on the table.
- **Школа је у центру.** — The school is in the center.

## What you write with

The tool you use takes the instrumental case, with no word for "with". Neuter **перо** becomes **пером**:

- **Пишем пером на папиру.** — I am writing on paper with a pen.
- **Брзо пишем овим пером.** — I am writing quickly with this pen.

## Male and female teachers

- **Он је мој учитељ.** — He is my teacher.
- **Она је учитељица.** — She is a teacher.
- **Он је ученик.** — He is a student.

## More sentences

- **Имам задатак.** — I have an assignment.
- **Ученик има књигу.** — The student has a book.
- **Мастило је још мокро.** — The ink is still wet.

> **Watch out:** **сто** means "table" or "desk" here, as in **Ово је мој сто**. It also means "hundred"; the sentence tells you which.
