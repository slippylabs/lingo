In this lesson: **drinks**, and the ending used for **"no", "without" and "a bottle of"**.

## The genitive case

After words of amount or absence, a noun takes the genitive case (an ending that often means "of"):

- feminine **‑а** becomes **‑е**: **вода** → **Немам воде.** — I do not have water.
- **кафа** → **Он нема кафе.** — He does not have coffee.
- masculine and neuter add **‑а** or change **‑о** to **‑а**: **сок** → **Имаш ли флашу сока?** — Do you have a bottle of juice?
- **вино** → **Чашу вина за мене, молим.** — A glass of wine for me, please.

## "Without"

**без** is always followed by the genitive:

- **Пијеш ли без млека?** — Do you drink without milk?
- **Волим сок без шећера.** — I like juice without sugar.

## A plain "not"

With an ordinary verb and **не**, the drink keeps its usual form:

- **Она не пије вино.** — She does not drink wine.
- **Ми не пијемо пиво.** — We do not drink beer.

## "To drink"

- **Он пије за тебе.** — He is drinking for you.
- **Ми пијемо пиво из флаше.** — We are drinking beer from the bottle.

> **Watch out:** **немам** ("I don't have") and **нема** ("there is no / he has no") take the genitive: **Немам воде**, not *Немам вода*.
