In this lesson: **food**, **eating** and **buying**, and the ending for **the thing you eat**.

## The thing you eat or buy

The thing that receives the action takes the accusative case (a different ending that shows the word's job):

- feminine words change **‑а** to **‑у**: **вечера** → **Ми једемо вечеру.** — We are eating dinner.
- **Видимо твоју пицу на столу.** — We see your pizza on the table.
- masculine things stay the same: **Ја једем хлеб.** — I am eating bread.
- neuter words stay the same: **Она једе месо.** — She is eating meat.

A masculine *person* adds **‑а**:

- **Видим Влада с храном.** — I see Vlad with food.

## "To eat"

**јести** is irregular:

- **Ја једем хлеб.** — I am eating bread.
- **Да ли једеш јаје?** — Are you eating an egg?
- **Они једу доручак.** — They are having breakfast.

## More sentences

- **Он спрема ручак.** — He is preparing lunch.
- **Купићу путер.** — I will buy butter.
- **Знам да волиш јаја.** — I know that you like eggs.

> **Watch out:** "that" joining two sentences is **да**, as in **Знам да волиш јаја**. It is not **то**.
