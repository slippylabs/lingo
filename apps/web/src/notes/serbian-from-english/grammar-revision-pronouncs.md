In this lesson: **pronouns** (words like "me", "you", "him") in their **different forms**.

## Pronouns change too

Like nouns, pronouns change their ending for their job:

- **Мене не видиш.** — You don't see me.
- **Да ли говориш о мени?** — Are you talking about me?
- **Говорим с тобом.** — I am talking with you.
- **Не, не говорим о теби.** — No, I am not talking about you.
- **Видим вас.** — I see you.
- **С нама је увек све весело.** — With us everything is always cheerful.

## Question words

**ко** ("who") and **шта** ("what") change in the same way:

- **Кога видиш?** — Who do you see?
- **Коме дајеш поклон?** — Who do you give a gift to?
- **О чему говориш?** — What are you talking about?

## Yourself

**себи** and **собом** point back to the subject:

- **Он себи даје одмор.** — He gives himself a rest.
- **Он воли да буде сам са собом.** — He likes to be alone with himself.

> **Watch out:** "that" also changes: **О тој ствари ништа не знам** — I know nothing about that thing.
