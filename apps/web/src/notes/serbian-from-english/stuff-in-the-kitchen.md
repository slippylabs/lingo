In this lesson: **kitchen things**, and how to say what you **use** and what something is **in**.

## Using a tool

The tool takes the instrumental case, with no word for "with":

- **Он једе виљушком.** — He eats with a fork.
- **Шоља је напуњена водом.** — The cup is filled with water.

## In and from

**у** ("in") takes the locative, often ending in **‑у**:

- **Ми кувамо у лонцу.** — We cook in a pot.
- **Моја палачинка се пече у тигању.** — My pancake is cooking in the frying pan.

**из** ("from, out of") takes the genitive:

- **Она пије из шоље.** — She drinks from a cup.

## "There is some"

**има** with the genitive means "there is (some)":

- **У тигању има меса.** — There is meat in the frying pan.
- **У чинији има много шећера.** — There is a lot of sugar in the bowl.

## Full of

**пун** ("full of") also takes the genitive:

- **Тањир је пун хране.** — The plate is full of food.
- **Кашика је пуна бибера.** — The spoon is full of pepper.

> **Watch out:** **лонац** loses its **а** when the ending changes: **у лонцу**, not *у лонацу*.
