In this lesson: **weather**, and sentences with **no real subject**, like "it is raining".

## "It is…"

English uses "it" for the weather. Serbian leaves it out and uses the neuter form ending in **‑о**:

- **Данас је топло.** — It is warm today.
- **У Русији је хладно.** — In Russia it is cold.
- **Данас је облачно.** — It is cloudy today.

## Rain and snow "fall"

- **Пада киша.** — It is raining.
- **Пада снег и киша.** — Snow and rain are falling.

## The sun and the wind

- **Сунце сија.** — The sun is shining.
- **Ветар јако дува.** — The wind is blowing strongly.
- **Хладан ветар дува са севера.** — A cold wind is blowing from the north.

## "There is frost"

- **Ујутру има мраза.** — There is frost in the morning.

> **Watch out:** **време** means both "weather" and "time": **Време је добро** — The weather is good.
