In this lesson: **asking the way**, and words for **left, right, near and far**.

## Where and which way

- **Где је станица?** — Where is the station?
- **Куда иде пут?** — Which way does the road go?

**где** asks where something is; **куда** asks where something is going.

## Telling the way

A command to one person ends differently from a polite command:

- **Иди право до моста.** — Go straight to the bridge. (one friend)
- **Идите право, а затим надесно.** — Go straight and then to the right. (polite)

- **Идемо надесно.** — We are going to the right.
- **Затим налево.** — Then to the left.

## Near and far

**близу** and **далеко од** take the genitive:

- **Продавница је близу библиотеке.** — The shop is near the library.
- **Трг је далеко од хотела.** — The square is far from the hotel.

## Asking politely

- **Можете ли ми показати пут?** — Can you show me the way?

> **Watch out:** when someone is moving, ask with **куда**: **Куда идете?** — Which way are you going?
