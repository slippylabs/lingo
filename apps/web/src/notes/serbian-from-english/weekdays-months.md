In this lesson: **days of the week**, **months**, and how to say **on** a day or **in** a month.

## Small letters

Days and months are written with a small letter, except at the start of a sentence:

- **Понедељак је први дан у седмици.** — Monday is the first day of the week.
- **Петак је последњи радни дан.** — Friday is the last working day.

## On a day

**у** plus the accusative: feminine days change **‑а** to **‑у**:

- **У среду имам задатак.** — On Wednesday, I have an assignment.
- **У петак увече идемо у биоскоп.** — On Friday evening we are going to the movies.

## In a month

**у** plus the locative, ending in **‑у**:

- **У јануару имам одмор.** — I have vacation in January.
- **У августу идем у Хондурас.** — In August, I am going to Honduras.
- **Дочек Нове године је у децембру.** — New Year's Eve is in December.

> **Watch out:** **недеља** is Sunday; the word for "week" in this course is **седмица**.
