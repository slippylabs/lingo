In this lesson: **sports**, the **thing you play**, and **where** you play.

## What you play

The thing that receives the action takes the accusative case. A case is just a different ending that shows the word's job in the sentence.

- masculine words stay the same: **Играм фудбал.** — I play football.
- feminine words change **‑а** to **‑у**: **кошарка** → **Она игра кошарку.** — She plays basketball.

## Where: у and на

After **у** ("in") and **на** ("at, on"), a noun takes the locative case, usually ending in **‑у** or **‑и**:

- **Ми пливамо у реци.** — We swim in the river.
- **Она игра на стадиону.** — She plays at the stadium.
- **Стадион је у граду.** — The stadium is in the city.

## Useful sentences

- **Он тренира сваки дан.** — He trains every day.
- **Они трче ујутру.** — They run in the morning.
- **Он брзо трчи.** — He runs fast.
- **Ја не играм хокеј.** — I do not play hockey.

> **Watch out:** "not" goes straight before the verb: **Ја не играм хокеј**.
