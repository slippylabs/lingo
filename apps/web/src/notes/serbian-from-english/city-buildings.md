In this lesson: **places in a city**, and how to say **in, at** and **to** a place.

## In or at a place

Some places take **у** ("in"), others take **на** ("at, on"). Both use the locative case, ending in **‑у** or **‑и**:

- **Купујемо хлеб у продавници.** — We buy bread in the shop.
- **Читам књигу у библиотеци.** — I read a book in the library.
- **На пијаци има много људи.** — There are many people at the market.
- **На тргу стоји фонтана.** — A fountain stands in the square.

## Going to a place

**до** ("to, as far as") takes the genitive, ending in **‑а**:

- **Идемо до трга.** — We are going to the square.
- **Улица иде до продавнице.** — The street goes to the shop.

## Near and over

- **Станица је близу центра.** — The station is near the center.
- **Мост иде преко реке.** — The bridge goes over the river.

> **Watch out:** a square and a market both take **на**, not **у**: **на тргу**, **на пијаци**.
