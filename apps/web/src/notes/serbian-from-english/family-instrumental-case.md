In this lesson: **family words** and how to say **"with"** someone.

## "With" changes the ending

After **с** ("with"), a noun takes a special form called the instrumental case. A case is just a different ending that shows the word's job in the sentence.

- masculine words add **‑ом**: **син** → **Он живи са сином.** — He lives with his son.
- feminine words change **‑а** to **‑ом**: **мајка** → **Она иде с мајком.** — She is going with her mother.
- **тетка** → **Они иду с тетком.** — They are going with their aunt.

Words like "my" and "your" change too:

- **Говорим с твојом сестром.** — I am talking with your sister.
- **Она говори с мојом баком.** — She is talking with my grandmother.

Before a word starting with **с**, "with" becomes **са**, as in **са сином** above. Other examples:

- **Он говори с дедом.** — He is talking with his grandfather.
- **Ми се играмо с нашом децом.** — We are playing with our children.

## Without "with"

After **је** ("is") the noun keeps its normal form:

- **Он је мој брат.** — He is my brother.
- **Он је твој отац.** — He is your father.

## Family words

- **мајка** — mother
- **отац** — father
- **бака** — grandmother
- **деда** — grandfather
- **ћерка** — daughter
- **син** — son

> **Watch out:** "With whom?" is **С ким?**, as in **С ким идеш?** — Who are you going with?
