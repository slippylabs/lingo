In this lesson: **shapes and lines**, and how describing words **match their noun**.

## Shapes and their gender

- **Ово је круг.** — This is a circle. (masculine)
- **Квадрат има четири стране.** — A square has four sides. (masculine)
- **Троугао има три стране.** — A triangle has three sides. (masculine)
- **Ова линија је дуга.** — This line is long. (feminine)

## Adjectives agree

A describing word ends like its noun. Feminine nouns ending in **‑а** take adjectives in **‑а**:

- **Линија је равна.** — The line is straight.
- **Форма је занимљива.** — The shape is interesting.

Masculine nouns take the short form:

- **Ово је једноставан облик.** — This is a simple shape.

## "Of a square"

"Of" is shown by an ending (the genitive): **квадрат** → **квадрата**:

- **Видимо обрис квадрата.** — We see the outline of a square.
- **Сто има облик квадрата.** — The table has the shape of a square.

> **Watch out:** after **три** and **четири**, the noun takes a "count" form: **три стране**, **четири стране**, not the plain **страна**.
