In this lesson: **numbers 0 to 10**, and the noun form that goes **after** each number.

## One

**један** ("one") matches the noun's gender:

- **један дечак** — one boy
- **једна жена** — one woman
- **једно поље** — one field

## Two, three, four

After **два / две**, **три** and **четири**, a noun takes a special "count" form. For masculine nouns it ends in **‑а**, for feminine nouns in **‑е**:

- **два дечака** — two boys
- **две жене** — two women
- **три мачке** — three cats
- **четири бицикла** — four bicycles

**два** is for masculine and neuter nouns, **две** for feminine ones.

## Five and up

From **пет** ("five") onward, the noun takes the genitive plural ("of boys"):

- **пет дечака** — five boys
- **пет мачака** — five cats
- **шест бицикала** — six bicycles
- **осам долара** — eight dollars

> **Watch out:** **два дечака** and **пет дечака** look the same, but **мачка** shows the difference: **три мачке**, **пет мачака**.
