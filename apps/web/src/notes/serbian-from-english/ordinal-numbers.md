In this lesson: **ordinal numbers** (first, second, third) and **telling the time**.

## Ordinals are describing words

An ordinal number ends in **‑и** and matches its noun like any adjective:

- **први** — first
- **други** — second
- **трећи** — third
- **стоти долар** — hundredth dollar
- **четиристо пета планина** — four hundred fifth mountain

## Long ordinals

Only the *last* number becomes an ordinal:

- **двеста трећи рачунар** — two hundred third computer
- **двеста први брат** — two hundred first brother

## Telling the time

Serbian uses ordinary numbers for the time, not ordinals. **у** means "at":

- **у пет сати** — at five o'clock
- **у један и двадесет** — at twenty past one
- **у два и петнаест** — at fifteen past two
- **у осам и педесет пет** — at fifty five past eight

> **Watch out:** "at five past" is said hour first: **у четири и седам** — at seven past four.
