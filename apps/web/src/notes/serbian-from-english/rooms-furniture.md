In this lesson: **rooms and furniture**, and words for **where** things are.

## Where things are

Words like "near", "under" and "above" are followed by the genitive case (often an ending **‑а**):

- **Столица је близу стола.** — The chair is near the table.
- **Сандук је испод кревета.** — The chest is under the bed.
- **Лампа светли изнад стола.** — The lamp shines above the table.

"In" and "on" use the locative case, often ending in **‑у** or **‑и**:

- **На столу лежи књига.** — A book is lying on the table.
- **Полица виси на зиду.** — The shelf hangs on the wall.

## "There is"

Serbian says **има** ("has") for "there is". After **много** ("a lot of") the noun takes the genitive:

- **У ормару има много одеће.** — The wardrobe has many clothes.

## Door

**врата** is always plural, so its verb and adjective are plural:

- **Врата су затворена.** — The doors are closed.
- **Врата воде у кухињу.** — The doors lead to the kitchen.

> **Watch out:** "the middle of the room" puts **соба** in the genitive: **Сто стоји у средини собе**.
