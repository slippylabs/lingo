In this lesson: **fruit**, **colours and tastes**, and **plurals**.

## Describing words match the noun

A describing word (adjective) takes the noun's gender. Most fruit words are feminine and end in **‑а**, so the adjective ends in **‑а** too:

- **Јабука је црвена.** — The apple is red.
- **Крушка је укусна.** — The pear is tasty.
- **Банана је жута.** — The banana is yellow.

A masculine noun keeps the short form:

- **Лимун је кисео и жут.** — The lemon is sour and yellow.

## More than one

Feminine nouns change **‑а** to **‑е**, and so does the adjective:

- **Поморанџе су слатке.** — The oranges are sweet.
- **Јагоде су у чинији.** — The strawberries are in the bowl.
- **Крушке леже на столу.** — The pears are lying on the table.

## Grapes

**грожђе** is one word for a whole bunch, used like "grapes":

- **Ми купујемо грожђе.** — We are buying grapes.

> **Watch out:** "made from" is **од**: **Он прави шљивовицу од шљива** — He is making plum brandy from plums.
