In this lesson: **nature words**, and the difference between **going somewhere** and **being somewhere**.

## Going into vs being in

With movement, **у** ("into") takes the accusative case; with no movement, **на** or **у** takes the locative:

- **Ми идемо у шуму.** — We are going to the forest. (movement)
- **Вода тече у реку.** — Water flows into the river. (movement)
- **Он стоји на камену.** — He is standing on the stone. (no movement)
- **Ружа расте на пољу.** — The rose grows in the field. (no movement)

## Through, between, behind

- **кроз** ("through"): **Река тече кроз долину.** — The river flows through the valley.
- **између** ("between") takes the genitive: **Долина лежи између планина.** — The valley lies between the mountains.
- **иза** ("behind") takes the genitive: **Поље је иза куће.** — The field is behind the house.

## Describing nature

- **Планина је висока.** — The mountain is high.
- **Трава је зелена.** — The grass is green.
- **Земља је мокра.** — The earth is wet.

> **Watch out:** **цвет** is one flower; **цвеће** means flowers in general: **Цвеће лепо цвета** — The flowers bloom beautifully.
