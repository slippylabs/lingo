In this lesson: **animals**, and how to say **to whom** you give or say something.

## The dative case

The person or animal that receives something takes the dative case (the "to" form). The thing given keeps its accusative form.

- feminine **‑а** becomes **‑и**: **мачка** → **Дајем мачки рибу.** — I give the cat a fish.
- **жаба** → **Ми дајемо жаби хлеб.** — We give bread to the frog.
- masculine words add **‑у**: **коњ** → **Дајемо коњу воду.** — We give the horse water.
- **пас** → **Она нешто каже псу.** — She says something to the dog.
- **миш** → **Он даје мишу сир.** — He gives the mouse cheese.

More than one: **‑ама**:

- **Ми помажемо птицама.** — We help the birds.

## "To help"

**помагати** takes the dative too:

- **Он помаже птици.** — He helps the bird.
- **Помажемо вуку.** — We help the wolf.

## "Will give"

- **Ви ћете дати коњу сено.** — You will give the horse hay.
- **Да ли ћеш ми дати рибу?** — Will you give me a fish?

> **Watch out:** **пас** drops its **а** in other forms: **псу**, not *пасу*.
