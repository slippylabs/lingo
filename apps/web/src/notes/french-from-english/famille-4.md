In this lesson: **in-laws**, **step-** and **half-** family.

## beau- and belle-

*beau-* (for men) and *belle-* (for women) make the "‑in-law" and "step-" words:

- **le beau-frère** — brother-in-law
- **la belle-soeur** — sister-in-law
- **belle-mère** — mother-in-law
- **beau-fils** — stepson

## demi- = half

- **demi-frère** — half-brother
- **demi-soeur** — half-sister

## I have …

- **J'ai une belle-soeur.** — I have a sister-in-law.
- **J'ai un demi-frère** — I have a half-brother.

**J'ai** = I have.

> **Watch out:** **belle-mère** can mean "mother-in-law" or "stepmother", and **belle-fille** can mean "daughter-in-law" or "stepdaughter". The context tells you which.
