In this lesson: **shopping for clothes**.

## Paying

- **Puis-je payer en espèces ?** — Can I pay cash?
- **Voici ma carte de crédit.** — Here's my credit card.

**Voici** means "here is" or "here are".

## Too …

**trop** = too:

- **Ce pantalon est trop serré.** — These pants are too tight.
- **Ces chaussures sont trop petites.** — These shoes are too small.

## Saying what you like

- **J'adore acheter des chaussures.** — I love buying shoes.
- **Je n'aime pas la coupe.** — I don't like the cut.

> **Watch out:** "pants" is plural in English but singular in French: **Ce pantalon est**, not *ces pantalons sont*.
