In this lesson: **jobs**.

## No "a" before a job

After **suis** or **est**, French doesn't use "a" before a job:

- **Il est ingénieur.** — He is an engineer.
- **Je suis entrepreneur.** — I am an entrepreneur.

## Men and women

Many job words have a version for each:

- **chanteur** / **chanteuse** — singer
- **coiffeur** — hairdresser
- **architecte** — architect (same for both)

## Out of work

- **Je suis au chômage.** — I am unemployed.

> **Watch out:** don't translate "an engineer" word for word. It's **Il est ingénieur**, not *Il est un ingénieur*.
