In this lesson: **sports**, and two ways to say you **do** one.

## jouer au + a game

For ball games, use *jouer au* ("play the"):

- **Je joue au tennis.** — I play tennis.
- **Je joue au football.** — I play soccer.

**au** is *à + le* joined together.

## faire de + an activity

For other sports, use **faire** ("do") with **du**, **de la** or *de l'*:

- **Je fais de la natation.** — I go swimming.
- **Je fais de l'escalade.** — I go climbing.
- **J'aime faire du jogging.** — I like jogging.

## Other ways

- **Je cours vite.** — I run fast.
- **Je suis le meilleur en karaté.** — I am the best at karate.

> **Watch out:** **football** means soccer, not American football.
