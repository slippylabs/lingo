In this lesson: **DIY and gardening**.

## Things you do

- **J’arrose mes plantes.** — I water my plants
- **Je scie du bois** — I'm sawing wood
- **Le soleil brille sur mes plantes** — The sun shines on my plants

French uses the same form for "I water" and "I'm watering".

## Have to

**Je dois** + a verb = I have to:

- **Je dois aller au magasin de bricolage** — I have to go to the DIY store

## Looking for

- **Je cherche mon marteau** — I'm looking for my hammer

> **Watch out:** *chercher* already means "look for". Don't add a word for "for".
