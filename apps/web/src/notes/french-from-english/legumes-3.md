In this lesson: **vegetables**.

## l' before a vowel

**le** and **la** both become *l'* before a vowel or a silent h:

- **l’ail** — garlic
- **l’asperge** — asparagus
- **l'oignon** — onion

That hides whether the word is masculine or feminine, so learn it with **un** or **une**:

- **J'ai un concombre** — I have a cucumber
- **J'ai une carotte** — I have a carrot

## Not liking

- **Je n'aime pas les épinards** — I don't like spinach

> **Watch out:** spinach is plural in French: **les épinards**.
