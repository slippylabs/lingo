In this lesson: **colours**.

## Colours come after the noun

- **Le vin est rouge.** — The wine is red.
- **J'ai des lunettes de soleil noires.** — I have black sunglasses.

## Colours change their ending

A colour matches the noun it describes. **lunettes** is feminine and plural, so:

- **noir** → **noires**: **J'ai des lunettes de soleil noires.**
- **rouge** → **rouges**: **J'ai des lunettes de soleil rouges**

A colour that already ends in **‑e**, like **rouge**, only adds **‑s**.

> **Watch out:** **blanc** has an unusual feminine: *blanche*. So it's **J'ai des lunettes de soleil blanches**.
