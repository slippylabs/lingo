In this lesson: **how are you?**, **please** and **see you tomorrow**.

## How are you?

- **Ça va, Jaques ?** — How are you, Jaques?

**Ça va ?** is the everyday way to ask. The answer can be the same two words: *Ça va !* ("I'm fine!").

## Please and thanks

- **s'il vous plaît** — please
- **Merci, Cecille** — Thanks, Cecille.

## Saying goodbye

- **À demain!** — See you tomorrow!

> **Watch out:** **À** has an accent. Without it, *a* means "has".
