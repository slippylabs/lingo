In this lesson: **going to the cinema**.

## Kinds of film

The describing part comes after **film**:

- **Film d'amour** — romantic movie
- **J'aime les films d'horreur** — I like horror movies.

## Saying what you think

- **Ce film est ringard.** — This movie is cheesy.
- **Les comédies d'action sont les meilleurs films.** — Action comedies are the best movies.

## Asking a friend

- **Peux-tu, s'il te plaît, acheter deux tickets de cinéma pour samedi ?** — Could you please buy two movie tickets for Saturday?

**s'il te plaît** is "please" to a friend. To a stranger, say **s'il vous plaît**.

> **Watch out:** "I like" is **J'aime**, never *Je aime*. **Je** drops its *e* before a vowel.
