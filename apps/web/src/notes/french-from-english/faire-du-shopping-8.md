In this lesson: more **shopping**, and **this** and **these**.

## This and these

- **ce** — this (masculine): **Ce pantalon est trop serré.** — These pants are too tight.
- **ces** — these (any plural): **Ces chaussures sont trop petites.** — These shoes are too small.

## Your size

- **Quelle est ta taille de vêtements ?** — What's your clothing size?

**ta** is "your" to a friend, for a feminine word like **taille**.

## Cash or card

- **espèces** — cash
- **carte de crédit** — credit card

> **Watch out:** **petites** has an **‑e** and an **‑s** because it describes **chaussures**, which is feminine and plural.
