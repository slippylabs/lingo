In this lesson: **colleagues, meetings and trips**.

## Going to

**vas** + a verb is "going to":

- **Tu vas signer le contrat ?** — Are you going to sign the contract?

## On my way

**en** is used for being in a state or on a journey:

- **Je suis en route pour le bureau.** — I am on the way to the office.
- **Je suis en voyage d'affaires.** — I am on a business trip.

## Describing work

- **Mon collègue est sympa.** — My colleague is nice.
- **Notre client est épuisant.** — Our client is exhausting.
- **Nous avons eu une longue réunion** — We had a long meeting.

> **Watch out:** **longue** is the feminine form of *long*, to match **réunion**.
