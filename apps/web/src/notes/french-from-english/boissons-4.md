In this lesson: **drinks**, and **"a glass of …"**.

## A glass of …

Between a container and what's in it, put **de**. Before a vowel it becomes *d'*:

- **Un verre d'eau** — A glass of water
- **Je bois un verre de vin blanc.** — I drink a glass of white wine.

## Colours come after

- **Vin blanc** — White wine
- **Vin rouge** — Red wine
- **Chocolat chaud** — Hot chocolate

## Cheers!

- **Santé** — Cheers (word for word, "health")

> **Watch out:** **chaud** means *hot*, not cold.
