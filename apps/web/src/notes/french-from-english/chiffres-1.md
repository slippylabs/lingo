In this lesson: the numbers **one to five**, and saying **how old** someone is.

## One to five

- **Un** — One
- **Deux** — Two
- **Trois** — Three
- **Quatre** — Four
- **Cinq** — five

## Age uses "have"

French says you *have* years, not that you **are** them:

- **Cecille a trois ans.** — Cecille is three years old.

**a** = has, **ans** = years.

## Has

- **Cecille a un frère** — Cecille has one brother
- **La mère de Cecille a quatre frères.** — Cecille's mother has four brothers.

> **Watch out:** **un** means both "a" and "one". **Cecille a un frère** can mean "a brother" or "one brother".
