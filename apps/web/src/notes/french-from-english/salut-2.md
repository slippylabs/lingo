In this lesson: **telling someone your name** and **saying good night**.

## My name is …

- **Je m’appelle Cecille.** — My name is Cecille.
- **Comment t’appelles-tu ?** — What's your name?

Word for word, **Je m'appelle** means "I call myself".

## Two ways to say who you are

- **Salut, je suis Cecille.** — Hello, I am Cecille.
- **Cecille est un joli nom.** — Cecille is a nice name.

**je suis** = I am, **est** = is.

## Thanks and good night

- **Merci** — Thanks
- **Merci et bonne nuit** — Thanks and good night

> **Watch out:** **Bonne nuit** is only for going to bed. It isn't a greeting.
