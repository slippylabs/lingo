In this lesson: **fruit**, and saying what you **like** and **don't like**.

## Liking things

To say you like something in general, French uses "the":

- **Cecille aime les oranges** — Cecille likes oranges

## Not

Put **ne** before the verb and **pas** after it. **ne** becomes *n'* before a vowel:

- **Je n'aime pas la mangue** — I don't like mango

## Eating one

- **Cecille mange une orange** — Cecille eats an orange

> **Watch out:** "I like mango" needs **la**: *J'aime la mangue*, never *J'aime mangue*.
