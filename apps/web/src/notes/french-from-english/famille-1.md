In this lesson: **family words**, and **"a"** and **"the"**.

## Two kinds of noun

In French every noun (a word for a thing or person) is **masculine** or **feminine**. These are just labels. They decide which word for "a" you use:

- **un** for masculine words: **Jaques est un garçon.** — Jaques is a boy.
- **une** for feminine words: **Cecille est une fille.** — Cecille is a girl.

## My

"My" changes the same way:

- **mon** for masculine words: **mon père** — my father
- **ma** for feminine words: **ma mère** — my mother

- **Ma mère et mon père.** — My mother and my father.
- **Oui, c'est ma mère.** — Yes, that's my mother.

## The brother of …

French has no *'s*. You say "the brother of Cecille":

- **Jaques est le frère de Cecille.** — Jaques is Cecille's brother.

> **Watch out:** **fille** means both "girl" and "daughter".
