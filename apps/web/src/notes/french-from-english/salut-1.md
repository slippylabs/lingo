In this lesson: **saying hello** and **saying who you are**.

## Hello

- **Salut** — Hello (friendly, like "hi")
- **Bonjour** — Good morning

**Bonjour** is the everyday polite greeting. You can say it all day, not just in the morning.

## Who you are

- **Je suis** — I am
- **Comment t’appelles-tu ?** — What's your name?
- **J’habite à** — I live in

## Je becomes j'

**Je** ("I") drops its *e* before a vowel or a silent h, and an apostrophe takes its place:

- **J’habite à** — I live in (not *je habite*)

> **Watch out:** French puts a space before **?**, as in **Comment t’appelles-tu ?**
