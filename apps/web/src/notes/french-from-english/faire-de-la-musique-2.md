In this lesson: **musical instruments**.

## jouer de + an instrument

For an instrument, use *jouer de* ("play of"). **de** joins with **le** to make **du**:

- **Je joue de la guitare.** — I play guitar.
- **J'aime jouer du piano.** — I like playing the piano.
- **En Écosse, on joue de la cornemuse.** — In Scotland they play the bagpipes.

**on** means "people" or "they" in general.

## Learn the gender with the word

- **la guitare** — the guitar
- **le piano** — the piano
- **le violon** — violin

## I can't …

- **Je ne sais pas lire la musique.** — I can't read music.

> **Watch out:** sports use *jouer au*, but instruments use *jouer de*: *jouer au tennis*, **jouer du piano**.
