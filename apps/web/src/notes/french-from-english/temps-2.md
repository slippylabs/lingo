In this lesson: **snow, clouds and the moon**.

## Nice weather

- **Il fait beau.** — It's nice.
- **Il neige.** — It's snowing.

## There is / there are

**Il y a** means "there is" or "there are":

- **Il y a quelques nuages dans le ciel.** — There are a few clouds in the sky.

## I see

- **Je vois la lune** — I see the moon.

> **Watch out:** **Il fait beau** is about the weather, so it means "the weather's nice", not "he is handsome".
