In this lesson: **learning languages**.

## Language names

Language names start with a small letter in French:

- **français** — French
- **allemand** — German
- **anglais** — English

In a sentence they usually take **le**:

- **Le français est une langue difficile.** — French is a difficult language.

## Talking about learning

- **J'écris une liste de vocabulaire.** — I'm writing a vocabulary list.
- **J'ai des problèmes de prononciation.** — I'm having problems with the pronunciation.

> **Watch out:** the **s** at the end of **français** and **anglais** is silent.
