In this lesson: **hiking**, and describing words that **change their ending**.

## Describing words match

A describing word adds **‑e** for a feminine noun and **‑s** for a plural one:

- **La montagne est haute.** — The mountain is high.
- **L'herbe est verte** — The grass is green
- **Mes chaussures de randonnée sont sales** — My hiking shoes are dirty

## This

**ce** is "this" for masculine words; **cette** is for feminine ones:

- **Cette pancarte est sale.** — This sign is dirty.

## Asking a question

You can turn a sentence into a question just by raising your voice at the end:

- **Tu as une carte ?** — Do you have a map?

> **Watch out:** **carte** means "map" here. It can also mean a card or a menu.
