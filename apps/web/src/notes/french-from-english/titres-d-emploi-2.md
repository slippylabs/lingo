In this lesson: **work words**.

## His and her

**son** means "his" or "her". It matches the thing owned, not the owner:

- **Mon ami est ingénieur et il aime son travail.** — My friend is an engineer and he likes his job.

## Looking for work

- **Je cherche un stage.** — I'm looking for an internship.
- **Mon frère est un travailleur indépendant.** — My brother is self-employed.

## At work

- **le bureau** — the office
- **ordinateur** — computer
- **téléphone** — telephone

> **Watch out:** with a describing word after the job, **un** comes back: **un travailleur indépendant**. With the job alone it's **Il est ingénieur**.
