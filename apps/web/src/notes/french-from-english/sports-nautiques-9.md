In this lesson: **water sports** and the **beach**.

## Going to …

**Je vais** + a verb talks about the future, like "I'm going to":

- **Je vais surfer avec Max demain.** — I'm surfing with Max tomorrow.

## Know how to

*savoir* ("know") + a verb means "can" in the sense of a skill:

- **Tu sais faire de la voile ?** — Can you sail?

## Don't want

- **Je ne veux pas sauter dans l'eau.** — I don't want to jump in the water.

## Have

- **Tu as de la crème solaire ?** — Do you have sunscreen?
- **Tu as une belle planche de surf.** — You've got a nice surfboard.

> **Watch out:** **belle** comes *before* the noun. A few common describing words (*beau/belle*, *petit*, *grand*) go first.
