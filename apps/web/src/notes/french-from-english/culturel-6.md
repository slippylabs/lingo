In this lesson: **free time**: museums, theatre and games.

## Two ways to ask a question

Put **Est-ce que** in front of a normal sentence:

- **Est-ce que tu joues aux échecs ?** — Are you playing chess?

Or swap the verb and the person:

- **Allons-nous au théâtre ?** — Are we going to the theater?

## Answers

- **Non, j'aime les comédies musicales** — No, I love musicals.
- **J'aime regarder les dinosaures** — I love watching dinosaurs.

## Age

- **Quel âge ont les dinosaures ?** — How old are the dinosaurs?

Age uses "have" in French: word for word, "what age have the dinosaurs?".

> **Watch out:** **aux** is *à + les* joined together. You never write *à les*.
