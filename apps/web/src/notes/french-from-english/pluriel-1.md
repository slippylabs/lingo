In this lesson: **plurals**.

## Usually add -s

Most words just add a silent **‑s**. Words ending in **‑eau** add **‑x**:

- **Je visite un château.** — I visit a castle.
- **Les châteaux sont beaux.** — Castles are beautiful.

## Describing words go plural too

- **J'ai une belle maison et ils ont de belles maisons.** — I have a nice house and they have nice houses.

**belle** ("beautiful") becomes **belles**. **beau** becomes **beaux**.

> **Watch out:** when a describing word comes *before* a plural noun, "some" is **de**, not *des*: **de belles maisons**.
