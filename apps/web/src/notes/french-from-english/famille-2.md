In this lesson: **grandparents**, **husband** and **"he is my …"**.

## "He is my …" = c'est

Before **un**, **une**, **mon** or **ma** plus a noun, French uses **c'est** ("it is / that is"), even for a person:

- **C'est mon père.** — He is my father.
- **C'est mon mari.** — He is my husband.
- **C'est un enfant.** — He is a child.

## Married to

- **Ma grand-mère est mariée à mon grand-père.** — My grandmother is married to my grandfather.

*marié* ("married") gets an extra **‑e** because it describes a woman: **mariée**. You can't hear the difference.

## Grandma

- **Mamie** — Grandma
- **Je suis une Mamie.** — I am a Granny.

> **Watch out:** *Il est mon père* sounds wrong to French ears. Say **C'est mon père**.
