In this lesson: talking about **the weather**.

## Il fait …

Much weather talk starts with **Il fait** ("it makes"):

- **Il fait chaud.** — It's warm.
- **Il fait soleil.** — It's sunny.

## Rain

Rain has its own verb:

- **Il pleut.** — It's raining.

## Asking

- **Quel temps fait-il aujourd'hui ?** — How is the weather today?

**temps** = weather. (It also means "time".)

> **Watch out:** **chaud** means warm or hot. It looks like "cold", but it isn't.
