In this lesson: **godparents** and **the youngest child**.

## Godparents

- **parrain** — godfather
- **marraine** — godmother
- **filleule** — goddaughter

## The youngest

The youngest child in a family is **le benjamin** for a boy and **la benjamine** for a girl:

- **Je suis un garçon et je suis le benjamin.** — I am a boy and I am the youngest
- **Je suis une fille et je suis la benjamine** — I am a girl and I am the youngest

> **Watch out:** the girl's version adds **‑e**: **benjamine**. This also changes the sound: the end is now said "-een", not a nasal "-ah(n)".
