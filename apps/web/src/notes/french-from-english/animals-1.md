In this lesson: **animals**.

## le, la, l'

- **le chien** — the dog
- **la souris** — the mouse
- **l'abeille** — bee
- **l'ours** — bear

## I have, she has

- **J'ai un chien et Cecille a un chat.** — I have a dog and Cecille has a cat.

## Likes in general

To say you like a kind of thing, French uses "the":

- **Mon frère aime les poissons.** — My brother likes fish.

## Something that happened

- **La souris a mangé le fromage.** — The mouse has eaten the cheese.

> **Watch out:** the **s** of **ours** is said out loud ("oorss"), unlike most final **s**.
