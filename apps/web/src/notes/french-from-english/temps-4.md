In this lesson: **the seasons** and a few **weather words**.

## The seasons

All four seasons are masculine. Before a vowel, **le** becomes *l'*:

- **le printemps** — spring
- **l'été** — summer
- **L'automne** — autumn

- **C'est le printemps.** — It's spring.

## Describing the weather

- **sec** — dry
- **humide** — humid
- **doux** — mild

> **Watch out:** in **automne**, the **m** is silent: say "oh-tonn".
