In this lesson: **aunts, uncles, cousins and grandchildren**.

## le, la and l'

"The" is **le** for masculine words and **la** for feminine words. Before a vowel, both become *l'*:

- **la tante** — aunt
- **le neveu** — nephew
- **l’oncle** — uncle

## Boy and girl versions

Many family words have a version for each:

- **le petit-fils** — grandson
- **la petite-fille** — granddaughter
- **le neveu** — nephew · **la nièce** — niece

Notice that **petit** becomes **petite** next to a feminine word.

## Only child

- **Je suis fille unique.** — I am an only child.
- **C'est mon petit-fils.** — He is my grandson.

> **Watch out:** "grandparents" is **les grands-parents**: both halves take an **s**.
