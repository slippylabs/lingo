In this lesson: **more fruit**, and the word for **"some"**.

## des = some

Before a plural noun, "a" becomes **des** ("some"). English often leaves it out, but French can't:

- **Il mange des fraises et des framboises** — He eats strawberries and raspberries

## We

- **Nous aimons les olives** — We like olives

**nous** = we. The verb ends in **‑ons**: **aimons**.

## I have

- **J'ai une pastèque** — I have a watermelon

> **Watch out:** the **s** on the end of **fraises** and **framboises** is silent. You see the plural, but you don't hear it.
