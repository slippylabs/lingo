In this lesson: **video games**.

## Something that already happened

To talk about the past, use *avoir* ("have") plus a past form of the verb:

- **J'ai battu ton meilleur score.** — I beat your highscore.

## Describing the game

- **Les graphismes du jeu sont super.** — The graphics in the game are great.
- **Les manettes de la console sont cassées.** — The console controllers are broken.
- **Les personnages du jeu ne me convainquent pas.** — The game characters do not convince me.

**du** is *de + le*: "of the".

> **Watch out:** **cassées** ends in **‑ées** because **manettes** is feminine and plural.
