In this lesson: **drinks**, saying **"without"** and **"not any"**.

## Without

After **bjez** (without) a noun takes the genitive case — the "of / without" form. Many words end in **‑a** or **‑y**:

- **Piješ bjez mloka?** — Do you drink without milk?
- **Ja lubju džus bjez cokora.** — I like juice without sugar.
- **Sy ty bjez fliški?** — Are you without a bottle?

## Not any

After a "not" verb, the thing you don't have or drink also takes this form:

- **Ja nimam wody.** — I do not have water.
- **Wona njepije wina.** — She does not drink wine.
- **Wón nima kawy.** — He does not have coffee.

Compare: **Ty piješ wino?** — Do you drink wine? (no "not", so **wino** stays)

## A glass of…

- **Čaša wina za mnje, prošu.** — A glass of wine for me, please.
- **Butelka wody leži na blidźe.** — A bottle of water is lying on the table.

> **Watch out:** "I don't have" is one word: **nimam** (and **nima** for he/she).
