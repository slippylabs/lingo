In this lesson: **hobbies** and how verbs change for **I, you, he, we…**

## Verb endings

A verb's ending shows who does it. With **čitać** (to read):

- **Ja čitam knihu.** — I am reading a book.
- **Čitaš ty romany?** — Do you read novels?
- **Wona sydźi a čita.** — She is sitting and reading.

Many verbs end in **‑u** for "I" instead of **‑m**:

- **Ja moluju wobraz.** — I am painting a picture.
- **Ja tancuju mnoho** — I dance a lot

## "We" and "they"

- **My hladamy film.** — We are watching a film.
- **My hrajemy šach.** — We are playing chess.
- **Woni posłuchaja muziku.** — They are listening to music.

## One form for "I am reading" and "I read"

There is no separate "‑ing" form: **Wón piše dźenik** can be "He is writing a diary" or "He writes a diary".

> **Watch out:** "to learn" comes with **so**: **Wuknjemy so tule.** — We are learning here.
