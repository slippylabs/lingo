In this lesson: **shapes** and the numbers **three** and **four**.

## Shapes

- **To je kruh.** — This is a circle.
- **Kwadrat ma štyri strony.** — A square has four sides.
- **Třiróžk ma tři strony.** — A triangle has three sides.
- **Ta linija je dołha.** — This line is long.
- **To je prosta forma.** — This is a simple shape.

## "Of a square"

The word after "of" changes: **kwadrat** becomes **kwadrata**.

- **Widźimy wobrys kwadrata.** — We see the outline of a square.
- **Blido ma formu kwadrata.** — The table has the shape of a square.

## Drawing

- **Ja moluju wulki kruh.** — I am drawing a big circle.
- **Wón moluje figury na papjerje.** — He is drawing figures on paper.

> **Watch out:** **forma** is feminine, so it is **prosta forma**, but **prosty třiróžk** for the masculine triangle.
