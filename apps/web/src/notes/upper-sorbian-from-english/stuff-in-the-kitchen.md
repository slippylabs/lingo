In this lesson: **kitchen things** and saying **with** and **from**.

## In the kitchen

- **Nóž leži na stole.** — The knife is lying on the table.
- **Talér je połny jědźe.** — The plate is full of food.
- **Honc je na wohmjenju.** — The pot is on the flame.
- **Kuchnja je čista.** — The kitchen is clean.
- **Nóž je wóstry.** — The knife is sharp.

## With and from

- **Wón jě z widlemi.** — He eats with a fork.
- **Wona pije z čaše.** — She drinks from a cup.
- **My warimy w honcu.** — We cook in a pot.

**z** means "with" or "from", and the ending shows which: **z widlemi** (with a fork), **z čaše** (from a cup).

## Full of

**połny** ("full") is followed by the "of" form:

- **Łžička je połna popjera.** — The spoon is full of pepper.

> **Watch out:** **widle** (fork) is plural in form, so it takes **su**: **Widle su slěborne.**
