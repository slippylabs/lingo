In this lesson: **animals** and saying **to** or **for** someone.

## Giving to someone

The person or animal you give to takes the dative case — the "to someone" form:

- **Ja dam kóčce rybu.** — I give the cat a fish.
- **Damoj konjej wodu.** — We give the horse water.
- **My damoj žabje chlěb.** — We give bread to the frog.
- **Wón da myši twarožk.** — He gives the mouse cheese.

Common endings: masculine **‑ej** or **‑u** (**konjej**, **psej**), feminine **‑e** (**kóčce**, **žabje**).

## Helping

**pomhać** (to help) also takes this form:

- **Wón pomha ptačkej.** — He helps the bird.
- **Wón pomha mjedwjedźej.** — He helps the bear.

## "We two"

Upper Sorbian has a special form for exactly two people. **damoj** and **pomhamoj** mean "we two give" and "we two help"; English just says "we".

- **Pomhamoj wjelkej.** — We help the wolf.

> **Watch out:** the ending can change the letter before it: **kóčka** → **kóčce**, **žaba** → **žabje**.
