In this lesson: **jobs** and what people **do**.

## Jobs

- **Mój bratr je wučer.** — My brother is a teacher.
- **Wona je lěkar.** — She is a doctor.
- **Mój nan je kuchar.** — My father is a cook.
- **Wón je inženjer w měsće.** — He is an engineer in the city.

## What they do

- **Lěkar pomha ludźom.** — The doctor helps people.
- **Kuchar wari wječerju.** — The cook is preparing dinner.
- **Dźěłaćer twari dom.** — The worker is building a house.
- **Šofer jězdźi awtobus.** — The driver drives a bus.

Many job words end in **-ar** or **-er**, like **kuchar** and **wučer**.

> **Watch out:** **dźěłać** is "to work", and **dźěłaćer** (the one who works) is a worker.
