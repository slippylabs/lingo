In this lesson: **the seasons** and talking about **the future**.

## Seasons

- **W lěće je mnoho słónca.** — In the summer, there is a lot of sun.
- **Nazyma ma mnoho kolorow.** — Autumn has many colors.
- **Zyma ma mnoho sněha.** — Winter has a lot of snow.
- **Nalěćo přinjese kwětki.** — Spring will bring flowers.

## Future: "will"

Use **budu**, **budźeš**, **budźe** and so on (forms of "to be") plus the basic form of the verb:

- **W lěće budźe ćopło.** — In the summer it will be warm.
- **W zymje budźe padać sněh.** — In the winter it will snow.
- **W zymje budźeće hrać ze sněhom.** — In the winter you will play with snow.
- **Woni budu widźeć zeleny kraj.** — They will see a green landscape.

> **Watch out:** **budu** is both "I will" and "they will" — the subject word tells you which.
