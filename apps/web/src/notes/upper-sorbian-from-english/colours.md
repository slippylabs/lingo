In this lesson: **colours**, which change their ending to match the thing.

## Colours

- **Mój pos je čorny.** — My dog is black.
- **Myš je běła.** — The mouse is white.
- **Trawa je zelena.** — The grass is green.
- **Njebjo je módre.** — The sky is blue.
- **Kwětki su čerwjene.** — The flowers are red.

## Matching endings

A colour is a describing word, so its ending follows the noun:

- masculine **čerwjeny**: **Ja lubju čerwjeny kolor.**
- feminine **čerwjena**: **Čerwjena róža rosće tule.**
- neuter **čorne**: **Jeho awto je čorne.**

## As the object

When a feminine word is the object, the colour takes **-u** too:

- **Wona ma zelenu trawu.** — She has green grass.
- **Wón ma oranžowu torbu.** — He has an orange bag.

> **Watch out:** neuter and plural look the same here: **módre** njebjo, **módre** woči.
