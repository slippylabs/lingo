In this lesson: **family words** and how to say **"with someone"**.

## Family

- **Wón je mój bratr.** — He is my brother.
- **Wón je twój nan** — He is your father
- **Wón bydli ze synom.** — He lives with his son.

## "With" changes the ending

After **z** ("with") a noun takes the instrumental case — the form a word takes after "with". The ending tells you the gender:

| | ending | example |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | **‑om** | **z dźědom** — with grandfather |
| feminine | **‑u** | **z ćotu** — with aunt |
| plural | **‑ami** / **‑emi** | **z bratrami** — with brothers |

- **Wón rěči z dźědom.** — He is talking with his grandfather.
- **Woni jědźa z ćotu.** — They are going with their aunt.
- **Dźemy z dźowku.** — We are going with our daughter.
- **Rěčim z twojej sotru.** — I am talking with your sister.
- **Wona rěči z mojej wowku.** — She is talking with my grandmother.
- **My hrajemy z našimi dźěćimi.** — We are playing with our children.

Some words are irregular: **mać** (mother) becomes **z maćerju**.

- **Wona dźe z maćerju.** — She is going with her mother.

> **Watch out:** before a word starting with *s* or *z*, "with" becomes **ze**: **ze swójbu**, **ze synom**.
