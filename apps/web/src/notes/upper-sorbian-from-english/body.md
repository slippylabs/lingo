In this lesson: **parts of the body** and saying **something hurts**.

## The body

- **Hłowa je na ćěle.** — The head is on the body.
- **Wona ma dołhu nohu.** — She has a long leg.
- **Wón ma módre rjane woči.** — He has beautiful blue eyes.
- **Jeje wutroba biha spěšnje.** — Her heart beats fast.

## It hurts

- **Moja ruka boli.** — My hand hurts.
- **Žołdk tebje boli.** — Your stomach hurts.

## Pairs

Body parts that come in twos use the dual, the special form for two:

- **Ja mam dwě ruce a dwě noze a tež dwě stopaj.** — I have two hands and two legs, and also two feet.

> **Watch out:** **woko** (eye) has an irregular plural, **woči**.
