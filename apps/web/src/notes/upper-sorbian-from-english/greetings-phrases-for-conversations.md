In this lesson: **greetings**, **thanks** and short **everyday replies**.

## Hello and goodbye

- **Dobry dźeń!** — Good day!
- **Dobre ranjo!** — Good morning!
- **Dobry wječor!** — Good evening!
- **Dobra nóc!** — Good night!
- **Witajće!** — Welcome!

## How are you?

- **Kak sy?** — How are you?
- **Sym derje, dźak!** — I am well, thank you!
- **A ty?** — And you?

## Names

- **Kak so wołaš?** — What is your name?
- **Ja so wołam Bartek.** — My name is Bartek.

Word for word this is "How do you call yourself?" — **so** means "yourself / myself".

## Short replies

- **Haj, móže.** — Yes, it is possible.
- **Ně, njeje móžno.** — No, it is not possible.
- **Njewěm.** — I don't know.
- **Tež ja.** — Me too.
- **Wulki dźak tebi.** — Thank you very much.

> **Watch out:** **dźak** is "thanks", but **Dźakuju za rozmołwu** uses the verb: "I thank (you) for the conversation".
