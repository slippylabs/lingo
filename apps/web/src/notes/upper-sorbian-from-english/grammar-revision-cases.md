In this lesson: a **review of the cases** — how a noun's ending shows its job in the sentence.

## The endings at work

- **Wón widźi swoju žonu.** — He sees his wife. (the object: **žonu**)
- **Dam žonje.** — I give to the woman. (to someone: **žonje**)
- **Wona je w měsće.** — She is in the city. (where: **w měsće**)
- **Rěčim z přećelemi.** — I talk with friends. (with: **z přećelemi**)
- **Wona rěči wo bratrach.** — She talks about brothers. (about: **wo bratrach**)

## Nothing there

"There is no…" uses **njeje** and the "of" form:

- **Brata tu njeje.** — There is no brother here.
- **Kość tu njeje.** — There is no bone here.
- **Hižo njeje bananow w domje.** — There are no more bananas in the house.

## Many

**mnoho** (many) is followed by the "of" form plural:

- **W měsće uwidźiš mnoho ludźi.** — In the city you will see many people.

> **Watch out:** a man's word changes the most: **bratr**, **Brata tu njeje**, **wo bratrach**.
