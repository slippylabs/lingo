In this lesson: **describing how people look** and comparing with **like** and **than**.

## Looks

- **Wón ma dołhe włosy.** — He has long hair.
- **Wona ma zelene woči.** — She has green eyes.
- **Stara žona nosy čapku.** — The old woman wears a hat.
- **Niska dźowka dźe do doma.** — The short girl is going home.

## Like

**kaž** compares two things that are the same:

- **Wona je niska a chuda kaž wón.** — She is short and thin like him.
- **A jeho rjane włosy su ćěmne kaž nóc.** — And his beautiful hair is as dark as the night.

## Than, less

**hač** means "than"; **mjenje** means "less". To say "taller", "wiser" and so on, the describing word takes its comparative form, usually with **‑ši**:

- **Wón je wyši a sylniši hač wón.** — He is taller and stronger than he is.
- **Stari ludźo su mudriši hač my.** — Old people are wiser than us.
- **To je rjeńši čłowjek hač tón.** — He is a more beautiful person than that one.
- **Chudy ma mjenje ćělesneje masy hač tołsty.** — The thin person has less body mass than the fat one.

> **Watch out:** **włosy** (hair) is plural, so its describing word is plural too: **Wón ma dołhe włosy.**
