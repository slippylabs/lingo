In this lesson: **food words** and the form a word takes when it is **the thing you eat, buy or see**.

## The thing you act on

That form is called the accusative case. Feminine words change **‑a** to **‑u**; most other words stay the same:

- **Ja jěm chlěb.** — I am eating bread.
- **Wona jě mjaso.** — She is eating meat.
- **Čitaš ty knihu a jěš snědanje?** — Are you reading a book and eating breakfast?
- **My jěmy wječerju.** — We are eating dinner.

A man or boy you see takes **‑a**: **Ja widźu Wlada z jědźu** — I see Vlad with food.

## Eating and drinking

- **Wón pije mloko a jě mjaso.** — He is drinking milk and eating meat.
- **Ty jěš jejo?** — Are you eating an egg?
- **Ja kupim masło.** — I will buy butter.

## "That…"

**zo** joins two sentences, like English "that":

- **Ja wěm, zo ty lubiš jejo.** — I know that you like eggs.

> **Watch out:** **jěsć** (to eat) is irregular: **ja jěm**, **ty jěš**, **wona jě**, **my jěmy**.
