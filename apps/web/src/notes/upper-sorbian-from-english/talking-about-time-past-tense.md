In this lesson: **telling the time** and talking about **the past**.

## Time words

- **Kelko je časow?** — What time is it?
- **Sekunda je krótka.** — A second is short.
- **Połnoc je ćicha.** — Midnight is quiet.
- **Kelko minut trwaje film?** — How many minutes does the film last?

## "Was"

- **Wčera běše póndźela.** — Yesterday was Monday.
- **Zaranja njeběše mnoho ćopło.** — In the morning it was not very warm.

## Past tense

The past is often made with **być** (to be) plus a verb form ending in **-ł**, **-ła** or **-li**:

- **Ja sym był w domje zaranja.** — I was at home in the morning.
- **Wječor smy šli do restawracije.** — In the evening we went to the restaurant.

> **Watch out:** the past form changes for a man, a woman and a group: **šoł**, **šła**, **šli**.
