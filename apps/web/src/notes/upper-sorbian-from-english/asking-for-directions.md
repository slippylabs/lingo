In this lesson: **asking the way** and giving **directions**.

## Asking

- **Hdźe je dwórnišćo?** — Where is the station?
- **Hdźe je bliski hotel?** — Where is the nearest hotel?
- **Móžeće pokazać puć?** — Can you show me the way?

## Directions

- **Dźemy naprawo.** — We are going to the right.
- **Potom nalěwo.** — Then to the left.
- **Dźeće runje a potom naprawo.** — Go straight and then to the right.
- **Dwórnišćo je nalěwo.** — The station is on the left.

## Near and far

- **Wobchod je blisko knihownje.** — The shop is near the library.
- **Knihownja je zdaloka.** — The library is far away.

> **Watch out:** **Dźeće** is the polite or plural "you go" — use it when asking a stranger.
