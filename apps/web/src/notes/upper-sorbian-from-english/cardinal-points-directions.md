In this lesson: **north, south, east, west** and **left / right**.

## The four directions

- **Sewjer je zymny.** — The north is cold.
- **Juh je ćopły.** — The south is warm.
- **Wuchod je tam.** — The east is there.
- **Zapad je nalěwo na mapje.** — West is left on the map.

## Describing words

Add **-ny** for "northern", "western"…:

- **To je sewjerna strona.** — This is the northern side.
- **Zapadny wětřik duje.** — A western wind is blowing.
- **Wuchodna muzika je zajimawa.** — Eastern music is interesting.

## In the north

- **Bydlimy na juhu.** — We live in the south.
- **Na sewjeru je sněh.** — In the north there is snow.

## Left and right

- **Dźemy hnydom naprawo a potom runje.** — We are going right immediately and then straight.

> **Watch out:** "in the north" uses **na**, not **w**: **na sewjeru**, **na juhu**.
