In this lesson: **hundreds** and **one thousand**.

## Hundreds

- **sto hólcow** — one hundred boys
- **dwěsćě žonow** — two hundred women
- **trista polow** — three hundred fields
- **pjeć stow žonow** — five hundred women
- **wosom stow hólcow** — eight hundred boys

From 5 on, "hundred" itself takes the "of" form: **pjeć stow**, **šěsć stow**, **dźewjeć stow**.

## Thousand

- **tysac wosom rublow** — one thousand eight rubles
- **dwaj tysac dźewjeć stow posow** — two thousand nine hundred dogs

> **Watch out:** 200 is one word, **dwěsćě**, not "two hundreds".
