In this lesson: **feelings** and words for **how** something is done.

## Feelings

- **Lubosć to je sylna emocija.** — Love is a strong emotion.
- **Měr je wažny za dušu.** — Peace is important for the soul.
- **Ja mam žałosć we wutrobje.** — I have sadness in my heart.
- **Strah je w nas.** — Fear is inside us.

## How: words ending in -nje

Add **-nje** to a describing word to say *how*:

- **Čuju so radostnje dźensa.** — I feel joyful today.
- **Žałostnje wón rěčeše.** — He spoke sadly.
- **Radostnje je domoj šoł.** — He joyfully went home.

## To feel

*čuć so* — "to feel" comes with **so**:

- **My čujemy měr.** — We feel peaceful.

> **Watch out:** **radostny** describes a person or thing; **radostnje** describes how something is done.
