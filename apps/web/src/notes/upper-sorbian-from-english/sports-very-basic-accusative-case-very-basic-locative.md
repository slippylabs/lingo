In this lesson: **sports**, the thing you **play**, and **where** something happens.

## Playing a sport

- **Hraju koparsku.** — I play football.
- **Wón hraje tenis.** — He plays tennis.
- **Štó hraje tenis?** — Who plays tennis?
- **Ja njehraju hokej.** — I do not play hockey.

The thing you play is the object, and a feminine word changes its **‑a** to **‑u**: **koparska** becomes **koparsku**. Masculine words like **tenis** stay the same.

## Where: "in" and "at"

After **w** (in) and **na** (on, at) the noun takes the locative ending, often **‑e**:

- **Stadion je w měsće.** — The stadium is in the city.
- **My plawamy w rěce.** — We swim in the river.
- **Wona hraje na stadionje.** — She plays at the stadium.

## How often, how fast

- **Wón trenuje kóždy dźeń.** — He trains every day.
- **Wón běha spěšnje.** — He runs fast.
- **My plawamy často.** — We swim often.

> **Watch out:** the ending often changes the consonant before it: **město** → **w měsće**, **rěka** → **w rěce**.
