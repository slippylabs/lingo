In this lesson: **fruit** and **describing words** that match the noun.

## Fruit

- **Jabłuko je čerwjene.** — The apple is red.
- **Kruška je słódka.** — The pear is sweet.
- **Banana je žołta.** — The banana is yellow.
- **Wišnja je kisła.** — The cherry is sour.
- **Melon je wulki a słódki.** — The melon is big and sweet.

## The describing word matches

Its ending follows the noun's gender and number:

- masculine: **słódki**
- feminine: **słódka**
- more than one: **Pomaranče su słódke.** — The oranges are sweet.

## More than one

- **Kruški leža na blidźe.** — The pears are lying on the table.
- **Truskalcy su w misy.** — The strawberries are in the bowl.

> **Watch out:** the describing word follows the noun, not the English: **Kruška je słódka**, but **Melon je wulki a słódki**.
