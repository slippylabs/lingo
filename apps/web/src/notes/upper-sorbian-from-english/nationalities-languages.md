In this lesson: **countries, nationalities** and **languages**.

## People

- **Ja sym Pólak.** — I am a Pole.
- **Wona je Chorwatka.** — She is a Croat.
- **Wón je Rus.** — He is a Russian.
- **Sy ty Angličan?** — Are you an Englishman?

A woman's form often adds **-ka**: **Chorwat**, **Chorwatka**.

## Languages

A language is **rěč** plus a describing word:

- **Rěčiš němsku rěč?** — Do you speak the German language?
- **Angličan rěči jendźelsku rěč.** — An Englishman speaks the English language.
- **Wón wě tři rěče.** — He knows three languages.

## Countries

- **Němc bydli w Němskej.** — A German lives in Germany.
- **Stat je Pólska.** — The country is Poland.

> **Watch out:** names of people from a country start with a capital (**Němc**), but language words don't (**němsku rěč**).
