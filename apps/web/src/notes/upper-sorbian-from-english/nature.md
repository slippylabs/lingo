In this lesson: **nature words** and words for **through, between** and **towards**.

## Nature

- **Hora je wysoka.** — The mountain is high.
- **Trawa je zelena.** — The grass is green.
- **Zemja je mokra.** — The earth is wet.
- **Pólo je šěroke.** — The field is wide.

## Direction words

- **Rěka ćeče přez doł.** — The river flows through the valley.
- **Doł leži mjez horami.** — The valley lies between the mountains.
- **My dźemy do lěsa.** — We are going to the forest.
- **Dźemy k dubu.** — We are going towards the oak.
- **Pólo je za domom.** — The field is behind the house.

Each one asks for its own ending: **do lěsa**, **k dubu**, **mjez horami**, **za domom**.

> **Watch out:** **do** means "into / to" a place, **k** means "towards" it.
