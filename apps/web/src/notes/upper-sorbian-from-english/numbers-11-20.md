In this lesson: **numbers 11 to 20**.

## Building the teens

Most teens end in **‑naće**:

- **jědnaće hólcow** — eleven boys
- **dwanaće žonow** — twelve women
- **třinaće kóčkow** — thirteen cats
- **pjatnaće hólcow** — fifteen boys
- **dźewjatnaće ličakow** — nineteen computers

## Twenty

- **dwaceći kóčkow** — twenty cats

## The noun after them

Like 5–10, all of these take the "of" form plural, usually ending in **‑ow**: **dwanaće mužow**, **štyrnaće dolarow**.

> **Watch out:** **šěsnaće** (sixteen) and **dwanaće** (twelve) look alike at a glance — read the start of the word.
