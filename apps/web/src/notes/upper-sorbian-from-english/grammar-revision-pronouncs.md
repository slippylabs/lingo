In this lesson: **me, you, him, us** and other forms of the **pronouns** — the small words that stand for a person.

## Me and you

- **Mnje njewidźiš.** — You don't see me.
- **Ja widźu was.** — I see you.
- **Rěčim z tobu.** — I am talking with you.
- **Daj mi wodu.** — Give me water.

## Who and what

- **Koho ty widźiš?** — Who do you see?
- **Komu daš dar?** — Who do you give a gift to?
- **Wo čim rěčiš?** — What are you talking about?

Like nouns, "who" changes: **koho** (whom), **komu** (to whom).

## About someone

- **Wo mnje rěčiš?** — Are you talking about me?
- **Ně, ja njerěčim wo tebi.** — No, I don't talk about you.

> **Watch out:** "you" has many forms — **tebi**, **tobu**, **was** — chosen by the word before it.
