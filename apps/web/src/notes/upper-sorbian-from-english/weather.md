In this lesson: **the weather**: rain, snow, sun and wind.

## Rain, snow, sun

- **Pada dešć.** — It is raining.
- **Pada sněh a dešć.** — Snow and rain are falling.
- **Słónco swěći.** — The sun is shining.
- **Wětřik duje sylnje.** — The wind is blowing strongly.

Word for word, "rain falls": the weather word comes after **pada**.

## "It is warm"

No word for "it" is needed:

- **Dźensa je ćopło.** — It is warm today.
- **W Ruskej je zymno.** — In Russia it is cold.
- **Dźensa je ćopło a sucho.** — It is warm and dry today.

## Clouds

- **Mróčki su ćěmne.** — The clouds are dark.
- **Dźensa njewidźimy mróčkow.** — Today we do not see clouds.

> **Watch out:** **ćopło** (warm) and **zymno** (cold) end in **‑o** when they mean "it is warm / it is cold".
