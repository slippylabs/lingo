In this lesson: **vehicles, roads** and **travelling**.

## Vehicles

- **Awto stoji na hasy.** — The car is standing on the street.
- **Ćah jězdźi na dwórnišćo.** — The train goes to the station.
- **Awtobus wjedźe mnoho ludźi.** — The bus carries many people.
- **Kóło je złamane.** — The bicycle is broken.

## Going by vehicle

**jězdźić** is "to go" by car, bus or train; walking uses *hić* (**dźe**):

- **Šofer jězdźi spěšnje.** — The driver drives fast.
- **Dróha dźe přez lěs.** — The road goes through the forest.

## On the road

- **Swětłofor pokazuje zelene.** — The traffic light shows green.
- **Móst je wysoki.** — The bridge is high.

> **Watch out:** **awto** and **kóło** are neuter, so they take **-e**: **Awto je nowe**, **Kóło je złamane**.
