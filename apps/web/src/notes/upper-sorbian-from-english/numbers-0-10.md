In this lesson: **numbers 0 to 10** and the noun that comes after them.

## One

"One" matches the noun's gender:

- **jedyn hólc** — one boy
- **jedna žona** — one woman
- **jedno pólo** — one field

## Two: a special form

Upper Sorbian has a form just for two things, the dual:

- **dwaj hólcaj** — two boys
- **dwě žoni** — two women
- **dwě wěcy** — two things

## Three and four

After 3 and 4, the noun is in the normal plural:

- **tři kóčki** — three cats
- **štyri dolary** — four dollars

## Five and up

From 5 on, the noun takes the "of" form plural, often ending in **‑ow**:

- **pjeć hólcow** — five boys
- **wosom dolarow** — eight dollars
- **dźesać časnikow** — ten clocks

> **Watch out:** the noun changes three ways: **dwaj hólcaj**, **tři**… but **pjeć hólcow**.
