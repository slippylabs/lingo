In this lesson: **shopping**: prices, buying and selling.

## Buying and selling

- **Chcu kupić chlěb.** — I want to buy bread.
- **Wona kupuje knihu.** — She is buying a book.
- **Wón předawa mjaso.** — He sells meat.
- **Wón předawa mloko.** — He sells milk.

## A piece of…

**kus** (piece) is followed by the "of" form:

- **Kus sera je dobry.** — A piece of cheese is good.
- **Kus chlěba je mały.** — The piece of bread is small.
- **Předawaćel da kus kołbasy.** — The seller gives a piece of sausage.

## Price

- **Cena je wysoka.** — The price is high.
- **Cena masła je dobra.** — The price of butter is good.
- **Kupc so praša wo cenu.** — The customer asks about the price.

> **Watch out:** **kupić** is "to buy" and **předawać** is "to sell" — don't mix up **kupc** (customer) and **předawaćel** (seller).
