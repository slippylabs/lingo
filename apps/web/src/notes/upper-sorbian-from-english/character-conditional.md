In this lesson: **describing character** and saying what **would** happen.

## Character

- **Mudry wě mnoho.** — A wise person knows much.
- **Sylny duch je wažny.** — A strong spirit is important.
- **Wón je słaby w sporće.** — He is weak in sports.
- **Lěniwy nochce ničo činić.** — Lazy people do not want to do anything.

A describing word can stand alone for a person: **mudry** is "a wise person".

## Would

Use **by** with a verb form ending in **-ł** or **-li**. "If" is **jeli**:

- **Jeli bych ja był zmužity, to ja bych so njeboił.** — If I were brave, I would not be afraid.
- **Jeli bychu woni nje byli lěniwi, to bychmy jim pomhali.** — If they were not lazy, we would help them.
- **Jeli bysće wy nje byli słabi, to bysće dźěłali.** — If you were not weak, you would work.

**bych** is for "I", **bychmy** for "we", **bysće** for "you all", **bychu** for "they".

> **Watch out:** the **by** form changes with the person: **bych**, **bychmy**, **bysće**, **bychu**.
