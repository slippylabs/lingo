In this lesson: **tens up to 100**.

## The tens

- **dźesać hólcow** — ten boys
- **dwaceći žonow** — twenty women
- **třiceći polow** — thirty fields
- **štyrceći mužow** — forty men
- **pjećdźesat žonow** — fifty women

From 50 on, the tens end in *‑dźesat*: **šěsćdźesat**, **sydomdźesat**, **wosomdźesat**, **dźewjećdźesat**.

## Adding units

Say the ten first, then the unit:

- **sydomdźesat wosom rublow** — seventy eight rubles
- **wosomdźesat sydom dolarow** — eighty seven dollars

## One hundred

- **sto časnikow** — one hundred clocks

> **Watch out:** 20, 30 and 40 end in **‑ceći**, but 50 to 90 end in *‑dźesat*.
