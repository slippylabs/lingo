In this lesson: **buildings and places in town**.

## In town

- **To je wulke město.** — This is a big city.
- **Cyrkej je stara a běła.** — The church is old and white.
- **Móst dźe přez rěku.** — The bridge goes over the river.
- **Wobchod je wotewrjeny dźensa.** — The shop is open today.

## Going somewhere

- **Dźemy na torhošćo.** — We are going to the square.
- **Hasa wjedźe na torhošćo.** — The street leads to the square.

## Being somewhere

- **Kupujemy chlěb we wobchodźe.** — We buy bread in the shop.
- **Čitam knihu w knihowni.** — I read a book in the library.
- **Na torhošću je mnoho ludźi.** — There are many people at the market.

The ending changes between "to" and "at": **na torhošćo** (going to) but **na torhošću** (being at).

> **Watch out:** **w** becomes **we** before a word that starts with **w**: **we wobchodźe**.
