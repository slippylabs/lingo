In this lesson: the verb **"to be"**, the words for **I, you, he…**, and **this / that**.

## "To be"

"To be" changes with the person, like English am / are / is:

- **Ja sym mudry.** — I am wise.
- **Štó sy ty?** — Who are you?
- **Ta žona je dobra.** — This woman is good.
- **My smy jara mudri** — We are very wise
- **Wy sće dobri.** — You are good.
- **Wone su jara miłe.** — They are very kind.

**Ja**, **ty**, **wón**, **wona**… can be left out, because the verb already shows who you mean.

## Two words for "they"

**woni** is for a group with at least one man; **wone** is for every other group. Describing words follow along: **Woni su jara miłi** but **Wone su jara miłe.**

## Masculine, feminine, neuter

Every noun has a gender (a grammar group). The ending usually tells you:

- most nouns ending in a consonant are masculine: **dom**, **přećel**
- most ending in **‑a** are feminine: **žona**, **sotra**
- most ending in **‑o** or **‑e** are neuter: **město**, **dźěćo**

Describing words change their ending to match: **dobry čłowjek**, **dobra** žona, **dobre awto**.

## This and that

- **To je mój dom.** — This is my house.
- **Tón muž je miły.** — That man is kind.
- **Tamtón dom je rjany.** — That house over there is beautiful.

## Saying "not"

Put **nje** in front of the verb: **je** becomes **njeje**.

- **To njeje moja sotra.** — This is not my sister.

> **Watch out:** to ask a question, put the verb first: **Je to dobre awto?** — Is this a good car?
