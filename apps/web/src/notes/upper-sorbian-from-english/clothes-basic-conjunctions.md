In this lesson: **clothes** and joining words: **and, or, but**.

## Clothes

- **Wón nosy módru košulu.** — He wears a blue shirt.
- **Šalik je ćežki a ćopły.** — The scarf is heavy and warm.
- **Nowa drasta je dróha.** — New clothes are expensive.
- **Košula je w šafje.** — The shirt is in the wardrobe.

## Joining words

- **a** — and: **Wona ma čorne kałsony, a wón běłe.** — She has black trousers, and he has white.
- **abo** — or: **Ty chceš šalik abo košulu.** — You want a scarf or a shirt.

## Putting on

- **Wón so woblěka nowu košulu.** — He is putting on a new shirt.

> **Watch out:** **drasta** (clothes) is one feminine word, so it takes **je**: **Drasta je nowa.**
