In this lesson: **days of the week** and **months**.

## Days

- **Póndźela je prěni dźeń w tydźenju.** — Monday is the first day of the week.
- **Wutora je po póndźeli.** — Tuesday is after Monday.
- **Štwórtk je před pjatkom.** — Thursday is before Friday.
- **Sobota je bjez dźěła.** — Saturday is without work.
- **Njedźela je čas za swójbu.** — Sunday is time for family.

## In a month

Use **w** (or **we**) and the "where" ending:

- **Mam w januarje prózdniny.** — I have vacation in January.
- **W awgusće dźemy do Honduras.** — In August, we are going to Honduras.
- **We februarje je mnoho sněha.** — In February there is a lot of snow.

## On a day

- **W pjatk wječor dźemy na film.** — On Friday evening we are going to the movies.

> **Watch out:** days and months are written with a small letter inside a sentence: **w januarje**, **po póndźeli**.
