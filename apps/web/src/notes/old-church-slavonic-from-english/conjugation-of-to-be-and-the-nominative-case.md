In this lesson: the verb **"to be"**, the words for **this** and **that**, and how describing words match their noun.

## "To be"

| who | "to be" |
|---|---|
| **азъ** (I) | **ѥсмь** |
| **тꙑ** (you) | **ѥси** |
| **онъ / она / оно** (he / she / it) | **ѥстъ** |
| **мꙑ** (we) | **ѥсмъ** |
| **вꙑ** (you, more than one) | **ѥстє** |
| **они / онꙑ** (they) | **сѫтъ** |

- **Азъ ѥсмь мѫдръ.** — I am wise.
- **Къто тꙑ ѥси?** — Who are you?
- **Вꙑ ѥстє добри.** — You are good.
- **Си жена ѥстъ добра.** — This woman is good.

Short "this is" sentences often leave the verb out:

- **Си моꙗ сєстра.** — This is my sister.
- **Сь нє твои домъ.** — This is not your house.

## Words are masculine, feminine or neuter

Every noun has a grammatical gender. You can usually tell from the ending:

- masculine words end in *ъ* or *ь*: **домъ**, **градъ**, **дьнь**
- feminine words end in **‑а**: **жена**, **сєстра**
- neuter words often end in **‑о**: **оутро**, **мѧсо**

A describing word takes a matching ending:

- **Сь добръ чловѣкъ.** — This is a good person.
- **Си жена ѥстъ вꙑсока.** — This woman is tall.
- **Сє добро аѵто?** — Is this a good car?

## This and that

- **Си жена** — this woman
- **Тъи мѫжь милъ.** — That man is kind.

> **Watch out:** many words end in a silent letter *ъ* or *ь*. Type it: **домъ**, not *дом*.
