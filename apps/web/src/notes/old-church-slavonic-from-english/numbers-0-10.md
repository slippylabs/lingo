In this lesson: **numbers 0 to 10**, and how the word after a number changes.

## One

"One" matches the gender of the noun:

- **ѥдинъ хлапьць** — one boy
- **ѥдина жена** — one woman
- **ѥдино полиѥ** — one field

## Two: a special "pair" form

The language has a special form for exactly two things, called the dual:

- **дъва хлапъци** — two boys
- **дъвѣ женꙑ** — two women

## Three and four

- **три коти** — three cats
- **чєтꙑри вєлосипєдꙑ** — four bicycles

## Five and up

From five, the noun takes the "of" form (genitive plural), like "five of boys":

- **пѧть хлапьцєвъ** — five boys
- **шєсть вєлосипєдовъ** — six bicycles
- **дєвѧть компютєровъ** — nine computers

> **Watch out:** "two" is **дъва** with masculine words but **дъвѣ** with feminine ones.
