In this lesson: **first, second, third …** and **telling the time** after the hour.

## Ordinals are describing words

"First", "second" and so on change their endings to match the noun:

- **сотый доллар** — hundredth dollar (masculine)
- **четыреста пятая гора** — four hundred fifth mountain (feminine)
- **двухсотые наручные часы** — two hundredth watch (**часы** is always plural)

In a long number only the last word becomes "first", "second" and so on:

- **двести первый брат** — two hundred first brother
- **восемьсот второй доллар** — eight hundred second dollar

## Telling the time: minutes into the next hour

Russian counts the minutes into the hour that is running. 1:20 is "twenty minutes of the second hour":

- **двадцать минут второго** — twenty past one
- **пятнадцать минут третьего** — fifteen past two
- **пятьдесят пять минут девятого** — fifty five minutes past eight

## "The fifth hour"

**пятый час** is the hour running from 4:00 to 5:00:

- **пятый час** — the fifth hour
- **девятый час** — the ninth hour

> **Watch out:** the hour named is one ahead of English. **двадцать минут второго** is 1:20, not 2:20.
