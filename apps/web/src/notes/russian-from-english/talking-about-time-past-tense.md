In this lesson: **time words** and the **past tense**.

## The past tense: ‑л

Past forms end in **‑л** and change for masculine, feminine, neuter and plural, not for I, you or he:

- **Утром я был дома.** — I was at home in the morning. (a man)
- **Вчера в восемь часов она была дома.** — Yesterday at eight o'clock she was at home. (feminine **‑ла**)
- **Утром было не очень тепло.** — In the morning it was not very warm. (neuter **‑ло**)
- **Они ничего не делали.** — They did nothing. (plural **‑ли**)
- **Вы ходили в кино?** — Did you go to the cinema? (**вы** always takes **‑ли**)

## Walked: шёл

**идти** ("to go on foot") has its own past form:

- **Он шёл домой.** — He was walking home.

## The time

- **Сколько времени?** — What time is it?
- **Вчера в час было лучше.** — Yesterday at one o'clock it was better.
- **Увидимся вечером в девять часов.** — We will see each other in the evening at nine o'clock.

## Tomorrow

- **Завтра будет слишком поздно.** — Tomorrow will be too late.

> **Watch out:** a woman says *я была*, a man says **я был**. The past tense shows who is speaking.
