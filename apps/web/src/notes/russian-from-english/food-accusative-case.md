In this lesson: **food**, **meals**, and the ending for **the thing you eat or buy**.

## Eating: an irregular verb

- **Я ем хлеб.** — I am eating bread.
- **Ты ешь яйцо?** — Are you eating an egg?
- **Она ест мясо.** — She is eating meat.

## The thing you act on

The thing a verb acts on is the object (grammar books call its form the accusative case). Masculine and neuter things don't change:

- **Он любит молоко.** — He likes milk.
- **Они покупают масло и хлеб.** — They are buying butter and bread.

Feminine words in **‑а** change it to **‑у**, and so does a describing word in front of them:

- **Он ест сладкую еду.** — He eats sweet food. (**еда** → **еду**)
- **Мы видим твою пиццу на столе.** — We see your pizza on the table.
- **Ты читаешь книгу и завтракаешь?** — Are you reading a book and having breakfast?

## Meals are verbs

- **Мы ужинаем.** — We are having dinner.
- **Они завтракают.** — They are having breakfast.

## Having something

Russian says "by you there is": **у** + person + **есть**:

- **У тебя есть масло?** — Do you have butter?

> **Watch out:** **есть** means two things: "to eat" (**Она ест мясо**) and "there is" (**У тебя есть масло?**).
