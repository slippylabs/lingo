In this lesson: **transport** and the verb for **going by vehicle**.

## Going by vehicle: ехать

Russian has one verb for going on foot (**идти**) and another for going by vehicle (**ехать**):

- **Автобус едет в центр.** — The bus goes to the center.
- **Поезд едет на станцию.** — The train goes to the station.
- **Шофёр едет быстро.** — The driver drives fast.

A road doesn't move, but Russian still says it "goes" with **идёт**:

- **Дорога идёт через лес.** — The road goes through the forest.

## "Is broken", "is open"

Short describing forms say what state something is in:

- **Велосипед сломан.** — The bicycle is broken.
- **Станция открыта.** — The station is open.
- **Скорость ограничена.** — The speed is limited.

## Traffic

- **Светофор показывает красный.** — The traffic light shows red.
- **Мост связывает два города.** — The bridge connects two cities.

> **Watch out:** the everyday word for a car is **машина**, a feminine word: **Машина новая и красивая.** You may also see *авто*, which is neuter and never changes its ending.
