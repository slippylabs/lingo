In this lesson: **animals** and the ending for **"to someone"**.

## Giving to someone: the dative case

The person or animal you give, show or bring something to takes the "to" ending, called the dative case:

- **Я дам коту рыбу.** — I will give the cat a fish. (masculine: **кот** → **коту**)
- **Он помогает медведю.** — He helps the bear. (**медведь** → **медведю**)
- **Он помогает птице.** — He helps the bird. (feminine: **птица** → **птице**)
- **Мы даём лягушке хлеб.** — We give bread to the frog.

More than one ends in **‑ам** or **‑ям**:

- **Мы помогаем птицам.** — We help the birds.
- **Они несут медведям еду.** — They are carrying food to the bears.

## Helping

**помогать** ("to help") also takes the "to" ending:

- **Помогаем волку.** — We help the wolf.

## Giving now, giving later

**даём** is "we give" (now or often). **дам** and **даст** describe one completed act, so they mean "will give":

- **Мы даём коню воду.** — We give the horse water.
- **Он даст мыши сыр.** — He will give the mouse cheese.
- **Ты дашь мне рыбу?** — Will you give me a fish?

> **Watch out:** **мышь** is feminine even without **‑а**, so "to the mouse" is **мыши**: **Она несёт мыши сыр.**
