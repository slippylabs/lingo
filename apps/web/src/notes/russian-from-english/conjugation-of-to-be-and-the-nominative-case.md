In this lesson: **"to be"**, the words for **I, you, he, she**, and **this / that**.

## No word for "am", "is" or "are"

In the present tense Russian leaves out "to be". You put the two parts side by side:

- **Я мудрый.** — I am wise.
- **Он добрый друг** — He is a good friend
- **Это мой дом.** — This is my house.
- **Кто ты?** — Who are you?

To say "not", put **не** in front: **Это не моя сестра.** — This is not my sister.

## I, you, he, she

| | one | more than one |
|---|---|---|
| I / we | **я** | **мы** |
| you | **ты** | **вы** |
| he, she, it / they | **он**, **она**, **оно** | **они** |

**ты** is "you" for one person you know well. **вы** is for several people, or to be polite to one:

- **Вы добры.** — You are good.

## Describing words match the noun

Every Russian noun is masculine, feminine or neuter (its "gender", a grammar group). A describing word (an adjective) takes a matching ending:

- **Это добрый человек.** — This is a good person. (masculine **‑ый**)
- **Эта женщина добрая.** — This woman is good. (feminine **‑ая**)
- **Это добрые друзья?** — Are these good friends? (more than one: **‑ые**)

## This and that

On its own, **это** means "this is" or "these are". In front of a noun, "this" matches the noun: **этот**, **эта**, **это**. "That" is **тот** or **та**:

- **Это моя сестра.** — This is my sister.
- **Эта женщина добрая.** — This woman is good.
- **Тот мужчина милый.** — That man is kind.
- **Та женщина высокая.** — That woman is tall.
- **Вон тот дом красивый.** — That house over there is beautiful.

> **Watch out:** don't add a word for "is". **Я мудрый** is the whole sentence.
