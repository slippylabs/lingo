In this lesson: **nature words** and small words for **through**, **between**, **into** and **on**.

## Where something is vs where it goes

**в** and **на** with **‑е** say where something is. With the normal form (or **‑у** for feminine **‑а** words) they say where something goes:

- **Мы плаваем в реке.** — We swim in the river. (where)
- **Вода течёт в реку.** — Water flows into the river. (where to)
- **Он стоит на камне.** — He is standing on the stone.
- **Мы идём в лес.** — We are going to the forest.

## Through, between, towards

- **Река течёт через долину.** — The river flows through the valley. (**через** + **‑у**)
- **Долина лежит между горами.** — The valley lies between the mountains. (**между** + "with" form)
- **Идём к дубу.** — We are going towards the oak. (**к** + "to" form)

## Growing and blooming

- **Трава растёт быстро.** — The grass grows fast.
- **Роза растёт в поле.** — The rose grows in the field.
- **Цветы красиво цветут.** — The flowers bloom beautifully.

> **Watch out:** **в реке** is "in the river", **в реку** is "into the river". The ending tells you which.
