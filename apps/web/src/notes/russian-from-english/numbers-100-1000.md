In this lesson: **hundreds** and **a thousand**.

## The hundreds

- **сто** — one hundred
- **двести** — two hundred
- **триста** — three hundred
- **четыреста** — four hundred
- **пятьсот** — five hundred
- **девятьсот** — nine hundred
- **тысяча** — thousand

## The noun after hundreds

Round hundreds take the plural "of" form:

- **двести женщин** — two hundred women
- **четыреста котов** — four hundred cats

As with smaller numbers, the last word decides:

- **семьсот один велосипед** — seven hundred one bicycles
- **двести три компьютера** — two hundred three computers
- **триста четыре вещи** — three hundred four things

## Thousand works like a noun

**тысяча** changes like any feminine word in **‑а**: after **две** it becomes **тысячи**:

- **две тысячи девятьсот псов** — two thousand nine hundred dogs

> **Watch out:** 200 is **двести**, 300 and 400 end in **‑ста**, and 500 to 900 end in **‑сот**.
