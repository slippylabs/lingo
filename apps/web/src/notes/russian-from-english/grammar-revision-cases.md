In this lesson: a **review of the six cases**, the different endings a noun takes for its job in a sentence.

## One noun, six jobs

- **Глаза — зеркало души.** — Eyes are the mirror of the soul. (the subject: nominative)
- **Брата тут нет.** — There is no brother here. ("of" or "no": genitive)
- **Я дам это жене.** — I will give it to my wife. ("to": dative)
- **Он видит свою жену.** — He sees his wife. (the object: accusative)
- **Мы говорим с друзьями.** — We are talking with friends. ("with": instrumental)
- **Она в городе.** — She is in the city. (place: locative)

The locative is also used after **о** ("about"):

- **Она говорит о братьях.** — She talks about brothers.

## "No" and "many" take the "of" form

- **У него нет имени.** — He has no name. (**имя** → **имени** is irregular)
- **В доме больше нет бананов.** — There are no more bananas in the house.
- **Во вселенной много солнц.** — There are many suns in the universe.

> **Watch out:** one word can look very different from job to job: *жена*, **жене**, **жену**, **женой** are all "wife".
