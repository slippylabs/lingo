In this lesson: **fruit**, **tastes and colours**, and **plurals**.

## Describing words match the fruit

- **Банан жёлтый.** — The banana is yellow. (masculine **‑ый**)
- **Груша вкусная.** — The pear is tasty. (feminine **‑ая**)
- **Яблоко красное.** — The apple is red. (neuter **‑ое**)
- **Апельсины сладкие.** — The oranges are sweet. (more than one: **‑ие**)

After the letters *к, г, х, ж, ш, ч, щ* Russian writes *и*, never *ы*. That's why it's **сладкие** but **красные**.

## More than one

Most words add **‑ы** or **‑и**:

- **Груши лежат на столе.** — The pears are lying on the table.
- **Ягоды лежат в миске.** — The berries are in the bowl.

## Juice from fruit

- **Она пьёт сок из апельсинов.** — She is drinking orange juice. (literally "juice from oranges")

> **Watch out:** **виноград** is one word for the whole bunch, so it stays singular: **Мы покупаем виноград.** — We are buying grapes.
