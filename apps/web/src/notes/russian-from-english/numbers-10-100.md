In this lesson: **tens up to 100** and **numbers made of two words**.

## The tens

- **двадцать** — twenty
- **тридцать** — thirty
- **сорок** — forty
- **пятьдесят** — fifty
- **девяносто** — ninety
- **сто** — one hundred

## The last word decides

In a number made of several words, the noun's ending follows the last word:

- **сто один велосипед** — one hundred one bicycles (ends in 1: normal form)
- **девяносто три компьютера** — ninety three computers (ends in 3: singular "of" form)
- **сто два солнца** — one hundred two suns
- **семьдесят восемь рублей** — seventy eight rubles (ends in 8: plural "of" form)

Round tens take the plural "of" form:

- **сорок мужчин** — forty men
- **шестьдесят котов** — sixty cats

> **Watch out:** **сорок** and **девяносто** don't follow the pattern of the other tens. Learn them on their own.
