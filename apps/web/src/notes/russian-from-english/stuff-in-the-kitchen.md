In this lesson: **kitchen things**, the **tool you use**, and **full of**.

## The tool: just the "with" ending

For the thing you use, Russian changes the ending and adds no word for "with":

- **Он ест вилкой.** — He eats with a fork. (**вилка** → **вилкой**)
- **Чашка наполнена водой.** — The cup is filled with water.

## Full of, a lot of: the "of" form

- **Тарелка полна еды.** — The plate is full of food.
- **Ложка полна перца.** — The spoon is full of pepper.
- **В миске много сахара.** — There is a lot of sugar in the bowl.

## In, on, out of

- **Мы готовим в кастрюле.** — We cook in a pot.
- **На сковородке есть мясо.** — There is meat in the frying pan.
- **Она пьёт из чашки.** — She drinks from a cup. (**из** + "of" form)

## Describing

- **Нож острый.** — The knife is sharp.
- **Банка пустая.** — The can is empty.

> **Watch out:** **с** means "together with" a person. For a tool, use the ending alone: **Он ест вилкой**.
