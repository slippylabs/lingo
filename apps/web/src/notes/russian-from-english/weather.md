In this lesson: **weather**, and sentences with **no "it"**, like "It is warm".

## "It is warm": one word

Russian has no "it" in these sentences:

- **Сегодня тепло.** — It is warm today.
- **В России холодно.** — In Russia it is cold.
- **Сегодня облачно.** — It is cloudy today.

## "It is raining": the rain goes

- **Идёт дождь.** — It is raining. (literally "rain goes")
- **Падают снег и дождь.** — Snow and rain are falling.

## What the weather does

- **Солнце светит.** — The sun is shining.
- **Ветер дует сильно.** — The wind is blowing strongly.
- **Погода изменяется.** — The weather is changing.

## Describing the weather

- **Погода хорошая.** — The weather is good.
- **Мороз сильный.** — The frost is strong.
- **Облака тёмные.** — The clouds are dark.

> **Watch out:** **тепло** is "it is warm" on its own. With a noun use the describing word **тёплый**: **Юг тёплый.** — The south is warm.
