In this lesson: **hobbies** and the **present tense** of verbs.

## The ending shows who

A Russian verb changes its ending for each person:

- **Я читаю книгу.** — I am reading a book. (я: **‑ю**)
- **Ты смотришь сериал?** — Are you watching a series? (ты: **‑шь**)
- **Она пишет письма.** — She is writing letters. (он, она: **‑т**)
- **Мы смотрим фильм.** — We are watching a film. (мы: **‑м**)
- **Вы учите язык.** — You are learning a language. (вы: **‑те**)
- **Они слушают музыку.** — They are listening to music. (они: **‑ют** or **‑ут**)

Because the ending already says who, the "we" word can be left out: **Учимся здесь.** — We are learning here.

## One form for "I read" and "I am reading"

Russian has one present tense. **Я читаю книгу** is both "I read a book" and "I am reading a book".

## Playing a game or an instrument

- **Он играет в футбол.** — He is playing football. (a game: **в**)
- **Мы играем в шахматы.** — We are playing chess.
- **Она играет на гитаре.** — She is playing the guitar. (an instrument: **на**)

> **Watch out:** some verbs change inside, not just at the end: **писать** becomes **пишет**, and **петь** becomes **поёт** (**Он поёт песни.** — He is singing songs).
