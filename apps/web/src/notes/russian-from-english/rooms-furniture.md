In this lesson: **rooms and furniture**, and words for **where things are**: under, above, near.

## Standing, lying, hanging

Russian says how a thing is placed. Furniture **стоит** (stands), a book **лежит** (lies), a shelf **висит** (hangs):

- **Стол стоит в середине комнаты.** — The table stands in the middle of the room.
- **На столе лежит книга.** — A book is lying on the table.
- **Полка висит на стене.** — The shelf hangs on the wall.

## Under, above, near

Each of these words asks for its own ending:

- **Сундук стоит под кроватью.** — The chest is under the bed. (**под** + "with" form)
- **Лампа светит над столом.** — The lamp shines above the table. (**над** + "with" form)
- **Стул стоит около стола.** — The chair is near the table. (**около** + "of" form)

## In the wardrobe

A few masculine words end in stressed **‑у** after **в** or **на**:

- **В шкафу много одежды.** — There are many clothes in the wardrobe.

## Open and closed

- **Окно открыто.** — The window is open.
- **Двери закрыты.** — The doors are closed.
- **В комнате два окна.** — The room has two windows.

> **Watch out:** a table or a chair **стоит**, but a book or a plate **лежит**. Russian doesn't use one word for "is" here.
