## Eating and drinking

German verbs change their ending with the person:

| | essen (eat) | trinken (drink) |
|---|---|---|
| I — **ich** | **esse** | *trinke* |
| he / she / Josef | **isst** | **trinkt** |

- **Ich esse Nudeln** — I eat pasta
- **Josef isst Brot** — Joseph eats bread
- **Cäcilie trinkt Wasser** — Cecilia drinks water
- **Das Paar trinkt Bier** — The couple drinks beer

*essen* is irregular: the *e* becomes **i** for he/she: **isst**.

## Genders

**das Brot**, **das Paar** (neuter), **die Milch** (feminine). **die Nudeln** is plural — like English "noodles".

## At the table

- **Bitte** — please: **Brot, bitte** — Bread, please
- **Guten Appetit!** — Enjoy your meal!
