In this lesson: **food and drink**, **eating and drinking**, and **please**.

## Eating and drinking

The verb's ending changes depending on who is doing it:

- **ich esse** — I eat
- **Josef isst** — Joseph eats
- **Cäcilie trinkt** — Cäcilie drinks

For he, she or a name, the verb usually ends in **‑t**.

- **Ich esse Nudeln** — I eat pasta
- **Josef isst Brot** — Joseph eats bread
- **Cäcilie trinkt Wasser** — Cäcilie drinks water
- **Das Paar trinkt Bier** — The couple drinks beer

## The foods and their "the"

- **das Brot** — the bread
- **das Paar** — the couple
- **die Milch** — the milk
- **die Nudeln** — the pasta (more than one, like English "noodles")

## Please

- **Bitte** — Please
- **Brot, bitte** — Bread, please
- **Guten Appetit!** — Enjoy your meal!

> **Watch out:** **essen** (to eat) doesn't follow the pattern: the **e** turns into **i** for he or she, so it's **isst**, not *esst*.
