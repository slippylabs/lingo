# About this copy

LibreLingo is a community-owned language-learning platform. This site runs a
Slippy Labs fork of [LibreLingoRelive](https://codeberg.org/LibreLingoRelive/LibreLingoRelive),
source at [github.com/slippylabs/lingo](https://github.com/slippylabs/lingo) (AGPL-3.0).

## How it teaches

- **Teach first.** A lesson introduces a few new words (picture, sound, meaning
  and an example sentence) before any exercise asks for them.
- **Harder as you go.** Each word moves from recognising it, to reading it, to
  typing it and hearing it.
- **Spaced review.** Every word has its own review schedule: right answers push
  the next review further out (1, 3, 7, 16, 35, 90 days); a miss brings it back
  sooner. A word is *mastered* after four right reviews in a row.
- **Your plan.** Your goal (travel, conversation, reading or a bit of
  everything) sets the mix of exercises; your daily minutes set how many new
  words each day. If reviews pile up, new words wait until you catch up.
