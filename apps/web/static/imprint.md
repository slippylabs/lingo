# About this copy

LibreLingo is a community-owned language-learning platform. This site runs
the LibreLingoRelive fork (https://codeberg.org/LibreLingoRelive/LibreLingoRelive)
with Slippy Labs changes, source at https://github.com/slippylabs/lingo
(AGPL-3.0). Courses are written by their own authors and keep their own
licences; each course's "Course Feedback" link goes to its repository.

Lesson audio: Piper text-to-speech voices (https://github.com/rhasspy/piper),
generated locally. Voice credits are listed in the repository's
VOICES-CREDITS.md.

Hosted by Slippy Labs: https://slippylabs.com/
