# Privacy

This copy of LibreLingo is hosted by Slippy Labs at lingo.slippylabs.com.

- **No account, no sign-up.** Your progress — the words you've learned, their
  review dates, your study plan, your daily counts — is saved only in this
  browser's local storage. It is never uploaded, and clearing your site data
  erases it.
- **Progress files stay with you.** "Download progress" saves a file to your
  own device; "Load a progress file" reads it back in your browser. Neither
  sends anything to us.
- **No cloud AI.** Lesson audio was generated ahead of time on our own server
  with the open-source Piper text-to-speech engine. Nothing you type or say is
  sent to any speech or AI service.
- There are no analytics, ads or trackers in this app.

The full Slippy Labs privacy policy: https://slippylabs.com/privacy.html
