# Privacy

This copy of LibreLingo is hosted by Slippy Labs at lingo.slippylabs.com.

- No account, no sign-up. Your lesson progress is saved only in this
  browser's local storage. It is never uploaded, and clearing your site
  data erases it.
- Lesson audio was generated ahead of time on our own server with the
  open-source Piper text-to-speech engine. Nothing you type or say is sent
  to any speech or AI service.
- There are no analytics, ads or trackers in this app.

The full Slippy Labs privacy policy: https://slippylabs.com/privacy.html
