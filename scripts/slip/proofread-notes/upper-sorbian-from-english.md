  Slavic course except Ukrainian). The exported JSON carries "undefined" as the definition, so every word

- wnuknja (granddaughter; standard wnučka?), dźwidr "doors" (standard durje), čmjatwa used for BOTH
  weather and season (weather = wjedro, season = počasy/doba?), mróčk (cloud = mróčel), plawanja/plawać
  (swim = płuwać), kósna (basketball), jagoda = strawberry (berry), kastanowy = brown (bruny),

  tanćować vs "tancuju" (dance = rejować), wstać (wostanje) = to rise and "Słónco wostanje na wuchodźe"
  (wostać = remain; rise = schadźeć), kupec = customer, ser vs syr for cheese.
- Negation of chcyć: "nechce" (standard nochce) in Drinks and Character.
- Dative of pos: course has both "posu" and "pasej" (standard psej).
- Instrumental fem. possessives inconsistent: z twojej sotru / z mojeju babku.
- "Wona je lěkar" (female: lěkarka); "Dźowka njesy běłu sukńu" (njesć = carry, not wear; njese?).

  "w ... hodźinje", the phrases "wo". Cardinals used as ordinals (triceći muž = "thirtieth man").

  dual is mój smój / wój staj / wonaj staj; the note's forms look invented. Left as is.

  instrumental takes no z. Note "a a" conjunction section looks invented.
