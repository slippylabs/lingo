# church-slavonic-from-english: unsure / not fixed

Resolved on 2026-10-19: глаголати/ꙗсти paradigms, тъ copula, зѣло, часъ clock times,
conditional бꙑ/бꙑшѧ and the single items. What is left needs a Church Slavonic reader:

- **казати (кажєтъ)** is taught as "to show" (its Church Slavonic sense) but used as "tell/order":
  "Она кажєтъ пьсꙋ" = She says to the dog, "Онъ гнѣвьно ѥмꙋ кажєтъ работати" = He angrily tells him to work,
  "Оучитєль кажєтъ оучєникамъ дѣлати". CS казати also means "teach, command", so the uses may stand, but
  "She says to the dog" is not a sentence anyone would teach. Needs a reader to choose the verb and the English.
- **"Тꙑмъ кътори лєжѧтъ сѧ спати спокойноѥго съна."** (To those who are going to sleep, a peaceful dream) is
  garbled: тꙑмъ (dative plural is тѣмъ), лєжѧтъ сѧ, spelling спокойноѥго. No attested Church Slavonic wording
  of the wish was found to replace it with.
- The course as a whole is a transliterated Polish/Interslavic template, not real Church Slavonic; vocabulary
  such as кошꙋлꙗ, тꙑжьдьнь, бързо, вилица are modern Slavic words in Church Slavonic letters.
