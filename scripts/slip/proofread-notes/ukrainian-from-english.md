# Ukrainian: plausible but not fixed

- Western/dialect or colloquial words left as is: стрий (paternal uncle; standard дядько), помагати/помогла
  (standard допомагати), грушка (груша), кусень (шматок), торговище (ринок/базар), роза (троянда), прошу as
  "please" (будь ласка), перо for "pen" (ручка), каштановий for "brown" (коричневий; fine for a horse).
- Many sentences keep a redundant present-tense є ("Гора є висока"). Grammatical but unnatural; the course
  notes themselves say є is normally omitted. Not changed (dozens of items).
- Archaic "Я єсмь / Ти єси / Вони суть" listed in the first note as Ukrainian archaic forms: these are really
  Church Slavonic. Left.
- "Вона говорить слов'янською мовою" / "знає російську і слов'янську": "the Slavic language" is a template
  leftover (Interslavic course); no such language. Left, it is course design.
- "Мій записник повний записів з пером" ("full of notes with a pen") is odd but parses. Left.
- "Він дає собі спокій" = "He stops troubling others": idiom is closer to "he lets it go / gives himself a
  rest". Left.
- "Новий рік є в грудні" (New Year is in December): arguable. Left.
- "Вона є лікарем", "Мій брат є вчителем": є + instrumental is accepted formal Ukrainian. Left (only the bare
  instrumental with no verb was fixed).
- Stress marks in the notes have a few slips beyond the ones fixed (e.g. учи́тел**я́**). Left.
- Every English mini-dictionary has noun-for-verb hints beyond the ones fixed (e.g. "drinking: пиття",
  "talking: говорить" is fine). I fixed those that point at the wrong word in the lesson.
