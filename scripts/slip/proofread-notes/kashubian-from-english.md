# kashubian-from-english: unsure / not fixed

Items a native speaker should check. Everything else from the first proofread was resolved on 2026-10-19
(see course_fixes.json; sources were Wiktionary, csb.wikipedia.org, pl.wikibooks Kaszubski and pansolo.pl).

- weekdays: "Móm w stëcznikù warkòwé. = I have vacation in January." *warkòwé* is not a Kashubian word for vacation that any
  source attests; the right word (*ùrlop*? *wòlné*?) is unconfirmed.
- greetings: "Dôj! = Let's go!" *dôj* is the imperative of *dac* (give). The Kashubian "let's go" could not be confirmed.
- grammar revision: "Tim chtërni leżą spac spòkòjnégò sënã. = To those who are going to sleep, a peaceful dream." is garbled
  (leżą = they lie; the genitive wish is odd).
- numbers: "jeden chłôpczã" (one boy) — if *chłôpczã* is neuter like *dzéwczã* it should be *jedno/jedné chłôpczã*; no source found
  for *chłôpczã* at all. The number skills still say *wieszcz* (thing) where *rzecz* is now taught in the pronouns skill; its plural and
  genitive plural (*rzeczë*? *rzeczi*?) were not confirmed, so the number phrases were left.
- *dwadzescë* (20) was chosen from pansolo.pl and pl.wikibooks; csb.wikipedia uses *dwadzesce* more often (38 hits). Both are valid
  spellings; consider adding the other as an accepted answer.
- *słuncé* (sun) everywhere in the course; Wiktionary spells *słuńce*. The new genitive plural *słuńców* follows Wiktionary.
- Adjective spelling: the course writes most adjectives with -i (sëlni, òdwôżni, brunétni, smaczni) while Wiktionary has -y after n
  (zelony, czôrny, czerwòny); only the forms named above were changed.
- Future of bëc: the course has *bãdzësz / bãdzëce*; Wiktionary gives *bãdzesz / bãdzece*.
- Past tense "Dzysô të jes nie miôł gòdzëne za to" and "Jô jem béł w dodomù": the compound past with *jem/jes* is literary/archaic
  (pl.wikibooks lesson 7); plain *jô béł* is the everyday form.
