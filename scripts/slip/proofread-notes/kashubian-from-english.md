# kashubian-from-english: unsure / systemic (NOT in fixes.py)

## Systemic (whole course; needs a decision or a native speaker)
- **"undefined" hints, all 16 Slavic courses.** Every mini-dictionary gloss is literally `undefined`
  (4,318 in this course), and the export ships them: a chip hint reads e.g. "kòt\nundefined", and "give" → "undefined".
  Best fixed once in code (drop definitions equal to "undefined" in the exporter or the hint UI), not per entry.
- **Pronoun spelling.** Standard Kashubian is jô / òn; the course writes Ja (35×) and On (107×) in phrases, but
  Jô, Òna, Òni elsewhere. Left as is: changing 140 phrases is a call for the parent.
- **"very" = wiele (28×).** Kashubian "very" is *baro*; *wiele* is "much/many". Left.
- **Verb "to have":** 1sg written mëm (3×) and Ja nie mô (should be jô móm/môm?). Course also has Móm. Unsure of form.
- **Numbers disagree between note and exercises.** Course: sedem, òsem, dwiesce, trzësta, dwadzescãt, trzidzescãt,
  jednasce, sedemnasc, osemnasc, zérod. Note: sétmë, òsmë, dwie sta, trzëdzescë, jedenôscë, sétmënôscë, null. I believe
  the note is closer to real Kashubian (sétmë, òsmë), so I did not "fix" either side.
- **2pl of bëc:** course "jeste"; I think standard is "jesce". Note now matches the course (jeste).
- **Conjunctions note** teaches ë (and) / abò (or); exercises use i / czë.
- **Comparison note** teaches jak/niż and wëżi; exercises use wicy … jakno.
- **Ordinals note** uses òb + locative; exercises use ò.
- Notes are full of words that look invented or non-Kashubian: juńdżi (other), Pòkné, cékaższô, Westerdô (yesterday;
  the course says wczora), stëny (100th), kamraci, sztudént, mòwic. A rewrite in our own format would be better than patching.
