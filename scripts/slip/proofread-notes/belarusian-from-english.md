# Belarusian: things NOT fixed (unsure, or needs a decision)

- "Ён дае сабе спакой" = "He stops troubling others" (grammar-revision pronouns): looks like a calque of Polish
  "dać sobie spokój" (give up / let it go). ТСБМ lists no "даць сабе спакой" idiom; a native speaker should say
  whether it is natural and what it means.
- The course is a word-for-word adaptation of the Interslavic template; a native review would still find
  unnatural (but grammatical) phrasing, e.g. "Мост ідзе праз раку", "Ён меў адну гадзіну".
